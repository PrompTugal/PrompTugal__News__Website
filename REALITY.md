# Reality: what exists (site v0.1.9)

A thing that is not in this file does not exist, whatever a brief says. Kept current by whoever ships; CI updates the version above on every release.

## Exists (5 November 2026)

- The repository SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Newsroom__Web-Summit, branch `dev`, served at https://web-summit.newsroom.sgit.ai (CNAME in the repository; DNS pointed at GitHub Pages). `main` holds only the initial commit and is not protected.
- The build (`admin/build/build.mjs`, no dependencies): since 5 November a small core with modules in `admin/build/lib/` (the page shell alone in `lib/shell.mjs`, for the design agent) and one module per section in `admin/build/features/` (content, articles, agenda, companies, events, people, organisations, topics, places, api, versions, 404), auto-loaded in order; the nav, footer and llms.txt groups come from `data/site/nav.json`. A new feature is one file plus at most one line there; the contract is `admin/build/README.md`. Every page carries `<meta name="page-type">` and `<meta name="page-date">`.
- The gate (`admin/build/validate.js`, 13 checks, prints OK), with the key-leak shapes in `admin/build/secrets.cjs` (copied from the team's site). Check 1 refuses any ignored file the build did not write; check 7 (no contact details) covers `content/`, `data/` and `desks/` (`desks/` is served as is); check 8 refuses an article title of 12 words or more; check 11 validates every data file against its schema, every reference between entities, and that the derived files are fresh; check 12 runs each feature's own checks (the graphs keep the ontology's grammar, every view is a part of the whole, the API manifest matches); check 13 wants page-type and page-date on every page.
- CI: `.github/workflows/deploy-pages.yml` runs the gate on every push and pull request; on a push to `dev`, `admin/build/tag_release.sh` numbers, commits, tags and deploys the release. `admin/tools/release.sh` is the release script for agents.
- The look (since 5 November, after `design/`, newsroom-designer@web-summit.dinis): **The Lisbon Wire** (a working name, set only in `data/site/site.json`), "The independent newsroom at Web Summit Lisbon 2026", "Powered by RiskMandate" with RiskMandate's mark (`assets/brand/riskmandate-mark.svg`, copied unchanged from riskmandate.ai), our own tile mark (`assets/brand/mark.svg`); paper, ink and azulejo cobalt with a dark mode (`assets/tokens.css`); Newsreader, IBM Plex Mono and IBM Plex Sans self-hosted in `assets/fonts/` (SIL OFL); navigation Now, Agenda, News, Companies, People, Portugal, About (Dinis Cruz, 5 November), Admin as a utility link once `/admin/` exists; "Independent. Not affiliated with Web Summit." and the RiskMandate disclosure in every footer. Components come from layout hooks in the markdown (`::: class`, `{.class}`, dropped from the twins); the shell renders three empty mount points (`#nr-tools`, `#nr-live`, `#nr-pane`) for the browser features. Contract: `admin/build/README.md`, "The look".
- Pages: the front page (the agenda from the build's day to 12 November, at most six highlights a day; the latest articles; the companies), the summit (`/summit/`), the full agenda (`/summit/agenda.html`, every item linked to its event page and its source), the companies index and a profile per company (`/companies/<id>/`, since 5 November with its sessions, its people, the companies related to it through a shared event or field, and its JSON), the events (`/events/`: the official programme and, apart, the others, by day; a page per event), the people (`/people/`, name, role and organisation as listed), the organisations (`/organisations/`), the topics (`/topics/`, the industry map in text), the places (`/places/`), the data (`/api/`), Portugal (`/portugal/`: the research desk's Portugal captures, the Portuguese companies, the events in Portugal around the summit and the summit sessions involving Portugal), the articles index and one article, about, the participant disclosure, the RiskMandate page, the brief pack (`/brief/`, ten pages), the release history, a 404: 879 pages. During the summit (9 to 12 November) a live strip under the sections shows what is on now and next, filled in the reader's browser by `assets/live/live.js` from `api/v1/live/agenda.json`. Each with a markdown twin, a version at the top and an agent block at the end; `llms.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`.
- The data (`data/`, described in `data/README.md`): one folder per entity, JSON, 24 schemas in `data/schemas/`, the grammar in `data/ontology.json` (13 types in four worlds, 20 verbs each with a distinct inverse). Hand-edited: `companies/` (109, five files each), `places/` (77), `topics/` (17), `curation/`, `site/`. Derived from the desks' files by `admin/tools/derive.mjs` and committed: `events/` (211: 187 official, 24 other), `people/` (248), `organisations/` (188), `evidence/` (26 captures), `stories/` (9 candidates) and `matching.json` (67 speakers' organisation names matched to a company, 156 not, 0 near misses left open, 2 company sessions not in the programme).
- The JSON API (`/api/v1/`, built at every release, never committed): an index and a file per entity, one graph per company, event and person, the industry, summit and people graphs, the whole graph (1,441 nodes, 2,510 edges; also as Cytoscape elements and N-Triples), the ontology, search documents and a manifest with every file's SHA-256 and schema.
- The agenda: `desks/programme/agenda.json` (210 items, 5 to 12 November, read 4 November by programme@web-summit.vicky), derived into `data/events/` and rendered by the build at every release. "Today" is the build's day, so the front page moves on only when something is released.
- The companies: `data/companies/<id>/` (109 companies), made by `admin/tools/companies.mjs` from the team's `data/companies.json` (read 1 November by the team's curator): public fields only, the team's notes left out, and this newsroom's re-check of each company's summit page and website on 5 November (`recheck.json`). It replaced `data/companies.json` and `admin/tools/companies.py` on 5 November.
- Articles: `content/articles/newsroom-status-four-days-out.md` (5 November), signed with the newsroom's session key (`articles/newsroom-status-four-days-out.md.sig`).
- The newsroom's session key: made 5 November 2026 (`pki-agent.mjs keygen --session`), record sha256:2cbb6cfafdcbbce7, encryption sha256:aea91e7f8f429363, linked locally to the operator's key sha256:db7f79fdd87e030a; the public half is the entry `newsroom-dinis` in the team site's `data/keys.json` (team site v0.2.429). It dies with the session; the next session makes a new one.
- The desks (brief 4): Vicky's researcher (into the research vault, in pack mode, four runs a day), curator (`desks/curator/`, two maps, nine story candidates) and programme desk (`desks/programme/`, and the interview shortlist in its confidential vault, never in this repository). Their reports are on the team's site, `people/vicky/reports/`.
- `CLAUDE.md`, `BRIEF.md` (the brief in full), `BRIEF-CORRECTIONS.md`.
