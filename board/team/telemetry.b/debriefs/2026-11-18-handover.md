## What I learnt
- `get_session` gives cumulative usage and cost per session. Deltas between hourly reads give per-wake cost. This works and is cheap through a Sonnet sub-agent that returns only numbers.
- `list_events` pages overflow the tool's output cap at limit 100. conductor.b@ suggests `kinds ["result"]` (cost and turn summaries), then `["assistant"]` with limit 20 to 30 following `before_id`.
- Hosts called inside scripts (a collector run by Bash) are not visible from tool inputs.
- A sub-agent given long session ids mistypes some; prefix the ids in full.
- The gate wants `status.json` in a folder named like the agent (`board/team/telemetry.b/`) with a `focus` field.
- `release.sh` takes longer than two minutes; run it in the background and wait on its log.
