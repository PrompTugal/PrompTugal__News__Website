The sales cycle, in Dinis's words

See it, ask for more, pay for it

1. They see something we madea company page, the Brazil story, a graph, the vault
2. They ask"can I have more of these?" "can you change this?" "can you do this for me?"
3. We send a link or a billa Stripe Payment Link, or an invoice, sent by a person
4. We make itagents do the work, a person checks, a person hands it over
5. They ask for changesand a change has a price

The first five or six are one-off jobs. When a seventh person asks for the same thing, it becomes a package with a link.

Dinis: "we do it for 5 or 6. And then if we have the 7 asking, we just have ... a sale."

The line that lets everything else exist

What we never sell

A buyer can buy a service made for them.
They can never buy what the publication says.

No coverage. No ranking. No removal of a fact. No favourable article. The editors decide what is published, and the page that offers any service says so in one sentence.

  • No ads. "We have no ads."
  • No selling readers. Privacy first: no cookies, no tracking.
  • No paywall. The meter never blocks.
  • No private facts. Public and professional only; no contact details, ever.
  • No agent sends a bill. A person does.

Stripe, from its own documentation (read 6 November 2026)

Two tools, two kinds of sale

Payment Link: the menu

Reusable; anyone with the link can pay. One-time or subscription; customer-chooses-price; adjustable quantity; receipts and refunds without code. A subscription's buyer can cancel in Stripe's customer portal.

For: top-ups, the vault month, B1, B3, B4, E1 to E3.

Invoice: the named job

For one customer. Can start from a quote, take part payments, be emailed from the dashboard or shared as a link.

For: the delegation schedule, a graph, the professional services packs, a workshop.

A Payment Link's address is public by design and may sit in the register. A Stripe key, a webhook secret or a customer's detail never does.

docs.stripe.com/payment-links · docs.stripe.com/customer-management

Turning a package on

Back pocket first, live on the seventh ask

  1. Dinis's yes, in the session.
  2. The row's "needs before live" cleared or accepted.
  3. A Stripe link (menu) or an invoice template (named job), its public address in the register.
  4. One sentence on the site: what it is, what is not included, that nothing buys coverage.
  5. A line in REALITY.md once it runs. The site says only what runs.

My suggestion: the vault month (by hand) and your schedule first, as they are the smallest and Dinis's own; a summit brief the moment someone asks; the rest on the seventh ask.
