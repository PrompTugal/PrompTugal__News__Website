# The event personalisation platform

## What Dinis said

"One of the things that we're also creating here is an amazing personalisation and event management platform, which is kind of what we're creating by proxy ... something that we could sell for other events, but let's get it right for this event." And, in the same breath, the thing it rests on: a passkey sign-in to his vault so the experience is the same on every device, and "creating the schedule, mapping up what's going to happen here and mapping up my personalised environment ... this should become the best scheduling and even personalisation that happens on this event."

## What it means for the commercial side

1. **It is the flagship.** The pieces already on the shelf (the event site, the vault, the personal schedule and debrief, the sourced graph) are the parts of one product. The products page now leads its publishing shelf with it: *A personalisation and event management platform, for your event* (`products.json`, id `event-platform`).
2. **Right for this event first.** Nothing is sold as a product until this summit has shown it works; the order is what Dinis said. What can be sold now is the bespoke piece (a hand-made schedule or debrief, rows B1, B2 and E5 in the register).
3. **What must be true before it is a product** (the test, not a date):
   - a person can pick who they are and get a schedule, and it is saved (browser first, then the vault);
   - the same schedule opens on a second device by passkey, with the vault key issued (today it cannot be created);
   - a debrief exists for at least one real person, sourced;
   - the people who used it at the summit say, unprompted, that they would use it again, and some say what it was worth.
4. **Who buys it:** an organiser of another event, an agency running one, a delegation or a company that sends people to many. A conference app is a crowded market; what this one has that others may not is the sourced graph under the schedule, the vault the attendee owns, and the debrief afterwards. That is a hypothesis; the summit week is the test.
5. **How it is priced (proposals):** Starter 500, Team 1,500, Build 5,000 pounds, the same ladder as every product, and for a real event a Build conversation with a named scope. After the summit, a per-event price can be set from what the summit cost us in agent time and what attendees would pay (the newsreel tests, E5).
