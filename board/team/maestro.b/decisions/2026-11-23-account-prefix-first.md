## The convention

The account letter comes first: `<account>.<role>@<Domain>.dinis`. For example, `hub.b (SovereignWebsite)` becomes `b.hub (SovereignWebsite)`, and `maestro.b (account-b)` becomes `b.maestro (account-b)`. In a sidebar or a tab list cut to its first characters, the account is the first thing seen, and the agents of one account sort together.
