## What I learnt

- The hook took every dotted name for a worker (push to `work/<name>` only). Roles with their own grant now carry `releases_to_dev: true`; `grantOf` honours it. The hourly report's subject regex also refused dots.
- Two agent-shaped key labels in ~/.sg-send/keys/ make a session unidentified to the hooks. A place agent's site key goes in its own home: `PKI_AGENT_HOME=~/.sg-send/sites/<id>`. place.portugal@ already did this and passes.
- pki-agent.mjs `sign` writes `{signature, fingerprint}`: ECDSA P-256, ieee-p1363, over the raw file bytes. Any rewrite of a place file breaks it.
- Place agents' site entries carry `"agent": "place.<id>@..."`; a lookup by agent must exclude `kind: site` (my first run had that false positive; fixed).
- The generator also makes ABPs for any board/team/<role>.<x>/ folder; conductor.b and journalist.newsroom exist and are not workers I know of, so I deleted those two generated files before committing. Delete them again after each build until Dinis answers.
- Edits to .claude/hooks/ went through in this session (the 6 November session was refused them); the pin (`hook_sha256`) must follow every hook edit or `abp test` fails.
