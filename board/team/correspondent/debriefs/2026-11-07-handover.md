## What I learnt (this session)

What works: fetch, rebase, on conflict take `origin/dev`'s file and re-apply my change by script (rename the old entry to `-0`, superseded; append the new one), continue, release at once; retry after 20 seconds.
- **The inbox:** `sealed` in `board/inboxes/correspondent.json` is sgit's hybrid envelope as one base64 string: write it as-is to a `.enc` file in `~/.sg-send/lanes/`, `sgit pki decrypt` with the encryption fingerprint and an empty passphrase on stdin (the key store is plain), then delete the `.enc`. Clone the vault into `~/vaults/<vault_name>` with the key on stdin (`sgit clone -`); the drain is the team site's `admin/tools/feedback_drain.py`, fetched into the scratchpad from raw.githubusercontent.com. The reissued inbox is a new vault: what was sent to the old one stays there.
