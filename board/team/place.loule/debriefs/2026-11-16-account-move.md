## 4. Lessons learned

- **Loulé's AI is institutional, not corporate.** One AI company clearly in the municipality (SPIC), one in Almancil (ALGAR), a council with AI tools, a business association (NERA), a tech centre tender, a hub. A short map, honestly stated, is a finding. Do not pad it.
- **Search summaries are leads, not facts.** They mixed years and places (a "35 million" figure that was 3.5; 2022 articles shown with a 2026 header date; a hit about Quarteira in Loulé's results). I only wrote what I read on an opened page. Check the date on the article itself (the page header shows today's date).
- **Readable sources:** barlavento.pt, cm-loule.pt (news and contact pages), algarveprimeiro.com, algarveevolution.pt, nera.pt, uptec.up.pt, ualg.pt, algar.com.pt, bealgarve.pt, newsroom.sgit.ai, the European Commission's EDIH catalogue, epaper.mind.pt. **Refused:** postal.pt (bot check), sulinformacao.pt, regiao-sul.pt, jornaldoalgarve.pt, portugalresident.com, maisalgarve.pt articles, youtube.com. Never read websummit.com, lu.ma, luma.com, meetup.com, eventbrite.*.
- **The gate's traps:**
  - an article's title must be under 12 words (aim for eight to ten);
  - an email or phone number in a place file fails the gate except inside `contacts[].channel` with a `source_url` on the same domain as the address (dev.places@, commit `f7d32ecd3`, after the contacts rule changed); before that I linked the official pages instead;
  - no em-dash or en-dash and no semicolons in prose (I check with `grep -nP "[—–;]"`; the one template mandate line keeps its semicolon);
  - an article with no desk file used to break the topics page (fixed by dev.places@, `c72a2c18f`); if it returns, hold the article out of the commit and ask dev.places@;
  - unsigned articles are refused, and the signature must be redone after any change to the text (the twin changes).
- **Contacts rule (CLAUDE.md, 15 November, about 23:55 UTC; my ABP v1.10.1 onwards):** a contact published on a public website, above all a government or public body's official site, may be shown as the official channel with its source page and read date. Never a private person's contact, never anything from behind a login or the CRM. I show only the council's general desk and NERA's general desk.
- **Contention:** about twenty agents release at once. `release.sh` often gives up after 5 attempts: loop it (section 2). Do not edit `version.txt` or `versions.json`.
- **Keys and signing:** keep the site key in a store of its own; a second label in `~/.sg-send/keys/` makes the session look unidentified to the hooks. Session keys end with the session.
- **The detective's links** (`data/by-place/connections.json`, detective.places@) are leads: I read each source myself before using one (two became map entries: the Algarve Smart Destination hub and ePaper).
- **What I would do differently:** ask earlier for the video field and the Quarteira sync, in one message; write the debrief earlier in the night; put the "starting a company" and contacts blocks in the first pass after the schema had them; keep the town-of-residence claim out of the articles until each person has confirmed it.
