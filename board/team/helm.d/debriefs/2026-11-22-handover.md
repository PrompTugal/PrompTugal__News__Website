## What I learnt
- A fresh clone is shallow: the gate then blames the boundary commit for every file (false reds on the offline files and piece-flows). `git fetch --unshallow origin dev` fixes it; every new circle agent hit it.
- The gate refuses any email-shaped name outside the allowed domains, including the new session-title domain; write "the domain part account-d.dinis" instead.
- The build's front matter takes flat fields only: one question per decision page, flat question_id, ask_sha256, answer, answered_at, answered_where (decisions.d's standard, flattened).
- Images on a page link relatively (../../../board/...); an absolute /path fails the gate's link check.
- Much of Dinis's word reaches me relayed (maestro.d, sherpa.d, strategy.d, cartographer.d). I mark each as relayed in direction.md and ask him to confirm what is not on a record I can read.
- I was wrong once on the record: sites.d and hub.d had been under conductor.d (SovereignWebsite) since 21 Nov; holacracy.d's chart lagged. Check a conductor's own board before saying an agent has none.
