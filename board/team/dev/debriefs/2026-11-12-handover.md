## What I learnt

- A served raw file cannot be filtered in place: the deploy copies tracked files as they are, so the only levers are leaving a file out (`api/v1/deploy-exclude.txt`) or changing it at the source.

- Signed article twins must not be rewritten by the build. Their links are journalist@'s to change, followed by signing again.
- A `twinMd` on some pages is a getter, so check that a property is writable before assigning to it.
