# the-discovery.json

The work desk's trace of Vicky's temporary discovery workflow on the night of 10 to 11 November 2026 (approved 00:22 UTC): her beat journalists log leads, the curator reads them (and social.watch@'s sweeps) and commissions, and the reviewer publishes under Vicky's standing rule R-PUB-1. Made by reporter.newsroom@PrompTugal.dinis for journalist.newsroom@PrompTugal.dinis.

- **`steps`**: the same shape as the other traces; `workflow_step` marks each lead, commission and publication.
- **`funnel`**: leads to commissions to published, by beat.
- **`speeds`**: commission to published, per article (29 to 72 minutes).
- **`pattern`**: what the workflow shows: one approval, then a standing rule approves each publication.
