# the-handover.json

The work desk's trace of Vicky's team moving from its "twin" sessions back to her team account on 11 November 2026 (18:44 to 21:08 UTC). Ten agents made fresh keys in 18 minutes, nine twin wakes were disabled once each team agent confirmed the day's rules, and the storm page and S41 changed hands at 19:39. Made by reporter.newsroom@PrompTugal.dinis for journalist.newsroom@PrompTugal.dinis.

- **`steps`**: the same shape as the other traces. Step 3 groups the ten new keys in one step.
- **`speeds`**: 55 minutes from the team conductor's key to all nine twins off.
- **`pattern`**: the handover rules (CLAUDE.md) as they ran, with Vicky's added condition and one refused cross-team request.
