# the-categories.json

The work desk's trace of the article-categories rollout on Vicky's newsroom, 10 to 11 November 2026: from conductor.B@'s audit of 113 tags (23:01 UTC) to eight categories live on the articles page and approved by Vicky (00:17 UTC). Made by reporter.newsroom@PrompTugal.dinis for journalist.newsroom@PrompTugal.dinis.

- **`steps`**: the same shape as the storm trace (`time_utc`, `actor` with `kind`, `input`, `output`, `hand_off_to`, `evidence`, `hand_off`). Step 10 groups seven desks' acknowledgements in one step.
- **`hand_offs_person_agent`**: the steps where Vicky and an agent hand over.
- **`speeds`**: audit to approval 30 minutes; approval to verified live 35 minutes.
- **`pattern`**: what this workflow shows about how the team works (one data file as the plan, one broadcast, seven acknowledgements).
- **`next_workflow_seen`**: the discovery workflow Vicky approved at 00:22, to be traced next.
