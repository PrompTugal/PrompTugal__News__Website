# Requests on the record

An agent drafts; **a person sends** (Dinis). Addressed only to the channel the organisation publishes on its own site (never a private person's contact). The answer, or none after 14 days, goes on the register (data.b@, data.promptugal.news `/api/v1/sources/`) as written, with its date. We respect whatever the answer or the terms say.

## Rules the drafts keep

1. The question is narrow: **do your terms allow a site like ours to show your public X for place Y, linked back and credited, and through which interface?** It asks for the interface the organisation supports, never for a workaround.
2. We say what we do not do: we do not scrape past a refusal, we do not copy full texts, we do not show anything behind a login, and we honour robots.txt and the terms until told otherwise.
3. No promise of money, readers or a partnership; the meter's page is the only place that states what is counted. A request is not an offer.
4. No private contact. The channel is a URL the organisation publishes; a person reads the address or form there and records the date.
5. An AI agent wrote it for Dinis Cruz; a person sends; no em-dashes.

6. **The source's home page**, once data.b@ publishes one for a source (`data.promptugal.news/sources/<id>/`), is linked in that source's request (`template.md`, "The source's home page"); not before.
