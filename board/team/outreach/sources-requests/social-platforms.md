# One note, seven platforms

Subject: How PrompTugal.news uses organisations' public posts on {{platform}}: a note on the record, and a question

Hello {{platform}} team,

PrompTugal.news is a newsroom for Portugal's places made by AI agents, with every fact linked to its source and the date we read it: https://promptugal.news/

We would like to use organisations' public posts on {{platform}} to connect the dots between places, venues, events and companies, and we want to tell you exactly how, and ask for your view.

- **Only organisations:** the official public accounts of venues, câmaras and public bodies, organisers, news outlets and companies. Never a private person's account or post, and no private data.
- **{{interface_line}}**
- **We show a post by a link, or by your own embed, credited to the account that posted it.** We do not copy its text or its pictures. The licence we show for it is your terms.
- **We keep a post only as evidence** for something we publish (an event, a story): its link, the account, and the date we read it.
- **Everything we make is CC BY 4.0**; everything we take from someone else carries a link to its own licence.

Questions: Is this use within your terms? Which interface should we use, and under which conditions? Is there anything here you would ask us to change?

Your answer, or no answer after 14 days, goes on our public register of sources as written, with the date: https://data.promptugal.news/sources/ (being built).

Thank you,
[the sender, for a person to choose]
PrompTugal.news

Written by an AI agent for Dinis Cruz, reviewed and sent by a person. We found this contact on your website. If you would rather not hear from us again, reply and we will not write again.

## Holds

- Bluesky and Mastodon first (open by design); the rest after Dinis decides the order. YouTube waits for its key (the credential form, Dinis); Instagram through a Meta app waits for Dinis.
- For X, LinkedIn and TikTok the note is written for the day we link to their posts; send it before any such link is shown.
