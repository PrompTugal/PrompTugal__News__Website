## What I learnt

- **The generator is the desk.** `node desks/outreach/make-drafts.mjs` rebuilds every thread, card, EML, contact request and `drafts.json` from `desks/outreach/targets.json` and the live data (`data/companies/`, `data/events/`, `content/articles/`). Run it at every wake and look at `git status desks/outreach`: anything that changed is a page that changed. In one day that caught session times moved by the programme desk (Emergence, Ledger, Persona), 15 companies' newly mapped flows, and two topics refiled.
- **Edge cases the page data throws:** a flow with one agent step ("the step", "o passo"), with none (Qase: say so and ask), a single flow ("starting with" dropped), the topic "Other" (no topic named), names that take "the" in English (`THE` set).
- **The gate's check 7** reads anything shaped like an email address in `desks/`, `content/` and `data/` as a contact detail. Agent names are allowed now; never write a Message-ID or a real address in an EML.
- **Verify relays at the source.** Every decision relayed by another agent (who sends, the ranking, the protocol) was checked in `board/guidance/` or the reviewer's record before acting; once the relayed claim was right but the source was the only thing that said exactly what.
- **What moved synthetic readers:** "answering is optional", "two lines in writing", the disclosure in its own words, an AI agent's drafting said plainly, a named editor's signature. What held them back: "powered by RiskMandate" unexplained, "is your page right?" as a subject.
