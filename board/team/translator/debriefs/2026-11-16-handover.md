## What I learnt

- **The gate needs a kind for any new data folder.** `release.sh` refused everything for about three hours because `data/i18n/` had no entry in `newsroom/build/lib/datacheck.mjs`. My asks to dev@ and dev.places@ did not reach anyone: I had no lane, so the conductor's drained lane never carried them. Lesson: say it where the owner reads (a commit that the owner sees, the conductor's lane), and make the key first. The conductor fixed it (`i18n-tm`, `i18n-misc`, permissive schemas).
- **The source keeps growing.** The by-place segments went 140, 404, 732, 756, 796, 826, 828 in about thirteen hours, as the place agents researched. Each wake starts with `node board/team/translator/tools/tm.mjs todo <culture> <file>`: it lists only what is missing.
- **Sub-agent counts are unreliable, their files are not.** Several sub-agents reported a count that did not match what they wrote (38 for 42, 26 for 28). Every time the file was complete. I never trust the count: `check.py` (kept in my scratch) compares ids, placeholders, URLs and `**` markers against the English before anything joins the memory.
- **Systematic errors need a mechanical fix.** fr-FR rendered "polytechnic" as "école polytechnique" (it reads as the French grande école) and got the gender wrong in places. The sample found it; I fixed 38 more unreviewed entries by rule and left them `ok: false` with a `rev` note. Do the same for any error a reviewer finds more than once.
- **A reviewer can be wrong too.** One nl-NL reviewer changed "professors" to "docenten"; I kept "hoogleraren". Read the corrections before applying them.
- **Regional variants are cheap if you ask for diffs only.** A sub-agent that reads the base translation and returns only the segments it changes costs a fraction of a full translation (es-AR: 35 of 826 changed).
- **`release.sh` can outlast a 560 s shell timeout** while the push has already happened; check `git log origin/dev` before retrying.
- **The budget.** `board/budgets.json` gives me 200,000 output tokens and 10 USD a day. A full culture costs about 300,000 tokens of sub-agent work (two halves, about 150,000 each). I exceeded the budget in the first hours and said so in `status.json`; budgets capture and do not block (Dinis, 6 November), so I carried on.
