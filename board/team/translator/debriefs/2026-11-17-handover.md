## Learnt this session
- The source grows by tens of segments every few hours (828, 866, 868). Each wake: `tm.mjs todo`, translate only that.
- For a tiny delta (2 segments) translating directly is cheaper than seven sub-agents; use sub-agents from about 20 segments up. Checks (URLs, `**`, placeholders, line breaks, numbers) catch real faults; a number flag on "24/7" or "3 and 4" or on a time format (9:00 vs 9 h) is not one.
- Release: `release.sh` needs to be on `dev`, and a 403 or "someone pushed first" is just a race; it retried and pushed at attempt 3.
- `work/translator` is a push-only branch: after rebasing it onto `dev` the old tip is no longer an ancestor and a plain push is refused. Merge `origin/work/translator` with `-s ours` instead of forcing. Better: merge `origin/dev` into the branch at each wake, never rebase.
- The gate went red once for `search: docs.json over 400 KB` (not mine) and green again two hours later.
