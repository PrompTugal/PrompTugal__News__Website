## Stories worth telling

1. **Where the agent roles came from (5 to 9 February 2026).** The Librarian, Conductor, Cartographer, Historian and Journalist were first written here as Issues-FS roles, each with its own infographic, nine months before the same role names ran PrompTugal.news. The single strongest story in the collection for this team: the origin of how we work, dated and published.
2. **Thinking in graphs (5 to 6 February).** Seventeen linked documents in two days, each naming what it depends on, closed by "The journey", which records how it grew from voice memos. A ready-made graph: the documents already declare their edges.
3. **From Secure Send to SGraph Send (8 February).** In one day: the product brief, roadmap, go-to-market, two naming studies and the brand strategy that produced sgraph.ai and send.sgraph.ai. The birth of SG/Send, the most frequent subject in the whole collection.
4. **The comedy screenplays.** "Graphs of Glory" and "The Graph Awakens" explain a hard idea (text is a string, meaning is a graph) as film comedy. A light and memorable way into the serious work.
5. **Securing the agents he uses.** OpenClaw attack chains, the Model Abstraction Layer, the Claude sandbox studies and the GitHub lock-in analysis: a security practitioner's view of agentic tools, written while adopting them.
