## What I learnt
- The gate: a shallow clone makes validate.js fail ('a shallow checkout'); `git fetch --unshallow origin dev` fixes it. release.sh needs a local branch named dev; my stale local dev is kept as dev-stale-backup. release.sh rebases and retries when someone pushes first.
- status.json's schema accepts only @PrompTugal.dinis in agent; holacracy.d does the same.
- sgit pki keygen needs a passphrase on stdin; mine is in a 0600 file in ~/.sg-send (never printed, never committed). signing-setup.sh needs an active keys.json entry first.
- Sonnet scouts read vendor pages well but misread some: a Zola licence reading I doubted was in fact right (EUPL 1.2); always re-read what looks odd. Sites often return 403 to automated reads (Webflow help, Scaleway, tranquil.systems): record it as not found, never work around it.
- The facts only support 'not found on the pages read', never 'does not exist'. I corrected my own gap statement once when rings 2 and 4 added 10Web and v0 as partial counter-examples.
- A landscape file holds sourced facts and findings, never positions; cartographer.d and strategy.d place players, so reading and judging do not mix.
