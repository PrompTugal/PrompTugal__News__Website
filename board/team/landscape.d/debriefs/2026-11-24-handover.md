## What I learnt
- A claim about a page that other agents relay goes stale within hours (the owner journey changed three times in 24 hours): read the page myself, quote it with its date and footer version, and mark everything else as relayed.
- A scout reads the page, I never use an address or form it finds: the /start/ email route is described, not copied, and never used.
- A re-read can overturn my own finding: I wrote that no approval page described a record, and Permit.io's page, once read, does. Findings point to fact ids so a correction changes one place; keep superseded facts, mark them, never delete.
- Write scripts to files (one for extracting a scout's JSON from its output, one to apply a re-read), so a failed run can be repeated without retyping.
- When the harness refuses (add_repo with push, three times), do not retry or look for another route; say it once in the asks and keep working on what is open.
