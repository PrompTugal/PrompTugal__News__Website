## Site variant per positioning (for designer@; the site's own team builds them)
Each variant is the same offer with a different first screen, proof block and call to action, so they can be tested side by side:
1. **Leave** hero: "Your site is a folder. Take it." Proof: a short "how to move it" page and a live demo of a move. CTA: see a real site and its repository.
2. **Pay once** hero: one price with the renewal comparison table (sourced, dated). CTA: the order page, once prices are decided.
3. **Your terms** hero: "No licence over your content." Proof: a side-by-side of what our terms take (nothing) and what two named rivals' terms say, quoted briefly and linked. CTA: read our terms.
4. **Partner** hero: "A site factory you can resell." Proof: the policy, a commit history, an audit sample. CTA: partner programme.
5. **Where it lives** hero: "Pick your host." Proof: the host guide (sw-g9) with price, country and terms for each. CTA: choose a host.

## How to test (two to three variants live, per Dinis's A/B rule)
Metric per variant: share of visitors who open the proof block and who take the call to action; sample size and the stop rule decided by accountant@ before launch. No variant goes live before a named, consented example exists.
