## What nobody read does, together
Plain files you own and can move + built by agents for a non-technical owner + edited by talking to your own AI + priced once. Pieces exist apart:
- Open-source static engines give you the files and the freedom, free, but ask for a terminal and Node or Ruby (sw-g2).
- Open-source AI builders ask for developer set-up and your own model key; one hosts the output itself and one keeps part of its code fair-source (sw-g3).
- Hosted builders give the no-skill experience but none read states an export tool on the pages read, and several keep broad licences over your content (sw-g1, sw-g6, sw-g7).

## Strengths (our side, as published)
- Output is plain static files in the owner's own repository, any host: the four tests in our plan (move it, own the schema, set the terms, nothing changes if we are bought) all speak to what the field is silent on.
- One-off price against subscriptions that renew higher (Hostinger, IONOS: sw-g5).
- Privacy by default (no cookies unless added), multilingual, euro prices: a European wedge.
- Open pattern first, hosted second: the open-source engines cannot match the owner-ready hosted offer; the hosted builders cannot match the open pattern.

## Gaps and risks (ours)
1. **Nothing has been sold and no example is public** (plan, 19 Nov, as relayed). Every positioning below needs one named, consented example before it is a claim.
2. **The owner needs a Claude plan, or a partner, to edit** (as relayed): that cuts against "no technical skill" for the hosted-builder buyer. It is the sharpest honest weakness; the partner programme is the answer.
3. **Our price line is inconsistent between sources:** our page names a £500 anchor and a £50 proposal; the relay adds a £250 standing price. Settle it before any price is compared on a page.
4. **"Sovereign" is not a legal or certification claim** in our own plan. Hosts differ (sw-g8); we should say what we do (files you own, a host you choose) and not borrow a certification.
5. **Hosting choice is the owner's cost.** GitHub Pages says it is not for running an online business; Netlify's free plan allows commercial use within credits; Hetzner starts at EUR 1.60 a month plus domain (sw-g9). "Run it anywhere" needs a short, true guide to which hosts suit a shop.
6. **Field gaps in my own reading:** Webflow could not be read; saldiaBuild has no price; export terms were often not found rather than found absent; three rows are flagged for re-read.

## Overlaps to be honest about
Durable, Wix and Squarespace sell the same small-business customer an AI-made site. WordPress with managed hosting (10Web) is the nearest "you can leave" story (SFTP/SSH listed). We should not claim the move-out test as unique; we can claim it as published and tested.

## Who the first customer is (open, sherpa.d)
The field splits the buyer: the owner fears lock-in and renewals; the partner (freelancer, consultancy) wants a repeatable build; the developer already has the engines. Our plan's channels favour the partner. The positionings below are written so the first two lead for the owner and the fourth for the partner.
