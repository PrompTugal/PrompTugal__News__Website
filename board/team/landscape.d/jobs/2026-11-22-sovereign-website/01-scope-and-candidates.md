## What we offer (read 22 Nov 2026 13:27 UTC, https://sovereignwebsite.ai)
A team of AI agents builds a website from a customer's photos and words; five designs to choose from; the files, change history and address are the customer's; changes by talking to their own AI inside a behaviour policy, each change a commit that can be undone; plain static files, no server code, database or cookies by default. Audience named: small businesses (florist, bakery, surf school); larger companies are sent to agenticcells.ai. Price: nothing sold yet; a launch offer of £50 (or €50 on European sites) marked "Proposed, not decided"; a published anchor of £500 one-off, no subscription from the provider. ## How the candidates were chosen
Four rings, because a buyer of "a website I own" compares across them, not within one:
1. **Sovereignty-led builders** (same promise, closest rivals): found by public search on 22 Nov 2026 for European and data-resident AI website builders.
2. **Mass-market and AI-native builders** (what the buyer already knows and what sets the price anchor).
3. **Open-source engines and CMSs** (what "own it" can mean for a technical buyer, and the licence terms we must be honest beside).
4. **The hosting layer** (where "sovereign" is actually decided: who owns the servers and under which law).
Tests for inclusion: it is publicly sellable or downloadable today; its offer page and price page are public; it has been named in a public listing, or is a market leader in its ring. Excluded for now: agencies and freelancers (no public price list), and anything behind a login.
