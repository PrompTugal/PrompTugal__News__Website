# Designer: one brief per variant (22 Nov 2026, 17:25 UTC)

For whoever builds the Sovereign Website variants (conductor.d decides; designer.d or the site's own team). These are briefs and page drafts, not built pages. The same offer, three first screens, so the variants can be tested side by side. Copy is a draft in plain British English; every claim cited must stay linked to its source in `../../landscapes/sovereign-website/landscape.json`. Rules for all variants: no claim of certification or legal sovereignty (our plan makes none); no price on the page until the price is decided; no named customer or example without their consent; no quoting a competitor beyond a few words, always linked with the date read; mobile first (first phone screen says what this is, what is new and whether to trust it: sherpa.d's n-reader-first-screen); no cookies added by the variant.

## Variant A, the owner: "Agents build it. You own it, and can move it."
- **Audience and need:** a small-business owner (florist, bakery, surf school); n-owner-own-and-move first, then n-owner-no-technical-skill and n-owner-clear-price.
- **First screen:** headline as above; one sentence: "Your site is a folder of plain files in your own repository, on a host you choose." Button: "See a real site and its folder" (needs one consented example; until then it points to an evaluation site marked as such).
- **Proof block 1, the way out:** a short page "How to move your site", written as steps a non-technical owner can follow, with the honest note that today the owner needs a Claude plan or a partner to edit (the sharpest weakness; do not hide it).
- **Proof block 2, the terms:** a small table "What a platform's terms say about your content": two or three named rivals with a few words each, linked and dated (Squarespace and Lovable from sw-g7), and our own line: no licence over your content (to be confirmed against our own terms by rights@ before it goes on the page).
- **Proof block 3, where it lives:** a host guide (sw-g8, sw-g9): for each host, country, starting price and one term that matters for a business site (e.g. GitHub Pages is not meant for an online business). No ranking.
- **Call to action:** "Start with a free evaluation site" if that stays in the plan; the order button waits on the price.
- **Test:** share of visitors who open the move-out page; share who start an evaluation.

## Variant B, the visitor: "Try it on something you love, then try to break it."
- **Audience and need:** a visitor who has agents or may, met at a stand or on a topic site; n-visitor-try-before-buy and n-lead-first-small-step (sherpa.d; Dinis's stand account, not measured).
- **First screen:** a live Sovereign Website on a topic people like (football, CVEs, almost anything; the topic and platform are the owner's to choose, Dinis decides). Headline as above. Button: "Try to break it".
- **The policy moment:** an interaction on the page that shows the policy working, not described: the visitor asks the site's agent for something the written policy forbids, and sees it refused, with the rule that refused it and the record of the attempt. Draft only; the mechanism belongs to the site's team and must be safe by design (no real data, no outside action).
- **Next step:** "Make it yours": a button that starts a fork or an evaluation site, no call and no purchase. What the smallest step is, is Dinis's decision (asked of sherpa.d).
- **Proof:** a plain page "What the policy is" (the mandate and the grant), linked from every attempt.
- **Test:** share who try to break it; share who take the next step; do not report visitor numbers as buyers.

## Variant C, the partner: "Your web person, upgraded."
- **Audience and need:** a freelancer, consultancy or agency that maintains sites for clients; need to be confirmed with sherpa.d (a repeatable, auditable build).
- **First screen:** "Keep the client and the fee. Add a site factory that holds every change to a written policy." Button: "Read the partner terms".
- **Proof:** the policy, a commit history with signed commits, a sample change and how it is undone (all real, from a real site). The partner page is a first draft with no partner signed (per strategy.d): say so on the page.
- **Message discipline:** maintain, do not replace; start with one client.
- **Test:** partner enquiries per visitor; the question they ask first.
