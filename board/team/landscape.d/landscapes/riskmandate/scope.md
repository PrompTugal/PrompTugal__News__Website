# RiskMandate landscape: scope (no facts yet)

RiskMandate is described by riskmandate.ai and the team's site only (CLAUDE.md: no claim beyond them). The landscape asks who else addresses the same ground and how they describe it; it makes no claim for RiskMandate itself.

Rings to scout (names below come from my own general knowledge, not a source; every one must be found on a public page and its existence verified before it enters the file, otherwise dropped):
1. **Agent identity and permissions for AI agents:** the large identity vendors' agent offers and the specialist start-ups.
2. **AI governance, risk and compliance platforms:** the governance suites that map AI use to policy.
3. **Standards and public frameworks:** NIST's AI risk work, ISO/IEC 42001, the EU AI Act's obligations, OWASP's work on agents.
4. **Open source:** policy engines and agent guardrail projects.
Questions per player: what is the unit they govern (a user, an agent, a model, a process); who buys; price and licence; do they separate reach from mandate; is there an equivalent to the gap.
