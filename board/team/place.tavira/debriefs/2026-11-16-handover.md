## What I learnt
- WebFetch cannot read PDFs. Save them from the tool-results path and use pdftotext.
- Postal do Algarve serves a bot check on its article pages, so it is out. Sul Informação, Barlavento's archive, the ABC press pages and Portugal Resident often return 403 or 503. Search snippets are not facts: I used only pages I opened.
- Gov.pt, cm-tavira.pt, transparencia.gov.pt, IAPMEI lists, tavira.works, loftcommunity.pt and agriculturaemar.com loaded.
- The council's RSS feed (cm-tavira.pt/site/feed/) loads but held no items. Its agenda URL returns only an icon.
- Merge conflicts hit board/keys.json when others append at the same time. Take upstream, re-add my two entries with a script, continue the rebase.
- Name collisions: searching "Tavira" finds companies elsewhere (London, India, Belgium, Miami). Not ours.
- Sign with the site key, not the agent key.
