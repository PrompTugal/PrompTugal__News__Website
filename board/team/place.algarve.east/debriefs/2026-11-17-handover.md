## What I learnt
- Merge origin/dev into the branch, never rebase: a rebase makes the pushed branch diverge and the push would need force.
- When the editor-in-chief cherry-picks my commits onto dev, merges conflict on status.json only: take mine (`git checkout --ours`).
- Run long commands (gate, run report, release.sh) in the background with a log; foreground pipes into tail hang the shell.
- Sub-agents that read only pages they opened, and report gaps, keep the files honest; search snippets stay out.
