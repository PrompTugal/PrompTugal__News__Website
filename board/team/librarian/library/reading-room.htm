    Pick a node

    Tap a node to read its record and open its file; double-tap to expand it.

    The graph is drawn by the site's own explorer (the same engine as /graph/) from library-graph.json and reality-tree.json, generated from the cards and their typed links. A card that is an entity page (a company, a person, an organisation, a place) is not a node of its own: a connection to one ends at its collection. Everything is read from this folder and the site's own files; nothing is sent anywhere.
