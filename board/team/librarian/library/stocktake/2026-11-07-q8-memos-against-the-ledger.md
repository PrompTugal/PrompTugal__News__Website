None found. I spot-checked every row whose memo number I could compare with the memo text (all full-ledger rows that carry an M number, about 400 including repeats). I did not audit the Later and Not captured tables row by row, nor the SU (stand-up) rows, which have no memo to compare with.

## Method

I read all 49 memos in full and listed the ideas in each. I extracted every full-ledger row (id, memo, one-sentence idea) and matched each idea to a row by substance, searching by keyword where a row was not obvious. A paraphrase or a broader row counted as covered. I flagged an item as missed only when no row carried it. I judged as covered, not missed: the pasted brief of memo 1 (its first-run tasks: the key, the plan, the domain check and the one-page brief to Dinis are executed instructions, with their substance in PB-01, OU-01, CO-16, AB-26, AB-27, NR-37); the greetings and sign-offs (memos 39, 41, 44); and memo 45's "specially now that I'm online" (a context remark). I did not use the stand-up rows beyond checking that a "(also M20)" cross-reference existed (CO-15). No file other than this one was created or edited.
