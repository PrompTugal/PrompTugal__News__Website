# Lagoa: local news, read 16 November 2026

All items opened on the council site cm-lagoa.pt (single source). Only item 2 touches technology.

1. **About 120,000 m2 of rural land to be reclassified as urban for housing (Estombar e Parchal).** A simplified procedure covers 119,957 m2 and allows up to 71,374 m2 of construction for public, affordable or cost-controlled housing. Public consultation runs 17 November to 16 December 2026. Source: https://www.cm-lagoa.pt/noticia/aviso-n-28523-2026-2-procedimento-simplificado-de-reclassificacao-de-solo-rustico-para-urbano-com-a-finalidade-habitacional (published 16 Nov, notice signed 24 Oct, read 16 Nov). Significance: region (Algarve housing shortage). Neighbours: Portimao and Silves border Estombar and Parchal.
2. **Volta deposit-return kiosk opens at the FATACIL fairground.** An automated return point for plastic and metal drink containers, about 120 per minute, refunding EUR 0.10 each via the volta app, MB WAY or charity donation. Source: https://www.cm-lagoa.pt/noticia/municipio-de-lagoa-reforca-aposta-na-sustentabilidade-com-a-entrada-em-funcionamento-do-quiosque-volta-no-parque-municipal-de-feiras-e-exposicoes-de-lagoa-recinto-da-fatacil (published 13 Nov, read 16 Nov). PrompTugal test: 1 step (automated machine, app and digital payments), no AI mentioned, so labelled so. Significance: town, part of the national deposit return scheme.
3. **Non-essential municipal services closed on 11 November** ahead of the depression, with IPSS, nurseries and private schools advised to close and emergency teams on alert. Source: https://www.cm-lagoa.pt/comunicacao/noticias/noticia-60/municipio-de-lagoa-reforca-dispositivo-de-prevencao-e-determina-encerramento-de-servicos-municipais-nao-essenciais-devido-a-passagem-da-depressao (published 10 Nov, read 16 Nov). Significance: region. Neighbours: Portimao, Silves, Carvoeiro, Ferragudo.
4. **Rental support: EUR 1,306,821.81 paid since 2013** to 416 low-income families. In 2023 the maximum monthly aid doubled from EUR 240 to EUR 480 (up to 60% of rent). Source: https://www.cm-lagoa.pt/comunicacao/noticias/noticia-60/municipio-de-lagoa-ja-investiu-mais-de-1-3-milhoes-de-euros-no-apoio-ao-arrendamento-e-reforcou-resposta-as-familias (published 9 Nov, read 16 Nov). Significance: town.
