# Two open leads, read 17 November 2026

## 1. Equipa UAlg: 81 free school talks in 16 areas (the one real AI thread this morning)
The University of Algarve (a public university) offers schools 81 free talks and activities in 16 areas in its 2026/2027 "Equipa UAlg" programme. Named topics include artificial intelligence (in health, teaching and mathematics), cybersecurity, sustainability and financial literacy. The full list of 16 areas is not given. For basic and secondary schools, adapted to age, mostly at schools or campuses, online also possible. Last year: over 100 talks, about 6,000 students and teachers. Information sessions on admissions, fees and mobility from January 2027. No request deadline given. Sign-up is through the university's own form, linked from its page.
- Barlavento, published 16 Nov: https://www.barlavento.pt/equipa-ualg-palestras-escolas/
- The university's own page, 16 Nov: https://www.ualg.pt/equipa-ualg-renova-se-e-regressa-escolas-com-81-palestras (programme page https://www.ualg.pt/equipa-ualg-palestras seen linked, not opened).
Read 17 Nov. PrompTugal test: AI named in the source, 1 step (a university offering AI talks to Algarve schools). Significance: region. Fits the regional page better than any one town: Silves, Lagos, Portimao schools are in reach.

## 2. Portimao emergency department: what three opened outlets say (neutral wording: "reported deaths of elderly patients after long waits")
- DN, 11 Nov (https://www.dn.pt/sociedade/igas-vai-investigar-a-qualidade-e-segurana-dos-cuidados-prestadosna-urgncia-de-portimo): two suspected deaths after hours of waiting (ages given, withheld here). IGAS opened an urgent extraordinary inspection by four members (quality and safety, pre-hospital emergency, triage and referral, complaints). Says the board president's dismissal was done on Monday 9 Nov. Does not reconcile with a further case reported by CNN.
- Observador, 9 Nov (https://observador.pt/2026/11/09/ministra-da-saude-vai-demitir-presidente-da-uls-do-algarve-apos-mortes-nas-urgencias-de-portimao/): two deaths linked to long waits, details and waiting times given and withheld here, and a separate death after a transfer to Faro that the minister said was not linked. The dismissal is only announced ("vai demitir"). The minister signed a despacho for the whole department.
- Barlavento, 9 Nov (https://www.barlavento.pt/ministra-saude-vai-demitir-presidente-uls-algarve/): same as Observador.
- None of the three mentions the ERS. The ERS "evaluation" comes from a separate Observador article of 11 Nov (https://observador.pt/2026/11/11/regulador-da-saude-vai-analisar-todas-as-situacoes-de-morte-conhecidas-no-hospital-de-portimao/), which I have not re-opened.

**What can be said safely:** reported deaths of elderly patients after long waits, an IGAS inspection, a regulator process, the minister announcing the board president's dismissal on 9 Nov. **Do not print:** ages, a death count, whether the dismissal was done, or the child's case, until journalist@ picks one outlet's figures. Counts and ages differ across outlets. I leave the president's name out of this file.
