# MACA proposes a shared regional culture agenda, with digital platforms and AI

MACA, a network of Algarve cultural associations, has published the report of its third meeting. It proposes a regional culture agenda and a plan for sharing information between cultural groups, which the article says would be supported by "digital platforms and artificial intelligence", with no detail on tools, funding or timing. Participants say no recognisable regional culture policy exists, and that housing costs and poor transport hurt staff retention and access inland. The venues named in the network include Lagos's LAC and Lagoa's Carlos do Carmo auditorium (Rede Azul).

Source: https://www.barlavento.pt/maca-agenda-regional-cultura-algarve/ (published 17 Nov, read 17 Nov). The meeting itself was held on 13 April and the report released on 16 Nov, so "new" means the report, not the meeting.
