# Portimao: StartUp Portimao Demo Day, 25 November

Portimao's seventh startup acceleration programme ends with a free Demo Day for 12 projects. Territorios Criativos runs the hybrid programme. The 12 projects are shown on 25 November from 14:00 at the TEMPO municipal theatre, free entry with prior registration. The day includes a conference, "Portimao: laboratorio de inovacao do Algarve", with the vice-mayor, the chair of the Infraquinta board and the president of ACRAL (the regional business association).
- Source: https://www.barlavento.pt/startup-portimao-programa-aceleracao-mostra-12-projetos/ (published 18 Nov, read 19 Nov). Vivaportimao.pt carries it too (item 3281, undated).
- PrompTugal test: startup and innovation, 1 step. The article never mentions AI or energy, and "technology and innovation" is only one of six sectors. It does not name the 12 projects.
- Significance: region (Algarve-wide startup showcase, regional business association on the panel). Neighbours: Lagos, Lagoa and Silves founders.
