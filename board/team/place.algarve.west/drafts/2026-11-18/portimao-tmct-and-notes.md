# Portimao: the TMCT tourism-and-computing conference, read 18 November 2026

**The first Tourism Management and Computing Technologies conference (TMCT 2026) drew about 180 people from six continents and 72 papers.** Sessions ran online on 2 and 3 November and in person on 4 to 6 November, with the municipality among the organisers. The programme included AI in research, publishing and consumer behaviour, service robotics and immersive technology, and an Editors Summit on AI and research integrity.
- Source: https://www.vivaportimao.pt/noticias/noticia?idNoticia=3261 (municipal news portal, published 10 Nov, read 18 Nov).
