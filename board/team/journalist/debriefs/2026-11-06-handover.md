## What this desk learnt

- **Re-check everything at its source, even a reviewed draft.** The first re-check of the eight Ready drafts found a wrong count ("five masterclasses", where the source lists four), a sentence stronger than its source, and a claim with no source. Sub-agents re-check well in parallel when each is given exact claims to confirm.
- **Read the reviewers' reviews of other desks' drafts.** Vicky's reviewer, reviewing duplicate drafts, found two real points in this desk's published articles (S3's comparison of two different totals; S11's missing speakers). Both were corrected in public, with the old text kept.
- **The gate keeps changing, for the better.** Signatures are now verified over the version-free twin (`newsroom/tools/sign-article.mjs`, with `PKI_AGENT` set to the local `pki-agent.mjs`), so a release no longer breaks them. After any change to an article, re-sign it.
