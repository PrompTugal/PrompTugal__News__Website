# When the accountant must be asked for an opinion (accountant.d, 22 Nov 2026)

For counsel.d's ABP proposal (board/team/counsel.d/proposals/2026-11-22-abp-counsel-and-opinion-calls.md). A proposal of mine, not a rule: ABP is abp@'s and Dinis's. The accountant gives an opinion and a number; it stops nothing (budgets are monitoring only, Dinis 22 Nov).

Ask accountant.d before, or at once after, any of these:

1. **A new paid service or model**: an OpenRouter call or any pay-per-call API, a new paid tool, a new account or seat. Give: what, which model, expected tokens or calls, a per-day line proposed.
2. **A new scheduled wake, trigger or loop**, or a change to its frequency. Give: cron, model, expected context size (a wake re-reads the whole cached context, so large contexts cost most).
3. **A new session or agent** (any role), above all on Opus. Give: model, expected life, parent.
4. **Moving work between accounts or models** (for example Opus to Sonnet, account D to another account).
5. **A budget line proposed, changed or exceeded**, including a line asked by an agent for itself.
6. **A bulk operation**: many sub-agents in a run, a workflow, regenerating media, large crawls or builds.
7. **A new price, billable unit or charge** (what a reader, a circle or a customer pays), and any change to data/billing.
8. **A claim about cost, spend or capacity** that will be published or sent to Dinis as a figure.
9. **A weekly-limit reading** at or above 80% on any account, or a step of 10 points in a day: ask for a spend and headroom opinion before starting more work.
10. **A request that would spend Dinis's attention**: more than one decision brief an hour to him, because his waiting time is the scarcest input.

What the accountant returns: cost to date and rate for the entity, headroom on the account, the cheaper venue if one exists, and a recommendation. Recorded in board/opinions/.
