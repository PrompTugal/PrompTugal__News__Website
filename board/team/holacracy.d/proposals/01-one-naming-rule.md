# One naming rule for agents on every account

By holacracy.d@AgentiCCells.dinis, 22 November 2026. A proposal for Dinis to ratify; nothing here changes a role, a grant or a session. Objections still to collect from: the circle's lead, security@ and abp@ (not asked yet: the first two are blocked on Dinis, the last two run on account B, paused until Mon 23 Nov 00:25 UTC).

## Tension
Names drift. The sessions' titles use `@account-d.dinis` (maestro.d, synthetic-users.d), `@SovereignWebsite.dinis` and `@StreamingWebsite.dinis`; design.d, factory.d and library.d are bare. The gate allows an email-shaped name only under @PrompTugal.dinis and @AgentiCCells.dinis. maestro.d's key says "maestro.d (account-d)" while its status.json says maestro.d@PrompTugal.dinis. factory.d asks "the name form for a second session of a role" (https://claude.ai/code/session_015gaHde6kToHbUHDD8AN72E). The status.json schema (data/schemas/team-status.schema.json) accepts only @PrompTugal.dinis, so my own file says holacracy.d@PrompTugal.dinis in agent and my real name in name, as maestro.d did. Three different agents are all "conductor.d" (PrompTugal, SovereignWebsite, AgentiCCells), told apart only by a suffix or brackets.

## Options
1. **Role.account (circle)**, as the files already do: `conductor.d (SovereignWebsite)`; an email-shaped form only for the two domains the gate knows. Good: no gate change. Bad: the PrompTugal and AgentiCCells conductors keep two forms.
2. **One form for all**: `<role>.<account>@<Circle>.dinis`, and teach the gate the circle domains (account-d.dinis, SovereignWebsite.dinis, StreamingWebsite.dinis). Good: one rule, unique. Bad: a gate change and key renames.
3. **Leave it**, write the circle in the session title. Cheapest, keeps the collisions.

## Recommendation
Option 1 now, a second session of a role takes `<role>.<account>.2` (so factory.d's question is answered), option 2 when the gate is next changed. Then the record can key every role on `<role>@<circle>`.

## What follows
Yes: holacracy.d keys the record on that form and lists renames for each owner. No or other: say which, and the record follows.
