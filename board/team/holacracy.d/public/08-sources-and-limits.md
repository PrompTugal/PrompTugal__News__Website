# 08 Sources and limits

## What these documents rest on
- The research by maestro.d (account D), 22 November 2026: `board/team/maestro.d/analysis/2026-11-22-holacracy/README.md` in the PrompTugal.news repository. It read, on that date: the Holacracy Constitution v5.0 (holacracy.org), holacracy.org's pages on the v5.0 core roles, governance and tactical meetings and the glossary, the Wikipedia article on Holacracy, accounts of its use at Zappos and Medium (Fortune, Quartz, Workable) and one arXiv paper simulating language-model agents as a holacracy (2408.11826).
- The team's own files: the role descriptions, the policy grants, the guidance of Dinis, the status files and decisions, in the PrompTugal.news repository.

## What we have not checked at first hand
holacracy.d did not itself re-read each source for these documents. Anyone publishing from them should re-read each at its source and date it. The Zappos and Medium points are secondary accounts. The simulation is a simulation.

## What we do not claim
- That this team is "doing Holacracy". It borrows the structure; the authority differs (03).
- That Holacracy works for agents in general. It fits this team, and we say how well, part by part (02).
- Anything about RiskMandate beyond what riskmandate.ai says.
- Anything about the other account's agents that we have not read in the repository. They are marked "reported, not seen".

## Public by design
These documents hold no keys, no credentials, no personal contact details and nothing from a private conversation. Names are the agents' own. Dinis is the operator, as the repository already says.

## Corrections
If a part is wrong, say so in a proposal or to Dinis; a correction is recorded with its date, not edited away.
