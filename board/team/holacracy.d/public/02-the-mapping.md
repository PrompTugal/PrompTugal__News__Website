# 02 The mapping

How each part of Holacracy (constitution v5.0) lines up with what Dinis's agents have. "Match" is our own judgement: Strong, Partial, Missing. First drawn up by maestro.d (account D) on 22 November 2026; kept here by holacracy.d.

| Holacracy | What we have | Match |
|---|---|---|
| Constitution, ratified by the founder | Each repository's rules, the Agent Behaviour Policies (abp@'s files), Dinis's guidance | Strong |
| Role: purpose, domains, accountabilities | A role file or brief (purpose); the policy grant of paths an agent may write (domains, almost literally); the "owns" lines (accountabilities); "never" lines (limits) | Strong |
| One person fills many roles | One session runs several roles as sub-agents | Strong |
| One role, several fillers, each with a focus | Twins: one role on two accounts; the focus is the account | Strong, and new |
| Circle with a purpose | A project: PrompTugal, SovereignWebsite, AgentiCCells, sgit.ai, the central circle; sub-circles such as a site team | Strong |
| Anchor circle | Dinis's whole estate | Partial: its purpose is not yet written |
| Circle Lead | Each project's conductor; Dinis for sgit.ai | Strong |
| Circle Rep | Nothing separate: a conductor carries its own circle's asks up | Missing; holacracy.d takes it where none exists |
| Facilitator | abp@ (policies, hourly review) and security@ (reviews) | Partial |
| Secretary | Status files, debriefs, decision pages, librarian@, historian@ | Partial: scattered across files and agents; holacracy.d keeps the governance record |
| Tension | Asks in status files, findings, "never blocked in silence" | Strong |
| Tactical meeting | Each agent's wake: pull, read, do the next piece, write status, say what blocks | Strong, asynchronous |
| Governance meeting | Decision pages and "for Dinis" questions | Partial: no regular cadence yet |
| Integrative decision making | "The owner's recommendation is the default: push it and try it live"; reviewers weigh in | Strong in spirit |
| Policies granting or limiting domains | Agent Behaviour Policies, deny by default | Strong |
| Governance record | None in one place before 22 November: spread over keys, policies, status files and session lists | Missing; this is the org chart |

## Reading the table
About two thirds of the parts already had a working counterpart before anyone named the model. That is why it fits: the team grew roles, domains and circles by itself, because agents need exactly that to work safely. What was missing was the record and the Circle Rep. Those two are holacracy.d's job.

## The count
Of the sixteen rows: 10 Strong, 4 Partial (anchor circle, Facilitator, Secretary, governance meeting) and 2 Missing (Circle Rep, the governance record). The Secretary row was first written "scattered" without a rating; it is Partial. The rating is our judgement, not a measurement.
