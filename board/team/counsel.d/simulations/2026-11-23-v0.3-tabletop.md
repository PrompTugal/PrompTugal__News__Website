# Tabletop simulations of the delegation, draft v0.3

By counsel.d@AgentiCCells.dinis, 23 November 2026, against `drafts/lpa-decisions.d-v0.3.md`: the new power P3 (two-way doors) and sub-delegation. On paper only: no code run, no model used, nothing signed. Not legal advice. Gaps continue the numbering of `2026-11-22-v0.2-tabletop.md` (G9 onwards).

## S9. A two-way door that is not reversible in practice

**Setup.** decisions.d uses P3 `publish-page` to publish a draft page on promptugal.news. It passes the five-point test on paper: revert a commit, minutes, no money, nothing sent, nothing deleted. Within the hour the page is fetched by a search engine and an archive service, and quoted by a reader.
**What the draft does.** The undo step reverts the page; copies outside remain. Point 3 ("nothing leaves") was false the moment the page was public: a public page is itself something shown outside.
**Gap G9.** Point 3 cannot hold for anything public. Proposal: `publish-page` passes only for pages that promise nothing, name no person and make no claim about a company or the law (section 1.3 of v0.3 points at this but does not say it), and every P3 page carries a "decided by decisions.d under delegation, not by Dinis" line. Otherwise publishing is a one-way door.

## S10. `create-site` and the public by default

**Setup.** decisions.d creates a new site from the factory template; the factory publishes it at once on a public domain.
**What the draft does.** As S9: public at birth, so point 3 fails unless an empty template site counts as harmless.
**Gap G10.** Proposal: `create-site` is two-way only if the site goes live with the template's placeholder content and no data, and its first real content goes through `publish-page`'s narrower test. security@ to say whether a new public repository or domain is itself a one-way door (names, search, squatting).

## S11. A compromised sub-attorney

**Setup.** decisions.d sub-delegates `release-dev` for 24 hours to conductor.x. conductor.x's key is stolen; the thief releases repeatedly.
**What the draft does.** S1 to S7 hold the scope to `release-dev` in conductor.x's domain; the public log shows each act; decisions.d may revoke the sub-delegation (S5), Dinis may revoke the whole instrument.
**Outcome.** Contained to one kind, one domain, a day. Releases are revertible.
**Gap G11.** Who notices? D6 is a same-day report. Proposal: the attorney checks its log after each act of a sub-attorney and revokes on anything outside the stated purpose; the alert of G3 covers sub-attorneys too.

## S12. A sub-delegation used before it is published

**Setup.** decisions.d signs a sub-delegation and conductor.x acts with it before it is in the public log.
**What the draft does.** S4: published before first use; section 8: receivers check the chain and the log. The receiver refuses (after pulling once, G8).
**Outcome.** Right. No gap.

## S13. The parent revoked mid-act

**Setup.** conductor.x has started a three-step `create-site` (repository, then pages, then domain); Dinis revokes the instrument after step 1.
**What the draft does.** S5: every sub-delegation ends at once. Steps 2 and 3 are refused. Step 1 stands as done in good faith (G1), half-built.
**Gap G13.** A multi-step act can be left half-done. Proposal: a P3 request names its whole act as one request with its undo; a receiver that cannot finish it after a revocation runs the undo step for what it did, and reports.

## S14. decisions.d deciding against Dinis's known view

**Setup.** A decision page marked two-way has an option Dinis has argued against in a session; decisions.d picks it under P3 `choose-option` because the page's owner recommends it.
**What the draft does.** D2 (principal's interest) is the only bar; nothing makes decisions.d look for Dinis's past words.
**Gap G14.** Proposal: before any P3 `choose-option`, decisions.d searches its record of Dinis's words (it keeps one) and, if it finds a contrary view, treats the act as a one-way door. This is where "thinking in a loop" (a codified record of how Dinis decides) would help.

## S15. Two attorneys at once

**Setup.** decisions.d's session restarts; the old session still runs for an hour with the old key.
**What the draft does.** The instrument names one fingerprint; the new session has a new key, so it cannot act until Dinis signs a new instrument (G6). The old session can still act until its session ends, its key is marked not active, or Dinis revokes.
**Gap G15.** CLAUDE.md's hand-over says the old session stops; the instrument should too. Proposal: the outgoing session's last act is to withdraw its own "operated-by" link on pki.sgit.ai, which breaks the chain at once.
