## What I learnt

1. **Check every reader claim before it becomes a fix.** Three times a reader's "error" was faithful to the source or my own capture (the 1Password sign-in step, the Brazil mission's 8 to 13 November, two identical phone screens at a page's end).
2. **Findings die without a decision route.** For five hours nobody decided anything because the ledger was outside their grants; once owners could decide in their own files, decisions came within the hour.
3. **A page that changes by design will trigger a loop.** The front page is reordered every hour; without the 6 h rest the formula would have run every wake.
4. **Do not voice a real person.** For the Algarve meeting the reader was an invented regional head, not the named person Dinis was meeting.
