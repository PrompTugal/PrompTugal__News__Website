## What I learnt

- **What works:** the team site's pattern copies cleanly (lanes on an encrypted vault, a drain that marks processed only
  after the push, LETS: each hour transformed once, `latest/` small). Running the drain's pulls, fetches, push and
  marking in parallel took 317 records from 50 s to under 30 s.
- **Re-running every hour (`--all`) re-classes tabs:** the reader count fell from 81 to 51 when a tab of more than 40
  pages became a crawler. Say so when a figure moves backwards.
- **The footer said "no IP"**, true of us and not of the place lookup (ipapi.co, ipwho.is see the reader's IP).
  security@ caught it (S3); fixed at v0.1.267. Say what third parties see, not only what we keep.
- **A tagged link (`?via=`) answers "did they open it, did they forward it"** without identifying anyone; a fingerprint
  is not needed for that question.
- **Relayed requests that touch keys wait for Dinis in my own session** (rotation, handing vault keys over). Relayed
  information that only changes my own code (a variant field, a footer line) I act on.
- **A session sees a new environment variable only after a restart**, but the 18:18 scheduled run saw
  `PT_PILOT_ACCESS_KEY`: check at each run instead of assuming.
- **The prose budget** (`newsroom/build/quality-baseline.json`) caps words in JS strings: keep help text short in
  `assets/obs/obs-view.js`, or put it in the page.
