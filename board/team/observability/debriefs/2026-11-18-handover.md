## What I learnt

- A role's second session needs the first one's credentials and key to be handed over before the first stops; a
  pause is not a handover. Check that the role's session exists (`list_sessions`) before starting another.
- A drain's own `git pull --rebase` fails halfway when local commits conflict with another session's `status.json`;
  keep local `dev` clean and release often.
