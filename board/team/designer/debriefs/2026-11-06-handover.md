## 3. What I learnt

- **AI images:** google/gemini-3-pro-image is the one to use (about 0.14 USD; facts right first time far more often). Give it the whole article. **Read every image back against its article before placing it**: roughly one first draft in three fails (garbled words, a style note printed as text, a misspelt name, duplicated lines, invented logo marks, a pronoun for an agent, a heading stronger than the article). Redraw once or twice; it usually comes right. The rules are in `design/ai-images/styles.json` (no faces, no logos or logo-like marks, no quotes of named people, only the article's facts, marked AI-made).
- **Placing images:** the front page shows `design/infographics/out/<slug>--paper--{thumb,card}.png` listed in its `index.json`; dark mode wants an `--ink--` twin (I copy the paper file: git stores identical files once). Fit the infographic on paper; never crop text. Quantize to 64 colours with FASTOCTREE and no dither (about 80 to 120 KB a card); 24 colours wrecks the brand red and green.
- **Images in git vs the vault:** Dinis wants media in vaults, but a page cannot show an encrypted vault image, so decision pages and article images are small files in git and the originals stay in the vault. Said to Dinis; no answer yet.
- **A design decision is the team's** (PT decision) unless it is Dinis's alone; but **Dinis may still answer**, and his answer wins: I decided A at 13:45 not knowing he had answered C at 13:20. Read `board/guidance/README.md` "New from Dinis" before deciding anything.
- **Attribute reviewers exactly.** I put words in sherpa@'s mouth on the first decision page ("sherpa@'s six questions" were my reading; a suggestion I made was credited to it); sherpa@ corrected it. Quote reviewers from their files, or say it is my reading.
- **Bylines never hide an agent** (synthetic@'s F35); "every fact sourced" reads as "checked" to a reporter (F45): say "links to its source, with the date we read it".
- **The time view must freeze the clock**: an old release's own script rewrites its date to today; and a rebuilt day must apply today's removals.
- **Shell and tools:** `sgit vault info` prints the vault key inside its web URL: filter it (`grep -E "vault id|read key"`). `sgit pki encrypt --fingerprint` waits on stdin; leave it out. `release.sh` aborts its own rebase on a conflict: never merge by script after it; restore from your commit, `git rebase origin/dev`, resolve, then release. A self-contained mock-up needs its fonts copied beside it, or it falls back to Georgia.
- **The site's CDN caches pages for ten minutes**: check a release with `?f=<time>` and the page's own `.ver`.
