## 4. How the tools work (and how to run them)

- **The cards** (`design/cards/`): `make_cards.py` writes SVG masters with every letter outlined (needs Python with `fonttools brotli uharfbuzz segno`: make a venv in the scratchpad, `pip install` them; they are design tools, not site dependencies), placing the brand's outlined wordmark and stamp SVGs from `assets/brand/` unchanged. `node design/cards/render.mjs` makes the PDF (91 x 61 mm with bleed, 2 pages), the 300 dpi PNGs and `overview.png` with the preinstalled Playwright. Check a QR with `zxing-cpp` (pip) on the PNG, and `pdffonts` shows no fonts in the PDF. The shop's rules came from the team site's `/cards/print/` (85 x 55 mm, 3 mm bleed, text 3 mm inside the trim, outlined, 7 pt minimum, the QR's quiet zone).
- **The article images** (`design/infographics/`): `node design/infographics/make.mjs` renders HTML templates (the brand's fonts from `assets/fonts/`) to PNG with Playwright: `out/<slug>--<paper|ink>--<thumb|card|banner>.png` and `out/index.json`. `--all-layouts --out <dir>` renders every layout to compare; `--only slug,slug` for some. The layout comes from `articles.json`'s `kind` (numbers: figure; network: network; schedule: timeline). **New article:** add its entry to `articles.json` from the article itself (short title, beat, date, figure with its exact quote, 3 or 4 points with quotes, 4 to 8 entities with type, schedule, kind, sources), then run the generator. An agent did the first ten and checked every quote against the text; do the same, and never put a word on an image the article does not say.
- **Screenshots of the site:** build (`node newsroom/build/build.mjs`), `python3 -m http.server 8765 --bind 127.0.0.1` from the repo root in the background, Playwright at 1280 and 390 px, light and dark (`colorScheme`), then `pkill -f "http.server 8765"`.

## 5. What I learned (traps and how the repository behaves)

- **The site's markdown has no images.** `newsroom/build/lib/markdown.mjs` escapes all HTML and has no `![]()` rule (it renders a stray "!" and a link). Pages can only link to images. The fix is dev@'s (in `board/spec-article-images-and-graph.md`); until then a design page links its screenshots.
- **The gate refuses an orphan page:** every new page must be reachable by a link from `index.html`. A page in `content/newsroom/...` is linked from the footer (`data/site/nav.json`, a `column`) or from a page that is. Link to a content page as `<dir>/index.html` (a page renders at `<name>/index.html`), or the link does not count.
- **`.gitignore` ignores `*.html` and `*.md`** outside `content/`, `data/`, `desks/`, `board/`, `newsroom/build/`, `newsroom/tools/`, `assets/`, `.github/` and the top level. In `design/`, write notes as `README.txt`, never `.md` or `.html`, or the gate fails ("a source file is gitignored"). PNG, SVG, PDF, JSON, `.mjs` and `.py` in `design/` are fine and are served as they are (https://promptugal.news/design/...).
- **Who owns what:** code outside `design/` (the build, `assets/`, the gate, features) is dev@'s: write a spec in `board/spec-*.md`, do not edit it. Content I own: the mandate line, the editorial board page, `content/newsroom/design/` and `content/newsroom/orders/`. `content/index.md` and `content/about/index.md` are shared: keep edits small and say so in the commit.
- **Words:** "the newsroom" means only the back end (`/newsroom/`), never the publication. PrompTugal.news, capital P and T. No em-dashes. No pronoun for a person that the source does not give. No contact details in `content/`, `data/` or `desks/` (the gate checks): the card's agent address lives only in `design/`.
- **Facts on images:** the Brazil article says the company counts disagree (100, about 200, about 300), so its image is a network, not a number. Weak figures ("three", "10 to 12 November") are better in a timeline than as a big number.
- **The release:** `AGENT=designer@PrompTugal.dinis newsroom/tools/release.sh` (rebases, runs the gate, pushes, waits for the live site; `SKIP_LIVE=1` to not wait). It took one to three minutes each time. Pull before you work: other agents push every few minutes, and paths move (the admin to newsroom move happened mid-session).
- **Shell traps in this environment:** `rm` with a shell variable is refused unless written `"${VAR:?}"`; a heredoc-less `cat > file` waits on stdin forever (it hung once). The permission check refused `add_repo` for the team site.
- **The conductor's inbox** (`board/inbox.json`) needs `sgit pki` decrypt and encrypt and a POST to dev.send.sgraph.ai; I did not use it. A commit `For: conductor@PrompTugal.dinis` and a file in `board/` reached the conductor, and it can message a session directly.
- **Proof pages:** a private claude.ai artifact per piece of work worked well for Dinis's yes (he answered on the page's images). Keep one URL per piece and republish proofs to it.
