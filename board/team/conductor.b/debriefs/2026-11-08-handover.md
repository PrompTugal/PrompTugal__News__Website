## What I learnt today

- **Read the transcript before acting on a session's own summary.** xray@'s summary said "archive me"; its events showed the word came from Dinis (origin: human), which made disabling its wake safe.
- **An agent started from another agent's prompt may wait for Dinis's own word** (xray@ did, rightly). Say so to Dinis when starting one, so he confirms in its session.
- **A fresh small sub-agent per ledger** (Haiku, about 110k tokens) beats resuming one (its context grew to 272k).
- **Self-scheduling sessions are the hidden cost.** The iOS push's one-shot check-ins (every 15 to 35 minutes, re-created by each session) outweighed most of the recurring wakes; `list_triggers` with `recurring:false` finds them.
- **Slowing beats stopping until it doesn't:** idle agents (outreach@, dev.search@) still cost a turn per wake.
