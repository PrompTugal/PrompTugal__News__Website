# Memo 3: two appliances, their boxes, and the field production workflow

## Dinis's words (8 November 2026, about 13:55 UTC, spoken, lightly cleaned)

**On the device accounts** (corrects memo 2, point 7):
> "The Apple ID that we're going to use is fundamentally the email account that we're creating and giving to the agents. So it's connected to a human, it's still a human on it, but it's that account that has a full-blown Google Workspace with everything set up. Which for us is also a great example of security: it's way more secure if you actually have a dedicated iPad account and a dedicated iPad with all this stuff."

**Two products, about 1,500 each:**
> "We're going to create two products, which we're going to price probably about 1,500, feels about the right price: a device for RiskMandate and one for PrompTugal."

**The boxes:**
> "We need to visualize the whole thing, but ideally we also work with the designer that created the T-shirts for us. Ping them to say if they can create product boxes that literally look like a product, software, hardware, a bit like the iPad's, or maybe a sleeve that we can put on the iPad boxes, but it could just be the box itself."

**1. The RiskMandate Appliance** ("the RiskMandate auditor iPad ... the RiskMandate appliance, that's a good name for it"):
> "A device that somebody can take and bring on, and that device is going to manage the data, hold all the data securely for that particular RiskMandate environment. It's going to be isolated. It might even run a local model, let's see if we can make it there. It's going to be used by the customer to do all the mappings, and help them with all the reviews and all the RiskMandate data analysis. It's not an iPad, it could be Android, but for the moment we go with iPad because we have the App Store sorted out. We already have a RiskMandate app: we can even have a mode for that app, which is when you are in appliance mode."

**2. The PrompTugal Field Reporter** (Indi first, and Vicky):
> "Indi is going to act as our field journalist, our field podcaster, social media creator. Indi already has a camera and a tripod, but we now have to think about the publishing workflow: how does everything fit together, the development pipeline, the production pipeline. And we need a production device. We're also going to sell the field reporter, an appliance for the PrompTugal field agent, or journalist."

> "From a news organisation's point of view: every news organisation has to provide devices for their staff. Instead of sending the journalist a half-baked laptop with a whole bunch of stuff they don't need, not integrated, not customised to their workflow, we ship them a device, this iPad, which has everything they need. We could even sell or pre-install other tools (Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro, other apps). What matters is that we give the customer a price."

> "And it matters for us: we go from a publication with an interesting website to a publication that has an app on the App Store and products for sale, and then it's a question of scale, a very different proposition."

> "Indi needs this already: the production app and the production workflow. Vicky needs it too, to publish things live. Between Indi and Vicky we need very visual, newsroom-style publishing workflows, a unified app, and maybe more: maybe everybody in the team starts to create their own app, because it's actually not that hard. Which is why we need a device with the developer tools installed. I had to install some of this on my own iPad and flick developer mode: that should not run on individuals' devices. It should run on the agentic iPads, which we will then sell."

## The two products (to map; nothing priced as decided)

| | RiskMandate Appliance | PrompTugal Field Reporter |
|---|---|---|
| For | a company assessing its agents (an auditor, a CISO, a risk team) | a field journalist, podcaster, social creator; a newsroom equipping its staff |
| The device | an iPad (Android later), its own account, isolated | an iPad with its own account, the production apps |
| Holds | one RiskMandate environment's data, securely | the reporter's drafts, media and publishing queue |
| Does | the mappings (mandate and reach), the reviews, the analysis; perhaps a local model | capture, edit, publish, live, through a newsroom-style workflow |
| Software | the RiskMandate app in appliance mode | the PrompTugal app (the iOS team's), with optional extras (Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro, ...) |
| Price | about 1,500 (Dinis's sense; to decide) | about 1,500 (to decide) |
| Box | product box or sleeve in RiskMandate's look | product box or sleeve in PrompTugal's look |
