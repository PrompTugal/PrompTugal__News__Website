## What I learnt
- Search summaries are often wrong on dates and numbers (one dated a 2025 event to 2026, another a 2024 article to 2026); always read at source and say which. Wikipedia is a tertiary source: use it to find, then read the organiser.
- The bare `algarveevolution.pt` does not answer; `www.algarveevolution.pt` does. Several news sites (Sul Informacao, Portugal Resident, rr.pt, postal.pt for some pages) answer 403, 401 or a verification wall to WebFetch; others work. An event's own "register" page names organiser and partners: a calendar listing is not the organisation running it (two of the three events on its calendar are run by others).
- Official Web Summit numbers are in PDFs under websummit.com/wp-media/ (2022, 2023 read); the pages in the speakers and partners lists load more entries by script, so WebFetch sees only the first page.
- Names: the conductor's brief used "Antonio Pinto Guerreiro"; no source gives that form. Every source says "Antonio Guerreiro, executive director". Do not confuse with the president of Algarve STP (Joao Guerreiro) or Joao Pinto Guerreiro.
