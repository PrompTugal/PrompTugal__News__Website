## What I learnt

1. **A channel is not working until the receiver has read on it.** The agent inboxes were issued at 04:25 and most agents could not drain them until 14:00 (sgit missing, installs refused). Directions sent only on lanes were lost (designer@ shipped A after Dinis chose C). Now: every direction from Dinis goes into `board/guidance/` and the "New from Dinis" table first (agents read it at every wake), and urgent ones also session to session.
2. **Strict-first policies stall a team; observe first.** ABP in deny mode blocked real work within an hour; observe mode with an hourly review kept the team moving.
3. **A budget that stops an agent costs more than it saves.** Budgets are measured and adjusted, never a stop (Dinis, 10:10); the dollars are API-equivalent, the real limit is the subscription's weekly limit.
4. **Decisions are pages, and most are the team's.** Tick boxes in a PDF were not enough; a page with every option in full is. Dinis decides only what is his alone; the owner's recommendation is the default, pushed and tried live.
5. **Ask the other team before building.** The Web Summit team already had a feedback form; the conductors' sync (`board/the-conductors-sync.md`) now checks the other board before opening work.
6. **A sprint works when the reviewer merges on every push.** The Algarve Evolution page went from a page to a mapped section in 35 minutes because dev@ watched the branches every 150 seconds.
7. **Never paste or print a secret.** Five inbox credentials reached transcripts today; those inboxes were re-issued. Keys go in the environment, read without printing.
