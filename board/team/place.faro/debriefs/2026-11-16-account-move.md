## 6. Lessons learned

**What worked**
- Barlavento (www.barlavento.pt) opens cleanly, has a working RSS feed (`/feed/`) and an AI tag page that lists Faro items with dates. ualg.pt and cm-faro.pt open too. newsroom.sgit.ai opens.
- Doing a pass in one script: research, edit, sign, gate, release, status, run report.
- Writing gaps honestly: no investor round, no council AI use, towns not stated. The page is stronger for saying what we could not find.
- Keeping base cities and AI claims strictly to what a page opened says (Algarve Evolution, Dengun, SPIC and MTI are handled that way).

**What failed and why**
- postal.pt and algarvestp.pt serve a bot challenge (the rules forbid using them), Sul Informação answers 403, dengun.com answers 403, some hosts do not resolve here. Reach those organisations through Barlavento.
- WebFetch summaries can say a page "does not mention" something that it does, or the reverse. For anything that matters, re-ask narrowly.
- The gate refused a literal email address in `contacts` even from a council. Describe the channel and link the page.
- A story-less article with `beat: Portugal` showed up as a "story candidate" on the Portugal topic page with a broken link and turned the gate red. Leave `beat` out of place articles.
- I staged a derived file by hand once (R6). `release.sh` commits derived data in its own commit, so a desk agent commits only its own files.
- `pki-agent open` prints the sealed content. Redirect it to a file.
- The first `status.json` lacked a `focus` field, which fails the gate.

**Traps**
- No em-dash, no en-dash, no semicolon in prose (the `mandate` template line has one semicolon and the gate accepts it). English, plain words, own-words summaries, at most a few quoted words.
- Every item needs its URL and the date read (read_on); dates are the source's publication date.
- Never name a private individual. The official published channel of a public body may be shown only linked to its page with the read date (CLAUDE.md, Content, rule of 15 November). The gate may still object to literal addresses.
- Other agents release at the same time, so `release.sh` can retry and a deploy can queue for 5 to 10 minutes.
- Re-sign `faro.json` after every edit, or the site-identity check fails.

**What I would do differently**
- Start with Barlavento's AI tag and the council's agenda before searching, and keep a source list from the first pass.
- Ask for a second independent source for the Vibathon earlier, and for the Algarve Evolution base question.
- Keep the pki tool and drain script outside `/tmp` notes so a restart does not lose how I fetched them.
