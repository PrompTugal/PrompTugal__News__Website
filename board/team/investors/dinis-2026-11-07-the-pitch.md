# The pitch, in his words (lightly cleaned from dictation; nothing added)

## What we sell

- "What we're selling is the PrompTugal service. Forget about My Feeds: My Feeds is a story." It is the legal company he created in Portugal, "which now is actually a nice story because it's connected to publishing. Ironically, it all aligns really well."
- RiskMandate "happened to be the company that was more mature, so that's why we're going to the summit with it."
- "We're building the next generation of news publishing, and we're already making it work: it's working, and it's released." Look at the features being added, at the fact "we just created this publication", and imagine the speed "even next week".
- An iOS application has just been made: "hopefully it will be approved by the App Store; if not, we can at least deploy it on our devices."
- "The future is already here": micropayments, the technology stack, user levels, serverless, "an agentic workflow, which is basically what we're selling. A lot of people talk about creating agentic workflows; we have one here."
- "We are selling the participation of humans and agents working together. It's the whole operationalisation of news."
- "The last count, we already had 111 agents flying around. It's scaling, and we're publishing."

## The angle at the summit

- If it manages attendees and participation, if people are excited, try it, "get the news from it", "then we can say: this is the future of the news publishing industry". Then it is whether someone wants to invest.
- The value proposition: "look at what we've done in this amount of time. Look at the team. Look at the execution. Look at the agentic flows."
- For investors: "they can invest in an open source company and be part of the journey. And then we talk about terms."

## What to publish

- "Publish a lot of the strategy. Everything except the ask and the terms." No numbers committed: "we don't have them, so we should be transparent."
- "Map a commercial strategy. Start executing. As we sell things, improve and add features, update the pitch deck." The deck: "a super powerful pitch deck, similar to the other pitch decks I created, super rich", mostly showing the features; managed by agents.
- Read what he has published on newsroom.sgit.ai, its publishing part included: "a lot of the business models, the justification and the rationale is there."
- "A couple of presentations to position ourselves as thinkers": where technology is going, where the news industry is going, what we wish the industry would do, even as customers.
- Investors (for example those around Algarve Evolution) "are going to ask for a business plan. Let's give them one": provide services, be profitable; the tech stack costs very little; revenue from usage; **no advertising, no tracking of users**; services on top, customisation, customised views of the app for participants; the same technology for other events ("worth a lot of money"); a publishing platform that here focuses on an event but could manage content of all sorts. "The power is the agentic team that we put in place."
- **The rules of engagement:** an investor consumes it first, "even agentically", so when they talk to us they are already warm: open source, everything shared. "The only people we want to talk to are the people excited about the technology", who will be users and have tried it. Gentler than investor.myfeeds.ai ("I was probably a bit aggressive on that one").
- If revenue comes, "that changes the whole game"; until then no numbers.
- **The history:** "this is not my first rodeo of building in the news industry": My Feeds, then Newsroom, and more, and then this; "we only built this in a week because we were piggybacking on everything else". Show that evolution in the investor section.
- **A competitive analysis section**: how we compare, where we are.
- Reference his articles, or variations of them: "the philosophical, the strategic and the thinking is already all done."
