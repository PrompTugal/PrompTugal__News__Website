PrompTugal.news was built in days because it stands on everything before it. This is not Dinis Cruz's first newsroom.

## The strategy behind it {#strategy}

Dinis Cruz runs several startups under one strategy, written up in full at [open-source.sgit.ai](https://open-source.sgit.ai/): "open source is a strategy, not a charity". The code is open; what is sold is "the running, maintained, certified service"; and "a competitor who forks our code today gets our position as of today. They do not get our velocity." Code is Apache-2.0 and documents CC BY 4.0.

Having many startups is the point: each is built on the others. The ones the strategy names ([open-source.sgit.ai/about](https://open-source.sgit.ai/about/index.md), read 7 November 2026):

| Startup | What it is |
|---|---|
| [sgit.ai](https://sgit.ai/) | Encrypted vaults with git's semantics; the keys, signatures and vaults under PrompTugal |
| [sgraph.ai](https://sgraph.ai/) | The commercial home of SG/Send and hosted vaults |
| [MyFeeds.ai](https://investor.myfeeds.ai/) | Role-aware briefings on semantic knowledge graphs; its company, My Feeds, now publishes PrompTugal |
| [The Cyber Boardroom](https://thecyberboardroom.com/) | An AI platform between security teams and the board |
| [RiskMandate.ai](https://riskmandate.ai/) | The business risk layer for autonomous systems |
| [VoiceDebrief.ai](https://voicedebrief.ai/) | Voice recordings to transcripts, in the browser |

PrompTugal is the newest, and the first built from all of them at once. Each is its own company or project; investing in PrompTugal is investing in My Feeds only.

## The timeline {#timeline}

| When | What | What it added |
|---|---|---|
| November 2024 | **My Feeds** ([investor.myfeeds.ai](https://investor.myfeeds.ai/), version of 22 November 2024): explainable, personalised briefings for each role, built on knowledge graphs, open source and serverless | The idea: one feed per reader, every item with its source |
| February 2025 | **The Cyber Boardroom's** public investment material ([cbr-investment](https://github.com/the-cyber-boardroom/cbr-investment)): news providers paid for trust and personalised feeds on semantic graphs | The business case for trusted, personalised news (inspiration only: that company is not part of this investment) |
| February to October 2025 | **A library of ten long articles** on trust, provenance and paying for knowledge (archived at [newsroom.sgit.ai/library](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/library/index.html)), among them "Micro and nano payments" (2 April 2025, [kept here](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/economics/micro-and-nano-payments.html)) | The thinking behind pay-per-read and fact provenance |
| 2025 to March 2026 | **SG/Send becomes sgit** ([eight months of SG/Send](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/from-send-to-sgit.html)): encrypted vaults, keys and append lanes; sgit's command-line releases from 20 March 2026 | The vaults, keys and signatures every PrompTugal agent and reader uses |
| 17 August 2026 | **The Newsroom** ([newsroom.sgit.ai](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/)): one editor and agents; its articles begin, its roles designed on 23 August | The agent-run newsroom, reused here |
| September 2026 | **Event and country beats:** Startup Summit Lisbon (from 13 September, later [pt.newsroom.sgit.ai](https://pt.newsroom.sgit.ai/)) and the World News Day corpus (28 September) | The shape of an event newsroom and a country newsroom |
| 11 October 2026 | **Pay for what you read goes live:** the reading meter, a starting credit and a card top-up ([the reading account, as built](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/the-reading-account-as-built.html)) | The micropayments and the reader's vault, reused here |
| October 2026 | **A newsroom for a conference** ([php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai](https://php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/)) and a book written by nine agents and one editor ([the case study](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/the-ultimate-insider-case-study.html), 29 October) | The multi-agent team method at scale |
| 4 November 2026 | **PrompTugal.news:** first commit at 00:08 UTC; four names in its first 30 hours; a live publication with signed articles, the summit's days, the graph, search and an agent team within days | Everything above, reused, at the speed of an agentic team |

The record of each step is public: the site's [timeline by our historian agent](https://github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Website/blob/dev/board/team/historian/timeline.md) and the repository's history.
