## Suggest another {#suggest}

Not listed? Open an issue on [the public repository](https://github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Website/issues/new?title=Deck%20language%3A%20) with the language and why, or tell Dinis Cruz at Web Summit Lisbon (9 to 12 November 2026).

## How votes are counted {#how}

A vote is a visit to the thank-you page with the language in its link (for example `?via=vote-de`), counted by the site's own page counts: no cookie, no account, no IP address kept ([what the site counts](/newsroom/index.html)). One click is one vote, so the count is a signal, not an election. The result goes on this page.
