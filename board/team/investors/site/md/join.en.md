## Help this week {#help}

The summit is 9 to 12 November 2026. The seven of us at the venue are the front of house; we need people behind and beside them, working the same way the current team does: openly, with visibility, unpaid, in a win-win where everyone learns. The team today is a mix of Gen Z and Gen X, and it was onboarded in days.

What helping looks like: a few hours on a beat (a day of the summit, a country, a sector), with the agents doing the repeatable work and you deciding what is true, useful and well told.

## Join the founding team {#founders}

There are no founding shares to hand out on a website, and we will not pretend otherwise. What there is: a company that is raising, a plan, and a promise of how it will work. When investment comes, everyone on the team has a stake in the game (shares that come alive on milestones or a sale, like UK growth shares, or according to how they come in), and everyone is paid; then we accelerate. Who gets there first is the people who are here before it is official.

## The partner we need {#partner}

Dinis Cruz is the founder and CTO: he builds the technology that powers PrompTugal, full time, across his companies. What PrompTugal needs is a **CEO**: a business partner who has run companies, ideally senior in the news industry, to run the operations, the customers and the company, and to bring the industry's experience the team does not yet have. Maybe also an investor. From anywhere in the world.

## Journalists {#journalists}

Hiring journalists is one of the first uses of the investment: very technical people, who want to work with agents as their research and production desk, with every fact sourced and every article signed, and no tracking of the people who read them.
