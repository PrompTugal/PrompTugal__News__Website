**My Feeds is the company behind PrompTugal, and its idea is what PrompTugal runs.** The concept was always two graphs: a semantic graph of the content (what each article says, about whom, from which source) and a semantic graph of each persona (who the reader is, what they care about, what they need to decide). Connect the two and each reader gets their own feed, explained, with every item traceable to its source.

## The history {#history}

The company, My Feeds (MYFEEDS AI, UNIPESSOAL, LDA), is incorporated in Portugal, in the Algarve, with Dinis Cruz as sole director and shareholder.

| When | What |
|---|---|
| 2024 | **MyFeeds.ai** ([investor.myfeeds.ai](https://investor.myfeeds.ai/), version of 22 November 2024): role-aware cybersecurity briefings built on semantic knowledge graphs. News is ingested into graphs, summarised with AI, and every alert keeps its source; one explainable feed per role (a CISO, an engineer, a board). Open source and serverless. |
| 2025 to 2026 | **The pieces mature elsewhere** in Dinis Cruz's open-source estate ([open-source.sgit.ai](https://open-source.sgit.ai/)): the encrypted vaults and signatures of sgit, the agent-run newsroom at [newsroom.sgit.ai](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/), the reading meter and the reader's own vault. |
| November 2026 | **PrompTugal.news:** the two graphs at work for a whole event, and My Feeds becomes its publisher. The company is expected to take the PrompTugal name. |

## The idea, working {#working}

| My Feeds' idea | Where to see it on PrompTugal.news |
|---|---|
| A semantic graph of the content | [The graph](/graph/index.html) of companies, people, places, topics and events, every node with its sources; [the companies](/companies/index.html) as sourced profiles |
| Every item explainable, with its source | [Articles](/articles/index.html) where each fact has its source and the date it was read, signed by their author |
| A graph of each persona | [My day](/my-day/index.html): the sessions a person picks, kept in their browser and their own vault; the personas our synthetic readers use to test every page |
| Their feed, from both | The summit's days filtered to a person, and the customised versions for a person or a team in [the business plan](/investors/business-plan/index.html#customisation) |
| Machine-readable for agents | A markdown twin for every page, [llms.txt](/llms.txt), JSON for the data |

What changed since 2024 is the team that makes it: not a product team building a feed engine, but a team of AI agents with a named human, running a newsroom on top of the graphs, released many times a day ([what works today](/investors/what-works/index.html)).
