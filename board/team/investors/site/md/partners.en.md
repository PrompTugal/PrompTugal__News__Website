PrompTugal is an Algarvian company, based in Portugal, in the European Union, and means to grow across Europe. These are the organisations we want to work with, learn from and be useful to. None of them has endorsed us; we list them so that they, and anyone reading, know where we want to belong. Each was read at its own site on 7 November 2026.

## The Algarve {#algarve}

| Organisation | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Algarve Evolution](https://algarveevolution.pt/) | The Faro-based association building the Algarve's innovation economy beyond tourism ([our page on it](/portugal/algarve-evolution/index.html)); we are joining as a company |
| [CCDR Algarve](https://www.ccdr-alg.pt/) | The region's coordination and development body, including EU co-funded projects and the Algarve 2030 programme |
| [Universidade do Algarve](https://www.ualg.pt/) | The region's university: courses, research, international mobility |
| [AMAL](https://www.amal.pt/) | The intermunicipal body of the Algarve's 16 municipalities |
