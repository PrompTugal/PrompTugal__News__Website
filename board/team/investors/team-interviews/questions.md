# The questions (for each person on the team, answered in their own words, short is fine)

**Consent first.** Your answers become a public profile on promptugal.news (/investors/team/) only if you say yes to the final text. You can answer some and skip others, use your first name only, and withdraw it later. No contact details are ever published.

1. **What do you do on the team,** and what do your agents do for you?
2. **Why are you here?** What made you say yes?
3. **What have you learned** that you could not have learned elsewhere, or not as fast?
4. **What is the coolest thing** you have seen the team or the agents do?
5. **You are doing this unpaid.** Why does it work for you? What do you get from it?
6. **Why does the model work** (people and agents, in public, learning together), and where does it not work yet?
7. **What would you tell someone** thinking of joining for the summit week?
8. **Anything else** you want a future colleague, partner or investor to know?

## How the answers become profiles

investors@ drafts each profile from the answers (their words, lightly edited, nothing added), sends the draft back for a yes, and publishes only what is approved. The profiles go on /investors/team/ and feed the "join us" deck (deck 7): the strongest argument for joining is the people already here, in their own words.
