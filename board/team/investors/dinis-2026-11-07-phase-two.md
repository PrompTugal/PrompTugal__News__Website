## Copy us: the moat is decisions and execution
- "People look at open source and worry that everybody can copy. Others could do what we've done; RiskMandate is open source. The reason people can't is that part of the power is decision making: the way we make decisions, the speed: from Lisbon Wire to PrompTugal, a website in days; the team assembled; the work on RiskMandate. It's that execution, that ability to keep doing it."
- "My vision is shaped by having been an executive, doing security, doing new things, publishing content, the Open Security Summit: experience running shows and events is what allows me to set up this team."
- "If somebody creates newsrooms or events like this, they (a) validate the model, (b) validate the structure, and (c) are a company we could work with or eventually acquire, or collaborate with. That's a power of open source: if they don't do it open source, they never get our acceleration; if they do, we can leverage what they do. The market is big enough."
- "What makes this work is the agentic workflows, the thinking, making decisions, the orchestration, connecting business and technology, the ability to execute."
- "Say it: go and copy, go and do it." Read his article on why open source is good for investors. The investor's choice: invest here, or run with it."

## The two phases and the exit
- "Two phases, and we should be very realistic. The second phase is when there is already an exit for the first round of founders; then you choose whether to stay. I like to start these companies. When it moves into the villagers and town planners phase, more professional, more execution, more events, the growth stage, there is less to develop; for me it's less interesting. It's a handover: I'm already making myself redundant, which is what good leadership should do. I'm preparing for my exit from the beginning."
- "It comes down to: (a) can this be a profitable business, (b) can this company be purchased in the future, (c) is this the team to execute. If the investor says yes, yes, yes, they should invest."
