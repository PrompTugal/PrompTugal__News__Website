# The investor site, and personalised versions

His words where they carry the decision; the rest summarised.

## What he thinks of the work so far

- "it's an amazing piece of work ... I love the whole sort of like, here we go and do it. I thought we positioned you really well."

## Phase one: a website, navigation first, for feedback

- The problem: "the website looks pretty rough ... it's not very appealing. There's a lot of text ... it takes me a while and a lot of kind of text in there to actually go and click the view."
- "Imagine that you're building a homepage for this thing ... the same quality ... the same design, the same sort of workflows ... a big design push for the website."
- Two rounds; the first "start from the point of view of navigating. So less around the content. I think the content now is good."
- "the main thing to do now is get feedback ... I want feedback."
- Align everything on the audience: "do you want to invest in the company? Are you looking to be a partner? You're going to be involved? ... what's the flow?" and "a lot of this is also for a company that wants to partner with us ... give us projects. They kind of want to understand where we are."
- Articles "that I can then post on the LinkedIn and other places."
- "treat that investors area as literally a standalone website, which also should be in the four languages ... every page I can easily swap language ... a full-blown website ... very visual."
- Infographics: OpenRouter (Gemini, ChatGPT images) "where needed ... if it looks better"; try it: "Give a full PDF or all the visuals, all the text to ChatGPT, and then see what you can create."
- "a very effective website to do, again, contact us"; observability: "a dedicated observability lane just for this ... different audiences"; and articles about it on the main publication will use its visuals. "At the moment, the deck has better visuals ... than the website. So now focus on the website."

## The master deck and reading in sequence

- "you actually need one, which is the master slide deck, which basically tells what the other slide decks [are] ... a cohesive narrative, how they fit together" and an article on it, "so that excites somebody to go and read the decks"; "a nice collage of the decks and ... the best arguments that are made in all the decks."
- "I also need a way to read them all in sequence, visually."

## Phase two: personalised versions

- "a version of this website that is dedicated for an Algarve Evolution investor": "an access key ... a QR code ... fundamentally probably a vault key ... that unlocks the website for a particular audience. And then from that moment on, all of the pages of the website reflect that." It might be in one language.
- "an agent here that ... on a loop keeps creating these personalized versions of the website for a particular individual ... hi, here's who you are, here's what we know about you ... here's the slide deck ... you should view first, or even some of the slides might even be modified ... brand the things for this."
- "make up 3 or 4 personas": an angel investor in Portugal, an angel investor in the Algarve, a seasoned journalist, a not-for-profit organisation around the publishing industry; "maybe you have a 5th."
- Later: "I can give you names and maybe their LinkedIn PDF and ... background story of where I know them, why I think they'll be a good fit"; delivered by QR code, which can carry "a bit of metadata ... where he came from."
- The examples "should already work": "here's the QR code for them, click here or copy and paste this ... drag and drop this QR code there, and then you see the website in that sort of new mode."
