# An Algarvian startup, and the commercial model (his words, lightly cleaned; nothing added)

## The narrative

- His strategy is at open-source.sgit.ai. "You should not shy away from the fact that I have all these startups. That's the point: I have this strategy and I'm executing it."
- "Push the idea that this is a company from the Algarve. We're going to join Algarve Evolution as a company. It is an Algarvian company": a startup "that is going to disrupt the news publishing industry, but we are actually doing this stuff", commercialising content, "part of the Algarvian ecosystem".
- "I'm looking for partners: investors and business partners to run with this." Some of the team "might be involved, but for now we can't make anything official. We haven't talked about this; this is all me accelerating." (Name no one: they might or might not be part of it.)
- "A company that I created, that I've been working on. It's only possible because of all the work I've been doing on sgit and the newsroom: although this is coming together now, I've been working on this for the last years."
- My Feeds is incorporated in Portugal, and "now we want to apply for grants and for investment. We need to start talking to people: what do we need to do, what's the flow, how can we be supported, what exists here."

## The commercial model

- **Multiple revenue streams.** First, **pay per use** (micropayments): the more people use the site and find it useful, the more pages they read, "and they're already paying from day one". They top up because they see the value. "Instead of people buying based on future value, people buy because they already had value": they discover talks, where to go, what to learn; "by the time they get to pay, they will be happy with it". Content pays, especially original content.
- **Customisation** ("ridiculously powerful"): for this summit alone, the logistics of a team going (where, what to see, the information, the preparation, briefings before and after a talk), a version with only the materials a person will see, their day, their meetings. "Not very far from where we already are"; these core features are what we sell.
- **Customised versions for other events,** as in his other work: "we sell customised versions of open source."
- **The agentic team, deployed** for projects, for events, for content.
- **Agentic workflows:** companies want to learn them; sell around that; companies may deploy with this technology.
- **Data for agents:** feeds, objects, triplets, JSON files, graph objects, processed data that make agents faster and cheaper; "the agents should be paying us" (the librarian's work, the semantic knowledge graphs).
- **Data packs:** intelligence packs (someone suggested threat-intelligence-style packs), for someone who wants to invest in something: who is who, AI activity in Portugal, mapped and tracked.
- **The publishing industry:** "they should be investing in what we do, because they need to change"; work and revenue around existing publications, in Portugal and around the world.
- **Multilingual and multicultural** pages, once it matures a little more: a much wider market.
- **Events:** most events do not manage their data well before, during or after.
- **Use of investment:** development, user experience, paying the team to work on it, and go to market.
- **Validation at Web Summit:** if the app succeeds there is investor interest; if not, less. "Let's assume we can have interest."
- **Timeline:** "try to be profitable in the next couple of months and take it from there". Discover the market's appetite to pay; there are events regularly.
