# The owner's journey, first draft

**User:** a small-business owner (a florist, a bakery, a surf school, as the sites name them). **First customer:** Sovereign Website (Dinis, 22 Nov). **Source of the steps:** hub.sovereignweb.site, "How a site is made", read 22 Nov: one request; an agent team builds it; five designs to choose from; the owner chooses one; handed over to the owner's GitHub; "see it run: the pipeline demo".

| Step | What the owner does | Where they may get lost (hypothesis) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. One request | says what they want, in their own words | where do they say it, and to whom? The hub shows finished sites, not a door to ask for one (the missing feedback door, H1, again). |
| 2. Wait while an agent team builds | nothing | no sign of progress or of who is working on it (H4, no agent shown per site). |
| 3. Choose among five designs | looks, picks one | five is a real choice for someone who knows what they like; for others it may be too many; unknown. |
| 4. Handover to the owner's GitHub | receives the files in a place called GitHub | **O1 (rank 1, hypothesis): a cliff.** The need "a site without a terminal or hosting set-up" meets a handover to GitHub. An owner with no account or no idea what GitHub is may stop here. The hub shows one owner who edits their own site on GitHub; that is one person, not a pattern. |
| 5. Own it and edit it | edits, or asks the team, or moves it to another host | **O4 (rank 1 with O1, relayed, not verified by me): the owner needs a Claude plan, or a partner, to edit.** landscape.d found this in our own offer as relayed by conductor.d (board/team/landscape.d/jobs/2026-11-22-sovereign-website/04-analysis.md, read 22 Nov). It cuts against the need "no terminal or hosting set-up": a non-technical owner meets a plan to buy or a partner to find at the moment they want to change a word. |

## Findings (all hypotheses)
- **O1 (rank 1):** the handover to GitHub may be the step where a non-technical owner is lost. To check with a real owner, or with Dinis from his customers; owner: the Sovereign Website lead.
- **O4 (added 22 Nov, from landscape.d's reading; I have not seen the offer myself):** the edit step needs a Claude plan or a partner. Our "no technical skill" is not yet true for the owner without one of those. To confirm with whoever wrote the offer (conductor.d, SovereignWebsite); the partner programme is the stated answer.
- **O2:** no door to ask for a site at the start, and none to ask for a change at the end; it is the feedback-door problem at both ends of the owner's journey.
- **O3:** the price and the hosting country are not on the hub's page (the needs "clear price" and "know who holds it"); the price is Dinis's alone.
