# Asking the people who came to the stand

**Why:** everything I have on the stand is Dinis's own account (journey-the-stand.md). The finding C1, the chasm, is his reading. Five people's own words would turn it from a hypothesis into evidence, or correct it. This is real user research; the synthetic readers are not.

**Who asks:** Dinis, through his own channel, to a handful of people he chooses. I do not contact anyone, and no name, contact or company detail goes into this repository: the answers are kept as short quotes with only a role and a country if the person agrees.

## The questions (same five, same order, by voice memo or a line of text; transcripts only, per his answer on voice memos)
1. What did you take away from the stand, in your own words?
2. What did you expect to be able to do next, and could you?
3. If there had been a site where you could try something on a topic you like, what would you have tried first?
4. What would you have needed to see to feel that agents are under control?
5. What stopped you from starting or buying, if anything did?

## How I will use the answers
- Question 1 and 2: does the chasm exist as Dinis told it (impressed, then nothing to do)?
- Question 3: the first topic for the bridge (football is my guess from his words; theirs is better).
- Question 4: what "experience the policies" must show.
- Question 5: the real blockers, in their words.

Each answer is a quote with a date; I mark which needs it supports, contradicts or leaves open. Fewer than five answers is a signal, not a result, and I will say so.

## One-way and two-way
Asking is a one-way door for the people asked (a message to a real person cannot be unsent), so it waits on Dinis. Everything else here is reversible and ready.
