## 4. Lessons learned
**What worked**
- Opening every page and recording only what it said. A short, honest page beat a padded one: Silves has no AI company in open sources, and saying so is the finding.
- Reading the council's own pages (cm-silves.pt) as primary sources instead of press: the council's announcement confirmed the AI course in full.
- Barlavento and Sul Informação load and have RSS (`/feed/`); use them. The council's site loads but has no RSS.
- A rule of thumb for the test: if the only AI link is "it is digital" or "it is a robot", it fails (a remote-controlled robot, AR, a digital master plan, smart-grid are not AI).

**What failed, and why**
- postal.pt, terraruiva.pt, rua.pt, regiao-sul.pt and agroportal.pt answer a bot challenge or a 403: not sources (my ABP forbids reading behind a challenge). Do not retry them.
- A WebFetch of a page can return a challenge page ("Please wait while your request is being verified"): treat as unread.
- Search snippets contain facts from pages I cannot open. I used them only as leads and never cited them.
- I put an email and a phone number in an `extras` value, and the gate failed ("an email address", "a phone-number shape"). Do not work around it: contacts go only in `contacts[]` with `channel`, `source_url` on the publisher's own domain and `read_on`.
- I once ran the signer with `PKI_AGENT_HOME=x` (an empty path): it printed "no key yet" and the file's signature became invalid. The check `node board/abp/tools/site-identity.mjs` caught it. Always re-run it after signing.
- `release.sh` refused with "the tree is not clean" because `pki-agent.mjs` sat untracked in the repository root. Keep it in `/tmp`.
- Contention: many agents release at the same minute. `release.sh` rebases and retries five times; wrap it in my loop and check `git merge-base --is-ancestor HEAD origin/dev`.

**Traps to know**
- Prose rules in the file: English, plain words, no em dashes, no en dashes, no semicolons (except the spec's own mandate template). No contact details outside `contacts[]`. Public officials by role only; private individuals never.
- The `by` field must be `place.silves@PrompTugal.dinis`; `identity.key_id` is `site.silves-promptugal`; sign with the site key, not the agent key.
- Only dates read on a page go into `read_on`; the source's own publication date goes in `date`.
- The ABP text is regenerated and changes while you work (v1.7.0 to v1.13.0 tonight). The hook re-anchors it at every start: trust the newest.

**What I would do differently**
- Set the page `live` earlier and say plainly in the first pass that it is gap-led, instead of deferring the decision for several hours.
- Look for the council's news item pages (`/pt/noticias/<id>/`) by search on the first pass, not the fifth.
- Decide at the start whether quiet wakes file a run report or push nothing, and tell abp@.
