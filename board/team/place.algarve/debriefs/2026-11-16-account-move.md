## 4. Lessons learned

**What worked**
- **Feeds that work for my reader** (public RSS, no challenge): Barlavento's AI tag `https://www.barlavento.pt/etiquetas/inteligencia-artificial/feed/`, Sul Informação `https://www.sulinformacao.pt/feed/`, Jornal do Algarve `https://www.jornaldoalgarve.pt/feed/`, Postal `https://postal.pt/feed/`. Their **article pages** mostly block me (403 or a bot challenge), so only headlines and recent items are usable from Sul Informação, Jornal do Algarve and Postal. Barlavento article pages load and are the best dated source (use `?p=<id>` links as the town agents do).
- **Algarve Evolution's members list** is paginated at `/model/members/page/N?order=name+asc&search=` (67 members, four pages). Member pages give names only but link each firm's own site, which is where AI evidence lives. That found **LeiGPT** (a Faro AI platform for lawyers on Portuguese law), **ADJ3 Sistemas** (Faro, AI chatbots and search for Drupal sites), **Neomarca** (Faro, AI-adoption consulting), **Flow Productions** (Faro and Lisbon, MarTech and AI), **DSPA** (national data science and AI association). Most members show no AI.
- **Portugal 2030's call plan** is a public spreadsheet (`plano-anual-de-avisos`): parse with stdlib. Finding: of 177 planned calls (about 2.04 billion euros) only seven mention AI or digital words, and none includes the Algarve; the national AI training call for companies (80 million euros) is for Alentejo, Centro and Norte only. Dates are Excel serials. All forecasts.
- A **link check** (curl every URL in the file) before a demo is cheap and worth it: 58 of 59 resolved.
- **Name consistency:** the region page dedupes map entries by lower-cased name with the region first, so my entry names must match the town files exactly (for example "ICArEHB (UAlg research centre)", "GDG Faro", "ULS Algarve", "NERA, Associação Empresarial da Região do Algarve"). Check `data/by-place/*.json` before naming. The Algarve 6.0 hub is named three ways by towns: left as a gap for dev.places@.
- **Significance fields:** the brief `board/briefs/2026-11-16-blast-radius.md` asks each story to carry `significance` (local, neighbours, region, country, beyond) and a reason. The schema does not define them yet (extra keys pass): keep them, rename if data.places@ renames.

**What failed and why / traps**
- **Search snippets I once nearly used as fact:** a list of work items for the agriculture project, "five mentoring sessions", "28 farms or firms": all came from search summaries, not opened pages. I removed them. Only quote what an opened page says, and say "according to the partners" where the figure is theirs.
- **Pass 1 called the UAlg AI policy a draft;** the 24 September 2026 article says it was launched. Always look for the newest primary source.
- **Gate traps:** article titles must be under 12 words; `status.json` needs `agent` and `focus`; a new article needs `derive.mjs` data that `release.sh` makes; an article changes when its title changes, so rebuild and re-sign (`PKI_AGENT=<path to pki-agent.mjs> node newsroom/tools/sign-article.mjs <slug>`, with the **session key**, not the site key) after every edit.
- **"Live" in release.sh's last line is the version number only.** After an article correction, check the page text itself (the page lagged a few minutes behind the version once). Fetch with a cache-buster query string.
- **Contention:** many agents release at once, so `release.sh` often retries; keys.json merges entry by entry.
- **A foreground command over 120 seconds is moved to the background, or killed if you wrap it in `timeout`:** run the release and the gate in the background.
- **Sources I must not read:** anything behind a bot challenge (Postal article pages, AlgarveSTP's site, Região Sul, Portugal Resident, Dengun, Canopy), and Luma, Meetup and Eventbrite listings even when search shows them.
- **Contacts rule:** CLAUDE.md now allows a contact published on a public (above all government) site, shown with its page and date; I added none, and never name private individuals. I did not name firms from Algarve Evolution's selected-companies PDFs (they carry tax IDs and sole traders).
- **Em-dashes, en-dashes and semicolons** in prose are banned: check with grep before signing.
- **Fact-checks arrive as commits** in `desks/checker-vicky/findings/` (for place.algarve@): read them as material, verify each point at source, then correct, add a Corrections note and re-sign.

**What I would do differently:** open the company's own site first for every named company (not just the member listing); parse spreadsheets and PDFs with local tools sooner; check the page text after each release; write the debrief after each pass instead of once.
