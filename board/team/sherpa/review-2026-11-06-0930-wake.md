# Wake at 09:30 UTC

One reviewer's reading; not user research.

## 1. The summit's days, round 1: designer@'s proposals against my needs D1 to D7

The mock-ups live in the designer's vault; the newsroom page (/newsroom/design/summit-days-round-1/) describes them in words only, so a reader of the site cannot see them. I rendered them from `design/mockups/days/make.mjs`.

| Need | Met? | What I see |
|---|---|---|
| D1 one strip, eight days, on a phone | **yes** | Fri 6 to Fri 13 fit in 390 px; today filled ink; the summit tinted; "Opening", "Day 1" to "Day 3", "After" under the numbers |
| D2 a past day says it is past | **yes** | a pale amber banner: "This is PrompTugal.news as it was at the end of that day ... Today is Friday 6 November", "Back to today" |
| D3 a future day says it is planned | **yes** | a green banner: "What is on, as listed on Friday 6 November. Times and rooms can move; each item links to its source" |
| D4 each day short | **yes** | "Day 1: five sessions to start with", the rest collapsed |
| D5 the same strip in the same place | **yes** | under the bar on a phone, a block on the right on a desktop |
| D6 the release of that day | **yes, in principle** | Thursday 5 rebuilt from v0.1.37 with the clock frozen (I could not see the image) |
| D7 personalisation later | not in this round | as planned |

**New, ranked:**

| # | Finding | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| D8 | **On a phone the past day you are on is not on the strip.** The strip starts at Fri 6; on Thursday 5 nothing is ringed, so the reader loses where they are. The desktop has "‹ Earlier days"; the phone needs the same (or the strip scrolls to show the day, ringed). | designer@ |
| D9 | **The chips' second line ("Opening", "Day 1") is about 8 px on a phone**: too small to read in a queue. 11 px at least, or drop it on a phone and keep the tint. | designer@ |
| D10 | **"What changed since: 116 releases"** speaks the team's language; an attendee does not know what a release is. "The site has changed 116 times since" or "See what changed". | designer@ |
| D11 | The proposals are invisible from the site: the before-and-after page needs its images inline (or at least one per view) for Dinis and any reader to judge them without opening a vault. | designer@, dev@ |

**Verdict for Dinis:** the days proposals meet the reader's needs; with D8 and D9 fixed, they are ready to build.
