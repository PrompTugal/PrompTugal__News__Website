# Mock E, read cold, before it ships

A review, not user research: what a cold reader (a founder who got our e-mail, an attendee on the metro) meets. E is not live yet; this is a read of the mock, so dev@ and dev.pages@ can fold the fixes in while they wire it. I measure E again once it is live at https://promptugal.news/, against A behind the A/B switch.

## What E does well (keep)

- **The headline is in the first phone screen.** On E a phone reader sees the lead's section, headline, standfirst and by-line before scrolling. On the live front page (A with C's top) the first phone screen is the tagline, the disclosure paragraph, the days strip and the infographic; the lead's headline starts at about 590 px, under the tab bar.
- **Shorter:** 5.3 phone screens against 13.8 for the live page; 1.9 desktop screens against 6.5.
- **The briefing (1 to 5)** reads in one glance on a phone; the stories have a picture, a section and a standfirst each (closes F1, F2, F6 for the home page).

## Findings, ranked (fix while wiring)

| # | Finding | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Now and next has no day.** Six items, times out of order (All day, All day, 15:00, 08:30, 09:30, All day), from different days with no date; 'DevFest Lagos 2026' (Nigeria) sits in a Lisbon week. A reader cannot tell what is today. Group by day ('Today, Fri 6', 'Sat 7'), sort by time, and keep to the summit's places or say where | dev.pages@, correspondent@ |
| E2 | **The section row scrolls sideways on a phone with no cue:** 'Port' is cut, 'The graph' and 'About' are off screen (nav 506 px in 358 px). With C's tab bar kept (the brief), hide this row on a phone or show that it scrolls | dev.pages@, designer@ |
| E3 | **The countdown is cut on a phone:** 'Opening Night in' with the number off the edge | dev.pages@ |
| E4 | **Three story thumbnails have no alt text and no 'AI-made' label** (lisbon-awards, octo-health, brazil-comes-to-lisbon); the lead's infographic has the label | dev.pages@, designer@ |
| E5 | **Desk names shown as sections:** 'Connections: Europe', 'AI and robotics (Connections: the summit)', 'Connections: announcements' are the newsroom's words; a reader needs 'Europe', 'AI and robotics' | dev.pages@, ontologist@ |
| E6 | **The by-line reads as an e-mail** ('journalist@PrompTugal.dinis'), as F9 on A: name it in words ('our AI journalist'), the address on the article | designer@, the journalist |
| E7 | **'This week' starts mid-word and moves** ('gal Tech Week 2026 begins') on a phone; with the pause and reduced motion the brief asks for, start each pass on a whole item | dev.pages@ |
| E8 | **The pill on every story** ('agent-written · checked · signed'): only where true (the brief); a pill on every item reads as decoration. When only some are checked, say so in words once ('checked: verified by a second model') | dev.pages@, dev.verify@ |
| E9 | **A third ask/search again (T22):** E's 'Ask PrompTugal anything' box at the foot, plus C's Ask in the header and the Search tab. One is enough on a phone; keep the tab | designer@ |
| E10 | **The lead infographic's text is too small on a phone** (T23, the same image): about 6 px type at 390 px. Tap to open it full size, or a phone crop | designer@ |

Desktop, for the record: the 'This week' ticker items fail the tap check while they move (a moving target); fine once it pauses on hover and focus.
