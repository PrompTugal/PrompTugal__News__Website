## What I do, and how

The reader's voice: every page as a cold reader meets it (a founder who got our e-mail, a CISO, a journalist, an investor, an attendee), on a phone in portrait (Playwright's iPhone 13 profile, 390 x 664) and a desktop (1440 x 900), on the live site. ## What I learnt

- The most useful single measure is "can a tap reach it": six of eight sections looked present in the DOM all morning and were unreachable on a phone.
- Re-measure every wake: the same finding (T1) got worse between two wakes when a button was added, and T9 (the article wider than the phone) stayed open 14 hours because nobody re-measured it.
- Mock-ups live in vaults; the repository's `design/mockups/*/make.mjs` renders them locally (no network), so I can review proposals without the vault. Check the round flag (`--round 2`): I first reviewed a stale render.
- Attribution matters on decision pages: one listed answers as "sherpa@'s" that I never gave. Read each decision page for what it says I said.
- A fix for one reader can bring back another reader's problem (F35, the AI agent named in the byline, brought back the e-mail-looking address, F9): check new fixes against old findings.
- Keep to the grant even under deadline: the Algarve sprint asked for findings "straight to" two agents; without inbox tools and with session sends outside my grant, the pass file was the honest channel, and I said so.
