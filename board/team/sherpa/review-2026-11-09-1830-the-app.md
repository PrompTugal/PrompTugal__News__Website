# The news app on the web, read cold

A review, not user research. The persona: an attendee at 18:30 on Monday, Opening Night under way, who taps 'App'.

## What works (keep)

- **It opens on today** (Mon 9), with Programme and My picks one tap apart and a search ('a session, a company, a stage'); 4.9 phone screens for the day.
- **Picks:** 'Add' turns to a green 'Added'; My picks lists them by time, each with 'Calendar', and 'Add all my picks to my calendar'. Empty days say what to do ('No picks on this day yet. Add sessions from the programme.').
- **Privacy said plainly:** 'Your picks stay in this browser; this site never receives them. Times are Lisbon time and can change.'
- **On a desktop the pane works as briefed:** 420 px on the right, the site pushed left (not covered), 'Full screen' and 'Close'.

## Findings, ranked

| # | Finding | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| AP1 | **At 18:30 the app opens at the top of the day, not at now.** The first phone screen is 'All day: Portugal Tech Week', the 07:30 registration and 10:00; Opening Night (18:00, under way; the site's live bar says 'NOW 18:00 Opening Night') is screens below. On today, open scrolled to the current time, or put 'Now' and 'Next' first; past items folded | dev@ |
| AP2 | **The days row hides the summit's last days on a phone:** Fri 6 to Wed 11 show, Wed 11 cut, Thu 12 and Fri 13 off screen, and the chips have no 'Day 1 / 2 / 3' as the site's strip does. Start the row at today (as /days/ now does) and label the summit days | dev@ |
| AP3 | **'Full screen' on a phone** at /app/ does nothing a phone reader needs (the page is already the whole screen); there is no 'back to the site' but the wordmark. On a phone show 'Add to Home Screen' or the site's tab bar instead | dev@, designer@ |
| AP4 | The header line 'Web Summit Lisbon, 9 ...' is cut on a phone; 'Web Summit Lisbon' alone fits | dev@ |

Not tested: a clash between two picks (the two I added were back to back, 09:55 to 10:00 and 10:00 to 11:00), 'where I need to be next' (needs a pick still ahead today), Add to Home Screen and the venue map.
