# The top, A to D: my verdict

One reviewer's reading of four mock-ups, not user research. Until this note I had not assessed the four options: the lines headed "sherpa@'s six questions" on the decision page are designer@'s reading of my questions, not my answers, and I did not suggest a synthetic run on C (that suggestion is not in my reviews). Below is my own reading, for the page to quote or link.

## What a cold reader meets in each

- **A** reads instantly: name, search, credit, Menu; then the four sections people come for. It is the only option where the two things this site is for (the news and the companies) are both in view on a phone. "More" and "Menu" in the same top are two doors to the same room: keep one (the Menu), and let the row show four sections and nothing else.
- **B** shows the most and asks the least: every section is a visible chip, which is the answer to T1 in its purest form. The price is height: two rows of chips plus a dateline put the first story lowest of the four, on every page that uses it.
- **C** is the most app-like and gives the page the most room, but for this site it hides the wrong things: the founder who got our e-mail looks for **Companies**, and it is under More. A bottom bar also sits where Safari's own toolbar is on an iPhone, so the reader has two bars at the bottom. Not before Monday.
- **D** is A without A's best parts on a phone: no search in the top row and no row of sections, replaced by the days. The strip itself is good (my D1 to D6 are met), but in this mock-up its eighth day (Fri 13) is cut off at the right edge of a 390 px phone, so "all eight visible" is not yet true.

## My verdict

**A on every page, with the strip of days added under A's two rows (not instead of them) on the front page and the summit pages until 13 November.** That is designer@'s recommendation with one change: the strip must not take the place of the search and the sections, as it does in D's phone mock-up. Two details for the build: one door (Menu, not also More), and the eighth day must fit at 390 px (narrower chips, or "13" without its weekday). If Dinis prefers every section visible with no menu at all, B is the reader's second choice, at the cost of height.

## Meanwhile, live (v0.1.242): nothing of the top has changed since 06:30

On a phone 1.5 of 8 sections, no Newsroom link, "Powered by RiskMandate", the Vault, a bare "£5.00". The quick fixes Q1 to Q9 hold for every option (the decision page says so itself) and wait on nobody. Also still open, six hours on: **the article page lays out 591 px wide on a 390 px phone** (T9): the whole page, its top included, pans sideways.

## The reader's own area: its name (ideas ledger SR-08, "you, logged in, or maybe user")

Today the same thing is "You" on a desktop, a person icon ("Your profile") on a phone, and "Sign in" in the desktop dateline: three names for one door. For the reader: **one entry, top right after the credit, that says "Sign in" when signed out and "You" when signed in** (short, and this audience gets it). "Profile" suggests a public page about the reader, which is not what it is.
