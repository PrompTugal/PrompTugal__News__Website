# The scale phase, and account A's second focus: Gmail and CRM (2026-11-23T14:32Z)

Dinis, in a.maestro's session, about 14:35 UTC 23 November 2026: an architecture and strategy brief "so that you can also brief the other agents and see kind of where we are now and also capture this". In his words, condensed with care (quotes are his):

## The phase: scalability
- "We're now entering a phase of scalability". The question is how to manage:
  - the 5 accounts;
  - the 2 external ones (Mistral and ChatGPT);
  - the 7 more coming from the RiskMandate team;
  - the other team members who "will still want to be involved and contribute a bit", and more people who want to be involved.
- "Now it's a question of scale of decision making". "We are going to be selling fundamentally this decision making capability ... this ability for us to scale. So that's why the decisions are so important."
- Many of the decisions he makes are "not needed for me". The agents have "strong behavior policy, which is great, but we need a way to also make them work". "This is why these things are hard in the real world. Like we need to have a workflow, and I think we're starting to have."
- On the signed decisions now being added: "where he gets signed, he gets done properly, that's actually ridiculously powerful. And we should basically be enforcing that and learning from that and documenting that. And that's, I think, what we also will sell."

## The next stage: reach out
- "Reach out and go out to all the people that we met in the summit, and also I have tons of people to follow up". "Almost every website that we create here, there's somebody we should nudge ... somebody that we could contact, and that's why we really need to have this workflow, even from a CRM point of view, an email point of view".
- What was built before was "almost working". Now there is a way to work with:
  - dedicated websites;
  - Git and the vault together;
  - agents both in the browser and in sessions;
  - the team and the cells.

  "A lot of this stuff gets very complicated very fast, and we need to make sure that it's done correctly."

## Account A's focus: Gmail and CRM, with a whole agentic team
- "The first key focus here, and I want to have a whole agentic team focus on this, is the whole Gmail CRM stuff". "I have my inbox, I can follow up the emails, I can write emails, I can send messages, I can communicate with you via the site, I can coordinate these things. This is really our focus now".
- "The AWS is important, because that's going to give us some extra capabilities that we need. But the other big focus I want to have on this account is the whole Gmail CRM."
- **The tools:** "we call it platform, and then Google Gmail". "We need to have one for CRM too, and we need to have one that kind of integrates the whole lot", maybe "a risk mandate specific one".
- **Where things live:** public repositories hold the code; vaults and sessions like these are private.
- **The team:** "I want to get the rest of the team involved ... we need documentation pages".
- **The first steps:** "start with Gmail ... the ability to send emails ... a very, very simple UX to send emails and a way to process and manipulate the data that is there."
- **His ask now:** "give me a briefing of where we are and a page for that".
