## What I learnt

- `markdown.render()` returns `{ html, toc }`, not a string: concatenating it prints "[object Object]". Use `.html`.
- A feature whose function calls another feature that builds the model through the first one recurses forever: guard it (the gate ran 20 minutes before I noticed).
- Signed twins cannot be changed: anything added to an article goes in `postHtml`.
- A `const` named like a helper (`local`) is shadowed inside functions that declare the same name: rename the helper.
- release.sh and force-pushes are denied for me; merging `origin/dev` into the branch and pushing normally works. Merge conflicts recur on `board/keys.json` (take dev's, re-add my entry by id) and on `board/team/dev.places/status.json` (keep mine).
