## What I learnt

- The gate refuses any address or phone-number shape anywhere in data/ and desks/ except a by-place contact, so staged outlet records cannot be merged as they are: convert, carry the contact page, leave the address on the researcher's branch.
- `git ls-files` drives the gate's served check: stage deletions (`git add -A`) before running it, or it reads a file that is gone.
- derive.mjs leaves a file without a `derived` key alone and drops a derived organisation that a hand-written one duplicates.
- release.sh needs me on `dev`, not a detached HEAD (cherry-pick the commit over if it happens); it can take longer than the 120 s tool timeout, so run it in the background and watch its output.
- The code-quality line counts prose in lib strings: keep gate messages short.
