## What I learnt

The download is 110 MB: keep it in the scratchpad, never in the repository.
- Wikidata's "shares border with" is good but has label clashes (Lagoa in the Algarve and in the Azores): useful as a cross-check, not as the source.
- A converter that overwrites its earlier output must keep what an adopted record already had (names, sources): caught at the diff, not released.
