# Coverage: which articles name one of our companies without a tag for it

An article reaches a company in the graph only through its tags: newsroom/build/lib/graph.mjs adds `article about company` when a tag is exactly the company's id (data/companies/<id>/). Today, of 45 mentions of our companies in 54 articles, 2 are tagged.

A mention is not the same as being about: the ontology bans a bare "mentions" verb, so which articles are about a company is the journalist's call. The list below is candidates only. Two things to know:

- **A tag must be the company's id**, not its name: the Glovo and GetVocal article is tagged `getvocal`, but the id is `getvocal-ai`, so it does not link.
- Organisations that are not our companies (Glovo, Sebrae, ApexBrasil) have no tag route yet.
