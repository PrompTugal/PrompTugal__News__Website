## Pre-review: personalised versions of the investor site (investors@, at Dinis's word, 16:00)

The design: a pack (greeting, why you, route through the decks, slide wording, language, accent colour) sealed with AES-256-GCM under a random 256-bit key; the ciphertext in `board/team/investors/site/personas/<id>.json`; the key only in the link fragment `/investors/#pk=<id>.<key>` and its QR code; `persona.js` (dev@'s reviewed copy, never on keyring pages) opens it and keeps it in `sessionStorage` for the tab. Five fictional personas now; no real person's pack in git.

**Go, with these conditions:**

1. **No real person's pack in this repository, ever, even sealed.** The repository is public and permanent: a sealed pack names nothing today, but one leaked link or one weak key later opens it for good, and its file name, size and date are visible now. Real packs wait for the vault Dinis chooses (investors@ proposes the same). The fictional five are marked FICTIONAL on every page they change.
2. **The link is a bearer token.** Whoever has it, or a photo of its QR code, sees the pack. So a pack holds only what may be forwarded: nothing private, no figure that is not public or Dinis's, nothing said in a conversation (the comms protocol).
3. **The fragment leaves the address bar at once** (`history.replaceState`, as the sign-in does), so it is not in a screenshot, a shared URL or the history.
4. **Observability never records a fragment.** `obs.js` sends the path only today; keep it so, and `persona.js` sends nothing anywhere but the same-origin fetch of the pack.
5. **Strict parsing:** `<id>` matches `^[a-z0-9-]{1,40}$` and is fetched only from `/board/team/investors/site/personas/`; the key is exactly 43 base64url characters; the accent colour is `^#[0-9a-f]{6}$` or ignored; any failure shows the plain site.
6. **Text, never markup:** every field of the pack is set with `textContent` (or a DOM setter), never `innerHTML`; slide overrides replace text only.
7. **Crypto:** a fresh random 96-bit IV per pack; one key per pack, never reused; made with `crypto.getRandomValues` or Node's `crypto.randomBytes`; nothing derived from the persona's name.
8. **"Leave this view"** clears `sessionStorage` and reloads without the fragment; closing the tab ends it.
9. **Review:** `persona.js` follows the deck player's rule (S29): served only from dev@'s copy, the gate warns on a difference. security@ post-reviews it before the first real pack.
