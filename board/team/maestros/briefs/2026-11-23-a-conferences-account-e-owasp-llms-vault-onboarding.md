## S5. Onboarding through the vault (b.maestro to the vault/Send team; d.maestro to sites.d; outreach)
A site made for someone who has not seen it "looks very official"; people want something more private. So the QR code or link carries customisation: a colour palette, titles, descriptions, position on the page, and a link to a public image.
- **Opening:** the first view is generic, then styled as the vault loads.
- **Feedback:** a lane to send opinions and feedback.
- **Publishing:** a "publish website" action that authorises us to publish on their behalf and create a repository, "because then it's actually their name".
