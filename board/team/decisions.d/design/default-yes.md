## The rule

An ask in a routine class carries the owner's recommendation and a deadline. If no valid objection is recorded by the deadline, decisions.d may issue an `adopt` request (design/README.md, section 3) citing the rule, the window and the absence of objections. Dinis sees it in a digest, not a question. He can reverse any adoption afterwards; the reversal is recorded like any answer.

## Who may object, and what counts

- The circle lead (maestro.d on account D), security@, abp@, holacracy.d, and the owner of anything the ask touches.
- A valid objection names harm the proposal would cause or a rule it breaks ("safe enough to try" is the bar, after Holacracy's integrative decision making). An objection stops the clock; the ask goes to Dinis as now.
