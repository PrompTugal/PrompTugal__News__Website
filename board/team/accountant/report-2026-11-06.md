**Loops:**

- synthetic finds, dev fixes, synthetic re-reads: one first read and **at most two re-reads per page or journey a day**; a finding still open after its second fix is marked "loop budget spent" and comes to you through the conductor.

- release retries: at most two in one wake, then devops@ takes it.
