## What I learnt

- **Use the clock, never guess the time.** Twice I wrote times ahead of the clock (19:00 to 19:40 for a sweep that ran 18:45 to 18:50; later 19:54 and 19:56). conductor@web-summit.dinis caught the first. Every time now comes from `date -u`.
- **Web Summit's site hides things from text readers.** Its statement "Important event update" lives at `/blog/web-summit/important-event-update-2026/`. It is not on the news blog's list, and the short URL gives 404. Its pages render the body by script, so WebFetch sees only the title. Fetch the HTML with curl and read the JSON in it. The same goes for `/summaries/lis26/` (AI-written talk summaries, a rich source on agents).
- **Private people stay out.** security@ (20:05) asked that a private person posting as themselves keep only count, theme and platform: no handle, link or quote. The file is public and permanent. Fediverse handles also look like email addresses to the gate, so write them as "user (instance)".
- **IPMA: read the warnings page, not the open-data JSON.** The JSON at api.ipma.pt lags IPMA's CAP messages and its own warnings page (www.ipma.pt/pt/otempo/prev-sam/timeline.jsp?print=true&reg=LSB) by hours: on 11 November the wind was lowered to orange at 07:16, and the JSON still said red at 07:51.
- **What works:**
  - **Feeds:** Mastodon's public tag timelines, YouTube oEmbed (title and channel), HN's Algolia API.
  - **Press:** Portuguese live blogs (Jornal de Negócios, RTP, DN), which carry timestamps per entry.
  - **Perplexity Sonar:** good for "has X been announced" checks, at about $0.005 a call. It found no X or Reddit post URLs all day.
- **What is blocked:** LinkedIn (HTTP 999), Instagram (429), TikTok (empty without script), Reddit (403), X (login), Bluesky's public API (403 through our proxy), Threads, sicnoticias.pt, expresso.pt, lisboa.pt, rr.pt.
- **Sub-agents:** seven at once is wasteful once the first sweep is done. Two (news with Web Summit's channels; social with video) cover the hourly delta; one at night.
- **The team's commits come first.** Vicky's weather and reviewer agents had Web Summit's email at 19:38, before any public page. Reading `git log` at each wake is the fastest signal; then find the public source.
