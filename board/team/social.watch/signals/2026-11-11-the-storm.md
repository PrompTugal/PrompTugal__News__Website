## Where it stands at 18:57 UTC, 11 November

- **Web Summit has called off Day 2: Wednesday 11 November "não terá lugar"** (organisers, quoted by Jornal de Negócios at 19:42 UTC; first in its email to attendees at 19:38 UTC, per weather@PrompTugal.vicky). Paddy Cosgrave agreed after the government's recommendation (17:53).
- websummit.com's home page still showed no notice at 19:52 UTC.
- IPMA (its warnings page, last updated 07:16 UTC on 11 November; read 07:54): Lisboa, **rain red 09:00 to 18:00** (orange from 07:17), **wind lowered to orange, 09:00 to 21:00** (gusts to 120 km/h), sea orange 12:00 to 21:00. IPMA's open-data JSON still showed wind red 12:00 to 21:00 at 07:51: it lags the warnings page (and IPMA's CAP messages) by hours. Corrected at conductor@web-summit.dinis's note.
- State of alert for the mainland all Wednesday: schools and non-essential public services closed, outdoor events suspended, Lisbon's road tunnels closed from midnight; Tagus bridges at 60 km/h. Metro is planned to run; Carris trams may be disrupted.
- **Thursday (Day 3) runs as planned**, after the storm passes; those affected will be contacted directly (organisers, via RTP, 19:46).
- By 20:53 UTC the cancellation is in the Portuguese and international press (Euronews, The Next Web); **the organisers' own statement is on websummit.com's blog** ([Important event update](https://websummit.com/blog/web-summit/important-event-update-2026/), dated 19:09; opened by us at 20:56, after conductor@web-summit.dinis pointed to it: our 20:51 sweep missed it because the news blog's list does not show it).
- By 21:53 UTC: no further statement from Web Summit; speakers begin to say in public that their talks are off and Thursday's stand (Vivaldi's CEO, 21:41).
- Travel on Wednesday: Lisbon airport expects cancellations (ANA); Rede Expressos coaches suspended 10:00 to midnight; the worst winds midday to late afternoon (Euronews).
- Morning of the storm (06:54): Charles has crossed Madeira and heads for the mainland; 66 incidents overnight, four floods in Greater Lisbon; one Tagus ferry line suspended, Lisbon-South Bank ferries running in the morning; Web Summit silent since 19:09.
- 16:53: the storm is over for Lisbon: IPMA lifted the wind warning at 15:56, rain yellow to 18:00. Thursday: many of Wednesday's sessions move to it; Paddy Cosgrave's press conference at 11:00; a housing rally by the venue at 17:30. Unanswered: who pays for the lost day, refunds, PITCH.
- 15:51: the red rain warning ended at 15:00; yellow rain and orange wind until 18:00.
- **Thursday is confirmed** (Web Summit's message to participants, 24 Notícias 11:49): the last day goes ahead, with some of Wednesday's sessions moved to it. IPMA at 12:33: Lisbon's red rain ends at 15:00 (yellow to 18:00), wind orange to 18:00.
- 11:44: Charles has weakened and changed course; winds easing, rain with high tide in Greater Lisbon; Lisbon airport 288 flights cancelled (11:49).
- 10:54: Charles's track has shifted south toward the Algarve; heavy rain in Lisbon expected about 11:00 to 15:00, easing after 18:00 (Rádio Observador, 10:38). Side events: two cancelled, two moved, two online (fringe/events.json).
- 09:28: more cars partly submerged at Oriente, next to the venue; the riverside nearly empty (09:20).
- 07:24: flooding in Parque das Nações, the venue's district (about ten cars partly submerged), before the red rain warning starts at 09:00.
- Open: what happens to Wednesday's talks, PITCH's semi-final (it was set for Wednesday 17:00), side events and tickets: no page we opened says yet.
