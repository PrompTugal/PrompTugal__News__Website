## 1. The storm passes; Thursday is on

- **The weather:** Charles weakened and turned south. Lisbon's red rain ended at 15:00, and IPMA lifted the wind warning at 15:56. Rain is yellow until 18:00.
- **The damage:** trees down in Algés, flooding in Alfama, cars under water near Oriente station ([Correio da Manhã](https://www.cmjornal.pt/sociedade/detalhe/chuva-vento-forte-e-agitacao-maritima-mau-tempo-deixa-pais)). Lisbon airport cancelled 288 flights.
- **Thursday:** Web Summit's message to participants calls it "a quinta-feira mais movimentada de sempre da Web Summit". Many of Wednesday's sessions move to it, and the schedule is updated through the day ([24 Notícias](https://24noticias.sapo.pt/atualidade/artigos/web-summit-confirma-regresso-na-quinta-feira-apos-cancelamento-devido-ao-mau-tempo), [PME Magazine](https://pmemagazine.sapo.pt/web-summit-regressa-esta-quinta-feira-com-programa-reforcado/)). **Paddy Cosgrave holds a press conference on Thursday at 11:00** ([Marketeer, with Lusa](https://marketeer.sapo.pt/web-summit-promete-o-dia-mais-intenso-de-sempre-apos-cancelar-evento-devid)). The Next Web's headline now reads "returns Thursday with rescheduled sessions".

## 2. "Cancelled for nothing?" and "who pays?"

- **The mood turns:** in the afternoon, private posts say the storm passed Lisbon with little damage and the sun came out, and call the cancellation needless (count and theme only).
- **The costs:** PME Magazine asked who bears the lost day's costs, and Lusa asked about the city council's payments. No answer is reported, and no refund policy.
- **Thursday at the venue:** the housing movement Acampada de Lisboa holds a rally on Thursday at 17:30 on Alameda dos Oceanos, by the venue ([Rádio Comercial, Lusa](https://radiocomercial.pt/noticia/acampada-de-lisboa-marca-concentracao-para-quinta-feira-frente-a-w)).
