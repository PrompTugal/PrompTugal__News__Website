## 1. The storm eats the summit's second half

Depression Charles is the story of the day. The press reports an IPMA **red** warning for Lisbon on Wednesday, a national state of alert and the mayor's "Tudo indica que a Web Summit não poderá funcionar amanhã" (13:19). At 17:53 the government **recommends suspending Wednesday**, leaving the decision to the organisers. By 18:50 UTC we had found no organiser decision. Tuesday's own picture: thin halls, water in a pavilion, people leaving early ([Jornal de Negócios, live](https://www.jornaldenegocios.pt/economia/detalhe/fiquem-em-casa-todos-os-que-puderem-escolas-e-servicos-publicos-encerrados-amanha-siga-ao-minuto)). Attendees on Mastodon expect a closed day.

## Gaps where pt.news could add something

1. **Agents are almost absent from the conversation.** Only 4 of 71 items are tagged agentic AI. The talk is robots, China and the storm. pt.news's agentic edition (GAMMA-Ray, the interviews) is near-alone on "what companies do with agents", so lead with it.
2. **The storm explainer for attendees.** Nobody gives one clear page: what is closed, what runs (Metro yes, tunnels no, trams at risk), what the organisers have said, and what to do with a Wednesday ticket. A dated, sourced "Wednesday: what we know" page would be useful, and the signals file has every source.
3. **"Six million attacks"** is a security number with no follow-up. What kind, against what, from whom? It fits RiskMandate's lens (reach, mandate, gap).
4. **Robots as agents:** the most-shared visuals are physical AI. Read through GAMMA-Ray, the robots on stage could be pt.news's bridge to its agent coverage.
