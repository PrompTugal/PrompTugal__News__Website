## What we offer that others do not (to be tested by landscape.d's map of the tool landscape)

The brief names candidates that already exist (an open-source text-to-map editor, a Wardley diagram in Mermaid, whiteboard templates); none of them is verified yet, and landscape.d's tool landscape is the check. Where we can differ, from what we already run:
1. **History: a map is a sequence of dated plays,** each with its source, played as an animation with narration. A static map shows a position; ours shows the movement, and movement is the point of a Wardley map.
2. **Maps from sourced data, made by agents.** Our maps are generated from landscapes and needs files in which every fact carries a URL and a date read (landscape.d's 145 facts; sherpa.d's needs). A map whose every node can be checked is rare, and it is our provenance rule applied to strategy.
3. **Kept current.** Agents re-read the sources and re-draw the map at each run, with a dated change log. A map that ages is the usual failure.
4. **Open by default:** one plain format (`carto-map/1`), files in a repository, import and export, forkable. It is the Sovereign Website promise applied to maps.
5. **Frameworks that connect:** Cynefin, Holacracy, Scrum, SWOT and RACI explained and linked to each other where their licences allow (rights@ first). Our own Holacracy analysis (maestro.d, holacracy.d) is a ready example.

## The map in short (Map 5, data once cartographer.d draws it)

- **Commodity or product:** drawing a map (diagram tools and text formats exist), SVG, hosting, the explanation of the basics (books and talks exist, licences to check).
- **Custom:** a good, tested explanation for a cold reader; a consolidated gallery.
- **Genesis:** maps with history and narration; maps generated from sourced data and kept current by agents; frameworks linked to each other; maps as the context of a decision (Map 1).

Play: use the commodity parts (Mermaid's Wardley diagram where it fits, any open format we can import), build only the genesis parts, and keep everything open so the community can fork and contribute. Do not build another blank-canvas editor first; the research and the explanation come before our own editor (the brief's order agrees).

## Why it matters to the estate

- **It is a topic site for exactly our visitors.** By Dinis's account the Wardley community and our target customers almost coincide. It is the B to C to B route at its purest: they come for maps, they see maps made and kept by a governed agent team, they try the policies, and they meet the decision-making framework (Map 1) and Sovereign Websites (Map 4).
- **It dogfoods the strategy circle.** Every map of ours (decision-making, the estate, Sovereign Website, the platform sites) is an example on the site, with its history and sources. The site is our working, not a brochure.
- **It is the shop window for the decision-making framework.** A map is the context a decision needs; the site shows maps, and the framework sells the decisions made from them.
