# One set of principles and one way to report, for every site; an encrypted drop and a monitored inbox

> Dinis, in conductor.b@'s session, 18 November 2026, about 15:00 UTC, answering conductor.b@'s questions (one shared principles and report process for all sites; the private reporting channel). His earlier words, in events.b@'s session at about 14:45 UTC: "very strong guidelines and principles ... across the board ... we aim to be accurate, we aim not to represent anything else ... mistakes might happen, but we'll try to correct them as soon as possible. And we should also have a process for people to report stuff" (PrompTugal__News__Events board/guidance/2026-11-18-sources-principles-and-reports.md).

> "On your questions before, yes. Create the encrypted drop. That's something that goes to you as a vault, right? Or somebody, that observability, correct? And you can also put the agent at riskmandate.ai as the inbox, because that's been monitored."

## What follows

1. **One `/principles/` and one `/report/` on promptugal.news**, from events.b@'s draft (events.promptugal.news/principles/ and /report/): the aims (accurate; never presented as other than it is; provenance and licences shown; mistakes corrected in the open; we listen and change; public and professional only), the rules of engagement with sources, and the report process (take down first, look, answer, change, record) with a corrections log. Every site (main, data, weather, civic, startups, travel, events, signals) links both from its footer.
2. **Three ways to report:** a public GitHub issue on the site's repository; **an encrypted drop** (a form that encrypts in the reader's browser and appends to a lane on the agents' inbox system, readable only by conductor.b@ and Dinis, drained into a vault like the observability lanes); **the inbox the agent inbox at riskmandate.ai (address on /report/ once published)**, published at Dinis's word as a monitored address.
3. conductor.b@ drains the drop at each wake and routes each report to the agent that owns the fix; every report and its outcome is recorded (without the reporter's details) in the corrections log.
