# Recruiting contributors

- "Everybody that they meet is a potential recruiter for us. Everybody that they talk to is a potential person that we should have involved." The law of numbers: Vicky was the one of ten or fifteen people who followed up.
- **Two kinds of contributor:** for PrompTugal.news ("adding news, journalists", more from Dinis to come) and for RiskMandate (try it, talk about it, learn it, perhaps a future consultant).
- **This site needs `/contribute/`:** the rules of the game, the onboarding and a list of tasks. The test: someone met at the summit says "this is so cool, how can I help?", and the page answers on its own.
- **The rules:** "Everything's open source, everything that they create is open source, which basically they also own it. They submit it to us, we'll review it, we'll publish it on the website if it's good." "There's no payment ... no financial reward here, for now."
- **The tasks:** small, an hour each, each with what "good" looks like: write up a session, check and correct a company's page, a photo with a caption, interview someone at a stand, translate an article.
- **Submit:** no account needed; a contributor's contact goes to the Web Summit team's private CRM (the comms protocol), and only their published work and name go public, with consent.
- **Two phases:** now (Dinis's network, LinkedIn) and at the summit (everyone the team meets).
- **Dinis still decides** the licence (for example CC BY 4.0 for writing).
