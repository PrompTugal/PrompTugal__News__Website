| Decision | Answer |
|---|---|

## The standing rule

The owner's recommendation is the default: push it, try it live, and where the team is unsure, A/B test it on the site (observability@ measures, synthetic@ reads both). Dinis is asked only where a decision is his alone (CLAUDE.md, Decisions are pages).
