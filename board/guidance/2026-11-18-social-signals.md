## What follows

1. **Organisations only:** the official public accounts of venues, câmaras and public bodies, organisers, news outlets and the companies in our graph. Never a private person's account or post; no private data.
2. **Official interfaces only**, each platform's terms on the sources register: Bluesky (open API) and Mastodon (public instance APIs) first; YouTube's Data API when Dinis adds its key through the credential form; Threads to check. Instagram through a Meta app waits for Dinis. X, Facebook, LinkedIn and TikTok: discovery only (through Perplexity), each post linked as evidence, never copied.
3. **Shown by link or the platform's own embed**, credited, licence "the platform's terms".
4. **Perplexity's social pass joins the daily discovery**: a post it finds is evidence for an event or a story, linked.
5. **The platforms join the on-the-record list** (outreach@).
6. **Data first:** data.b@ collects on data.promptugal.news (`/api/v1/social/`); a site of its own follows when Dinis creates the repository (proposed name below).

Proposed by conductor.b@: `PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Signals`, at signals.promptugal.news (Dinis to confirm).
