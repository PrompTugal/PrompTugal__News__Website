# Cameras as links, and the map: Portugal big, London and the EU small

At Dinis's word, 19 November 2026, about 17:40 UTC, in conductor.b@'s session. It answers live.b@'s camera questions and adds London and the EU to every site's map.

## The cameras

- **MEO BeachCam:** the 21 Algarve cameras removed from the register on 18 November come back, as **credited links only**. MEO's terms (section 1) need MEO's prior, express authorisation before any third-party site retransmits its streams, so none is embedded. Each entry has the name, the place, MEO's camera page and the credit "MEO BeachCam"; coordinates are approximate, from the beach name, since beachcam.meo.pt refuses our requests and is not fetched again.
- **SkylineWebcams** (Faro, Portimão marina): **links for now**. Its terms allow its own Embed code, unmodified, but bar commercial and journalistic use without VisioRay's written authorisation, so we link and credit and do not embed. No camera page is read by code.
- **Where:** videos, live and weather, all three. This widens the 18 November line that videos keeps to recorded video: the cameras appear on videos as links.
- Nothing is re-hosted, recorded or analysed.

Dinis's reason for links now: the London work already shows what we want to do.

## The map: Portugal big, London and the EU small

Every PrompTugal site gets **London**, as the travel and data sites already have it. The map every site uses shows:

- **Portugal**, big, the main subject;
- **London**, small, as an inset or a marker beside it;
- **the EU**, small, represented by **Brussels**, wherever we have EU data (ENISA, the EU registers, Eurostat and others already read).

The places come from ontologist@'s data/site/places-registry.json (London and the UK are in it; Brussels and the EU are added if missing). designer@ keeps the look and the one shared map; each site lead adds London (and the EU where its data allows) to its own pages and map.
