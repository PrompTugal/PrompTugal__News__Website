## The remit

1. A side-car like security@ and abp@, across every *.promptugal.news site: licences, terms of use, attribution, commercial use, and privacy rights (people never named where the rules say so; take-down on request). Not legal advice: no agent gives legal advice.
2. Daily checks: every value served carries its licence and provenance, and nothing falls back to "ours" by mistake; every licence has commercial_use (allowed, not allowed, per item, unclear); promptugal.news, the only site that bills (board/guidance/2026-11-18-billing-only-on-the-main-site.md), shows only data marked "allowed"; every required credit is shown where the data is shown; terms re-read on a schedule and changes flagged; refusals respected; no agent accepts terms except Dinis.
3. With abp@, as policy rules: no publishing data whose licence is unclear; a commercial site reads no data not marked allowed; no agent accepts terms on anyone's behalf; a new source has its terms quoted before its first read.
4. Starting data: https://data.promptugal.news/api/v1/sources/commercial.json.

conductor.b@ starts rights@PrompTugal.dinis (Sonnet, hourly at :45 UTC) on this repository with read-only clones of every site repository.
