# Positioning, collaborators, local media, paid

## What Dinis asked (as relayed, his words where it matters)

1. **Positioning.** The app is still "PrompTugal: Lisbon Summit" on TestFlight, with a summit description. Rename the app's listing and the site's positioning around **Portugal and local, regional news made by an agentic newsroom**, and say what makes it unique. Dinis asked for the "ambassador agent" on it; this team has none by that name, so **sherpa@** (the reader's voice) leads, with **designer@** and **correspondent@**, and **synthetic@** tests it on personas.
   - The draft to beat (from conductor.a.ios@): name **PrompTugal.news** (the brand rule: always PrompTugal.news); subtitle "Local news, town by town, every fact sourced"; unique: one agent per town researching in the open, every fact linked to its source and the date read, you can watch the agents work, anyone can correct a fact, and it sends readers, and money, to local media.
   - Deliver: one name, one subtitle (30 characters at most for the App Store), a two-line description, and the site's own tagline to match. The brand and the name are Dinis's: put the proposal on a "For Dinis" decision page (`/newsroom/decisions/`) and send its link to conductor.a.ios@promptugal.dinis, who sets App Store Connect once Dinis approves.
2. **A call for collaborators, big, on the site and in the app** (the site's `/contribute/` page exists from 9 November): journalists, local media, local bloggers and editors, anyone making local content. Lead with places: "write for your town".
3. **Local media as a source and a partner.** For every town and region, list and link every local news organisation, blogger and editor (public sources only), and record which offer data we can read automatically (RSS, an API). The place agents add them to `data/by-place/<place>.json` and the town pages link them prominently, so readers go to them. Outreach to each (offer it an agentic team of its own, reading and amplifying its work, and in time sending revenue its way) is **drafted by outreach@ and sent by a person**, never by an agent.
4. **Paid from day one, site and app alike** (as VoiceDebrief and SGit do): people pay for usage. **`api/v1/billing/meter.json` is the one source of prices** for the site and the app; nothing else states a price. The local-sources page's wallet (what each source would earn) reads the same meter. accountant@ keeps it.

sherpa@, designer@, correspondent@ and synthetic@ (1); dev.pages@, designer.places@ and the place agents (2 and 3); outreach@ (3, drafts only); accountant@ and dev@ (4); conductor.a.ios@promptugal.dinis for the app side.
