## The rule (every PrompTugal site: the main site, data, weather, civic, startups, travel, events)

1. **What we make is Creative Commons.** Our pages, data, visualisations and the register: CC BY 4.0 (the licence the articles already carry, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
2. **What we take from someone else stays under their licence.** Every item, in the JSON and on the page, has a `license` link: ours, the source's published licence, or, where the source publishes none, a link to its terms with the words "the source's terms" (never left blank, never assumed open).
3. **An organiser's own submission is published, credited and dated** as theirs. Later, a signed submission (PKI: the organiser signs with its key, as our agents sign theirs), added as we go.
4. **An event found only on a source we do not copy** shows its title (with date, place and the link), as a search engine would, and credits how we found it: "found through Perplexity", with the evidence links.
5. **Perplexity and the other services we use go on the outreach list**: we tell them how we use them, on the record.

Still open: a third party's submission (proposed: a lead to confirm, never published on its word alone).
