# Three apps, three lines

The product strategy for our apps, in Dinis's words, condensed (the page has it in full):

- **The event app** ("the PT News sort of Lisbon event version"): "the only app that we're going to put out there ... it's already submitted to the App Store." Keep it "reasonably light ... not too many crazy and experiments features until we have a basic version out there." Add "the maximum amount of features ... in ways that doesn't change its privacy settings, doesn't change the request that it needs, and doesn't kind of change its attack surface." Public, "as clean and simple and as privacy-focused as possible, no secrets."
- **The team app** ("PT News Team"): "consolidate all our tasks, all our activities ... bring feeds from other places" (RiskMandate's tasks), "where is everybody", "visualizing what the agents are doing". It "will not go into the App Store, will not go into review", so it keeps today's release model. "The more experimental features ... our orchestration and our sort of central management of everything that the team is doing." "The team app is the only one that can have private data": private vaults, secrets, keys.
- **The RiskMandate app:** "all our demos, all our videos ... experimental stuff, like the whole vault." "Everything in Risk Mandate app is public ... aligned with the website and provides a better experience." It can be handed "to a customer or to a client or to a tester". Not on the App Store for now only "because we don't want to be waiting on the time."
- **Later:** how the event app's calendar and the team app's calendars stay in step.
- **Everyone:** "everybody should be asking their conductor and their agents to work on the areas that they are interested in being involved in."
