## How to read this

**Columns.** `#` is the item's id (theme prefix and number), used by the Later and Not captured lists. `Memo` is the memo number and its UTC time in the conductor's session (`M13 03:39`), or `SU` and the minute into the 18:00 stand-up of 5 November (`SU 00:16:19`). `Dinis's words` is a short exact quote (" ... " marks a cut). `The idea` is one plain sentence. `When` is one of the statuses below. `Captured in` is the file on the board that already holds the idea, or **NOT CAPTURED**. `Owner` is the agent (or person) who carries it.

**When.**
- **now**: asked for the night of 5 to 6 November or this morning; work on the board.
- **now (done)**: asked and already delivered (the evidence is in `Captured in`).
- **today**: for Friday 6 November.
- **tomorrow**: for Saturday 7 November (the Algarve Evolution meeting).
- **later**: framed by Dinis as later, next, "for now ... later", after the summit, or plainly a longer arc. All of these are also in the Later list.
- **standing rule**: how things are always done from now on.
- **decided**: a decision Dinis made; it settles a question.
- **question**: Dinis asked something; see whether it was answered in the last section.

**Path shorthands in `Captured in`.** `g/stand-up` = `board/guidance/2026-11-05-1800-the-stand-up.md`; `g/design` = `board/guidance/2026-11-06-design-and-passes.md`; `g/front` = `board/guidance/2026-11-06-front-page-timeline-publication.md`; `g/clickable` = `board/guidance/2026-11-06-clickable-updates-sections-views.md`; `g/brazil` = `board/guidance/2026-11-06-the-brazil-story-and-story-projects.md`; `g/companies` = `board/guidance/2026-11-06-companies-graphs-media-backups.md` (section numbers as `§n`); `g/budgets` = `board/guidance/2026-11-06-budgets-capture-not-block.md`; `p/<name>` = `board/passes/2026-11-06-<name>.md`. Every other path is in full.
