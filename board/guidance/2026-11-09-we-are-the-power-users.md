# We are the power users

- **Names:** "the team app" is the private PrompTugal Team app; "the news app" is the public one on the App Store.
- **Why:** "We already have a problem of management ... We don't know where everybody is ... We are a team of 6. Imagine teams of 10, 20, 50, 100 ... if we make it work for ourselves, it will work for others, and that's kind of what we're going to sell."
- **Where to experiment:** the iOS team app, the iOS RiskMandate app and the Android RiskMandate app, "as much as possible". The news app stays light.
- **First feature, "where is everybody":** a live, collaborative map, where people have been and where they are going, who is seeing what; "kind of like WhatsApp or Snapchat"; visualisations, animations, infographics, maps. It starts from the team site's WS-181.
- **A team is "a human plus a conductor plus a bunch of agents"**, with its own lane; each person gets their own customised version of the app: feature toggles, change management, many variants live, "a Darwinian evolution", votes.
- **"Make a decision, coordinate here, map that out: those are fundamental core primitives."**
- **Everyone asks for features:** "everybody from the team needs to experience the power that I have here, which is the ability to just come up with an idea, come up with a flow, and then have that been executed by the team."
