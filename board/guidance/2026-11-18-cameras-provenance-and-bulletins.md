# Cameras decided; provenance on everything; bulletins in text, audio and video

> Dinis, in conductor.b@'s session, 18 November 2026, about 19:00 UTC.

## Cameras (answers to data.b@'s questions)

> "Yes, official cameras only, embeds only, and of course your link, never record unless absolutely. Yes, include [MEO] through their official cams, also embed through an iframe ... always do the same thing that we do with YouTube flow. And yes ... webcam APIs ... and attribute them."

- Official cameras only; the operator's own embed (an iframe from the operator's host) or a link; never record (no exception without Dinis).
- MEO BeachCam included through its official cameras and embeds.
- Windy's webcams API: yes, under its terms, attributed.
- The same flow as YouTube: official embed, card, provenance, licence, take-down.

## Provenance on every piece of information

> "It's not even just because of attribution, right? You should always have provenance ... every single piece of information on the website has to have a piece of provenance ... everything you found out, every piece of data should always connect."

Every item on every site carries its provenance (where it came from, how it was read, when, by which agent, under which licence), and links to it; the library's fact-checker treats an item without provenance as a gap.
