# The front page, the summit's days, and becoming a publication

## 1. The front page

- "This looks like a mess ... a lot of stuff happening here ... it looked like it's just been glued together ... we need to do a very good couple design passes ... multiple rounds of design, multiple rounds of comments ... our synthetic users should have an opinion here."
- "We really want this to feel very modern and very sort of slick and efficient."
- The two boxes beside the masthead ("Every fact sourced", "Independent"): keep what they say, but "we need a much better layout".
- The mandate breaker ("Our mandate: to map the universe of AI in Portugal ... Start with the companies, Portugal or the graph") repeats links the navigation already has; "we need to think about the bits".
- "The homepage of this has to be very visual, has to have lots of images, has to have lots of pictures. It's like a news organization."
- "Every article that we have here has to have an infographic ... an image ... a visual representation." "Every one of these little stories should have a mini article, a mini thing, or maybe an infographic."
- He likes Now and Next; "we need to think about how we're going to present that".
- Images made with AI: "we can give the designer an OpenRouter key ... create some infographics ... funny cartoons" where an AI image fits.

## 2. The summit's days, in time (from the PHP newsroom's idea of timing)

- "A block on the right that has Web Summit, and then it has the days": Friday 6 to Friday 13 November, say: the pre-summit days, the summit days (9 to 12), one day after. "A natural cadence of things leading to the summit, and then the summit days."
- Highlight which day we are in. "The ones before are the past, so they will show what the website looked like at that moment in time", with what you should know then (logistics, when registration happens).
- "If I click on the date that hasn't happened, that almost has the news that will happen in the future ... the talks, the things ... the creation of the homepage as that day will occur."
- "A newsroom that you can see over time ... go back in time and forward."
- Later, personalised: "once we have the ability to capture all the pages that the user has seen ... the homepage starts to be personalized to the user ... the things he has read, things that are not relevant".
- "Right now we're very focused on the summit ... that's why it's the summit edition": the summit section carries it.
- "Really explore this, because I think this is a really powerful concept."

## 3. Becoming an official publication

- "From a management point of view, and from a publication point of view ... go and read newsroom.sgit.ai ... the work we've done there to make this an official publication ... from policies to contacts to references to the comment, the right to remove stuff ... everything that we expect a publication to have ... we need to do here on steroids."
- "We need to figure out how to register this company as an official publication ... as official news", because "we want to go to the Web Summit and ask for a press pass", and to press conferences there.
- "We need to start mapping these materials here."
