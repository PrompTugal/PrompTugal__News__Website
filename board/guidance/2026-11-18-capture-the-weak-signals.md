> Dinis, in conductor.b@'s session, 18 November 2026, about 15:40 UTC: "Yes to start capturing all the data sources you mentioned above." (conductor.b@'s lists of 15:30 and 15:35 UTC: the weak signals and the cyber signals.)

## What data.b@ captures (on data.promptugal.news; each source on the register with its terms checked first, then collected through its supported interface, with source, licence link and time read on every item)

- **Writing in public:** blogs, newsletters (Substack and others) and podcasts by their RSS or Atom feeds, Portugal first; people who publish under their own name included (2026-11-18-people-blogs-cyber-and-simulated-payments.md).
- **Code:** GitHub's official API: public organisations, repositories and releases of Portuguese startups, companies, public bodies and projects in the graph.
- **Company and legal records:** Portal da Justiça's public company acts, the Diário da República, base.gov.pt public contracts (civic already reads these; one collector, shared).
- **Funding:** CORDIS (EU projects with Portuguese participants), the PRR transparency portal (Recuperar Portugal), Portugal Ventures' and accelerators' published portfolios.
- **Patents and trademarks:** INPI, the European Patent Office's open data, EUIPO's open data.
- **Research:** universities' news feeds, RCAAP (the national open-access repository), arXiv by Portuguese affiliation.
- **Wikipedia and Wikidata:** changes to entries about the companies, places and people in the graph (their APIs).
- **Hiring:** companies' own careers pages, only where their terms and robots allow; never LinkedIn.
- **Conferences:** calls for papers, speakers and programmes published by organisers (not by Luma, Eventbrite or Meetup).
- **Cyber signals:** CNCS and CERT.PT, CNPD decisions, ENISA and the EUVD, NVD and CVE.org, CISA's known-exploited list, Portuguese vendors' CVEs and disclosure programmes, Shadowserver's country dashboards, Cloudflare Radar, RIPEstat, IODA, abuse.ch and phishing feeds by country; aggregates only, with the published limits (no IPs or hosts, no victim data, anything specific reported privately to CERT.PT or CNCS, no scanning of our own).

Not yet (each needs Dinis): a YouTube API key; a Meta app for Threads and Instagram; a Shodan or Censys key.

**Who reads it:** signals.b@ (voices, mentions), startups.b@ (code, funding, patents, hiring), civic.b@ (records, contracts, funding), library.b@ (the graph), events.b@ (conferences), the cyber site when it exists. Each collector gets a threat model and an AppSec review; security@ reviews the cyber limits before the cyber collectors run.
