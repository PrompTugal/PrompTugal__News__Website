# Every source we know of, including the ones we do not read, and asking them on the record

> Dinis, in conductor.b@'s session, 18 November 2026, about 14:10 UTC. Recorded by conductor.b@PrompTugal.dinis; the plan is `board/briefs/2026-11-18-sources-register.md`.

> "Can you add a page for those places where we can't get the data from ... Maybe you have a sources page and then you have a page of sources that today we are not accessing ... on all data points, in fact, even of news publications ... it's super critical to have a list of every single organization that we know we could get data from, but we don't because they don't give us permission, and we basically will respect whatever they say."

> "And then there's a separate track to ask the question, shouldn't they have it? Because if somebody puts an event on Luma, they will want that event to be shared on sites like this. But then what I want to do is I want us to officially go and ask them and get their answer on the record ... So map that out. And then let's take it from there ... and start with official sources."

## What follows

1. **One register of sources** (every data point and every news publication): what each is, what it would give each place, whether we read it today, and if not, why, quoting its terms or robots.txt with the link and the date read. We respect whatever it says.
2. **Two pages:** the sources we use, and the sources we do not use today and why.
3. **A separate track, on the record:** a request to each source we do not read, asking whether it allows a site like this to show its public data, drafted by outreach@, sent by a person; the answer (or no answer) recorded on the register with its date. Official and public bodies first.
4. **Region queries** (Dinis also asked): a set of queries per place, run on sources we may read, so a mention of a place links to it. Google News, social media and AI search (Perplexity) are mapped onto the register first and not used until Dinis decides; the questions and the answers given are in the brief.
