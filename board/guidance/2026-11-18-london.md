# London: the first place beyond Portugal, and Dinis checking the weather on the ground

> Dinis, in conductor.b@'s session, 18 November 2026, about 22:20 UTC: "how hard would it be to also add London to the cities we collect data from (which is where I live and am now), this will also be a good way to show the international and scalability level of our platform." Then: "I can also check for things like the weather. Since I'm literally here and can see how accurate it is and can even use it every day."

## What follows

1. **London is a place**, the first outside Portugal, shown as "Beyond Portugal: London" (conductor.b@'s proposal; the label and where it sits are Dinis's, as a brand question, and he can change them).
2. **ontologist@:** a country layer in the graph (place, region, country), so no site assumes a place is Portuguese.
3. **data.b@: the UK connectors, each on the register with its terms first** (most under the Open Government Licence, attributed): Open-Meteo and the Environment Agency's flood monitoring (no key); TfL's Unified API ("Powered by TfL Open Data"; works without a key, rate-limited); adsb.lol around the London airports; TfL JamCams (official traffic cameras: the operator's images linked or embedded, never stored); the London Datastore; Contracts Finder; data.police.uk as area totals only; the London Air Quality Network and DEFRA UK-AIR; NCSC advisories; London councils' and venues' own agendas; London outlets' and bloggers' RSS feeds. Needing a free registration from Dinis, through the secure form, when he chooses: the Met Office DataHub, National Rail's Darwin, Companies House.
4. **The sites add London as data lands:** weather, travel, live, civic, events, signals, cyber, library, bulletins (a London page) and the main site.
5. **Forecast against what happened:** weather.b@ keeps every forecast it shows and compares it with what was then observed (the pipeline standard's history makes it possible), per place, starting with London and Faro: a forecast-accuracy page.
6. **Dinis's own observations count:** a simple way for him (and later any reader) to say what the weather actually was where they are (a short form or a GitHub issue template), shown as "observed by a reader on the ground", dated and placed to the area, never a home address; his captures count as a source, as since 11 November.
