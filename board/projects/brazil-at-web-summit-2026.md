## What round 1 found (the universe, in brief)

- **No public list names the delegation.** The one official list is ApexBrasil's results page: 188 tax numbers (CNPJ), no names, status frozen at 28 August 2026, a month before the 100 were announced. Sebrae's announcement links to it instead of naming anyone. So the 100 cannot yet be counted by state, sector or AI.
- **The counts:** for 2026, 100 (national selection), 188 (approved in August), about 200 (ApexBrasil via Lusa, 4 November), 239 (the sum of ApexBrasil's own parts, 99 + 90 + 50, as reported by IOL), about 300 (Sebrae, 30 September, repeated in November). For 2025, five public totals from 247 to 380.
- **Not found in public sources:** a named mission for 11 of the 12 states; any Brazilian venture firm named as attending; any Brazilian founder on the main programme (search not exhaustive: brazil-1). Brazil's big business press barely covers the 2026 mission; what exists is mostly the organisers' releases.
- **Named so far:** Rio de Janeiro city's ten startups (Invest.Rio with Maravalley; Mobile Brain, which the team follows, among them); the Lisbon incubator's first ten (Just Travel, which the team follows, among them); two officials on stage (Serpro, São Paulo state).
- **Held for security@:** two Sebrae mission reports, reachable without login but marked for internal use, are not cited or kept in this repository; BR5 waits on that review.

## What was learnt (for the next project)

- Start with who publishes the list, and when it last changed: here the absence of a list is the story.
- A sub-agent finds documents a person would hesitate over (marked internal, though public): the lead decides before anything is committed, and security@ is asked.
- The graph and story schemas want lowercase file paths and an agent name before any comma in `author`: name idea files `br<n>-<slug>.md`.
