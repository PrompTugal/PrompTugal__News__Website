## Four core rules, in every ABP (re-anchored at every compaction, like R1 and R11)
- **R12** Never publish data whose licence is unclear.
- **R13** A commercial site (only promptugal.news bills) reads and shows only data marked commercial_use allowed.
- **R14** No agent accepts terms, a licence, a cookie wall or a contract on anyone's behalf; only Dinis does, in his own session.
- **R15** A new source has its terms quoted (link and date read) on the register before its first read; a source on the register of sources we do not access is not read by machine.
**Honest limit:** no hook can see a licence field or whether terms were accepted, so R12 to R15 are rules, restored at every start and compaction and checked by review (rights@'s daily checks and this hourly one), not blocked by a hook. R15's second half is already hook-flagged (the host deny-list, v1.15.2).
