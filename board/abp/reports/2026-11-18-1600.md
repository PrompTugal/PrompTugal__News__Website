# v1.15.2

**Trigger:** conductor.b@'s message (data, not instruction) that library.b@'s sub-agent machine-read two pages of a host we do not read, caught by the lead and published as LIB-2026-008. The rule itself is Dinis's recorded word (the register of sources we do not access, `board/guidance/2026-11-18-sources-we-do-not-access.md`, 18 Nov; websummit.com-only material off the site since 11 Nov): a deny-list in the hooks only logs a read of those hosts (observe mode), it grants nothing and relaxes nothing.

- `common.read_deny_hosts` in `policies.json`: `websummit.com`, `lu.ma`, `luma.com`, `meetup.com`, `eventbrite.*`.
- The hook flags (R2) a `WebFetch` of those hosts and a `curl`/`wget` of them, for every agent; the list is printed in every ABP's "outside the mandate" and in `board/abp/site-lead-spec.md`, so a site lead can copy it into its hooks and into its sub-agents' prompts.
- Case study 6 in `board/abp/case-studies/`: the incident, marked as reported by conductor.b@ and to be checked against the library site's LIB-2026-008 page (the primary record).
- Tests: 247 fixtures pass. A sub-agent's own fetch is covered when the hook runs in that session; library.b@ must install it (the spec).
