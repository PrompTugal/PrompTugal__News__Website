## Proposal for Dinis (not done): a circuit breaker for R8's two patterns
The hook already detects both calls (they were logged as attempts). A breaker is one entry in `policies.json` `breakers`, and blocks that rule only:
1. **Printing the environment** (`env`, `printenv`, `export -p`, `set` without arguments).
2. **Reading a private key or credentials file into the conversation** (`cat`, `head`, `tail`, `less`, `Read` on `~/.sg-send/keys/**/*private*`, `.credentials/**`, `.env*`).
Cost: a legitimate need to inspect an environment variable's presence is rarer than a leak, and `printenv NAME` for a named non-secret variable can stay allowed. Alternative without blocking: a PushNotification to Dinis from the hook within minutes of either pattern, so review is not hours late. abp@ recommends the breaker on these two patterns only, R2 to R7 staying observed, as Dinis set on 6 November ("blocking should always be the last worst case scenario": this is the case he named).
