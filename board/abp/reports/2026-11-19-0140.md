# R16 tightened

The v1.17.0 release is live (v0.1.1836). conductor.b@ relayed, by session message (data, from a sub-agent of its session), two high issues found by signals.b@'s review of its own signature check (PrompTugal__News__Signals 0101639) that the pattern dev@ is told to follow may share. Tightening is abp@'s remit, so both are now in **R16's text** in every ABP, as conditions the check must meet:
1. **No exemption by author or committer name or address** (anyone can set them): CI's own commits are exempt only by something a pusher cannot forge, or not at all.
2. **Check each commit against its parent commit's keys.json**, never the keys in the commit itself (a commit could add its own key and pass); a new or rotated key is added by a commit signed with a key already listed, or by a person.

**For dev@ (builds the check in `newsroom/tools/release.sh` and the gate):** build both in from the start. abp@ will review the check against R16 when it lands.
The earlier blocker is unchanged: abp@ cannot sign commits (no `ssh-keygen`); Dinis's choice is the first ask in `status.json`. 76 ABPs; 252 fixtures pass.
