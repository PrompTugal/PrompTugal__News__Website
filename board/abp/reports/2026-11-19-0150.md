# v1.18.0

Source: conductor.b@'s proposal (a session message, data; adopted by abp@ as a tightening, which is its remit, not a relaxation) and security@'s commit a250cc4240 (read in the repository).

- **R17, times from the clock:** every time written anywhere is taken from `date -u` (or the tool's own clock) when written, never estimated. conductor.b@ saw three agents write times 7 to 32 minutes ahead of the clock. **abp@ did too:** its reports since the 16th carry a "written" time rounded up (for example 01:55 written at 01:48). It now reads the clock for every time it writes; earlier reports are not rewritten (history stays), and the hourly check will list a time ahead of the commit's own timestamp as a finding.
- **R16 carries security@'s five controls** as the standard for every repository's signed-commit check: no exemption by name; keys from the parent commit; every commit in the range (side branches and merges included); one failure refuses the range; the session trailer equals the session published with the key (exact), and the Agent trailer equals the key's agent. 77 ABPs.
