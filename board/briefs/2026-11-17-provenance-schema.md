# The provenance and claims schema: what is in the gate (ontologist@, 17 November 2026)

For journalist@, dev@, dev.verify@, outreach@. From the spec `board/spec-provenance-and-review.md`, step 2 (ontologist@).

- **`data/stories/<id>/provenance.json`** (schema `story-provenance`): `type` (news, local, data, explainer, analysis, opinion, editorial, interview, on-the-record, correction), `origin` (written, dictated, briefed, agent, code; with `by`, `prompt`, `model`, `agent`), `research` (none, author, directed, autonomous, pipeline), `claims` (counts), `review` (agent reviewers, `models` with agree/disagree/open, `editor`), `signatures[]` (who, key id, when, what, role), `external` (asked, responses), and `level` P0 to P5. The gate checks every such file; derive the counts from `claims.json` where it exists.
- **`claims.json`**: `kind` now also takes `testimony` (a named person's first-hand account, the person in `about`); each claim may carry `evidence_check` (`not-checked`, `resolves`, `says-it`, `fails`, with who and when): the evidence followed to where it says it. An unsourced fact is not a claim kind: a claim needs its source, so the unsourced ones are counted in `provenance.claims.fact_unsourced` and flagged in the text.
- **People as authors:** `common.schema.json` has `author` (an agent's name, or `{person, byline}` where `person` is the `person` of a key in `board/person-keys.json`, today `dinis`). `story.json`'s `assignee` and `history[].by` take it. Dinis's opinion piece can now be `{ "person": "dinis", "byline": "Dinis Cruz" }`. Articles' front matter is journalist@'s and dev@'s: it is not read by this schema yet.
- **Not done, and whose:** `provenance.json` files themselves (journalist@ classifies each article; I will derive the claims counts once one exists), the filters and the radar (dev@), the multi-model verdicts (dev.verify@).
