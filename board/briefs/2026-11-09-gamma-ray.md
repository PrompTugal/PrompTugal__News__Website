# GAMMA-Ray

## Why

Dinis, after the opening keynote: *"At the moment our website, PrompTugal, doesn't have a centre of gravity ... we already have 6 or 10 interviews with a bunch of people that we want to now convert ... when we publish this stuff, it is still missing an anchor."*

His idea: *"take 5 standards that exist to map agents, and use them, because we have the graph technology, to map these companies and the companies we want to talk to. Then we put the interviews in context, and every interview that we do and everything we present is about this mashup."* **The site, and everything we do, becomes about the agentic level of each company.**

## The source documents (Dinis's, with Perplexity)

- `board/briefs/gamma-ray/agentic-maturity-standards-selection.md`: the five standards, the graph model, and the evidence contract.
- `board/briefs/gamma-ray/gamma-ray-concept-spec.md`: the name, and the vocabulary of scan, profile, score and map.

**Checked by the main conductor against the sources (9 Nov, 23:40).** All five standards exist and match the documents:
- Salesforce's Agentic Maturity Model (10 April 2025);
- Microsoft's Agentic AI adoption maturity model;
- SEI/Accenture's AI Adoption Maturity Model;
- EAAMM (Emelyanov, ICAIMT 2026);
- AAGMM (Acharya, arXiv 2604.16338).

The evidence grades E0 to E5 come from AgentStripes (arXiv 2610.18603).

**The name stays as Dinis chose it:** **GAMMA-Ray**, *Graph-Analyzed Multi-framework Maturity Assessment with Risk-Adjusted Yield*.

## How it fits what we have

- **It is RiskMandate's lens, measured:**
  - capability (Salesforce, EAAMM) is the agent's **reach**;
  - governance (AAGMM) is its **mandate**;
  - the capability-versus-governance mismatch is the **gap**.

  Say so on every page. Explain the **Risk-Adjusted Yield** as the maturity that is real once the governance gap is taken into account.
- **The family with RiskMandate X-ray** (the booth demo that scans a company, WS-191): **X-ray** is the quick live scan of one company at the stand. **GAMMA-Ray** is the deep, evidence-graded scan across the five standards. Scans compose into the **map**.
- **The graph is already here:** the companies, the 249 events and the people, with a source and a read date on every fact. GAMMA-Ray adds deployments, ratings, evidence (graded E0 to E5) and controls, as in section 4 of the standards document.

## The rules (so it is credible and fair)

1. **No rating without evidence.** Each rating links to its evidence and its source, with a grade.
2. **Ratings attach to deployments (use cases), not companies.** A company's numbers are computed roll-ups.
3. **Show the five axes side by side.** The alignment across frameworks is approximate (the documents say so), so never collapse it into one opaque number.
4. **Publishing ratings of named companies:** each published scan is marked **provisional**, shows its evidence grades, and **invites the company to check and correct it**. A scan built only on E0 to E2 evidence (claims, marketing, press) stays unpublished until the company has seen it. That turns outreach into an invitation ("here is your provisional GAMMA-Ray scan; tell us what we got wrong"), not an accusation.

## What to build, in order

1. **A GAMMA-Ray page on promptugal.news** (`/gamma-ray/`):
   - what it is, and the backronym;
   - the five standards, with links and their caveats;
   - the evidence grades;
   - the mismatch quadrant;
   - how a company asks for its scan or corrects it (the sign-ups lane, `board/lanes/signups.json`).
2. **The schema** (ontologist@): `deployment`, `rating`, `evidence` (graded), `control`, `framework`, `level`, as JSON beside the existing entities, derived like the rest.
3. **A pilot:** scans of the companies the team interviewed tonight, plus the 50 in the outreach list, from public evidence only, kept unpublished until each company has seen its scan.
4. **The interviews:** every interview write-up opens with that company's scan, and the articles cite it.
5. **The map:** the mismatch quadrant across all scanned companies. It is the front-page anchor.
