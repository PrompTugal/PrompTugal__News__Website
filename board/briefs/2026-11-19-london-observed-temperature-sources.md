# London's observed temperature: sources we could add later (19 November 2026)

At Dinis's word (19 Nov, about 03:15 UTC): not now, only a list. Today London's "what we said / what happened" is rain only (the Environment Agency's gauges). None of these has been read. Each would go on the register with its terms quoted first (R15), and rights@ would check the licence (R12, R13) before a first read. The notes below are from general knowledge, to be checked at the source.

- **Met Office DataHub (observations, land surface).** The official UK observations, hourly, including London stations (Heathrow, St James's Park, Northolt). It needs a free account and a key. Dinis has said no key for now.
- **Met Office MIDAS Open, via CEDA.** Daily and hourly station records, published openly under the Open Government Licence. It lags by months, so it suits history, not yesterday. Its downloads may need a CEDA login, which is against our rule on sources behind a login; to check.
- **NOAA ISD and GHCN-Daily (Heathrow, London City).** Global station archives from the US government, keyless, with daily and hourly temperature for London airports. Their terms carry WMO data-sharing notes, which need reading.
- **Open-Meteo historical archive.** Already on the register (rights@'s R8): modelled reanalysis, not a thermometer reading. Free sites only, non-commercial, CC BY 4.0. Useful as a fallback, but it is not "what happened" at a station.
- **Meteostat.** It aggregates station data, including NOAA and DWD. CC BY-NC 4.0: free sites only, if its terms allow our use.
- **London Air Quality Network (Imperial College) and Breathe London.** Some sites record temperature alongside air quality. The terms need reading; this is secondary.
- **Personal weather station networks** (Weather Underground, Netatmo). Their terms restrict reuse, and the data come from private people's devices. Not recommended.

Recommendation for when Dinis wants it: NOAA's keyless station record for Heathrow first, if its terms allow, then the Met Office DataHub if a key is ever given.
