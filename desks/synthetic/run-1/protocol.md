## Each step

Observe (one screen), See (what this persona takes in), Think, Question (or null), Confusion (or null), Act (one action, with the reason in the persona's terms). Stopping is a result.

## Interview (the same for all five)

1. What is this?
2. Who made it?
3. Can I trust it? Why?
4. What do I do next, and when?
5. Would I reply to the email (the founder: the one received; the others: the same email, about their own company)?
6. One change that would make me act.

Each finding: severity (blocking, costly, minor, held = works), the screen it came from, and the owner of the fix from CLAUDE.md's table.
