OUR QUESTION

Many companies at the summit are building AI agents. Agents now act for people and companies. They log in, buy, book and send. So we ask three simple questions of every agent we see:

REACH.
What can it actually do?
MANDATE.
What was it asked to do?
GAP.
Who controls the difference?

The idea comes from RiskMandate. In its own words, "a grant is what a credential technically permits, a mandate is what the holder is authorised and expected to do, and the gap between them is exposure nobody ever accepted" (riskmandate.ai/llms.txt, read 4 November 2026).
