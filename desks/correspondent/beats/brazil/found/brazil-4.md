# brazil-4: State missions, North and Northeast

## Method

Shared budget with brazil-5. Searches in Portuguese ("Web Summit Lisboa 2026" with each state, "missão", "Sebrae <UF>", FAP and secretariat names, demonyms such as "startups amazonenses"), 17 searches in all for both tasks, and 17 page fetches in all (about 10 for this file). Opened: the Agência Sebrae articles of the national selection, the Sebrae Tocantins call (Conexão TO), the Amapá report (Manchete Popular), the Sebrae Startups programme page for Piauí, the O Povo page on the Siará Tech Summit, the Correio da Manhã report of 12 states. Every search was dominated by the same national pages (ApexBrasil regulation, Agência Sebrae). According to Agência Sebrae (read 2026-11-06, https://agenciasebrae.com.br/?p=43175) and Correio da Manhã, 12 states organised their own missions; neither names them. The ApexBrasil call reserved places for the North, Northeast and Federal District.
