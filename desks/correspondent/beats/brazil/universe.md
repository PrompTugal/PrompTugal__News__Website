Ideas and evidence for the journalist, not an article. Every fact below has its public source and was read on 6 November 2026 unless said otherwise; each claim is also in `evidence.json` beside this file. It builds on the published article `content/articles/brazil-startup-mission.md` (S9) and does not repeat its sourcing.

The short version: Brazil is one of the two largest national presences at Web Summit (with Germany, according to ApexBrasil), yet there is no public list of who comes. The one official list is 188 tax numbers with no names, frozen at 28 August. The figures for 2026 run from 100 to 300, and the public figures for 2025 run from 247 to 380. That absence is itself the finding.

## Who

- **ApexBrasil** (trade and investment agency): leads the national mission; opened a Lisbon office in 2025 in a historic building it shares with Embratur and Fiocruz (Startups.com.br, 12 Nov 2025). Jorge Viana, president (quoted 2025).
- **Sebrae / Sebrae Startups**: co-organiser; Cristina Mieko, head of Startups at Sebrae Nacional (Agência Sebrae, 30 Sep 2026). Décio Lima, president (quoted 2025).
- **Supporters**: Serpro, Embratur, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Brazilian Embassy in Lisbon (IOL).
- **State missions**: 12, unnamed by Sebrae. Only city or state groups found by name for 2026: Rio de Janeiro city (Invest.Rio with Maravalley, 10 startups, flights by Gol). Paraíba (30 startups, per S9's earlier capture) still unconfirmed. A 2023 São Paulo delegation (InvestSP with Creative SP, 20 companies) was found, nothing for 2026.
- **Hubs**: up to 10 by the hub track; four named by IOL: Porto Digital (Recife), ACATE (Santa Catarina), SENAI CIMATEC (Bahia), Instituto Caldeira (Porto Alegre). Maravalley (Rio). In 2025: Softex, Anprotec and Serpro nominated 84.
- **Portuguese side of the incubator**: partners named in the held reports (not cited); in public sources, 2025 curation by Plug and Play (Startups.com.br, 12 Nov 2025).

- The team already follows 11 Brazilian companies (ai-infinity, fido, fretefy, hablla, just-travel, mobile-brain, naporta, ola-doutor, prohound, rapidoc, raras-health); the team's record for Mobile Brain and Just Travel says each has a LIS26 page on websummit.com (re-checked 6 Nov 2026). Whether the other nine are in the 100 cannot be checked: the official list has no names.
- AI share of the 100: not knowable from public sources (no names). Mobile Brain describes itself as "an AI deep tech" (team record, from its own text).

## Sessions and speakers

From the Web Summit programme (desks/programme/agenda.json, read 5 Nov; the two linked sessions re-read 6 Nov):

- "Brazil's blueprint for cyber resilience", 12 Nov 16:10, Stage 8: Wilton Itaiguara Gonçalves Mota, President and CEO, Serpro (a supporter of the mission).
- "The next digital frontier", 11 Nov 10:30, Stage 11 (Policy Summit): Vahan Agopyan, Secretary of Science, Technology and Innovation, State of São Paulo, beside Bernardo Correia, Portugal's Secretary of State for Digitalisation.
- "German-Brazilian Startup Night", 10 Nov 17:30 to 22:30, Gale patio: no hosts or description listed.
- Brazil Pavilion programme (ApexBrasil via IOL): Internationalisation Seminar 9 Nov; AI pitch competition 10 Nov (Brazil, France, Portugal, Germany); panels on digital diplomacy, biotechnology, tourism tech; incubation cycle 2027 announced 10 Nov.
- No Brazilian founder was found as a named speaker on the main programme in the time given. A search of websummit.com found none; that is a gap to check, not a finding of absence.

## Investors

- No Brazilian VC fund was found named as attending Lisbon 2026. ApexBrasil's survey counts more than 120 funds and US$2.135 billion of VC in 2024 across 386 deals (ABES, 21 Aug 2026).
- Portuguese investors named in Brazilian sources: none found in public sources for 2026. In 2025, Atlantic Hub (Oeiras) said it brought 70 business people, entrepreneurs and investors from Brazil (DN Brasil, 10 Nov 2025).
- Money claims, unverified: US$700 million raised at earlier editions (ApexBrasil, 2026); nearly R$94 million in deals "in recent years" (DN Brasil, 2025).

## Connections

- **Mobile Brain** (team company) is one of the Rio ten, flown by Gol, through Invest.Rio and Maravalley (Startups.com.br, 26 Oct 2026).
- **Just Travel** (team company) was in the first cycle of the ApexBrasil/Sebrae Lisbon incubator (Startups.com.br, 12 Nov 2025); whether it still works from Lisbon is to be checked.
- **Serpro**: supports the mission (IOL) and puts its CEO on the Cybersecurity stage (Web Summit); in 2025 it nominated startups (Startups.com.br).
- **ApexBrasil Lisbon office**: hosts the incubator, Embratur and Fiocruz on other floors (Startups.com.br).
- **Porto Digital (Recife)**: named hub in 2026 (IOL); runs Porto Digital Europa in Aveiro for soft landing (ambassador's opinion, DN, 2024).
- **Instituto Caldeira** and Unicorn Factory Lisboa: a signed partnership (search result citing an April 2025 piece; not opened, to check).
- **Intuitivo** (Portuguese edtech, past PITCH winner) raised 1.35M euros to expand into Brazil (curator's capture, desks/curator/2026-11-04-1945.json): the other direction of the same bridge.
- **Gol**: Rio to Lisbon route opened September 2026, five weekly flights from 24 Nov (Startups.com.br, 26 Oct 2026).
