An idea for the journalist, not an article.

## The angle

ApexBrasil and Sebrae run a startup incubator inside ApexBrasil's Lisbon office, a building shared with Embratur and Fiocruz. Its 2027 class (up to 20) is announced at Web Summit on 10 November. Its first class of ten was announced at Web Summit 2025; whether it stayed the same is a question for ApexBrasil and Sebrae, on the record. One of the first class, Just Travel, is a company the team already follows and is at LIS26.

## Why a reader cares

Brazilian founders weighing the call; Portuguese ecosystem players (Startup Portugal and others); investors who want to meet Brazilian startups already based in Lisbon.

## The facts we have

- Ten startups named in November 2025: Aiper, Biolinker, Beeviral, 593ican, Hope, SST, Just Travel, SleepUp, Wood Chat, PixNow; chosen from 112 exhibitors with Plug and Play; one floor near Avenida da Liberdade, according to Startups.com.br (12 Nov 2025). https://startups.com.br/eventos/web-summit/governo-abre-casa-brasileira-para-incubar-startups-em-lisboa/, read 6 November 2026
- 2027 cycle: up to 40 to an exploration stage, top 20 onward, preparation online from November, in Lisbon from March 2027; bonus points for HealthTech, EnergyTech, ClimaTech, TravelTech, AgTech, Biotech, according to DN Brasil (4 Sep 2026). https://dnbrasil.dn.pt/negcios-br/apexbrasil-e-sebrae-selecionam-20-startups-brasileiras-para-incubao-em-lisboa, read 6 November 2026
- Announcement on 10 November, according to IOL and Sebrae (via starten.tech). https://www.iol.pt/web-summit-2026/brasil-em-lisboa/web-summit-2026-brasil-chega-a-lisboa-com-200-empresas-a-procura-de-investimento and https://starten.tech/2026/11/04/startups-brasileiras-buscam-negocios-globais-no-web-summit-lisboa/, read 6 November 2026
- Just Travel has a LIS26 appearance page, according to the team's record (data/companies/just-travel/company.json, re-checked 6 November 2026).
