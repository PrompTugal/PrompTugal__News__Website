**Caution from the lead (correspondent@, 6 November 2026, 10:45 UTC):** the company quotes here came through a fetch tool that summarises pages, so each must be re-read word for word on its page before it is printed. Where an idea reads one named company through reach, mandate and gap, the company is asked first (outreach@ drafts, a person sends) and its answer goes in; what a page does not say is reported as not described there, never as missing from the product.


## The angle

Put the companies' own descriptions side by side: an agent that works in your signed-in accounts, one that triggers workflows across 3,500 integrations, one that changes a booking by phone, one that gets an email inbox, one that searches the web. Reach is the question: what can each actually touch? Quote, do not judge. The finding is the shape of the language: counts and categories, rarely permissions.

## Why a reader cares

A CISO asking what an employee's agent could touch. A founder choosing a tool. An investor reading how vendors describe their own reach. An attendee deciding whom to ask at a stand.

## The facts we have

- Make says its agents "take action" and can "trigger real workflows with access to over 3500+ integrations". https://www.make.com/en/ai-agents (read 6 November 2026)
- Strawberry says its companions can "take actions on your behalf", work "Even behind login walls", and connect Gmail, Slack, Google Calendar, Linear, HubSpot, Notion, Google Sheets and LinkedIn. https://strawberrybrowser.com (read 6 November 2026)
- PolyAI shows an agent that "identifies him, changes it to 8pm for five, texts the details and resolves the call", with use cases including billing and payments. https://poly.ai/ (read 6 November 2026)
- Primitive says it gives agents "an inbox and outbox on email", sending from your own domain or one it provides. https://www.onprimitive.com/ (read 6 November 2026)
- Emergence says its agents cover the semiconductor lifecycle and automate "investigations, decisions, and corrective actions". https://www.emergence.ai/ (read 6 November 2026)
- Legora says its Agent "plans, executes, reviews and delivers". https://legora.com/ (read 6 November 2026)
- Exa describes search for agents over "the largest index of public web and private information". https://exa.ai/ (read 6 November 2026)

## What is missing

- Which actions are writes or irreversible; most pages do not say.
- Credential model for most vendors (only Strawberry states it: passwords encrypted on device, never visible to the company or LLM providers).
- Product behaviour was not tested; marketing pages only.
- Genspark and Cisco Outshift pages not reached.
