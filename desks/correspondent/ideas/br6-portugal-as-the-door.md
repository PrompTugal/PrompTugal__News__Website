An idea for the journalist, not an article.

## The angle

Behind the pavilion is a standing structure: ApexBrasil opened a Lisbon office in 2025 that hosts a startup incubator, Embratur and Fiocruz; Sebrae has an MoU with Startup Portugal; Recife's Porto Digital runs a soft-landing base in Aveiro; Brazil's ecosystem is large (13,000+ startups, 120+ funds, according to ApexBrasil). And the bridge runs both ways: Portugal's Intuitivo raised money to expand into Brazil. A piece about Lisbon as a year-round gateway, not a four-day event.

## Why a reader cares

Brazilian founders thinking about Europe; Portuguese startups selling into Brazil; investors looking at cross-Atlantic deals; Portuguese policy readers (Startup Visa, IAPMEI).

- The incubation house sits on one floor near Avenida da Liberdade, shared with Embratur and Fiocruz, according to Startups.com.br (12 Nov 2025). https://startups.com.br/eventos/web-summit/governo-abre-casa-brasileira-para-incubar-startups-em-lisboa/, read 6 November 2026
