An idea for the journalist, not an article.

## The angle

The published S9 article covers the 2026 spread (100, about 200, about 300). This goes further: for 2025 the same two agencies and the press give five public totals, from 247 to 380, and for 2026 ApexBrasil's own parts (99 + 90 + 50) add to 239, not its headline 200. The only official list of 2026 participants is 188 tax numbers with no names, frozen at 28 August. Brazil is one of the two largest delegations at Web Summit, and nobody outside the agencies can count it.

## Why a reader cares

An investor or a Portuguese founder looking for Brazilian partners cannot find out who is coming. A journalist using "the largest delegation" should know how soft the number is. Founders in the delegation lose visibility when no public catalogue exists.

## The facts we have

- 2025: "quase 380 empresas, sendo 247 startups" supported by ApexBrasil, Sebrae Nacional and Serpro, according to ApexBrasil's 2026 hub rules. https://click.apexbrasil.com.br/mkt/WebSummit%20Lisboa/Regulamento_Ambientes_Inovacao.pdf, read 6 November 2026
- 2025: 340 startups, 256 selected by ApexBrasil and Sebrae and 84 by Softex, Anprotec and Serpro, according to Startups.com.br (3 Nov 2025). https://startups.com.br/eventos/web-summit/apexbrasil-e-sebrae-levam-340-startups-brasileiras-para-o-web-summit-lisboa/, read 6 November 2026
- 2025: "mais de 370 startups" and 151 participants selected by ApexBrasil, according to DN Brasil (10 Nov 2025). https://dnbrasil.dn.pt/web-summit-brasil-chega-com-mais-de-370-startups-e-programao-intensa, read 6 November 2026
- 2026: 99 selected for Web Summit's programme, 90 nominated by partners, 50 linked to ten hubs, under the headline "200 empresas", according to IOL from an ApexBrasil statement. https://www.iol.pt/web-summit-2026/brasil-em-lisboa/web-summit-2026-brasil-chega-a-lisboa-com-200-empresas-a-procura-de-investimento, read 6 November 2026
- 2026: ApexBrasil's results page lists 188 approved companies by CNPJ only (105 confirmed, 33 waiting list, 26 awaiting confirmation, 24 withdrawn), "status em 28/8/2026". https://crm-apps.apexbrasil.com.br/orgbdeab873/inscricao-eventos/evento/web-summit-lisboa-2026/0714952e-98da-f011-8543-7ced8db0f987, read 6 November 2026
- History: 80 companies in 2022, a plan of 85 startups and 100 companies in 2023, according to Forbes Portugal with Lusa. https://www.forbespt.com/agencia-de-investimento-brasileira-leva-delegacao-recorde-a-web-summit-lisboa, read 6 November 2026

## What is missing / to check
