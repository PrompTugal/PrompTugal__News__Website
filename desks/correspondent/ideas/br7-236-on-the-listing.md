## The angle

The mission's organisers publish counts (100, about 200, about 300) but no names. Web Summit's own public startup listing for Lisbon 2026 names 236 startups based in Brazil: 169 in its Alpha track, 56 in Beta, 11 in Growth. 32 are in AI and machine learning, 37 in healthtech. And the press names two state missions Sebrae does not: Paraíba's 30 and São Paulo state's ten (SP Global Tech), most of whom are on that listing.

## Why a reader cares

An investor or a Portuguese founder can now see who comes, by field; the founders in the delegation get found; and the gap between the organisers' counts and the summit's own list is itself the story.
