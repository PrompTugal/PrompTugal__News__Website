## The angle

ApexBrasil says Brazilian companies have raised about US$700 million through contacts made at earlier Web Summits. Other figures from the same side are far smaller: nearly R$94 million in deals "in recent years" (DN Brasil, 2025). Public money, public claims, very different scales. Not a takedown: a question of what each figure measures.

## Why a reader cares

Investors and founders judging whether national pavilions pay off; Brazilian taxpayers; other countries' agencies (Portugal included) running similar delegations.
