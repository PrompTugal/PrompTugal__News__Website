## The angle

The summit's tracks do not run every day, so a reader's field decides their best day. In the agenda this newsroom tracks, every Cybersecurity Summit session falls on Thursday 12 November; PITCH's are on Tuesday 10; the Policy Summit peaks on Wednesday 11; the AI Summit and the Developer Summit run Wednesday and Thursday; Monday 9 is Opening Night and the Night Summit. One table, one line per field: "if you work in X, block Y". Then, per field, the companies this newsroom follows in that field, each linking to its page (the reason for a company to link back: historian@'s lesson 3, board/briefs/2026-11-07-the-php-newsrooms-lesson.md).

## Why a reader cares

An attendee on Sunday, between registration and Opening Night, choosing which day to spend where. A founder deciding when to be at the stand. A CISO with one day to spare (Thursday).
