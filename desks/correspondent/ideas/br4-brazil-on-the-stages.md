An idea for the journalist, not an article.

## The angle

Brazil brings one of the two largest delegations, but on the main programme the Brazilian names found are officials: the head of Serpro, the federal data service, on the Cybersecurity stage, and São Paulo state's science secretary on the Policy Summit stage beside Portugal's Secretary of State for Digitalisation. Most of Brazil's programme happens at its own pavilion. A guide to where to see Brazil in Lisbon, and a question: why so few Brazilian founders on the main stages?

## Why a reader cares

Attendees planning their days; CISOs (Serpro runs critical federal data); policy readers following Brazil-Portugal digital ties; founders asking how to get stage time.
