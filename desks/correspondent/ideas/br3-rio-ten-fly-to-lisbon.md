An idea for the journalist, not an article.

## The angle

Of the 12 state missions Sebrae counts, almost none are named in public. Rio de Janeiro's city mission is the exception: Invest.Rio and the Maravalley hub take ten named startups, for the second year, with flights paid by Gol on its new Rio to Lisbon route. One of them, Mobile Brain, an AI company the team already follows, has its own LIS26 page. A concrete, named slice of the Brazilian delegation, and a way into the city-to-city link between Rio (host of Web Summit Rio) and Lisbon.

## Why a reader cares

Attendees and investors get ten names to look for; Portuguese founders see a direct air and business link opening; readers in Rio see their city's delegation covered.

## The facts we have

- Invest.Rio and Maravalley take 10 Rio startups to Web Summit Lisboa 2026, second year; Daniel Barros (CEO, Maravalley) and Sidney Levy (president, Invest.Rio) quoted, according to Startups.com.br (18 Sep 2026). https://startups.com.br/eventos/web-summit/invest-rio-e-maravalley-vao-levar-10-startups-para-o-web-summit-lisboa/, read 6 November 2026
- The ten: Mobile Brain, sheerME, Paralelo.app, Predator Sports & Health, Embeddo, Lucca, Bizuu, Ray Pay, ATLANTICIT, AllPetSenior; Gol pays the flights; Luiz Borrego (CIO, Gol) quoted; Gol's Rio to Lisbon route began in September with five weekly flights from 24 November, according to Startups.com.br (26 Oct 2026). https://startups.com.br/negocios/ecossistema/gol-vai-bancar-passagens-de-10-startups-cariocas-a-lisboa/, read 6 November 2026
- The same ten in Correio da Manhã (25 Oct 2026), which dates the event 8 to 12 November. https://www.correiodamanha.com.br/rio-de-janeiro/2026/10/amp/329679-invest-rio-firma-parceria-para-levar-dez-startups-cariocas-ao-web-summit-lisboa.html, read 6 November 2026
- Mobile Brain describes itself as "an AI deep tech in computational neuroscience" and has a LIS26 appearance page, according to the team's record (data/companies/mobile-brain/company.json, re-checked 6 November 2026).
- Maravalley also led a mission to VivaTech in Paris in June 2026, according to Startups.com.br (18 Sep 2026), same URL as above.
