An idea for the journalist, not an article.

**Caution from the lead (correspondent@, 6 November 2026, 10:45 UTC):** the company quotes here came through a fetch tool that summarises pages, so each must be re-read word for word on its page before it is printed. Where an idea reads one named company through reach, mandate and gap, the company is asked first (outreach@ drafts, a person sends) and its answer goes in; what a page does not say is reported as not described there, never as missing from the product.


## The angle

Tabulate what each company says it offers (approval step, step-by-step visibility, audit log, override, limits, certification) and what its page does not mention. Report absence neutrally: a marketing page is not a security review. The point is that what is advertised is mostly data-handling certification, while the questions on the Web Summit stages (who let it in, who takes the fall) are about authority.

## Why a reader cares

A CISO writing a questions list before walking the floor. A founder who needs a procurement checklist. A journalist who wants a fair set of questions to put to vendors.

## The facts we have

- Make: "set clear rules, add manual approvals, or stop the Agent at specific points"; a Reasoning panel shows "every decision an agent makes, step by step"; spend or action limits not described. https://www.make.com/en/ai-agents (read 6 November 2026)
- GetVocal AI: Control Tower lets you "override any agent and audit every decision"; a high-value return is "Flagged for Human Approval". https://www.getvocal.ai (read 6 November 2026)
- PolyAI: "Nothing goes live without you"; "Guardrails, PII redaction, human-in-the-loop"; SOC 2+ Type 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, GDPR. https://poly.ai/ (read 6 November 2026)
- Strawberry: "Choose per app and per action what companions may do without asking"; audit logs, spend limits and send confirmations not described on the page. https://strawberrybrowser.com (read 6 November 2026)
- You.com: "spend caps on every API key" and zero data retention. https://you.com/ (read 6 November 2026)
- Legora: "Zero AI training on your data", SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA; permissions, audit and approval steps not described. https://legora.com/ (read 6 November 2026)
- SageOx and Manus: no controls described on the home pages read. https://sageox.ai/ and https://manus.im/ (read 6 November 2026)
- Agentic AI Foundation working groups: Security & Privacy and Governance, Risk & Regulatory Alignment. https://aaif.io/ (read 6 November 2026)
