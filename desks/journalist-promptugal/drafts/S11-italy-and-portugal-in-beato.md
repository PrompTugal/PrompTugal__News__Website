## In short

- European Tech Champions, Italy and Portugal, is on Wednesday 11 November, from 18:30, at the Unicorn Stage in Lisbon's Beato Innovation District.
- It is organised by [Unicorn Factory Lisboa](../organisations/unicorn-factory-lisboa/index.html), the Embassy of Italy in Portugal and the Italian Trade Agency (ICE), as part of the programme around Web Summit.
- Two panels of scaleups, investors and institutional leaders discuss how European tech companies raise capital, compete abroad and grow beyond their home markets, followed by networking.
- At the summit itself, ICE organises an Italian national pavilion for about 30 startups and innovative small companies.

## The story

**The evening.** AICEP, Portugal's trade and investment agency, describes the event as a meeting of founders, investors, corporate leaders and institutional representatives on how European technology companies can raise capital, compete internationally and scale beyond their domestic markets. There are two panel discussions with scaleups, investors and institutional leaders from Italy, Portugal and elsewhere in Europe, then a networking session for Portuguese and Italian startups, investors at Web Summit and the wider European tech community. Welcome and registration start at 18:30. Registration is through the event's website, AICEP says.

**Who it is for.** Entrepreneurs looking to grow internationally, investors looking for opportunities in European technology, and organisations interested in cross-border cooperation, according to AICEP.

**Italy at the summit.** ICE organises Italy's collective presence at Web Summit 2026, from 9 to 12 November: about 30 Italian startups and innovative small and medium-sized companies, showing their products in the Italian national pavilion, as Assolombarda, the Milan business association, reports ICE's call. Applications ran from 11 May to 8 June 2026.

**The host.** Unicorn Factory Lisboa is the city's innovation hub in Beato. On 27 October it named the winners of its 2026 Entrepreneurship Awards ([our article](lisbon-awards-and-the-algarve-plan.html)).

A suggestion: Italian founders in the national pavilion and Portuguese scaleups looking at Italy could meet at the networking session in Beato on the evening of 11 November.

## What this does not claim

- **Who speaks.** No speaker or panellist is named in the sources read.
- **Which Italian startups come.** The 30 or so in ICE's pavilion are not named in the sources read, and where the pavilion stands is not given.
- **Whether registration is open or free.** AICEP says only that it is through the event's website.
- **It is not an official Web Summit session.** It is part of the week's programme in Lisbon, at a different venue, as AICEP describes it; it was not found on Web Summit's schedule.
