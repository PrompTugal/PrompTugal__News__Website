## In short

- ApexBrasil, Brazil's trade and investment agency, and Sebrae, its small business service, bring a national mission to Web Summit Lisbon, with a pavilion in the MEO Arena.
- How many come depends on who counts: five public figures for 2026 run from 100 to about 300.
- **No public list names the delegation.** ApexBrasil's results page lists 188 companies by tax number only, with a status from 28 August.
- Named so far: ten startups from Rio de Janeiro, one national pick, four hubs and two officials on Web Summit's stages.
- If you are part of the delegation, tell us who you are. We are mapping all of it.

## The mission at a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who runs it** | ApexBrasil and Sebrae, with Serpro, Embratur, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Brazilian Embassy in Lisbon |
| **When** | 8 to 13 November: pitch training on 8 November for 18 startups, a seminar on 9 November, the summit from 10 to 12 November, a closing session on 13 November (the mission's rules) |
| **Where** | A 324 square metre Brazil Pavilion in the MEO Arena (ApexBrasil, through Lusa) |
| **What each startup gets** | A ticket for up to five people, a stand on one day, a place in the delegation's catalogue, meeting rooms in the pavilion; pitch training for the top 50 (the rules) |
| **Who pays** | Sebrae pays flights and travel insurance for one person from each of the 40 ranked highest, if they qualify (Sebrae) |

## How many come

| Count | What it counts | Who says it, and when |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | The national selection | Sebrae, 30 September |
| 188 | Companies marked approved, by tax number only, status as of 28 August | ApexBrasil's results page |
| about 200 | Brazilian companies taking part | ApexBrasil, through Lusa, 4 November |
| 239 | The sum of ApexBrasil's own parts: 99 selected for Web Summit's programme, 90 put forward by partners, 50 through ten hubs | ApexBrasil, through IOL |
| about 300 | Startups expected in the delegation Sebrae leads | Sebrae, 30 September |

The figures may count different groups. None of the sources reconciles them, and neither does this article.

## Who is named

- **Rio de Janeiro's ten.** Gol pays the flights of ten startups from the city, through Invest.Rio and Maravalley, as reported by Startups.com.br: Mobile Brain, sheerME, Paralelo.app, Predator Sports & Health, Embeddo, Lucca, Bizuu, Ray Pay, ATLANTICIT and AllPetSenior. [Mobile Brain](../companies/mobile-brain/index.html) has a page here.
- **One national pick.** Sebrae's announcement names one of the 100: Vox EPower, from Nova Iguaçu.
- **Four hubs.** Porto Digital (Recife), ACATE (Santa Catarina), SENAI CIMATEC (Bahia) and Instituto Caldeira (Porto Alegre), as IOL reports from ApexBrasil.
- **The states.** Sebrae says 12 states run missions of their own. It does not name them.

## What this does not claim

- **Which count is right.** The five figures above come from the organisers and the press on different dates, and may count different groups.
- **Who the 100 are.** Without names, the delegation cannot be counted by state, sector or use of AI.
- **The pavilion and the 700 million dollars.** ApexBrasil says, through Lusa, that Brazilian companies have raised about 700 million dollars at earlier editions. ApexBrasil's own announcement asks for a login, so neither figure was read at its source, and PrompTugal.news has not checked them.
- **The 12 states' missions.** Only Rio de Janeiro city's group was found by name. Paraíba's 30 startups, reported before, could not be confirmed: its official sites did not answer.
- **No meeting is planned** between anyone named here that we know of. The suggestion is ours.
