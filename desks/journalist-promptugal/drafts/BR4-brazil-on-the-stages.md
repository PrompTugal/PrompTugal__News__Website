## In short

- A search of Web Summit's public session pages found 27 Brazil-tied speakers in 31 appearances: 7 officials, 2 journalists and 18 founders or team members.
- The officials speak on the main and masterclass stages: Serpro, the federal data service, São Paulo state and its investment agency, Invest Rio, and a speaker listed as Brazil's Deputy Minister of Science, Technology and Innovation.
- The founders are all in the Startup Showcase, among them two of Rio de Janeiro's ten.
- The Brazil Pavilion, stand E369, has its own programme from 10 to 12 November: pitch sessions every morning, panels and happy hours.
- No Brazilian venture firm was found among the speakers. The search could not cover the whole speaker list.

## Founders in the Startup Showcase

Eighteen founders and team members from Brazilian startups appear in the Startup Showcase, on Stages 6 and 12. Their companies, as Web Summit lists them, are FM LABS Hairtech, Programe Studio, Ujamaatech, EMPREENDFY, Plantah, Agendart, Longevia, DUXlab, IseTool, [Just Travel](../companies/just-travel/index.html), ComuniClima, AME, Colega Médico, AllPetSenior, Gander Brasil, GLOBALD.ai, Bizuu and QualityStorm (Forlogic). Bizuu and AllPetSenior are two of the ten startups Rio de Janeiro flies to Lisbon ([our article on Brazil's mission](brazil-startup-mission.html)).

## What this does not claim

- **The search is not complete.** Web Summit's public speaker list shows its first 20 speakers a page; beyond the first pages it is behind a login, so the search worked from public session pages. More Brazilian speakers may be on the programme. Three speakers whose pages give no country were left out.
- **"No Brazilian investor"** means none was found in that search, not that none comes.
- **Who is Brazilian** is as Web Summit's pages tie each speaker to Brazil (organisation or country), not our judgement.
- **Times can change.** They are Web Summit's, read on 6 November; check the app on the day.
