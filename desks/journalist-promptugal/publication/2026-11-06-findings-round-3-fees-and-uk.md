# Round 3, last part: fees, the form, and the UK

## ERC's fees for registering a periodical

The fee table for the ERC's acts is the annex to Portaria n.º 785/2009 of 27 July 2009, as ERC publishes it (<https://www.erc.pt/documentos/legislacaosite/Portaria785_2009.pdf>, read 6 November 2026). Its first table prices each act in **units of account** (*unidades de conta*). The rows that concern us:

| Act | Units of account |
|---|---|
| Provisional registration of a periodical (*inscrição provisória*) | 0.60 |
| Definitive registration of a periodical (*inscrição definitiva*) | 0.10 |
| Deposit of a publication's editorial statute | 0.20 |
| Change to ownership, capital or logo | 0.40 |
| Cancelling a registration | 0.10 |

**Not established here:** the euro value of a unit of account today (it is set separately, and no source for it was read in this round), and whether the 2009 table has been amended since. ERC's own decisions also mention an annual regulation and supervision fee; that was seen only in a search result, not read, so it is not counted here.

## ERC's application form

ERC publishes a form, "Requerimento para registo de publicação periódica". The address found for it (<https://www.erc.pt/imagem/EDT_REQUERIMENTO%20PARA%20REGISTO%20DE%20PUBLICA%C3%87%C3%83O%20PERI%C3%93DICA.pdf>) returned a web page instead of the PDF on 6 November 2026, so its current fields were not read. What the application must contain is in Decreto Regulamentar 8/99, article 18 (round 1): the editorial project's synopsis with a draft statute, the logo, the director's acceptance with an identity document, and INPI's declaration that the title is free.
