# Draft: replies, corrections and removal

**What Portugal's Press Law gives**, in brief (Lei n.º 2/99, articles 24 to 27, read in the consolidated text):

- **A right of reply** for anyone, person or organisation, who has been referred to, even indirectly, in a way that may affect their reputation (article 24, n.º 1). **A right of correction** where the facts about them are untrue or wrong (n.º 2). Both apply to texts and images (n.º 3).
- **Who and when:** the person concerned, their legal representative or heirs. The deadline is 30 days for a daily or weekly publication and 60 days for one that appears less often, counted from publication (article 25, n.º 1). The text is sent, signed and identified, to the director (n.º 3). A reply is limited to 300 words, or to the length of the passage it answers if that is longer (n.º 4).
- **How it is published:** free of charge, in the same section, with the same prominence and presentation as what provoked it, in one piece, marked as a reply or correction (article 26, n.º 3). The editor may add only a brief note pointing out an error of fact in the reply (n.º 6). A reply may be refused only on the grounds the law lists: out of time, from someone without standing, manifestly unfounded, or over the limits. The refusal is given in writing with its reason (n.º 7).
- **If refused without grounds**, the person may go to a court within 10 days, or to the regulator (article 27).
- **The rights are lost** if, with the person's agreement, the publication has already corrected or clarified the text, or given them another way to put their position (article 24, n.º 4).

Whether this law binds PrompTugal.news is part of the open decision "For Dinis: registering PrompTugal.news". This draft proposes we honour it either way.

## Proposed page: Replies, corrections and removal

**If we wrote about you.** If an article names you or your organisation and you think it is wrong, or unfair to your reputation, tell us. Open an issue on [our repository](https://github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Website), or use the editors' address once it is published, and say which article and what you want changed.

- **A correction.** If a fact about you is wrong, we correct it on the page, with the date, the old text and what changed (see our corrections policy).
- **A reply.** If you want to give your side, we publish your reply on the same article, marked as a reply, free and as prominently as what it answers. A reply should be up to 300 words, or as long as the passage it answers, and should keep to that passage. We may add a short note only to point out an error of fact in it.
- **When we would say no.** Only if a reply is not about what we published, is abusive, or would make us liable for what it says. Then we tell you why, in writing.
- **How fast.** We aim to act within two working days of receiving it. In the summit week (9 to 12 November) we aim for the same day.

**If you want something removed.** We remove what we should not have published: a fact about a person that is not public and professional, a contact detail, anything unlawful. Tell us as above.

**What removal can and cannot do.** Removal takes the text off our pages, their markdown copies, our data files and our search, the same day. It cannot take it out of our repository's history, which is public and kept on purpose so that every change can be seen, or out of copies other people have already made. Where something must also come out of the history, that is for Dinis Cruz, the editor-in-chief, to decide, and we will say what we did.

**What we will not do** is remove an article because it is inconvenient, or change what it says without saying so.
