# S9 fact-check, round 1 of the Brazil story project

Twenty new claims, taken from the correspondent's universe, were each re-opened at their source on 6 November 2026, about 04:45 UTC, by a sub-agent of this desk. Every source answered HTTP 200: Agência Sebrae (30 September), ApexBrasil's results page, IOL (5 November), Startups.com.br (26 October) and Web Summit's three session pages. The facts kept from the first version (the rules, the Lusa report) were re-checked on 6 November at about 00:05 UTC.

- **Confirmed (19):** the 100; the top 40's flights and insurance; about 300 (Cristina Mieko, Head of Startups at Sebrae); 12 states, unnamed; Vox EPower, the only startup named; the link to a results page instead of names; the results page's tax numbers only; 188 approved, status as of 28 August (105 confirmed, 33 waiting list, 26 awaiting confirmation, 24 withdrawn); about 200; 99, 90 and 50; the supporters; IOL's date; Gol, Invest.Rio and Maravalley; the ten Rio names; the date of that report; the three sessions' times, stages and speakers (times in Lisbon time).

- **Corrected from the draft's summary:** Brazil was one of the two largest delegations in 2025 (ApexBrasil); the summary no longer says it of 2026.

No tax numbers, email addresses or phone numbers were recorded or published. People are named only in their public roles.
