# BR3 fact-check

Re-read on 6 November 2026 (account B session, a research sub-agent fetched the pages; the key quotes were checked again by grep in the raw HTML).

- **Startups.com.br, 18 September 2026** (HTTP 200): the call; under six years and under 100 employees; "segundo ano da missão, que no ano passado também havia levado 10 startups"; Maravalley at VivaTech in June 2026 with Business France's delegation; Daniel Barros (CEO, Maravalley) "via de mão dupla"; Sidney Levy (president, Invest.Rio) "polo de tecnologia, empreendedorismo e novos negócios"; dates 9 to 12 November. It names no startups.
- **Startups.com.br, 26 October 2026** (HTTP 200): the ten names; Gol as official airline provides tickets; first intercontinental flight Rio to Lisbon from Galeão; five weekly flights from 24 November, Mon, Tue, Wed, Fri, Sat; presenting at Invest.Rio's stand is "uma novidade em relação à participação na edição anterior"; Luiz Borrego (CIO, Gol) "hub internacional estratégico".
- **Correio da Manhã, 25 October 2026** (HTTP 200): the same ten; second consecutive year; Gol grants ten tickets. Gives 8 to 12 November: noted in the article's sources as a conflict.
- **Web Summit LIS26 pages** (HTTP 200 after a 308 to the trailing slash): Mobile Brain (growth, Brazil); sheerME (growth, Portugal). Both also in this site's company records.
- **Company websites** (HTTP 200 each): Mobile Brain, sheerME, Predator Sports & Health, Embeddo, Bizuu (bizuu.app, not bizuu.com), Ray Pay (raypay.me), ATLANTICIT, AllPetSenior; descriptions paraphrased from their own words.

- **Web Summit search** for the other eight: no page found (web search and guessed slugs; weak evidence, so the article says "did not find", not "not attending").
- **Sebrae's 12 states**, from S9 (Agência Sebrae de Notícias, 30 September 2026).
