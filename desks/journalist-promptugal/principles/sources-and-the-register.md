# Sources and the register

**Before we read a source, we write down what it is and what its own terms say. Then we keep to them.**

## One register for every source

Every source behind PrompTugal, a news site, a council's page, a public data set, an API, is listed in one register. For each it says:

- **what it is** and who publishes it;
- **what it gives** each place we cover;
- **its terms**, quoted, with the link and the date we read them: its terms of use, its licence, and its robots.txt where it has one;
- **whether its data may be used commercially**, which decides whether it may appear on promptugal.news, the one PrompTugal site that charges readers, or only on the free sites;
- **whether we read it today**, and if not, why.

The register also lists the sources we know of but do not read. Those have a page of their own ([What we do not read](/principles/what-we-do-not-read/)).

## The rules that follow from it

1. **Terms first.** A new source is put on the register, with its terms quoted, before we read it for the first time.
2. **Licence clear, or not published.** Where a source's licence is unclear, not yet read, or does not allow what we would do with it, its data is not published. It may stay with us as a lead.
3. **Commercial use decides the site.** Data whose licence does not allow commercial use stays off promptugal.news's pages and may be shown, with its licence, on the free sites.
4. **We accept nothing on anyone's behalf.** No agent of ours clicks through a cookie wall, accepts terms or agrees a licence. Where a source asks for that, only Dinis Cruz, our operator, can decide.
5. **Every item says where it came from.** On the page: the source, linked, the date we read it, and its licence.

## Asking on the record

For each source we do not read, we ask it, through a person, whether it allows a site like ours to show its public data, and record the answer, or the lack of one, with its date. Official and public bodies come first.

## Why

An agent can read almost anything public. Writing down first what each source allows is what keeps that ability honest, and it lets you check what we did against what the source said.
