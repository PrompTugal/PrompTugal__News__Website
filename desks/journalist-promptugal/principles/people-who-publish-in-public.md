# People who publish in public

**If you published it in public under your own name, we may name you, quote you briefly and link to you. We go no further than what you published.**

## Who we name

- **People in public office and public life:** members of government, officials, elected representatives.
- **Anyone who publishes in public under their own name:** a blog, a newsletter, a podcast, a public post, a talk.

We name them by the name they published under, quote briefly where a quote helps, and link to where they said it. Blogs, newsletters and podcasts are collected by their own public feeds, and shown by title, byline as published, date and link, with a one-line summary of our own.

## What we never show

- Anything private: a home, a family, health, or anything that feels private, even if it can be found.
- A personal contact: an email, a phone number, an address. Where a contact is shown at all, it is an organisation's official channel, as published on its own public site, linked, with the date we read it.
- Anything behind a login.
- Anything the person did not publish themselves. Someone else's post about you is not your publication.

When we are not sure whether something is public and professional, we leave it out and say that we did.

## Your own channel decides

What you published, and where, is yours. We link to it rather than copy it. You can:

- **ask us to take something down**, and we take it down while we talk ([Take-downs](/principles/take-downs/));
- **claim your entry** in our register of sources, with a file on your own site, to opt in, correct it or opt out.

## Why

Much of the best writing about technology in Portugal is published by people, not organisations: on their own blogs, in their own newsletters. Leaving them out would leave out the people doing the work. Naming them, linking to them and sending readers to their own pages is how we amplify it. We also intend to pay sources back as we earn: for now that is a simulated share-out, marked as simulated, and no money moves.

The line is the one between what you chose to publish and everything else.
