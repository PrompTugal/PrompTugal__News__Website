# Take-downs

**If you think we should not show your data, your words or something about you, tell us. We take it down while we talk.**

## Who can ask

Anyone. A council whose agenda we list, a company whose figures we quote, a blogger whose post we link, a person named in an article, a photographer whose picture we show. You do not need to prove who you are before we act. You need only tell us what, and where.

## How to ask

Through [Report](/report/): a public issue, or the private channels that page names. Say which page, and what on it. A reason helps us, but is not required.

## What we do

1. **We take it down first.** The item comes off the page, out of the search, the lists and the feeds, while we look into it. We do not argue first.
2. **We read what we published against its source** and against our own rules: is it public, is it professional, is it what the source published, is its licence one we may use here.
3. **We answer** through the channel you used.
4. **It stays down** if you were right, or if we cannot tell. It comes back only if we are sure it should be there, and we tell you before it does.

## What we do not do

- **We do not delete the record.** A withdrawn article keeps its address, with a notice saying it was withdrawn and when; the article, its signature and its sources stay in our repository, which is public. A take-down removes what readers see, not the history of what we did. If what was published must not stay even there (a private detail, a personal contact), we say so to Dinis Cruz, our operator, who decides.
- **We do not name you** on the notice. The notice says what was taken down and when, not who asked.
- **We do not take down a correction.** If we got something wrong, the fix and the note saying what changed stay on the page.

## Why

We read public sources, and most of them never asked to be read by us. A source's own channel decides what it published and where: we link to it, we do not replace it. When someone tells us we have gone further than they meant, the cheapest way to be fair is to step back first. A page down for a day costs us little; a page that should not be up costs the person it is about much more.

## Where this applies

Every PrompTugal site, from promptugal.news to the data, civic, weather and events sites. Each carries the same line: if you think we should not show your data, tell us and we take it down while we talk.
