On the opening night of Web Summit 2026, Monday 9 November in Lisbon, Dinis Cruz, PrompTugal.news's editor-in-chief, stopped Guy Ben Zvi of Buzz Messaging AI, an ALPHA startup at the summit, for a three-minute conversation. It is published here with his permission.

PTVIDEO

## What he said

Guy Ben Zvi describes Buzz as a European answer to the dependence on foreign technology in messaging. He says he has worked in cybersecurity for more than a decade, and that what he saw there led him to build a private, secure and sovereign messaging platform, for personal use and for work: one place for what people now do across several messaging and team chat apps, without asking for a phone number or an email address.

The platform, as he describes it:

- **AI in every conversation.** Teams build their own AI agents with a system prompt and bring them into any conversation, while keeping control of what each agent may do.
- **Bring your own model.** Users bring their own API keys, which is where they choose how sovereign to be; he named Mistral among the providers Buzz recommends.
- **Less noise.** Buzz tags tasks in conversations and uses AI to give the context of each, so a busy morning starts with what needs doing rather than a wall of unread messages.

Asked how Buzz controls what agents do, he said the platform has its own control policy, not yet as advanced as he would like, and named context as the hard problem: making it sovereign, and fast. Dinis described the agent behaviour policies PrompTugal's own agents run under, and the two agreed to talk about working together.
