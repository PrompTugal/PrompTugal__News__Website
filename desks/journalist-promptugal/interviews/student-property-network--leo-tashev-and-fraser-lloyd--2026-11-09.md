At Night Summit, Web Summit 2026's opening night in Lisbon, on Monday 9 November, Indi Gupte, an editor of PrompTugal.news, spoke to Leo Tashev and Fraser Lloyd, the co-founders of [Student Property Network](https://studentpropertynetwork.com/about), for just under three minutes. Leo Tashev's Web Summit 2026 badge, an attendee's, names him and Student Property Network.

PTVIDEO

## What they said

**The problem.** The founders describe the UK graduate job market as the hardest it has ever been. The figure they give is that, on average, one applicant in 140 is accepted for a graduate role: to get the job, a student has to beat 139 others.

**The app.** Student Property Network puts what a student aiming for a career in real estate and the built environment needs in one place: internships, placements, graduate opportunities and events. The founders say they work with 27 universities and their real estate societies, bringing events to students to help them move from education to employment.

**The agent.** Asked what their AI agents do, Leo handed over to Fraser, "because he's technical". Fraser explained that they collect every job they can find with their own scrapers, written in Python, into a database, and a large language model decides whether each job is accepted onto the list. If it went wrong, he said, it would start accepting bad jobs and could send students to dodgy companies. The bigger risk, in his view, is in the coding: an AI coding agent that went astray could download dodgy things or take more access than it needs while building the platform.

**Agent behaviour policies.** Indi then described agent behaviour policies, the approach of RiskMandate: boundaries for what an agent can do, should do and must never do, written for a team's own agents and the way it deploys them, which could stop an agent like theirs accepting the wrong jobs.

- **The figures are theirs.** "One in 140" and "27 universities" are what the founders said on camera; PrompTugal.news has not checked them. Their website, read on 10 November 2026, says "20+" universities and "300+" student members.
- **Who said which line.** The machine transcript does not tell the two founders apart reliably; where this page names a speaker, it is from what was said on camera (Leo handing the question to Fraser).
