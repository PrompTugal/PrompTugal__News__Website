# Sebrae's announcement of the selection, 30 September 2026

**What it is.** The news item from Sebrae's own agency announcing the national selection, dated 30 September 2026, 09:40.

**What it says.**
- 100 Brazilian startups were chosen; Sebrae pays flights and travel insurance for the 40 ranked highest.
- Cristina Mieko, Head of Startups at Sebrae, expects about 300 Brazilian startups in the delegation the Sebrae system leads.
- Twelve states organise missions of their own; hubs, incubators and accelerators can each put forward up to five startups.
- One startup is named: Vox EPower, from Nova Iguaçu (Rio de Janeiro), which works on charging for electric vehicles.
- "Confira a lista completa dos selecionados" links to ApexBrasil's results page.

**What it does not say.** The names of the 100 (the link goes to a page of tax numbers), or which 12 states run missions.

**How it fits with the others.** Its 100 is the rules' "up to 100". Its about 300 counts a wider group (the national selection, the states' missions and the hubs), so it does not contradict the 100; it is the largest of the five public counts for 2026.
