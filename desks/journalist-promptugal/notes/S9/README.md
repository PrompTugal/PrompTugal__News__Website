**What the four say together.** The rules plan a delegation of up to 100 startups and set 28 August 2026 as the day it is formalised. The results page shows 188 companies with their status on exactly that day, by tax number only, and has not changed since. Sebrae's announcement of 30 September names the selection as 100 but links to that page instead of naming them. Lusa's report of 4 November, from ApexBrasil, gives about 200 companies. None of the four names the delegation.
