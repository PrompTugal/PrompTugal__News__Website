# Lusa's report of 4 November 2026, as carried by Executive Digest

**What it is.** The Portuguese news agency's report of ApexBrasil's announcement, published on 4 November 2026. ApexBrasil's own news item asks for a login, so this is the nearest public text of what ApexBrasil said.

**What it says.**
- About 200 Brazilian companies take part, looking for investment and partners in Europe.
- Their base is a Brazil Pavilion of 324 square metres in the MEO Arena, with investor meetings, business presentations and debates from 10 to 12 November.
- Brazilian companies have raised about 700 million dollars (623 million euros) at earlier editions through contacts and negotiations begun there, "segundo a organização".

**What it does not say.** Which companies, how the 200 are counted, or how the 700 million dollars was measured.

**How it fits with the others.** About 200 is between the 188 approved in August and the 239 of ApexBrasil's own parts as reported by IOL a day later (99 + 90 + 50). It is ApexBrasil's figure, not an independent count, and the 700 million dollars has not been checked by anyone we have read.
