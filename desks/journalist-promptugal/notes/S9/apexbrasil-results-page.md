# ApexBrasil's results page for the mission's registration

**What it is.** The public page of the mission's registration on ApexBrasil's events system. Sebrae's announcement of 30 September links to it as "the full list of those selected".

**What it says.** A table headed "Lista das empresas APROVADAS com status em 28/8/2026", with two columns, the company's tax number (CNPJ) and its status. 188 rows: 105 confirmed, 33 on the waiting list, 26 awaiting confirmation and 24 withdrawn.

**What it does not say.** Any company's name, sector, state or product. A tax number can be looked up in Brazil's public registry, but PrompTugal.news has not done so and does not publish the numbers: a list of names built that way would be ours, not the organisers', and it would still show only the status of 28 August.

**How it fits with the others.** 28 August 2026 is the day the rules set for the delegation's formalisation; the page was not updated after it, though Sebrae announced the selection on 30 September. 188 is more than the 100 of the selection and less than the about 200 of 4 November; 105 confirmed is close to the 100.
