# ApexBrasil's rules for the mission (PDF, in Portuguese)

**What it is.** The call for applications and the rules of the national startup mission, published by ApexBrasil with Sebrae. It is the most detailed public document about the mission.

**What it says.**
- **Last year:** in 2025, Germany and Brazil brought the two largest delegations to Web Summit, and Brazil's had 380 companies, 247 of them startups and innovative companies supported by ApexBrasil, Sebrae Startups and Serpro.
- **Who it is for:** Brazilian technology startups with scalable solutions, a product of their own ready to sell, and the potential to open in Portugal or elsewhere in Europe.
- **How many:** up to 100 startups, each of which must be accepted into Web Summit's startup programme; places go in ranking order, confirmed by a signed agreement.
- **What it costs the startup:** a fee of 10% of the ticket, which ApexBrasil buys. The table gives 100, 200 and 300 euros for the ALPHA, BETA and GROWTH groups.
- **The timeline:** applications until 26 July 2026; **the delegation formalised on 28 August 2026**; a welcome webinar on 3 September; training from 4 September to 6 November; a farewell webinar on 5 November; the mission from 8 to 13 November; Web Summit "10 a 12/11/2026"; results followed up in November 2026 and June 2027.
- **How startups are chosen:** five criteria that eliminate (the profile, a product of their own, a tax number and operations in Brazil, not being a consultancy or agency, revenue or investment already) and then points for maturity, revenue, investment received, grants, standardisation of the product and more, up to criterion 16 (an active record on Sebrae's platform).
- **Penalties:** a warning, loss of eligibility or a fine for withdrawing after confirming, not attending or not filling in the required forms.

**What it does not say.** Who was chosen. The cover says 9 to 13 November; the timeline says the mission is 8 to 13 November and Web Summit 10 to 12 November (Web Summit itself says 9 to 12).

**How it fits with the others.** The 28 August "formalisation" is the date on which ApexBrasil's results page froze (see that note). "Up to 100" matches Sebrae's 100 selected.
