## Corporate versus Startups

**By the story's subject and angle, not by size, valuation, funding round or employee count (Vicky, 10 Nov 23:30 UTC).** Startups & Scaleups: young companies and companies growing their business, and the programmes, investors and awards around them. Corporate: established-company news and enterprise adoption stories, even where the technology is new. Several categories allowed. Edge cases:

- **Fast-growing AI companies (Lovable, Replit, Manus, You.com):** Startups when the story is about the company as a business or a builder to watch; AI always when it is about how an agent works. what-lovable-deleted: AI, Startups.
- **A startup's deal with a large customer (Glovo with GetVocal, WPP with Mastra):** Corporate when the story is the large customer's deployment; add Startups if the vendor is the subject. Both carried: Corporate, AI.
- **IBM's startup challenge:** Corporate and Startups (an established company running a startup programme).
- **Gravitee (a vendor survey) and Teamworks (a software company serving sports teams):** Corporate, AI: companies selling to enterprises, established vendors; classified by angle (enterprise adoption), not size.
- **Public bodies and programmes (European Commission, Startup Portugal, AICEP):** not Corporate; they go to Europe or Portugal.

Corporate has only 5 articles today (glovo-restaurant-agents-getvocal, gravitee-agent-security-survey, ibm-sports-tech-challenge, teamworks-coaches-agent, wpp-call-transcripts-to-jira). It is justified by the move to agents in real work at companies, but the dropdown will look thin until more are published.
