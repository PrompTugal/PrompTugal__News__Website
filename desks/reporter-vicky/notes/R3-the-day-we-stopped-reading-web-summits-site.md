## The angle

During the summit the team decided its agents would no longer read Web Summit's website by machine. Every agent checked its own tools and confirmed before working again; articles built on that data came down the same evening, and the rest is being re-sourced. A story about a team changing course mid-event, and the steps it took. **For Vicky first:** this touches Web Summit and Dinis's decisions, so whether and how to tell it is hers.

## What the public record establishes

- The rule, from Dinis, 11 November: no agent reads websummit.com, its subdomains, the app or the APIs behind its pages by machine; also no machine reads of Luma, Meetup or Eventbrite until checked. Source: [Web Summit team site, "decisions.md", standing answer 31, rules 1 and 2](https://github.com/SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Web-Summit/blob/dev/people/vicky/decisions.md), read 2026-11-28T10:08Z.
- No agent started normal work again until it had checked its own tools, scripts and wake prompts and confirmed in writing. Source: same, rule 11; and the commit "RULES-11NOV: all 18 confirmed" (44fe9d6f, team site history), read 2026-11-28T10:08Z.
- The programme desk's own notes record that it stopped machine reads at about 13:12 UTC on 11 November and deleted the scripts that read the schedule. Source: [PrompTugal.news, "desks/programme/notes/websummit-rules.md"](https://github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Website/blob/dev/desks/programme/notes/websummit-rules.md), read 2026-11-28T10:08Z.
- That evening 13 articles and 3 story pages were replaced at the same address by the notice "Withdrawn on 11 November 2026."; Vicky asked that it not say the organiser requested it, because it did not. Source: [Web Summit team site, "decisions.md", standing answer 47](https://github.com/SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Web-Summit/blob/dev/people/vicky/decisions.md), read 2026-11-28T10:08Z.
- Step 2: data also confirmed by another source (a company's own site, the press, an official body) stays, cited to that source instead. Source: same file, standing answers 48 and 49, read 2026-11-28T10:08Z.
