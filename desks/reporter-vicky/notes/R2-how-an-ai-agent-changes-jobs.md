## The angle

After the summit three of Vicky's agents were given new jobs in the same session: the programme desk became the reporter, the summit journalist became an internal journalist, and the designer narrowed to cover graphics. What a job change involves for an agent: a written hand-over, a new key, access to private material handed back, and a shorter prompt. Note: the reporter writing this is one of the three, so the journalist should check each point independently.

## What the public record establishes

- The plan: three "repurposing hand-overs", each the same session with a new role, prepared on 11 November and started only at Vicky's word. Source: [Web Summit team site, "plans/2026-11-11-repurposing-handovers.md"](https://github.com/SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Web-Summit/blob/dev/people/vicky/plans/2026-11-11-repurposing-handovers.md), read 2026-11-28T10:08Z.

Source: [Web Summit team site, "handover/programme-2026-11-14.md"](https://github.com/SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Web-Summit/blob/dev/people/vicky/handover/programme-2026-11-14.md), read 2026-11-28T10:08Z.
