## The angle

On 25 November every one of Vicky's agents stopped at once on "You've hit your weekly limit". The team cut its own schedule the next day and set a warning before it happens again. A plain story about the cost of running a newsroom with agents: what the limit is, what stopped, and what changed.

## What the public record establishes

- On 25 November every agent stopped on "You've hit your weekly limit", after the team accounts moved from Premium to Standard. Source: [Web Summit team site, "decisions.md", standing answer 103](https://github.com/SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Web-Summit/blob/dev/people/vicky/decisions.md), read 2026-11-28T10:08Z.
- Vicky's choice on 26 November, about 09:50 UTC: the research and curation loop twice a day instead of three times (researcher 07:15 and 15:15 UTC, curator 07:35 and 15:35), the dashboard's run paused, the conductor's brief at 08:45 and its afternoon check at 17:00; journalists and the reviewer still run on demand. Same source, read 2026-11-28T10:08Z.
- A warning at about 70% of the week's allowance, so Vicky can cut back further; the source notes that no agent can read the account's own usage page, so the conductor estimates. Same source, read 2026-11-28T10:08Z.
- An earlier rule (12 November) had the conductor warn at about 90%; the source says the service gives a state, not a percentage, so that warning only came when the service raised it. Source: [Web Summit team site, "decisions.md", standing answer 53](https://github.com/SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Web-Summit/blob/dev/people/vicky/decisions.md), read 2026-11-28T10:08Z.
- Each agent logs the tokens each run used, numbers only, in a public ledger. Source: [Web Summit team site, "decisions.md", standing answer 54](https://github.com/SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Web-Summit/blob/dev/people/vicky/decisions.md) and the files in [people/vicky/ledger/](https://github.com/SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Web-Summit/tree/dev/people/vicky/ledger), read 2026-11-28T10:08Z.
