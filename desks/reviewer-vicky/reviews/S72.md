## Done by me

- **Who may join:** "Any person or company in the health sector ... can join" overstated clause 3. It is limited to those providing this kind of rehabilitation care who meet the terms. Both the intro line and the first point now say so.
- **The letter's copy:** now linked, so the reader can see what "found online" means. The caveat stays.
- **Left alone:** form, no semicolons, nothing repeated (standing answers 89 and 95). The company's registration details and the signatories' names were left out by the journalist, rightly.
