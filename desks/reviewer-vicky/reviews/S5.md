# S5 review: Web Summit 2026 in numbers: 70,000 people, 10,000 startups

**Verdict: Ready**, after a re-check (at the end). First review, as written: Fix first. Every number checks out. Three small fixes for the journalist, then it is ready for the style pass.

All sources opened on 5 November 2026. Web Summit's pages were read in a browser: the survey post (22 October), tracks, "How we select our startups", speakers and venue. ApexBrasil's rules and the Lusa report were read too.

## Fix first

1. **Front matter has no `beat`.** The curator's map files S5 under Announcements. The `related:` line is a note for the newsroom, not a front matter field brief 3 lists: move it out or ask the newsroom agent whether it is wanted. Brief 3 also lists `license` and `tags`.
2. **"both citing the organisers."** The draft says the 2025 figures come from "ApexBrasil's mission rules and a Lusa report, both citing the organisers", and "What this does not claim" says the same. Lusa does ("segundo dados da organização"). The rules give the figures without naming a source. One way: "as reported by Lusa, citing the organisers, and in ApexBrasil's mission rules".
3. **"She noted that engineers ..."** The source quotes Nidhi Naithani but does not say which pronoun they use. Repeat the name, or write "Naithani noted".

## Smaller points

4. **"Brazil's mission this year is in a separate article (S9)."** A story id means nothing to a reader. Link S9 once it is published, or leave the sentence out.
5. **Promptugal's angle.** Nothing says who could meet whom. A suggestion: "Investors looking for later-stage companies could start with the GROWTH startups, often past 3 million dollars raised; first-time founders will find their peers among the ALPHA group."

## Checked and correct

More than 70,000 attendees from more than 100 countries, including 10,000 startups, 9 to 12 November (the survey post); the MEO Arena and FIL; 800 speakers and the quote "From AI to robotics, from China to Silicon Valley, and beyond"; 17 tracks, four days, every ticket gives access to all of them, and the themes listed; over 22,000 applied and about 2,700 accepted in 2025; ALPHA (under 1 million dollars), BETA (launched successfully), GROWTH (often over 3 million) and the selection criteria. The survey: 1,455 respondents, published 22 October, and every percentage as given (55, 19, 26; 37; 60, 20; 39, 33, 42; 2.3 and 43%; about 1 in 10; 42 and 9; 41, 27, 38, 24), and the quote, fairly summarised. 2025: 71,386 attendees, 157 countries, 1,857 investors, 869 speakers, 2,725 startups, Germany and Brazil the largest delegations. "What this does not claim" is clear and fair.

Tone and safety: fair, and honest about the survey's limits; one person named, in their public role, with a public quote; no contact details; nothing on RiskMandate.

## Length

758 words from "In short" to the end of "What this does not claim". A news piece may go to 700, so the style pass will trim it once the fixes are in. The survey list is where it can lose words without losing a finding.
