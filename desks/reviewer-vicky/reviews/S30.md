- **Nice Deer:** "insurers" for "payers" and "patients" for "beneficiaries" are loose but acceptable.
- **The image:** its title ("Eleven health startups / At Web Summit 2026, by the problem they tackle") reads as if these were all the health startups there; add that they are from page 1 of Web Summit's featured list. Its labels and counts (4, 3, 2, 2) match the article.
