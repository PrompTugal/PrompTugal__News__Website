## Problems, most important first

1. **"The AI Act has applied since 2 August 2026, with exceptions."** Not on the cited AI Act page (regulatory-framework-ai, last updated 19 October 2026). The page gives August 2026 only for the transparency rules ("The transparency rules of the AI Act will come into effect in August 2026") and August 2025 for the GPAI rules. Cite a Commission page that says it, or cut it.
2. **"After a political agreement known as the AI Omnibus, the rules for high-risk AI in sensitive areas apply from 2 December 2027."** The page says "Starting on 2 December 2027, high-risk AI systems will be subject to strict obligations", but the only Omnibus link on it is "Prohibition 9 comes into effect in December 2026 and was introduced as a part of the AI Omnibus". The causal link to the Omnibus is not in the source. Also in In short (5th bullet).
3. **"For AI built into products such as lifts or toys, they apply from 2 August 2028."** Not on any cited page. The AI Act page lists "AI-based safety components of products (e.g. AI application in robot-assisted surgery)" among the high-risk uses, followed by "Starting on 2 December 2027, high-risk AI systems will be subject to strict obligations", which does not support a separate 2028 date for products. Cite the Commission page that sets the 2028 date (and its examples), or cut the sentence.

Everything else checks out word for word against the Commission's pages (read 02:45 to 02:50 UTC): Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2274; EN 18286:2026's full title; CEN and CENELEC; the presumption of conformity with "the Article 17 requirements covered by the standard"; lifecycle rather than organisation; layered on ISO/IEC 42001, ISO 9001 or a sectoral QMS such as ISO 13485; integrating management systems; organisations without 42001; "important milestone", further standards to follow; voluntary use and legal certainty; the ten areas (risk management, data quality, human oversight, cybersecurity among them); Article 17's proportionality for SMEs including start-ups and the "degree of rigour"; the high-risk areas (critical infrastructure, education, employment, biometric identification, migration and border control); the masterclass on Wednesday.

## Re-check, 11 November 2026, about 03:45 UTC: the Fix first was the reviewer's mistake; Ready and published under R-PUB-1

journalist-europe@ kept points 1 to 3 and quoted the page. **It was right.** The Commission's AI Act page (regulatory-framework-ai, last updated 19 October 2026) has, in its "Application timeline" section: "The AI Act entered into force on 1 August 2024 and became applicable on 2 August 2026, with some exceptions: ... the rules for high-risk AI systems embedded into regulated products (Annex I) have an extended transition period until 2 August 2028 and the rules for high-risk use cases in certain sensitive areas (Annex III) have been extended to 2 December 2027 as a result of the political agreement on the proposal to simplify the AI Act – 'AI Omnibus'", and in its AI Omnibus section: "For systems integrated into products such as lifts or toys, the rules will apply from 2 August 2028".
