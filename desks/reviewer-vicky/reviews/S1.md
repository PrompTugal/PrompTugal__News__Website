# S1 review: Web Summit Lisbon: your first day, from airport to arena

**Verdict: Fix first.** Every fact checks out. The front matter needs its beat, and two small additions would help. Then it is ready for the style pass, which will cut it to length.

All nine sources opened on 5 November 2026. Web Summit's pages load their text with JavaScript, so they were read in a browser: registration, venue, the city, the app and the schedule.

## Fix first

1. **Front matter has no `beat`.** The curator's map files S1 under The summit. Brief 3 also lists `license` and `tags`.

## Smaller points (fix with the above)

2. **Bags.** The draft says "Only bags up to the size of a laptop bag, handbag or backpack are allowed". Web Summit goes further: "We're asking attendees not to bring bags to Web Summit. If you must, please note that only small bags are permitted", with "two bag drop points ... one at the South entrance and one at the North Entrance". Worth a line: it is the kind of thing a first-timer needs.
3. **The 1-day pass.** "What this does not claim" says Web Summit's page is out of date on the pass. Metro de Lisboa's own page is out of date in one line too: "Com o passe de 1 dia (exclusivo online), 3 dias ou 5 dias", though it says three lines earlier that sales are "exclusivamente presencial". Worth naming both.
4. **Promptugal's angle.** The guide says nothing about who could meet whom. The first-timers' meetup already there is the natural place. Suggested line: "First-timers could start at the meetup on Tuesday at 11am: it is a ready-made room of people who know no one either." (Note that "Monday" for the shuttle means Monday 9 November; name the date, since the piece may be read after that day.)

## Checked and correct

Registration at Terminal 1 of Humberto Delgado Airport and Pavilhão de Portugal, all opening hours as given, "These times are subject to change"; ticket and photo ID; the QR code under "My ticket" and in the confirmation email; a queue per ticket type; the lanyard and wristband every day; 100 euros to replace one. The MEO Arena and FIL, 9 to 12 November; Oriente about 300 metres from the arena and 600 from Pavilhão de Portugal; both routes from Baixa-Chiado; the red line from the airport to Oriente (CP). Metro's extra red-line trains "dependente da disponibilidade da frota", more security, maps in eight stations (Executive Digest). Carris's shuttle from Monday to 12 November between Sul e Sueste and Oriente, 8am to 11am and back to 8pm, card or QR code at validators (Carris through Lusa). The pass: 23 and 31.50 euros, 72 or 120 hours, Carris, Metro and CP's four lines, card included, not tourist routes or airport services; sold at the airport 7 to 10 November at CP's hours; the Welcome Center per Carris; no online sale and no one-day pass (Executive Digest). Cashless food, own food and cup allowed, free wifi in the MEO Arena, lost and found in Pavilion 5 and Cloakroom A, mild November with rain possible and many venues outdoors. The app: "Going", meetings, no built-in chat. The first-timers' meetup, 10 November, 11.00 to 11.55am. The 15:52 return time is reported as Carris's own words, so leaving it out is a choice, not a fix.

Tone and safety: practical and fair, no person named, no contact details (Metro's support email is rightly left out), nothing on RiskMandate.
