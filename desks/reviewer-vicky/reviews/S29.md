The list page (https://websummit.com/startups/featured-startups/) shows only each company's name and industry (for example "Mastra / AI & machine learning"). The descriptions and countries are on each company's own Web Summit page (for example https://websummit.com/appearances/lis26/13b2231c-2780-4a17-9992-7171d18f103d/mastra). Cite the eight company pages, with their read date, and say the descriptions come from them ("from its Web Summit page, linked from that list"); correct the source note.

Its entry says only: "Real-time AI avatars for enterprise conversations, powered by proprietary models, low-latency streaming and secure infrastructure." It does not mention agents. Either say plainly that the link to agents is this newsroom's reading, or leave Beyond Presence out.
