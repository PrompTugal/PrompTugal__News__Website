# S67 review: Portugal's Center for Responsible AI: the money, partners and goals

**Verdict: Fix first**, for problems of fact in who receives the money and who the partners are (standing answer 17). All three sources were read on 16 November 2026. Everything below the list of fixes holds and can stay.

## Fix first

1. **INESC-ID is not "the beneficiary, the body that receives the funding".**
   - The State's portal names IAPMEI as the intermediate beneficiary, with 51.49 million euros of contracted financing.
   - It then lists many beneficiaries, each with assigned funding. The largest are Unbabel (14.83 million), Sword Health (9.26 million), Priberam (3.48 million) and INESC-ID (3.17 million).
   - The PDF you cite is INESC-ID's own beneficiary sheet: "ENTIDADE BENEFICIÁRIA INESC-ID ... INVESTIMENTO DO BENEFICIÁRIO 3.168.360,91€".
   - Fix: describe INESC-ID as one of the beneficiaries, and the PDF as INESC-ID's project sheet, not "the programme's own sheet". Rename it throughout ("INESC-ID's sheet").
2. **"The sheet gives no partner list, so only these two names, and Unbabel below, can be given."**
   - The portal's Beneficiaries tab does list the partners and their funding.
   - Fix: give the largest beneficiaries from the portal, with their assigned funding, or say the portal lists them. Then drop the sentence.
3. **The money section.**
   - With 1 and 2 fixed, say what each figure is: 51.49 million euros contracted through IAPMEI under the Recovery and Resilience Plan (45.67 million paid), and 77.7 million euros as the "investimento total da agenda" on INESC-ID's sheet.
   - "Neither source says how the two relate" can stay.
4. **Goal 5.** The sheet says "líder mundial" (world leader). The draft has "a leader". Fix: "a world leader in responsible AI principles and policy".
5. **"18 products were in development"** is Diário de Notícias's own sentence, not the founder's quote. Attribute it to the paper: "Diário de Notícias reported 18 products in development". The 145 jobs and about 50 doctorates and master's degrees are his, as written.

## Wording (I will do these at the re-check, or you can)

- **"The sheet sets five":** the sheet lists five "among others" ("e, entre outros, 5."). Write "lists five, among others".
- **Closing line:** cut "Companies working on AI in health and language could watch what the programme's products turn into." It is padding (standing answer 92).
- **Front matter:** tags in the site's comma form. I will move the AI connection into the front matter at publishing (standing answer 91). It is recorded well in your notes.
