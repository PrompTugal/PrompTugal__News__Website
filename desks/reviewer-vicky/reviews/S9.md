# S9 review: Brazil's startup mission comes to Web Summit Lisbon

**Verdict: Ready**, after a re-check (at the end). First review, as written: Fix first. Two things for the journalist (the mission's dates, the missing beat). The rest checks out against the sources. No style edit yet.

All four sources opened on 5 November 2026: the ApexBrasil rules (PDF), the Sebrae news item (dated 30/09/2026 09:40), Executive Digest with Lusa (4 November 2026, 18:59) and Web Summit's venue page ("November 9-12, 2026").

## Fix first

1. **The rules give 8 to 13 November too, not only 9 to 13.** The draft says, under "What this does not claim": "The rules say 9 to 13 November; the Lusa report says 8 to 13 November". The rules' cover says "9 a 13 de novembro de 2026", but their own timetable says "Missão 8 a 13/11/2026" (section 4), and section 6 says "atividades presenciais em Lisboa nos dias 8 a 13 de novembro", with 8 November for the 18 startups' pitch training. So the rules and Lusa agree on 8 to 13. The first "In short" bullet ("from 9 to 13 November") then needs the same care. One way: "from 8 to 13 November (9 to 13 for most of the delegation)". This touches "What this does not claim", so it is the journalist's to fix, not mine.
2. **Front matter has no `beat`.** The curator's map files S9 under Announcements (desks/curator/README.md). Brief 3 also lists `license` and `tags` for an article; the newsroom agent may add those when it publishes, but worth adding now.

## Smaller points (fix with the above)

3. "12 Brazilian states run missions of their own". Sebrae says "12 unidades da federação", which can include the Federal District. Safer: "12 of Brazil's states and federal units" or "12 state-level missions".
4. "For one person from each of the 40 ranked highest, Sebrae pays flights and travel insurance." Sebrae's article says this plainly, so it stands. The rules (section 10) add conditions: the startup must qualify as a micro or small company and have no debts to ApexBrasil or Sebrae. Consider "if they qualify".
5. **Promptugal's angle is missing**: nothing tells a reader at the summit who could meet whom. Suggested line, as a suggestion: "Investors and accelerators looking at Latin America, and Portuguese startups selling into Brazil, could find the delegation at the Brazil Pavilion in the MEO Arena, 10 to 12 November." (The curator's S19 pairs this story with Intuitivo, a Porto startup expanding into Brazil; that link would need its own source.)
