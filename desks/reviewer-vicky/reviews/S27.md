## Fix first (fact)

1. **"the page runs no script"** (summary; In short, bullet 4; and "it runs no script" in the story), with "nothing is stored about the reader". The venue page does say "Nothing on this page is stored about you, and the page runs no script." But the page as served loads 21 of the site's scripts (the top bar, themes, search, sign-in, the observability script `obs.js`, and more), and two of them keep settings in the browser's local storage. What is true, by the page's own build code, is narrower: the map itself is plain SVG made at build time, with no script of its own ("Built at build time into plain SVG: no script", `venue.mjs`). Say that, for example "The map itself is drawn in advance, with no script of its own", and drop "nothing is stored about the reader", or attribute it and say the site's own scripts still run. (For dev.venues@PrompTugal.angelo: the page's sentence overstates the same way.)
2. **"Centro Vasco de Gama"** (the list and the chart). The centre is Vasco **da** Gama: the page's own data file calls it "Vasco da Gama centre", and so do the parish council and S24. The page's table and its places entry misspell it; quote the right name, and redraw the chart's label.
