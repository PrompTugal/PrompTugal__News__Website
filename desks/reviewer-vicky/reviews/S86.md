## Fix first, most important first

1. **Anthropic, Priority Tier is legacy and no longer sold.**
   - The draft says: "the Priority Tier, open only to organisations with a capacity commitment, 'targets 99.5 percent uptime'". In short: "Its 99.5% target is for one paid tier."
   - The page (https://docs.claude.com/en/api/service-tiers, read 26 Nov) says, at the top: "Service tiers are legacy. Priority Tier capacity commitments are no longer available for purchase. Organizations with an existing commitment can continue to use Priority Tier through their contract end date". It also lists Priority Tier as "Available only to organizations with an existing capacity commitment".
   - A reader would take the 99.5% target as something they can buy. Say that the tier is legacy, closed to new buyers and kept only for existing commitments to their end date, in the story and in "In short".
2. **Google, 99.5% is not for every model.**
   - The draft says: "The page sets 99.5% a month for two Gemini methods".
   - The page (https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/sla) marks the 99.5% with an asterisk: "When used with the models designated for shorter availability listed in the table described in the applicable Documentation, the SLO's Uptime Percentage will be 95%."
   - Add that some models, which Google's documentation designates, have a 95% objective instead.
3. **GitLab, its hosted service only.**
   - The page (https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/engineering/infrastructure-platforms/service-level-agreement/) says: "applies to SaaS Software purchased or renewed as Ultimate tier on or after 2026-01-01".
   - The draft has "its Ultimate tier, bought or renewed from 1 January 2026". Say it is GitLab's hosted (SaaS) service, not self-managed GitLab.
4. **AWS, efforts rather than a guarantee.**
   - The draft says: "makes two promises".
   - Both levels on the page (https://aws.amazon.com/compute/sla/) are worded "AWS will use commercially reasonable efforts to make ... available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.99%" (and "at least 99.5%" for a single instance), with credits when missed.
   - Credit the wording to AWS, for example "AWS says it will use commercially reasonable efforts to meet two levels".
