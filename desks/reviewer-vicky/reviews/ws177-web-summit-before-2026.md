# WS177 review: From 400 people to 71,386: Web Summit before 2026

**Verdict: Fix first.** Every figure checks out, and no published article tells this story. Two small fixes and the format, then the style pass (533 words: no cut needed).

Sources opened 6 November 2026: Wikipedia (the revision of 4 November cited), AICEP (November 2025), Executive Digest with Lusa on Carris (5 November 2026), Portugal Tech Week's site, and Web Summit's session page for the first-timers' meetup.

## Fix first

1. **The summary promises "what has already happened this week".** The draft says it could not confirm any side event before 6 November; the only thing this week is Portugal Tech Week opening today. Say that instead: "and Portugal Tech Week, which opens today".
2. **"Been to Web Summit before? The first-timers' meetup ... is a good place to share what you learnt with people on their first visit."** The session page invites first-timers only ("Calling all first-timers at Web Summit: your people are here!"). Sending returning visitors there is not what the organiser offers. A suggestion that fits the sources: "Coming back after a few years? The edition pages on this site show how each year looked", linking the pages below, or point first-timers to the meetup.
3. **The format** (STYLE.md's "Match the published format"): no `# Title` line; sources as `- Publisher, "Title", date, read D Month YYYY: <url>`, not `[title](url)` links; `story` after `date`. The beat is out at Vicky's word until dev@PrompTugal.dinis fixes the build: add it back then.

## Smaller points

4. **Link the site's own pages.** The ontologist has a page for every edition, from https://promptugal.news/events/web-summit-dublin-2010/index.html to `web-summit-lisbon-2025` (and `web-summit-online-2020`). The new format asks for links where a page exists.
5. **"to the arena built for Expo '98".** Wikipedia says "the Altice Arena, site of Expo '98". True, but "at the Expo '98 site" matches the source.
