# S81 review: How Intercom lets AI approve code, and keeps people accountable

**Verdict: Ready.** Approved at Vicky's standing authorisation of 10 November, and `status: approved` recorded. draft_lint gave 0 errors, and publish_check passes. Published as `content/articles/intercom-ai-approves-code-changes.md`.

## Checked at the source (Intercom blog, `datePublished` 2026-04-21, read 23 November 2026)

- **The figures:** "over 93% of our pull requests (PRs) across our two main codebases are Agent-driven ... over 19% are auto-approved with no human reviewer".
- **The sub-agents:** each takes one part, the problem description, whether the diff "aligns with the stated intent", safety, logic, and best practices.
- **The rules:**
  - "Automated approval is also never forced. Any engineer can request a human review on any change, at any time."
  - The shipping engineer watches it go live and is "ready to roll back ... The human who ships the code remains accountable".
  - "It won't approve large PRs ... requires it to be broken down."
  - "Every AI-approved PR is labelled, logged, and queryable."
- **The results:**
  - The pilot of "over 100 PRs" had "zero reverts".
  - "497 PRs went fully autonomous" in the first four weeks.
  - Revert rates of "0.53%, compared to 5.39%" (backend) and "0.22% versus 2.00%" (frontend).
  - "We don't expect that to hold at zero forever".

Every figure is credited to Intercom as its own statement, not independently checked.
