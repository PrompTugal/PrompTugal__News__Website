## Checked at the sources (read 19 November 2026)

- **Aviso n.º 3864/2026/2** (DR 2.ª série n.º 37, 23 February 2026):
  - **Price:** "o valor base a remunerar pelo período de 12 (doze) meses por utente tratado é de 200,00 €". All sessions for one or more body areas, "bem como sessões ilimitadas durante o período de 12 (doze) meses". A new referral for another area pays 100,00 €. Payment is "duodécimo proporcional" if treatment stops.
  - **Who qualifies:** patients referred by their doctor with the digital literacy to use the means on their own. Annex III's seven groups: chronic and acute musculoskeletal, pelvic floor, cardiorespiratory, neurological, cognitive and neurobehavioural, speech therapy.
  - **Provider duties (clause 9):** sessions "24h por dia, com uma equipa clínica disponível das 08h às 20h, todos os dias úteis", and the device delivered "no prazo de 5 (cinco) dias úteis após contacto do utente".
  - **Device and quality:** class IIa (clause 4), a supervising specialist in physical and rehabilitation medicine (clause 19), and a documented quality system (SGQ).
  - **Data (clause 6):** patient data is the property of the health unit or the patient. Training AI with it is "expressamente proibida ... exceto se" the patient opts in, and once authorised, "anonimização irreversível antes do processamento".
  - **Clause 21:** final validation by the patient's health-service physiatrist, "ainda que baseados em dados recolhidos pelo sistema de IA"; "ferramenta de apoio à decisão"; "prevalece a avaliação clínica humana".
  - **Clause 26:** 12 months, renewed unless a party objects 60 days before the end.
- **Despacho n.º 1573/2026** (DR 2.ª série n.º 27, 9 February 2026): the price table "Programa inicial de tratamento (12 meses) 200,00" and "Programas subsequentes de tratamento (12 meses) 100,00", with no currency printed.
- **Observador** (18 June 2026) and **portugal.gov.pt**: as checked for S69. The 200 euros fixed for a year is from a Sword vice-president, free to patients, and the conditions named at launch.
