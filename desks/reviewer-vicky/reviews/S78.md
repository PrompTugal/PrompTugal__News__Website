## Checked at the source (William Fry, `datePublished` 2026-05-12, read 21 November 2026)

- **What it reads:** the provisional agreement "in the early hours of 7 May 2026 ... subject to formal endorsement ... before adoption". The note says so in its intro and caveat, as the task asks.
- **Why the delay:** "operational rather than political". Standards are "not expected to be available in their entirety until late 2026", and authorities' designation "has lagged ... in a number of Member States".
- **Products:** only machinery leaves. "medical devices, in-vitro diagnostic medical devices, toys and the other Section A categories remain". The Commission is to specify the fit "by delegated act and within a period running to August 2027".
- **Marking:** Article 50(2) marking "from 2 December 2026", and the Code of Practice "itself still in draft".
- **Image and video tools:** "expected to evidence design-stage and deployment-stage controls ... capable of preventing or mitigating reasonably foreseeable misuse for the prohibited purposes".
- **Registration:** high-risk systems in the EU database "even if the provider considers the system to be exempt".
- **Sandboxes:** "The deadline ... to establish national AI regulatory sandboxes is postponed".

## Done by me

- **Image and video tools:** "controls against misuse" became "against foreseeable misuse to make the content the new ban covers". The firm's controls are against misuse "for the prohibited purposes", meaning the new ban that S77 describes, not misuse in general.
- **Left alone:** everything else, including the firm's reading attributed once in the intro, the May caveat, and the journalist's choice not to give the ban's dates.
