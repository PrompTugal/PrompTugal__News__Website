# S64 review: AMALIA and ANIA: what Portugal says about its own AI plans

**Verdict: Ready.** Published under R-PUB-1 on 16 November 2026 as `content/articles/amalia-and-ania-portugal-ai-plans.md`.

## Checked at the sources (all read 16 November 2026)

- **digital.gov.pt, ANIA page** (updated 9 January 2026): Resolution No. 2/2026, the 2026 to 2030 action plan, "at the forefront of AI in Europe", the four areas, 32 initiatives and who they involve, in force on publication. No budget given.
- **digital.gov.pt, AMALIA explainer** (2 July 2026):
  - the base version in September 2025 and the final version on 1 July 2026;
  - "first ... designed specifically for European Portuguese";
  - multimodal (text, voice, images, video), free and open use;
  - about 60 researchers, seven academic partners;
  - public administration first, with the use cases the draft lists.
- **INESC-ID** (15 July 2026): the launch on 1 July at the Técnico Innovation Center, the Prime Minister attending, 80 researchers, 5.5 million euros from the Recovery and Resilience Plan, the first LLM for European Portuguese.
- **NOVA FCT** (2 July 2026): the launch venue, the Prime Minister, the five consortium members, about 60 researchers, 5.5 million euros.
- **Hugging Face model page:**
  - licence Apache 2.0, a text-generation model;
  - partners including Beira Interior, Évora and ISEL;
  - training on MareNostrum5 (Barcelona) and Deucalion (Minho Advanced Computing Center);
  - warnings against high-stakes decisions without a qualified human and against use as a sole source of fact.
- **arXiv technical report** (27 March 2026):
  - built from EuroLLM-9B;
  - Arquivo.pt data, personal data removed, 5.8 billion tokens in the kept quality splits;
  - "matches strong baselines on translated benchmarks while substantially improving performance on pt-PT-specific evaluations".
