# S2 review: Europe in Pavilion 4: the Commission's week at Web Summit

**Verdict: Ready**, after a re-check (at the end). First review, as written: Fix first. One sentence says more than the sources do. Everything else checks out, and the format is complete.

## Fix first

1. **"The stands stay open all week."** Only HaDEA says this, for its own stand: "Throughout Web Summit, HaDEA representatives and EU-funded projects will also be available at stand E418 in Pavilion 4". The Commission gives the stand numbers for DG GROW, HaDEA and EuroHPC, but not their hours or days. One way: "HaDEA's stand is staffed throughout the summit, HaDEA says."
