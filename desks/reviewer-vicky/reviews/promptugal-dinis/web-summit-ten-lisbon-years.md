Checked 6 November 2026 against its three sources, each opened that day.

1. **The 2021 study is credited to ECO; ECO credits Jornal de Negócios, and in the conditional.** "In November 2021 an evaluation by the Ministry of Economy's Office for Economic Studies (GEE) found the impact ... "bem abaixo do estimado inicialmente" ... with gross value added 196 million euros lower than estimated, as ECO reported." ECO: "Uma avaliação divulgada pelo Gabinete de Estudos Económicos (GEE) do Ministério da Economia revela que ... o impacto económico da Web Summit em Portugal entre 2016 e 2019 terá ficado bem abaixo do estimado inicialmente, revela esta quarta-feira o Jornal de Negócios (acesso pago)", and "o VAB terá crescido menos 196 milhões". The figures are Jornal de Negócios's report, as ECO passes it on, and "terá" (reportedly) is not carried over. Also "a 2021 study for Portugal's Ministry of Economy" ("In short"): ECO says by the ministry's GEE.
2. **"Lisbon's contract with Web Summit runs to 2028" ("In short").** ECO, 2018: "O acordo entre o Governo, o Web Summit e a Câmara de Lisboa"; the deal is with the government and the city, as the story itself says ("In 2018 Portugal agreed to pay ..."). Small.

Every EY figure matches Executive Digest with Lusa (5 November 2026), and the 2018 deal (11 million euros a year to 2028, split between Lisbon's tourism fund and the ministry) matches ECO. All links open. Sources not clickable on the page: see README.
