# S22 review: From pineapple waste to fish feed: AgroGrINTech's pilot in Maia

**Verdict: Fix first.** A new story (nothing on AgroGrINTech is published or in the PrompTugal desk), every figure checks out; three small points for the journalist. 530 words: no cut needed.

Sources opened 6 November 2026: Pep4Fish (19 October 2026), AICEP Portugal Global (4 November 2026) and AgroGrIN Tech's home page.

## Fix first

1. **"The same process also makes pineapple flour, so almost nothing is wasted."** AICEP: "the process also produces pineapple flour, allowing further use of the raw material". "Almost nothing is wasted" is more than either source says. Say "allowing more of the fruit to be used".
2. **The quote is translated and shortened without saying so.** "This optimisation multiplied by four the production capacity planned in 2025," said Débora Campos. Pep4Fish: "Esta otimização, ainda no âmbito do PRR, permitiu multiplicar por quatro a capacidade produtiva prevista em 2025." Mark the cut ("This optimisation ... multiplied by four ...") and say it is translated from Portuguese.
3. **"The name is spelt AgroGrINTech by both reports and AgroGrIN Tech on the company's own site."** Pep4Fish uses both: "A AgroGrIN Tech, start-up portuguesa, arrancou ..." and later "AgroGrINTech". Only AICEP uses "AgroGrINTech" throughout. This is in "What this does not claim", so it is the journalist's to fix.

## Checked and correct

The pilot unit in Pedrouços, Maia; bromelain from pineapple peel and core, for protein hydrolysates; about 200kg a year from about 120 tonnes, four times the 50kg planned for 2025; the same extraction yields as in the laboratory; Débora Campos, CEO and founder, quoted by Pep4Fish; seabass and seabream, digestibility and fish health (AICEP, with its caveat on formulation); about 1.2 million euros by the end of 2026, PRR and private money, and the difference between Pep4Fish ("the unit") and AICEP ("the project"), rightly flagged; ETSA leading nine partners; the Blue Bioeconomy Pact; the Catholic University's Centre for Biotechnology and Fine Chemistry, licensed exclusively in 2025 (AICEP); small test batches, first supply contracts and an investment round in 2027, a team of six. Format: valid YAML, no title line, sources as links, story after date, no em-dashes; all five links to the site's pages open. One person named, in their public role as the source gives it; no contact details; nothing on RiskMandate. The connection line is there, as a suggestion.
