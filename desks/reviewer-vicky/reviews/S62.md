## Style edit (mine)

- "64% agree ... 38% say" in In short now "instinctively", the survey's measure, as the body has it.
- The workshops: "when participants were told a summary contained an error, many said they would check elsewhere", as the report puts it.
- The 51.3%: "accepted the AI's answer without checking it against the video", the paper's own description (not "skipped the video").
- "Checking worked" became "Watching the video helped": the smaller drops (9.4 and 5.1 points) are for people who watched the relevant part, not specifically those who checked the AI.
- The paper: first posted 20 March 2026, revised 31 August 2026, and, its arXiv page says, accepted at EMNLP 2026; the limits bullet no longer calls it unreviewed.
- The chart (420 wide, text at 12.5 to 16) redrawn as a 1200 by 630 card, text at 42 or more, "a significant error" as the report says; it is the cover too.
