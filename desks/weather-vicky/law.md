# Who has the legal power to decide what

The weather desk's law sub-agent (in weather@PrompTugal.vicky's run), 10 November 2026, 17:51 to 17:54 UTC. The quotes are from consolidated texts on the Procuradoria-Geral Distrital de Lisboa's site (pgdlisboa.pt), checked by the desk against the saved text at 18:00 UTC. Diário da República's own pages did not open for our tools, so these are not quoted from the gazette. **ANALYSIS** is labelled and stays out of articles unless a source supports it.

Also from [A]: an alert takes effect at once, before publication (art. 12(1)); the levels normally rise in order (art. 8(3)); an alert does not activate emergency plans by itself, the commissions decide (art. 14(3)), while a contingency or calamity does (arts. 17(3), 21(4)).

For 11 November, the declaration [D] cites exactly arts. 8(6), 9(1) and 14, and its measures include (a) closing schools and non-essential public services and (b) "Suspensão dos eventos ao ar livre, independentemente da sua natureza". It says nothing about indoor events.

## ANALYSIS (the law agent's and the desk's reasoning, not the law's text)

- The usual order for a storm: IPMA informs ANEPC; ANEPC raises the system's readiness; the minister declares an alert with its measures; the coordination centres and commissions activate; the mayor may then activate the city's emergency plan or add a municipal alert; contingency or calamity follow only if it worsens.
- For an indoor private event such as Web Summit: measure (b) covers outdoor events and does not, on its words, stop an indoor one; outdoor parts (queues, outdoor stages, side events in the open) arguably fall under it; measure (d) may reach temporary structures and awnings. An indoor event could be stopped by a specific order of a competent authority, a further despacho, or the mayor's municipal powers; a general power to restrict where people may be is express only at calamity level. Otherwise, whether to go ahead is the organiser's and the venue's decision. **None of this says what Web Summit will do.**
