# Then and now: past storms and what was decided, when

The weather desk's historian (a sub-agent in weather@PrompTugal.vicky's run), 10 November 2026. **Most sources here are press reports, labelled as press**: fine for the history of what happened, never used as fact about the present. Times are local (Portugal is on UTC in winter, UTC+1 in October and March). Items marked "snippet" were seen only in a search result, not on the page itself: treat them as leads, not facts. The desk opened the Kristin and Leonardo headline pages itself at 17:54 UTC; the rest is as the historian read it on 10 November 2026 and has not been re-checked by the desk.

## The pattern in one paragraph (ANALYSIS)

In every past case found, the government's declaration came **after** the storm hit, and closures were decided **locally** (councils, operators, organisers), often on the morning of the storm. For CHARLES, the government declared a nationwide alert situation with school closures and a suspension of outdoor events about **a day before** the red warnings start. The historian found no earlier case like that; "a first" is a claim to re-check before anyone publishes it.

### Martinho, 19 to 20 March 2025

Orange wind warnings for nine districts from 18 March; Lisbon orange for rain from 21:00 on the 19th; roads closed and trains stopped in Greater Lisbon on the 20th; Lisbon reported 22 schools damaged afterwards; no government declaration found. Sources (press): [Observador, 18 Mar](https://observador.pt/2025/03/18/nove-distritos-do-continente-sob-aviso-laranja-na-quinta-feira-devido-ao-vento/), [Observador, 20 Mar](https://observador.pt/2025/03/20/mau-tempo-fecha-estradas-na-grande-lisboa-e-paralisa-comboios/), [DN](https://www.dn.pt/local-geral/lisboa-faz-levantamento-dos-estragos-da-depressão-martinho-e-regista-já-22-escolas-afetadas).

### Elsa, 19 to 20 December 2019

Transtejo/Soflusa suspended the Tagus crossings from about 16:00 on the 19th (restart "when conditions improve"); limits on the two bridges; the Wonderland Lisboa fair closed by its organiser; no government declaration found. Source (press): [DN, 20 Dec](https://www.dn.pt/pais/depressao-elsa-ligacoes-fluviais-suspensas-entre-o-terreiro-do-paco-e-a-margem-sul--11637460.html).

### Leslie, 13 to 14 October 2018

Red wind warnings for 13 districts (Lisbon and Setúbal 21:00 Saturday to 03:00 Sunday); district commands reinforced; INEM's situation room activated; no government declaration found. Sources (press): [DN](https://www.dn.pt/pais/interior/autoridades-em-maxima-forca-a-espera-do-leslie-9996574.html), [RTP](https://www.rtp.pt/noticias/pais/inem-ativa-sala-de-situacao-nacional_n1104763).

## Lead times then (ANALYSIS, from the sources above)

| Step | What history shows | Now (CHARLES) |
|---|---|---|
| Red warning to government declaration | Kristin: about 1.5 to 2 days, **after** impact, backdated. Leonardo: declared the day it took effect. Others: none | Declared about a day **before** the red warnings start (page dated 12:10 UTC on 10 Nov; red from 12:00 on 11 Nov) |
| Declaration to school closures | No past case of the government closing schools; councils closed them on the morning of the storm or for the next day | Closed by the declaration itself, a day ahead |
| Warning to transport suspensions | Reactive: ferries on the afternoon of impact (Elsa); rail during the storm day (Kristin). No preventive Metro, Carris or airport suspension found | None announced as of 17:43 UTC on 10 Nov |
| Large events | Closed by their organiser or the council (Wonderland, 2019); none by a government order found | Outdoor events suspended by the declaration; Web Summit: nothing announced |
| End of warning to reopening | Mostly not sourced; schools in Ansião closed about four days after Kristin | To be recorded |
