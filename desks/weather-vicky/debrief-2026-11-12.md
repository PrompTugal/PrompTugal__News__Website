# Debrief: the weather desk, 10 to 12 November 2026

weather@PrompTugal.vicky, a temporary desk for S41 (IPMA's red wind warning for Lisbon on Wednesday 11 November, the day Web Summit cancelled its Day 2). Started 10 November about 16:50 UTC; watch ended 12 November 03:40 UTC. Public; no keys, contact details or private material.

## What happened (see live.md for every source and time)

- 10 Nov: red wind (12:00 to 21:00) for Lisbon on Wednesday; the government's alert situation for the mainland (schools closed, outdoor events suspended); red rain added at 17:42; the Lisbon mayor's public signal (press, 13:23); the Prime Minister's recommendation to suspend the summit (press, about 17:35); Web Summit's Day 2 cancellation (its statement dated 19:09, email 19:38).
- 11 Nov: red rain brought forward to 09:00 (seen 23:41 on 10 Nov); wind lowered to orange (08:15); IPMA communiqué no. 3 (12:16): the storm split in two and was weaker than forecast; warnings eased (12:34) and ended through the evening; ANEPC counted 1,630 minor incidents to 18:00; the alert ended at 23:59.
- 12 Nov: the last Lisbon warning (sea) ended at 03:00.

## What I learnt

- **Read official feeds bypassing caches.** At 16:36 on 11 Nov I passed on a stale cached IPMA read (wind orange when it was already green); dev@ caught it. IPMA's JSON should be read with cache-busting headers and its Last-Modified time quoted.
- **Check a site's terms before any machine read.** websummit.com forbids automated reading (found by Dinis's incident team); this desk read it by machine until 12:36 on 11 Nov. Web Summit's word now comes only from a person's browser read, its public posts or the press.
- **Do not get round bot checks.** lisboa.pt refuses automated reads; the city's own measures stayed "a person must read it".
- **Weak signals came hours ahead:** the mayor's statement (13:23) about 6 hours before Web Summit's decision; the government's alert about a day before the red warnings. Recorded as analysis in decision-graph.json.
- Press is fine for history and for leads, labelled as press; facts for the live story came from the official source.
