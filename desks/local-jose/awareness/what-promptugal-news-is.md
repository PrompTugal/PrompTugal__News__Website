# What PrompTugal.news is (draft 1)

A text a person can paste under a first contact to a local outlet, a course or an association. It says only what the site's own about and contribute pages say today. It carries no tagline of its own: the name's subtitle and description are Dinis's to decide (`board/guidance/2026-11-16-positioning-collaborators-local-media-paid.md`, point 1).

## Português

**O PrompTugal.news** é uma redação nova, feita em Portugal, que acompanha a tecnologia e a inteligência artificial, com uma página para cada concelho do Algarve.

- **Só publicamos factos com fonte.** Cada facto liga à página pública de onde veio, com a data em que a lemos.
- **Corrigimos à vista.** Um erro é corrigido na mesma página, com data, e o texto antigo fica visível. Se algo sobre a sua terra ou a sua organização estiver errado, diga-nos.
- **É feito por pessoas e por agentes de IA, às claras.** Os editores têm nome; os agentes também, e o trabalho deles está publicado.
- **Ligamos e damos crédito aos meios locais.** Enviamos os leitores para a notícia original e nunca a substituímos.
- **Pode escrever para a sua terra.** Não precisa de ser jornalista nem de ter conta. O que publicamos fica com licença CC BY 4.0, com crédito ao autor, e o seu nome só aparece se disser que sim. Por agora não há pagamento.
- **O que deve saber sobre nós.** O PrompTugal.news pertence a uma equipa que também trabalha na RiskMandate; não é cobertura independente da RiskMandate, e dizemo-lo em cada página.

As páginas estão, por agora, em inglês.

Sobre nós: https://promptugal.news/about/ · Colaborar: https://promptugal.news/contribute/ · A nossa declaração: https://promptugal.news/about/participant.html

## English

**PrompTugal.news** is a new newsroom, made in Portugal, that follows technology and AI, with a page for each council of the Algarve.

- **We only publish facts we can source.** Each fact links to the public page it came from, with the date we read it.
- **We correct in the open.** A mistake is corrected on the same page, dated, with the old text left visible. If something about your town or your organisation is wrong, tell us.
- **It is made by people and AI agents, in the open.** The editors are named; so are the agents, and their work is published.
- **We link and credit local outlets.** We send readers to the original story and never replace it.
- **You can write for your town.** You do not need to be a journalist or to have an account. What we publish is under CC BY 4.0, with credit to its author, and your name appears only if you say yes. There is no payment for now.
- **What you should know about us.** PrompTugal.news belongs to a team that also works on RiskMandate; it is not independent of RiskMandate, and we say so on every page.

The pages are in English for now.

About: https://promptugal.news/about/ · Contribute: https://promptugal.news/contribute/ · Our disclosure: https://promptugal.news/about/participant.html

## Gaps between this text and the site (for the pages' owners, not changed here)

1. **The about page still opens on the summit** ("Web Summit Lisbon 2026 is our first edition") and does not mention the towns or local outlets. Dinis's guidance since 15 November puts places first.
2. **The contribute page opens "Met us at Web Summit"** and its tasks are summit tasks (write up a session, interview someone at a stand). The guidance of 15 November asks for "write for your town". A local editor who follows the link finds no town task.
3. **Nothing on the site yet says how PrompTugal.news treats local outlets** (link, credit, send readers, never copy). The first contact says it; the page it points to should too.
4. **The about page says "We never publish contact details"**; the rule changed on 15 November (a contact published on a public site may be shown, linked to its page).
5. **The pages are in English** (`lang="en-GB"`); the first readers of these contacts read Portuguese.
