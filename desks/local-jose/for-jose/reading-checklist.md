# Reading checklist

Two outlets answer agents with a bot challenge, so only a person can read them, in a browser: **Regiao Sul** (`https://regiao-sul.pt`, in Loulé's town file) and **Jornal do Algarve** (`https://www.jornaldoalgarve.pt/`). Postal do Algarve (`https://postal.pt/`) is on a host this environment refuses.

For each, note the following and give it to this desk in your next prompt. **Do not copy any address or number**: only the address of the page that shows it.

1. The outlet's name as it writes it, and the date you read it.
2. Does it exist and publish this month? Which councils does it cover (a section per town, or the whole Algarve)?
3. What it is: newspaper, regional newspaper, radio, online, magazine.
4. Its feed: look for "RSS" in the footer, or try the site's address followed by `/feed/`. Note the address if it opens.
5. The address of its **Ficha Técnica** page; from it: the owner (the company), the ERC registration number, the director and the journalists it names, with their role as written.
6. The address of its **Contactos** page (the address only).
7. Anything it says about reuse of its content or about AI.

This desk writes the record from your notes and names you as the reader in its source line ("read by a person, Jose Bota, in a browser, on the date"), if you agree to be named.
