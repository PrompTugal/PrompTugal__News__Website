# The plan

Jose's goal: PrompTugal.news has its Awareness, its Contributors and its Local news partners for the Algarve's towns (cards 1, 2 and 3 of Dinis's roadmap of 16 November 2026). Dinis's rules come first and are unchanged by this plan (`README.md`).

## Done when

| Card | Done when |
|---|---|
| 3 Local news partners | Each of the 16 councils has a checked list of its outlets (feed, owner, contact page), each outlet is a record in `data/organisations/`, and each is linked from its town page. |
| 2 Contributors | Each council has its editors and journalists as the outlets name them (name and published role only), its courses and its news-publishing associations, local bloggers and newsletters included; each organisation has a draft invitation to `/contribute/`. |
| 1 Awareness | A short "what PrompTugal.news is" page is live, and every town page shares with its own title, summary and image, and its Portuguese version when the team's translations land. |

## The steps

1. **Catch up.** The Barlavento and Algarve Primeiro records in `data/organisations/` (Jose's rule J9, inside the grant); the first run report (J8); the answered asks cleared. Done on 16 November.
2. **The towns, two a run.** After Faro and Loulé: Portimão, Olhão, Albufeira, Silves, Lagos, Tavira, Lagoa, Vila Real de Santo António, São Brás de Alportel, Castro Marim, Aljezur, Vila do Bispo, Monchique, Alcoutim (the conductor's default, largest councils first; Jose agreed). Quarteira goes in Loulé's file. Each town: the method in the debrief, a file in `towns/`, records for new outlets, a request to the town's keeper.
3. **Contributors with each town**, from the same staff pages, and one regional pass for the courses and associations that serve the whole Algarve.
4. **Drafts towards Dinis's outbox.** One per organisation, only where the town page really links it. They stay in `contact-requests/`; outreach@PrompTugal.dinis takes them into the outbox (`board/guidance/README.md`, 16 November, 21:55 UTC); Dinis approves; a person sends. The "starter agent" of card 2 is a person's to give.
5. **Awareness, at most once a day.** The page text to journalist@PrompTugal.dinis to publish; five pages a day checked for sharing, findings to dev.places@PrompTugal.dinis.
6. **Close the loop.** A reply that confirms or corrects a page comes back as a public outcome; the page's owner fixes it; `status.json` records it.

## What keeps a town from being done

- ERC's public lists are on hosts this environment refuses; opening them is an environment change and waits on Dinis's yes (R4).
- Regiao Sul and Jornal do Algarve answer agents with a bot challenge. A person reads them: `for-jose/reading-checklist.md`.
- Sul Informação's robots.txt refuses Anthropic's agents by name; waits on Dinis.
- Every send waits on Dinis's open decision on what a first email may promise.
