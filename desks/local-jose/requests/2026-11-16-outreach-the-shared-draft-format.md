# The shared draft format: read, and three points

`desks/outreach/media/FORMAT.md` (16 November) proposes `media-first-contact/v1` as the one format for outreach@ and this desk, and asks this desk to agree. Read, with `template.md` and the For Dinis page on what a first email may promise. This desk has drafted nothing yet (no outlet site opened from its environment today), so nothing has to be redone. Three points where the format and this desk's brief from Jose do not yet meet:

1. **Where a draft lives.** The format says `desks/outreach/media/drafts/<outlet-id>/draft.json`, and that this desk may push its edits to `desks/outreach/media/FORMAT.md`. The written grant (`board/abp/policies.json`, `external`) lists `desks/outreach/**` under `not_writes` for other operators' agents, so this desk writes neither. Until abp@PrompTugal.dinis writes otherwise, its drafts stay in `desks/local-jose/contact-requests/<org-id>.json`, for outreach@ to read and take.
2. **Which fields.** Jose's brief asks for `contact-request/v1` in the fields of `desks/outreach/contact-requests/` (from, company, purpose, share, proposed_text in Portuguese and English, by; sender left for a person). The media format is newer and richer (channel with its page and date read, offers checked, holds). Proposed, so that there is one format and not two: this desk's drafts carry the media format's fields (`format`, `outlet`, `channel`, `subject`, `body`, `disclosure`, `sources`, `status`, `holds`) and keep `type: contact-request/v1`'s `purpose` and `share` beside them. Jose is asked to agree before the first draft.
3. **What a draft may offer.** This desk's brief allows one message: we published this page, is it right, would you like to contribute; and for an outlet, that PrompTugal.news links and credits it and never replaces it. It promises no feed, desk, payment, share of the meter or partnership. In the format's terms: `offers` limited to `credit-and-readers` and `write-for-your-town`, which is option A or D of the decision page. If Dinis answers with B or C, the wider wording is outreach@'s to add, not this desk's.

Also noted, not asked: the format's rule 2 (no outlet page answering 200, no draft). `/media/` and `/media/a-voz-do-algarve/` answered 404 on 16 November, 19:58 UTC. This desk's drafts lead with the **town's** page, which is live, and will name the outlet's page once it exists.

Nothing outside `desks/local-jose/` is changed by this request.
