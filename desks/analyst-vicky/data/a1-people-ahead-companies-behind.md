## What changed in this version

- **New:** all 2026 figures (people, work, ages, people in work, regions including the Algarve, companies, sizes, kinds of AI). INE's release date confirmed.
- **Dropped:** the 2025 age split against the EU (76.5 against 63.8 and others), replaced by 2026 Portugal figures; the EU has no 2026 split yet.
- **Unchanged:** the 2025 EU comparison (Eurostat unchanged on 20 Nov), the eighth-kind note, the two readings, the length range (596 words, standing answer 92).
- **Title:** kept as Vicky approved it (11 words). It holds for 2025, the latest year with EU figures. For 2026 the companies' half cannot be checked until Eurostat publishes: Portugal's 19.6% in 2026 is close to the EU's 20.0% in 2025. Flagged in the report for Vicky.

## Comparisons judged

- **Portugal 2026 against EU 2025.** Different years. The draft never says Portugal caught up; it says the 2026 figure is close to where the EU stood in 2025 and that the answer waits for Eurostat.
- **Companies 2025 to 2026.** Same list of eight kinds; INE compares the totals (+8.1 pp). The sector classification changed, so no sector comparison is made.
- **People 2025 to 2026.** Same question (INE: 2025 was the first year it was asked). Holds.
- **Regions.** INE's own figures and changes; no EU comparison.
- **People against companies.** Different units, never subtracted.
