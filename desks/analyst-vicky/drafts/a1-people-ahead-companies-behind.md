**Analysis.** This piece reads official statistics and explains what they show. Every figure is linked to its source below.

## In short

- In 2026, 54.4% of people aged 16 to 74 in Portugal used AI tools that answer questions or create text, images, code or video. In 2025 it was 38.7%, against 32.7% across the EU.
- 30.7% used AI for work, up from 19.9%. The share is of everyone aged 16 to 74, not only people in work.
- 19.6% of Portuguese companies with ten or more staff used AI in 2026, up from 11.5%. The EU figure for 2025 was 20.0%.

## The story

Every year, national statistics offices run two surveys to a common EU design, one of people and one of companies. In Portugal, INE, the national statistics institute, runs both and published its 2026 results on 20 November. Eurostat, the EU's statistics office, publishes every country's results side by side, but its 2026 figures are not out yet. So the latest EU comparison is for 2025.

That year, people in Portugal were ahead. Portugal was 13th of the 27 member states. Its companies were 21st, and the gap with the EU had grown from 0.2 percentage points in 2023 to 4.9 in 2024. In 2025 the companies' survey added an eighth kind of AI to its list, software that makes pictures, video or sound, so the 2025 gap of 8.4 points is on a wider measure.

In 2026, use by people grew by 15.7 points in a year. The young lead: 85.4% of people aged 16 to 24 used AI tools, against 13.7% of those aged 65 to 74. The biggest rise was among people aged 45 to 54, up 20.3 points to 59.1%. Among people in work, 63.4% used them.

INE also gives the regions. In the Algarve, 56.3% of residents used AI tools in 2026, up 16.6 points, the third-highest share of Portugal's nine regions after Grande Lisboa (63.5%) and the Península de Setúbal (62.5%). The Alentejo was lowest, at 46.7%.

Companies moved too. The share of small firms, with 10 to 49 staff, rose from 9.4% to 16.3%. Firms with 50 to 249 staff went from 18.2% to 32.1%, and those with 250 or more from 49.1% to 65.5%. Among firms that use AI, the most common kinds analyse written text (62.2%) and make images, video or sound (60.3%).

Portugal's 2026 company figure is now close to where the EU stood in 2025. Whether the gap has closed depends on the EU's own 2026 figure, which Eurostat has not published.

The statistics do not say why people use AI more than their employers adopt it. Two readings fit them. Many people may use free consumer tools on their own. Or the companies' question, about technologies the business uses, may not count staff using a chatbot on their own. Either way, a lot of AI use in Portugal happens outside company decisions.

## What this does not claim

- A comparison with the EU for 2026. Eurostat has not yet published the EU's 2026 figures.
- That the two shares can be compared directly. People and firms are different units, and the companies' figure leaves out finance and firms under ten staff. The shares are set side by side, never subtracted.
- Comparisons by sector. In 2026 INE moved the companies' survey to a new classification of economic activities. INE compares the totals across years, but not sectors in detail.
- How often or how well people use AI, or whether they use their employer's tools.
- Private surveys that give other shares for Portugal. They ask different questions of different samples, and were not read at source.
