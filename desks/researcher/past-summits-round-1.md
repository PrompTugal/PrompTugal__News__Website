## Cautions
- The 2025 record (71,386) is lower than the 2024 figure (71,528) as the sources give them, so "record" in 2025 refers to the organiser's framing at the time of the article (midweek, 12 Nov): check the final 2025 number before the page says either is the largest. Open.
- Wikipedia's table lists Doha (Web Summit Qatar, 26 to 29 Feb 2024) among the editions: not a Lisbon edition, and not to be mixed with the Lisbon series.
- Our data has hand-written entries for 2024 and 2025 (data/events/web-summit-lisbon-2024 and -2025, ontologist@) using the same sources.
