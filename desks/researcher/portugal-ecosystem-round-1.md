## Startup Portugal
Source: [startupportugal.com/about-us](https://startupportugal.com/about-us/) and related pages, read 7 November 2026.
- **What it says it is:** "Portugal's national reference for entrepreneurship", active since 2016, bridging the public and private sectors; "a public-interest organisation, funded through a programme contract with IAPMEI, European funds such as the PRR, and its own revenue, including sponsorships". It supports the Government in shaping and implementing public policy for entrepreneurship and promotes Portuguese startups at home and abroad. (read)
- **Three pillars, as it names them:** *Ignite* (Start from Knowledge, Empreende XXI, Tech Foundry Portugal, and the National Incubator Network, RNi); *Support* (Startup and Scaleup status recognition, the One Stop Shop, StartUP Vouchers and Incubator Vouchers); *Connect* (SIM Conference, the Above & Beyond Hangouts, Startup Matchbox, **Road 2 Web Summit** and Business Abroad). (read)
- **Road 2 Web Summit is one of its programmes.** This ties the organisation directly to the summit; it also appears among the 2026 event partners (see `centres-of-gravity-evidence-round-1.md`, round 5) and, in the Algarve, as the Road2Summit roadshow prizes announced at the Algarve Tech Hub Summit (see `algarve-evolution-round-1.md`, round 9). I did not read the Road 2 Web Summit programme page itself. (programme named: read; page: not read)
- **Who it works with, as it lists:** the Ministry of Economy, IAPMEI, ANI, AICEP, Portugal Ventures and Banco Portugues de Fomento; founding member of ESNA (European Startup Nations Alliance) and of Startups for Europe. (read)
- **RNi, the National Incubator Network:** "over one hundred incubators" accredited; four priorities starting with quality standards in incubation and collaboration among incubators. (read; the members list page not read)
- **Startup status:** the recognition requirements it states include being legally constituted in Portugal, under 10 years old, fewer than 250 employees, turnover under EUR 50 million, not majority-held by a large company, plus an innovation condition (e.g. recognised by ANI); the application is on the gov.pt portal. (read)
- **Reports:** Portugal's Startup Ecosystem Report 2025, and Startup & Entrepreneurial Ecosystem Reports for 2024 and 2023 are listed. The reports themselves (the numbers) are **not read**: the next step.

## Update, read 7 November 2026 (about 16:25 UTC): Startup Portugal's Ecosystem Report 2025, the landing page
Source: [ecoreport.startupportugal.com](https://ecoreport.startupportugal.com/) ("Portugal's Startup Ecosystem 2025: From Growth to Consolidation", by Startup Portugal with contributions from Informa D&B and other national and international sources). **Read:** the landing page's headline figures, which the organisation presents (numbers use Portuguese separators; I write them in English form):
- 5,091 startups (4,719 in 2024, +8%), 1.0% of all active companies; the president of Startup Portugal is quoted: "+5,000 Startups. We have met the RRF target and are now entering a consolidation phase" (a statement by the organisation's head, in role).
- Turnover EUR 2,856 million (EUR 2,602 million in 2024, +9%), about 1% of GDP by the page's own note (against 2024 GDP of EUR 289.4 billion); about 28,000 employees (+8%), about 5 per company on average (8 for all companies); average monthly wage about EUR 2,200, 81% above the all-company average; exports EUR 1,571 million (1.5% of total exports, +5%).
- The report positions Portugal against the Global Innovation Index, the StartupBlink Global Ecosystem Index, the S9+ Coalition and ESNA. The full PDF ("Download the report now") was **not** read; only the page's headline numbers.
Status: **read** (organisation's own page, figures as it states them, not checked at Informa D&B or elsewhere).
