By researcher@PrompTugal.dinis, read 6 November 2026 (UTC). Public sources only, no login. Each fact carries its source. Figures are as the source reports them (many are self-reported by the organisation). Names of people appear only in the professional capacity the source gives. No contact details.

## Who they are
- A Faro-based organisation strengthening southern Portugal's innovation ecosystem; a small group of local entrepreneurs began building the community in 2018; in 2026 it shifted focus from community building to business growth. Source: [Portugal Startup News, 1 Nov 2026](https://portugalstartupnews.com/2026/11/01/algarve-evolution-aims-to-support-500-companies-and-help-create-150-startups-by-2032-in-push-beyond-tourism/), read 2026-11-06.
- Described by a search summary as a private non-profit association registered in 2018, aggregating main ICT companies of the Algarve. Secondary, unverified here: needs the registry or its own site.
- Executive director: Antonio Guerreiro (Portugal Startup News, above). He co-presented the ADI network and the Algarve Tech Hub Summit at the Lagos launch, with Algarve STP and ANJE speakers. Source: [postal.pt, 11 Sep 2025](https://postal.pt/algarve/lagos-lanca-rede-algarve-digital-innovators-para-impulsionar-inovacao-e-startups/), read 2026-11-06.

## Mandate: three pillars and 2032 targets
Pillars: prepare talent for entrepreneurship and employment; support startups and SMEs; connect companies, institutions, investors and professionals. Targets by 2032: support 500 companies, help create 150 startups, impact 50,000 people. Source: Portugal Startup News, above.

## Self-reported scale
- 118 active members whose businesses generate about EUR 100 million turnover and about 1,000 direct jobs (member figures, not generated by the organisation).
- More than 142 events in 2024 and 2025, 5,597 participants; more than 500 students in entrepreneurship activities.
Source: Portugal Startup News, above.
