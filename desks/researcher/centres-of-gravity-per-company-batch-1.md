Only what the page says; a menu link, a banner or a customer's comparison is not a partnership, and each row says which it is. A page not showing something does not mean the company has no tie.

## What it shows so far
Of six pages, two state a tie to a cloud (Cerebras with AWS; Elastic on all three marketplaces), one covers all three as products (Datadog), one names AWS as the rival (Runpod), two say nothing. The pattern fits how the lens should be drawn: an edge needs its kind ("on marketplace", "joint offering", "monitors", "compared with") and the source, because a company page rarely says "partner" outright. A partner-page or marketplace-listing read per company is the stronger evidence (not yet done), and AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft each publish partner and marketplace directories that name companies: reading those three directories for the summit's company names is the efficient route (next).
