## University of Algarve (UAlg)
- Founded 1979; headquarters in Faro; three campuses (Gambelas and Penha in Faro, one in Portimao); roughly 10,000 students, about 20% international; about 700 permanent teaching and research staff. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Algarve), read 2026-11-06 (a tertiary source: re-check the figures at ualg.pt).
- Its CRIA division (entrepreneurship and technology transfer) sits on the jury of the 2025 Web Summit competition and hosts the Startup Scale Journey events in Faro (ANJE regulation; Luma). Lead promoter of that 2025 project.

## Algarve STP (Algarve Systems and Technology Partnership Association)
- Non-profit association representing the Algarve's technology and innovation ecosystem; co-founded the Algarve Tech Hub brand; recognised as a Digital Innovation Hub (Algarve Smart Destination); manages incubation and acceleration infrastructure within national and regional incubator networks. Members and partners named: the University of Algarve, seven of the region's main municipalities, and strategic associations including DOCAPESCA, ANJE and Algarve Evolution. Source: [postal.pt, 21 Feb 2025](https://postal.pt/algarve/algarve-stp-junta-se-a-tecparques-e-impulsiona-a-inovacao-na-regiao/), read 2026-11-06.
- Joined TecParques (the Portuguese association of science and technology parks) as an associate member, decided at its General Assembly in Aveiro, reported 21 Feb 2025. A search summary that dated this to August 2026 was wrong: use February 2025.
- Roles named by that article: Paulo Santos, interim president; Jessica Costa, executive director (same source). Naming either is subject to the same rule as everyone: each person's yes first.
- Algarve Evolution is described as a strategic association in Algarve STP's network, and the two co-organise the Algarve Tech Hub Summit with the University of Algarve (Portugal Startup News, 1 Nov 2026).

## NERA (Associacao Empresarial da Regiao do Algarve), AMAL (Algarve intermunicipal community), ANJE (national association of young entrepreneurs)
- Named partners in the 2025 Web Summit project (NERA, AMAL) and in the ADI network and Venture Lab (NERA, AMAL, ANJE): see the Algarve Evolution note. ANJE ran the 2025 competition and is a member of Algarve STP.
- Only search summaries so far (not yet read at source, do not publish): NERA a business association headquartered in Loule, established 1989, 30th anniversary reported by Portugal Resident (that page returned 401, so not used); AMAL coordinates regional planning among the region's municipalities.
