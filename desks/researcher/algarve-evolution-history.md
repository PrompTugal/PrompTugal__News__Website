## Presidents and public roles (roles only, each person's yes still to ask)
- 2019: Miguel Fernandes, president (CEO of Dengun, per postal.pt 14 Sep 2019); a vice-president (Barlavento names Vanessa Nascimento; postal.pt does not name one); Marta Aragao head of communication, Nuno Antonio head of technology and innovation, Adelino Venturinha head of finance.
- 2024 to 2027: Joaquim Nascimento, president (CEO of Vanguard Strategy). Outgoing: Miguel Rocha Fernandes (the same person as the 2019 president? not stated: do not assume).
- Executive director: **Antonio Guerreiro**, named so by postal.pt (6 Jun 2024), the Algarve Digital Innovators launch (11 Sep 2025) and Portugal Startup News (1 Nov 2026). The conductor's brief says "Antonio Pinto Guerreiro": that full form is **not found in any source I read**; use "Antonio Guerreiro, executive director" and ask him how he wants to be named. Do not confuse him with Joao Guerreiro (president of Algarve STP 2026 to 2028, professor emeritus at UAlg) or Joao Pinto Guerreiro, who are different people.

## Numbers over time (self-reported, not like for like)
2019: 29 member companies. 2024: about 70 companies and individuals. 2026: 118 active members, about EUR 100 million combined turnover, about 1,000 direct jobs (members', not the association's); 142+ events in 2024 and 2025 with 5,597 participants; 500+ students; Grow Algarve Meetup 1,623 members on Meetup.
