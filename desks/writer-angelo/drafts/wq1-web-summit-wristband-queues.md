## In short

- You can collect your lanyard and wristband at Lisbon airport (Terminal 1) or at Pavilhão de Portugal, next to the venue.
- Past attendees say the airport desk usually has a short queue, or none.
- The long waits they report were at the venue on the first mornings: in 2022, "1 hour and more".
- So collect it today, Monday 9 November, if you can: the airport desk is open until 22:00, and Pavilhão de Portugal from 12:00 to 22:00.
- We found no public report of this year's queues yet.

## Where and when

PrompTugal.news's [first-day guide](your-first-day-at-web-summit.html) lists this year's pick-up points and hours. Web Summit says the times may change.

Bring photo ID and your ticket.

## What past attendees found

**At the airport: usually quick.** A 2025 attendee guide says the airport's registration tent "usually has no queues, and you can collect your badge right there". A 2022 guide calls the airport queue manageable. Another 2022 guide advises picking up the badge "right after you clear customs", and warns of "a long passport line" for travellers without an EU passport.

**At the venue: long on the first mornings.** The 2022 guide reports waits for the ticket exchange that were "quite crazy, especially on the opening day (1 hour and more)". The other 2022 guide says the entry queue "backs up into the plaza in front of the FIL and the arena" on the first couple of days. The 2025 guide says pick-up on the first night or first morning "can take time", and that on opening night the arena is closed once it is full, with the rest watching on screens outside.

**This year.** Two 2026 guides give the same advice: collect on Monday 9 November, before the conference opens, to avoid the queue on the first full morning. One calls Tuesday morning "the crush", with tens of thousands arriving through the same metro station and security lanes.

## Our advice

- **Landing today?** Collect at the airport desk as you arrive.
- **Already in Lisbon?** Go to Pavilhão de Portugal this afternoon or evening, from 12:00.
- **Arriving on Tuesday?** Collect at the airport before 15:00, or expect a queue at the venue in the morning.
- **Opening night:** get to the arena early.

## What this does not claim

- **No wait times for 2026.** Web Summit publishes none, and we found no public report of this year's queues, at the airport or the venue, as of 08:00 UTC on 9 November.
- **Past years are a guide, not a forecast.** The figures are from 2022 and 2025, from attendees' own guides.
- **The hours may change.** Check the Web Summit app before you go.
