## In short

- Web Summit's own app lists nineteen kinds of pass in its people search, each drawn in its own colours.
- Web Summit publishes no legend for the wristbands, so we compare the app's colours with the wristbands we see at the venue, and confirm them one by one.
- The first confirmed: the ALPHA startup wristband, an orange to magenta gradient with "ALPHA" in large letters, matching the app.
- Keep your wristband on all week: you need it to get back in, and for the Night Summit.

## The wristband

A 2026 check-in guide describes it: a fabric band, put on by a volunteer once your ticket and ID are checked. It "cannot be loosened later", so say if it is too tight when it goes on. You need it to re-enter the venue and for the Night Summit, and the guide's advice is not to take it off until the event is over: it is how Web Summit stops tickets being shared.

## The badge

You get it at the same time, on a lanyard. It carries your name and company, and a QR code; a 2022 account links the code to tracking who visited where. At check-in you need the QR code from the Web Summit app and a passport or other ID. For where and when to collect it, see [where the queues are short](web-summit-wristband-queues.html).

On Web Summit's own site, investors can apply for an investor ticket or buy a Platinum Investor one, volunteers "get a free ticket", and the startup programme (ALPHA, BETA, GROWTH) is "sold out" for 2026; the site does not define the three startup stages.

The ALPHA wristband also reads "VOID IF REMOVED OR TAMPERED WITH", with a dark metal clasp.

**From earlier years:** media badges were green in 2022, and investor badges red in 2015. This year's app colours are different.

We will add each wristband as we see it.

## What this does not claim

- **No published legend.** The colours are those of the Web Summit app's profile cards, read from phone screenshots on 9 November, and of the wristbands we have seen; the app's colours may look a little different on other screens, and the venue's lighting changes how a wristband looks.
- **Earlier years' colours may have changed.**
