## In short

- Collect your lanyard and wristband at Lisbon airport (Terminal 1) or at Pavilhão de Portugal, next to the venue. Bring your ticket and photo ID.
- Your ticket is a QR code under "My ticket" in the Web Summit app. You show it at registration.
- Wear the lanyard and the wristband every day. Losing one costs 100 euros. Losing both means buying a new ticket.
- Leave big bags at home, bring a bank card (food stalls take no cash) and clothes for rain.
- Night Summit is inside the venue this year, in Pavilion 5. Your ticket gets you in.

Every answer below comes from Web Summit's own pages, read on 9 November 2026. Times can change, so check the [Web Summit app](https://websummit.com/essentials/app/) on the day. For the trip from the airport to the arena, read our guide [Your first day, from airport to arena](../your-first-day-at-web-summit/index.html).

## Your ticket

**Where is my ticket?**
In the Web Summit app: tap "My ticket" in the menu. It is a QR code. It is also in the confirmation email you got when the ticket was bought ([app](https://websummit.com/essentials/app/)).

**I can't find my confirmation email. What now?**
Go to your ticket dashboard at [attend.websummit.com/lis26](https://attend.websummit.com/lis26) and enter the email address the ticket is under. A login link is sent to that address ([tickets](https://websummit.com/essentials/tickets/)).

**Someone else bought my ticket. Can I still see it?**
Yes. If a ticket is assigned to you, log in at the same dashboard with your own email address ([tickets](https://websummit.com/essentials/tickets/)).

**What is my ticket reference?**
A code of letters and numbers on the left-hand side of your ticket, in the dashboard. You can use it to log in to the app ([tickets](https://websummit.com/essentials/tickets/)).

**Can I give my ticket to someone else?**
Only the person who placed the order can. They log in to the dashboard and use "Manage items". Not every ticket can be passed on ([tickets](https://websummit.com/essentials/tickets/)).

**Can I get a refund?**
Only if you cancel within 14 days of booking. After that, Web Summit gives no refunds ([tickets](https://websummit.com/essentials/tickets/)).

**The name on my ticket does not match my ID. What do I do?**
Contact Web Summit's support team before you go to registration. The address is on the [tickets page](https://websummit.com/essentials/tickets/).

**I need a letter for my visa.**
Sign in to your ticket dashboard and click "Documents" to ask for a letter confirming you are attending ([tickets](https://websummit.com/essentials/tickets/)).

## Your lanyard and wristband

**What do I need to get in?**
A lanyard and a wristband. You collect both at registration with your ticket ([registration](https://websummit.com/essentials/registration/)).

**Where do I collect them?**
At one of two places ([registration](https://websummit.com/essentials/registration/)):

| Place | Opening times |
|---|---|
| **Lisbon airport, Terminal 1** (Humberto Delgado) | Mon 9 Nov: 7.30am to 10pm. Tue 10 Nov: 7.30am to 3pm. (It also opened on Sat 7 and Sun 8.) |
| **Pavilhão de Portugal**, next to the venue | Mon 9 Nov: 12pm to 10pm. Tue 10 and Wed 11 Nov: 7.30am to 5pm. Thu 12 Nov: 7.30am to 4.45pm. |

Web Summit says these times may change.

**What do I bring to registration?**
Your ticket (the QR code in the app) and photo ID ([registration](https://websummit.com/essentials/registration/)).

**Is there a separate queue for my type of ticket?**
Yes. Each ticket type has its own queue. Follow the signs at registration ([registration](https://websummit.com/essentials/registration/)).

**Do I have to wear them all the time?**
Yes, both, every day of the event. Web Summit also recommends wearing them at Night Summit ([registration](https://websummit.com/essentials/registration/)).

**What if I lose my lanyard or wristband?**
Losing one costs 100 euros to replace. Losing both means buying a new ticket at the current price, which Web Summit gives as 1,595 euros. Go to the ticket support desk ([registration](https://websummit.com/essentials/registration/)).

## The Web Summit app

**Where do I get the app?**
It is free for iPhone and Android, linked from [websummit.com/app](https://websummit.com/essentials/app/).

**How do I set up my profile?**
Log in at [attend.websummit.com/lis26](https://attend.websummit.com/lis26) and click "create your profile", or log in to the app with the email address on your ticket. You add your job, company, where you are based, your interests and a photo ([app](https://websummit.com/essentials/app/), [tickets](https://websummit.com/essentials/tickets/)).

**What can other people see on my profile?**
Your photo, full name, job title, company, industry and country. Your email address stays hidden until you connect with someone ([app](https://websummit.com/essentials/app/)).

**How do I make my own schedule?**
Open the "Schedule" tab, find a talk or meetup and tap "Going". It then appears under "My Schedule". Meetings you set up appear there too ([app](https://websummit.com/essentials/app/)).

**How do I find talks that interest me?**
Start with your Feed, which uses the topics you chose. Or browse the Schedule by stage, date or theme, or use the search bar on the home page. The [full schedule](https://websummit.com/schedule/) is also on the website ([app](https://websummit.com/essentials/app/)).

**Can I chat with people in the app?**
Not directly. Once you connect with someone you can email them with the "Get in touch" button. If you both link WhatsApp in the app, you can chat there. Connecting shares your email address with that person ([app](https://websummit.com/essentials/app/)).

**How do I book a meeting with someone?**
Go to "Discovery", open the person's profile and tap "Meet". Add a name, time, place and a few details. If they accept, it goes into "My schedule". To change it, use "Propose new time" in the "Invitations" tab ([app](https://websummit.com/essentials/app/)).

**Can I keep my contacts after the summit?**
Yes. Log in at [attend.websummit.com/lis26](https://attend.websummit.com/lis26), go to "Discovery" and click "Download Connections". Only confirmed connections are included ([app](https://websummit.com/essentials/app/)).

**Is there a map of the venue?**
Yes, inside the Web Summit app ([venue](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue/)). We also have [a map of the summit's buildings](../../summit/venue/index.html).

**What are perks, and how do I get them?**
Offers from companies for ticket holders. In the app, tap your profile picture, then "Perks", then "Apply now". You have to share your contact details with that company ([schedule](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule/)).

**Is there a jobs board?**
Yes. In the app it is under "Jobs", and on the attendee portal under "Perks & Jobs". Applying shares your contact details with the company ([schedule](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule/)).

## Talks, masterclasses and meetups

**When and where is Web Summit?**
9 to 12 November 2026, at the MEO Arena and FIL, Rossio dos Olivais, Parque das Nações, Lisbon ([venue](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue/)). What happens each day is on [our summit page](../../summit/index.html).

**What is a masterclass?**
A 45-minute session where an expert shares practical skills, often with questions at the end. Your ticket covers them, but places are first come, first served ([schedule](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule/)).

**What is a meetup?**
A small gathering of people who share an interest, an industry or a community. They happen day and night, inside the venue. The app suggests some, but you can join any ([schedule](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule/)).

**Why was I invited to some meetups?**
The app picked them from the role, industry, hobbies and communities on your profile ([schedule](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule/)).

## Getting there

**How do I get there by metro?**
Oriente station is about 300 metres from the MEO Arena and 600 metres from registration at Pavilhão de Portugal. From the city centre: take the green line from Baixa-Chiado to Alameda, then the red line to Oriente. Or take the blue line from Baixa-Chiado to São Sebastião, then the red line ([the city](https://websummit.com/essentials/the-city/)).

**Is there a bus?**
Yes. A Web Summit shuttle bus, run by Carris, goes between Praça do Comércio and Oriente. Carris lines 705, 725, 728, 750, 759 and 794 also stop at Oriente, from places such as Cais do Sodré, Restauradores and Campo Grande ([the city](https://websummit.com/essentials/the-city/)).

**Can I take a taxi or Uber?**
Yes. City taxis, Uber and Bolt all work. There are set drop-off and pick-up points at the venue every day ([the city](https://websummit.com/essentials/the-city/)).

**Is there a cheaper travel pass?**
Yes. Web Summit and Lisbon's metro offer a discounted pass for 1, 3 or 5 days ([the city](https://websummit.com/essentials/the-city/)). Prices and where to buy it are in [our first-day guide](../your-first-day-at-web-summit/index.html).

**Where can I stay?**
Web Summit works with b-network for hotel offers, linked from [the city page](https://websummit.com/essentials/the-city/).

**What will the weather be like?**
Usually mild in November, but it may rain. Many venues are outdoors and events go ahead whatever the weather, so bring the right clothes and shoes ([the city](https://websummit.com/essentials/the-city/)).

**Is Lisbon safe?**
Watch for pickpockets, mostly on public transport. Keep valuables out of sight, wait in busy, well-lit places and do not walk alone at night. In an emergency, call 112 ([the city](https://websummit.com/essentials/the-city/)).

## At the venue

**Will there be security checks?**
Yes, at every entrance ([venue](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue/)).

**Can I bring a bag?**
Web Summit asks you not to. If you must, only small bags are allowed: nothing bigger than a laptop bag, handbag or backpack. There are two bag drops, at the South and North entrances ([venue](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue/)).

**What is not allowed in?**
Among others: weapons, drugs, laser pointers, glow sticks, pets (except assistance dogs), drones, scooters, bikes and skateboards (wheelchairs are fine), sound systems, hotspot devices other than phones, umbrellas that do not fold, helmets and big bags. On Opening Night only, professional cameras and recording kit are not allowed either. Signs at security show the full list ([venue](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue/)).

**Can I film?**
You can film stands, the show floor and open outdoor areas. Filming stages, backstage, lounges, workshops and meeting rooms is not allowed. Web Summit asks for a plan in advance if you intend to film,, and its filming guidelines are linked from the venue page ([venue](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue/)).

**Is there wifi?**
Yes, free across the MEO Arena. Do not use a personal hotspot: it slows the network for everyone ([venue](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue/)).

**Is there food? Can I bring my own?**
Yes, food trucks and stalls across the venue, with vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options. You may bring your own food and your own reusable cup ([venue](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue/)).

**Can I pay with cash?**
No. Every food and drink stall is cashless, so bring a card ([venue](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue/)).

**Is there alcohol?**
Two places sell beer, ready-made cocktails and mocktails. Soft drinks, water, coffee and tea are sold across the venue ([venue](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue/)).

**I lost something. Where do I go?**
Lost and found is in Pavilion 5 and in Cloakroom A, next to Pavilion 1. Both are open during the day and during Night Summit ([venue](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue/)).

**Is the venue accessible?**
Web Summit has an [accessibility policy](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue/). Night Summit's venue is accessible throughout ([venue](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue/), [schedule](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule/)).

**Can I bring my children?**
Web Summit is for over-18s. Minors may be allowed under Web Summit's terms; ask Web Summit's support team first ([venue](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue/)).

**I rented a screen. Where do I collect it?**
In Pavilion 3, behind Stage 8 ([venue](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue/)).

## Night Summit

**What is Night Summit, and is it included?**
The evening programme: speed networking, more than 100 meetups, games, food, drinks and music. It is included with your ticket ([Night Summit](https://websummit.com/night-summit/), [schedule](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule/)).

**When and where is it?**
In Pavilion 5, at FIL, inside the venue ([schedule](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule/)):

| Day | Time |
|---|---|
| Monday 9 November | 8pm to 11pm |
| Tuesday 10 November | 6pm to 9pm |
| Wednesday 11 November | 6pm to 9pm |

**How do I get in?**
From inside Web Summit, walk along the Alameda to Pavilion 5 and use the tunnel entrance. From the city, use the South entrance until 5pm and the North entrance (near Moscavide metro) after that. Last entry is 9.30pm ([schedule](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule/)).

**Can I bring a friend?**
Only if they have their own accreditation ([schedule](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule/)).

**Can I leave and come back?**
Yes, as often as you like until 9.30pm, if there is room ([schedule](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule/)).

**Is there a cloakroom?**
Yes, in the Grand Hall. Lost and found is at the Info Point in Pavilion 5 ([schedule](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule/)).

**What should I bring?**
Comfortable shoes, a small bag and a refillable water bottle (there are fountains). Leave umbrellas of any size behind: none are allowed at Night Summit ([schedule](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule/)).

**What is Night Summit Downtown?**
Drinks in Lisbon bars after Night Summit, from 9pm to midnight: Tuesday 10 November at Quiosque Lisboa, Praça São Paulo (Cais do Sodré), and Wednesday 11 November at Quiosque Lisboa, Príncipe Real. Details are in the app ([schedule](https://websummit.com/essentials/schedule/)). Web Summit's [Night Summit page](https://websummit.com/night-summit/) also lists Time Out Market for Monday 9 November; check the app for Monday.
