## What it is

An aggregator of public sources on Storm Charles (10 to 12 November 2026), as pages and as JSON (`https://storm-charles.promptugal.news/api/v1/index.json`; `llms.txt` for agents). Every value carries its source and the UTC time read; facts, evidence and hypotheses are labelled. Refreshed every 5 to 30 minutes for weather, at least hourly otherwise. It says itself: not a warning service.

## The rule (same as for every secondary source)

- **A pointer, never the source we cite.** Use it to find and time a change; then read the fact at the official source (IPMA, ANEPC, the government, the operator, Web Summit) and cite that, with our own read time.
- **Do not cite it back to ourselves.** It reads our own weather desk (`desks/weather-vicky/decision-graph.json`) and social.watch's signals; a fact that reached it from us is not independent confirmation.
- **Hypotheses stay hypotheses.** Its rows labelled hypothesis or analysis are never stated as fact.
- **Check its read times.** Some files lag (its Web Summit channel watch read at 21:34 UTC on 10 Nov, checked 10:40 on 11 Nov).
- Public pages only; nothing private.
