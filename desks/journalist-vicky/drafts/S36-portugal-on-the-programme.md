## In short

- Tuesday 10 November, 12:00: [Startup Portugal](../companies/startup-portugal/index.html) hosts a masterclass on Portugal's startup scene. Places are first come, first served.
- Wednesday 11 November, 10:30: a press conference titled "New Unicorn in Lisbon: Discord Announces Expansion", with Lisbon's mayor.
- Thursday 12 November brings three: a global scaleups programme launch at 11:00, the Road 2 Web Summit winner at 12:30, and a meetup for founders building in Portugal at 13:30.
- Times are as Web Summit lists them; the press conferences are in the Media Village.

## The story

Portugal's startup scene has its own thread through the Web Summit programme. Here are five sessions to plan around, in order.

![Portugal on the Web Summit programme: five sessions from Tuesday 10 to Thursday 12 November](https://promptugal.news/desks/journalist-vicky/drafts/img/S36-cover-card-2026-11-09.svg)

*Times from Web Summit's session pages, read 9 November 2026.*

**Tuesday 10 November, 12:00 to 12:45, Masterclass 1.** "A guide to Portugal's startup scene", hosted by Startup Portugal. It covers trends and resources in the Portuguese ecosystem and how to connect with the startup community, Web Summit says. Places are first come, first served.

**Wednesday 11 November, 10:30 to 10:50, Stage 2.** A press conference titled "New Unicorn in Lisbon: Discord Announces Expansion". Web Summit lists Carlos Moedas, Mayor of [Lisbon](../organisations/city-of-lisbon/index.html); Stanislav Vishnevskiy, co-founder and CTO of [Discord](../organisations/discord/index.html); and Gil Azevedo, executive director of [Unicorn Factory Lisboa](../organisations/unicorn-factory-lisboa/index.html). It is held in the Media Village.

**Thursday 12 November, 11:00 to 11:20, Stage 2.** Unicorn Factory Lisboa, Hub71, [Instituto Caldeira](../organisations/instituto-caldeira/index.html) and Q Branch launch a global programme for scaleups, according to the session's title. Gil Azevedo speaks with Basma Albadi AlDhaheri of Hub71 and Pedro Zanetello of Instituto Caldeira. Also in the Media Village.

**Thursday 12 November, 12:30 to 12:50, Stage 2.** The press conference for the Road 2 Web Summit 2026 winner, with Miguel d'Aguiar, executive director of Startup Portugal, and Margarida David, Web Summit's startups manager. Web Summit marks it a media event only. This year's class has 78 startups (see [Beyond Lisbon: where Road 2 Web Summit's 78 startups come from](road-2-web-summit-beyond-lisbon.html)).

**Thursday 12 November, 13:30 to 13:55, Meetup 6.** The "Founders building in Portugal meetup", for international founders who moved to Portugal to build a startup. Web Summit says they will share what they have learned and swap tips on visas, hiring and finding a community.

Between sessions, the Portugal Stand is in Pavilion 1, stand E145, at the entrance, with AICEP's teams there all week, AICEP says.

Founders new to Portugal could pair Tuesday's masterclass with Thursday's meetup.

## What this does not claim

- **What is announced is not known yet.** The press conferences' titles are Web Summit's; this article does not say what Discord, the scaleups programme or the winner will be.
- **Who can attend the press conferences.** All three are in the Media Village; only the Road 2 one is marked "media event only".
- **Times can change.** They are as Web Summit lists them on 9 November; check Web Summit's app on the day.
- **Hub71 and Q Branch** are named as the session gives them; the newsroom has not read their own pages.
