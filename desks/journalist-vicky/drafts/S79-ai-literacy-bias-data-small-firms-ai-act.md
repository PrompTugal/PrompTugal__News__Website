## In short

- The amended AI Act says its AI literacy duty does not require anyone to guarantee a specific level of literacy for any individual.
- It lets providers of the systems it names process sensitive personal data, exceptionally, to detect and correct bias, under conditions.
- For small mid-cap companies, each fine is capped at the lower of the percentages or amount.
- The AI Office is exclusively competent for certain systems built on general-purpose models.
- Each Member State must have at least one national sandbox running by 2 August 2027.

## The story

This follows [our article on how the EU simplified the AI Act](../articles/how-the-eu-simplified-the-ai-act/index.html), which covers the dates and the new ban. Here are five changes in the text of Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 that touch smaller companies.

The text was read on the AI Act Explorer, a site run by the Future of Life Institute, which says it is not associated with the EU. The site says the texts are the complete, official ones as published in the Official Journal. We did not compare them with the Official Journal. We read only the provisions named below, not the recitals or the rest of the Regulation.

### Who counts as a small mid-cap

A small mid-cap enterprise is a company that has outgrown the EU's definition of a small or medium-sized firm but is still small. The text defines it by pointing to Commission Recommendation (EU) 2025/1099. This article gives no thresholds, because the Recommendation was not read.

### AI literacy

AI literacy, in plain terms, is the know-how to use AI systems well. The new Article 4 says providers and deployers must take measures to support it among their staff and others who operate or use their AI systems. It then says this "does not require providers or deployers to guarantee any specific level of AI literacy of any individual".

The Commission and the Member States are to support providers and deployers in this, "in particular SMEs". The text says the Commission must publish practical examples of how to comply.

Cuatrecasas, a law firm, says the Commission's first proposal dropped the duty for providers and deployers and kept only the support. It says the Parliament pushed to keep the duty in a "support" form, and that the final wording follows that.

### Sensitive data for bias

Special categories of personal data are the kinds of personal data the EU's data protection law treats as sensitive. The new Article 4a says that, to the extent strictly necessary, providers of the AI systems it names "may exceptionally process" them to detect and correct bias. The article sets conditions. Among them:

- Bias detection and correction cannot be done effectively with other data, including synthetic or anonymised data.
- The data must not be transmitted, transferred or accessed by other parties.
- It must be deleted once the bias has been corrected, or when its retention period ends, whichever comes first.

The article lists more conditions, on security and records, which are not quoted here.

### Fines for small mid-caps

The new Article 99(6a) says that for small mid-caps, each fine in the two paragraphs it names "shall be up to the percentages or amount referred therein, whichever is lower". Cuatrecasas says SMEs' simpler technical documents and proportionate management system were also extended to small mid-caps. It says this was in the Commission's proposal and stayed through the talks.

### The AI Office and sandboxes

A general-purpose model is an AI model that can do many different tasks. The text says the AI Office is exclusively competent to supervise and enforce the Act for AI systems based on such models, where the model and the system come from the same provider or the same group. It lists exceptions, and it covers very large online platforms and search engines.

A regulatory sandbox is a supervised space where companies can test AI under the watch of the authorities. The text says each Member State's authorities must establish at least one at national level, operational by 2 August 2027. Cuatrecasas says this deadline was postponed.
