# Lisbon's startup awards and the Algarve's plan beyond tourism

## In short

- Unicorn Factory Lisboa announced its 2026 Entrepreneurship Award winners on 27 October, on its fourth anniversary.
- BloodFlow won Most Promising Startup; RTBeamTech, a Técnico spin-off on Técnico's Web Summit stand, won the Youth Startup Award.
- Algarve Evolution, based in Faro, aims to support 500 companies and help create 150 startups by 2032.
- Its new growth programme, Founder2Founder, picked eight companies from 41 applications for its first cohort.

## The story

Two pieces of news from Portugal's ecosystem, from Lisbon and from the south.

**Lisbon's awards.** Unicorn Factory Lisboa named the winners of its 2026 Entrepreneurship Awards at a ceremony at the Técnico Innovation Center on 27 October, Portugal Startup News reported. The winners were:

- Ecosystem Award: Ricardo Marvão, co-founder of Beta-i.
- Entrepreneur of the Year (João Vasconcelos Award): Vasco Lopes, former CEO of DeepNeuronic, which Motorola Solutions acquired.
- Most Promising Startup: BloodFlow, a clinical decision-support system.
- Most Impactful Startup: knok, a digital healthcare platform.
- Youth Startup Award: RTBeamTech, which measures radiation beams for ultra-high-dose-rate radiotherapy.
- Rising Female Award: Rita Barbosa, founder of Guardian Labs EU, which works on stopping scam calls with AI.
- International Personality Award: Fernando Jardim, co-founder and advisor at the 351 Portuguese Startup Association.

RTBeamTech is a spin-off of LIP and Instituto Superior Técnico. Técnico lists it among the six spin-offs on its Web Summit stand, in Pavilion 2.

**The Algarve's plan.** Algarve Evolution works to grow southern Portugal's innovation ecosystem. By 2032 it aims to support 500 companies, help create 150 startups and reach 50,000 people, Portugal Startup News reported on 1 November. The goal is not to replace tourism but to widen the economy beyond it.

The base is small but growing. A study by Informa D&B, cited in the report, counted 115 startups in the Algarve in 2024. Their turnover grew faster than the national startup average between 2021 and 2024. Most are micro-businesses with fewer than ten people.

Algarve Evolution's incubator was recognised by Startup Portugal in January 2026. In October it launched Founder2Founder, a six-month growth programme that takes no equity. Eight companies were chosen from 41 applications. It also runs a pilot giving 20 tourism businesses free advice on using AI and robotics.

At the summit, visitors to Técnico's stand can meet RTBeamTech. Anyone following the Algarve's plan can watch for Algarve Evolution's news.

## What this does not claim

- **The funding rounds are not here.** The curator's S7 also lists Granter, Intuitivo and Seamoretech. Those tell the same story as S4, so they are covered in the S4 draft only.
- **Algarve Evolution's targets are its own**, as reported. The study's figures are cited by Portugal Startup News; the newsroom has not read the study.
- **Nobody here is confirmed at Web Summit** except RTBeamTech, through Técnico's stand. The sources do not say whether Algarve Evolution or the other winners attend.
- **The spelling of knok.** The report spells the startup "knok" in the heading and "knock" in the text; this draft uses the heading.
