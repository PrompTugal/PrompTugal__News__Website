# Brazil's startup mission comes to Web Summit Lisbon

## In short

- Brazil's trade and investment agency, ApexBrasil, and Sebrae, the national small business service, run a joint mission to Web Summit Lisbon, from 9 to 13 November.
- Its national selection is 100 startups. Sebrae pays flights and travel insurance for one person from each of the top 40.
- ApexBrasil says about 200 Brazilian companies take part, around a 324 square metre pavilion in the MEO Arena. In September Sebrae expected about 300 startups in the delegation it leads.
- The mission offers training before the trip, meeting rooms in the Brazil Pavilion, investor meetings and follow-up after the summit.
- In 2025 Brazil and Germany brought the two largest delegations, according to ApexBrasil.

## The story

Brazil comes to Lisbon in numbers again. ApexBrasil, the country's agency for exports and investment, runs a mission to Web Summit Lisbon with Sebrae, Brazil's service for small businesses. Serpro, the federal data service, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Brazilian Embassy in Lisbon support it, according to the mission's rules.

The mission is for Brazilian technology startups with a product ready to sell and the potential to open in Portugal or elsewhere in Europe. Its national selection takes up to 100 startups accepted into Web Summit's startup programme.

Sebrae and ApexBrasil announced the 100 selected startups on 30 September. For one person from each of the 40 ranked highest, Sebrae pays flights and travel insurance. Two more routes add to the group: hubs, incubators and accelerators can each put forward up to five startups, and 12 Brazilian states run missions of their own, according to Sebrae.

What each startup gets is set out in the rules. There is a startup ticket for up to five people, an exhibition stand on one day of the summit and a place in the Brazilian delegation's digital catalogue. There are networking spaces and meeting rooms in the Brazil Pavilion. The top 50 get pitch training, and 18 of them can give a sales pitch in English to investors and accelerators from other countries.

The week has its own programme. The rules list an internationalisation seminar on 9 November, three summit days from 10 to 12 November, and a closing session on 13 November on putting what was learnt into practice.

ApexBrasil announced on 4 November that about 200 Brazilian companies will take part, looking for investment and partners in Europe, as reported by the Portuguese news agency Lusa. Their base is a pavilion of 324 square metres in the MEO Arena, with a programme from 10 to 12 November: investor meetings, business presentations and debates with international guests. ApexBrasil also says Brazilian companies have raised about 700 million dollars at earlier editions, through contacts started at the summit.

Brazil has a history here. In 2025 its delegation had 380 companies, and Brazil and Germany had the two largest delegations at the summit, according to ApexBrasil's rules.

## What this does not claim

- **How many companies come.** The figures differ by source and date. ApexBrasil's figure, through Lusa on 4 November, is about 200 companies. Sebrae's expectation on 30 September was about 300 startups in the delegation it leads. The national selection is 100. This article does not say which is right; the numbers may count different groups.
- **The pavilion and the 700 million dollars** come from ApexBrasil, as reported by Lusa. ApexBrasil's own announcement could not be read: its site asked for a login. The newsroom has not checked the 700 million dollar figure.
- **The mission's dates.** The rules say 9 to 13 November; the Lusa report says 8 to 13 November, and the rules list a pitch training session on 8 November for 18 startups. Web Summit itself runs from 9 to 12 November, according to Web Summit.
- **The states' missions are not covered.** The Paraíba state government's 30 startups were left out: its official sites, and those of its research foundation, could not be reached on 5 November.
- **No company is named** and no result of the week is claimed. The list of selected startups is on Sebrae's page, linked below.
