## In short

- [Startup Portugal](../companies/startup-portugal/index.html) has picked 78 startups for Road 2 Web Summit 2026, from 24 sectors and 14 places.
- Lisbon has 41 of them. The other 37 come from Porto (11), Braga (9), Aveiro (4), the Azores, Madeira and Leiria (two each), and seven other places.
- Outside Lisbon they build drone detection, airborne wind power, stroke rehabilitation and water monitoring, among much else.
- The startups will pitch during Web Summit for the Most Promising Startup award, worth 15,000 euros.

## The story

Portugal's startups at Web Summit are not only from Lisbon. Startup Portugal's Road 2 Web Summit class of 2026 brings together 78 startups from 24 sectors and 14 places, it says. Its booklet lists each one with its home city or region.

Lisbon leads with 41. The other 37 come from across the country.

![Road 2 Web Summit 2026: 78 startups from 14 places. Lisbon 41, Porto 11, Braga 9, Aveiro 4, Azores 2, Madeira 2, Leiria 2, seven other places 1 each](https://promptugal.news/desks/journalist-vicky/drafts/img/S31-places-2026-11-09.svg)

*Where the 78 come from, counted from Startup Portugal's booklet, read 9 November 2026.*

**Porto** sends 11. UPWIND Energy builds airborne wind systems that turn high-altitude winds into electricity for remote and off-grid places. Braining combines AI and virtual reality for stroke rehabilitation. Scooli makes lesson plans, worksheets and tests for school teachers.

**Braga** sends nine. [RTBeamTech](../organisations/rtbeamtech/index.html) makes FLASHGuard, which monitors radiation beams for FLASH radiotherapy in cancer care. Savearth offers AI-powered hardware to help hotels save water in showers. Teemy turns real places into gamified digital experiences for tourist destinations and towns.

**Aveiro** sends four, among them RFisense, which detects and classifies drones with radar, radio sensing and AI, and Mundo Claro, which uses agentic AI to make civic engagement accessible for people with hearing impairments and non-native speakers.

**The islands** send four. From the Azores, Direto AI gives small businesses AI assistants for phone and chat, and Opsivision turns shopping-centre CCTV into visitor analytics. From Madeira, Humanos builds risk scoring for AI agents, and KBAI Research builds trustworthy, auditable AI for organisations, backed by years of university research.

**Smaller cities** add one or two each. [RipeNew](../organisations/ripenew/index.html), in Leiria, makes a device that removes ethylene from fruit stores to cut waste. Wada Solutions, in Coimbra, monitors water supply and wastewater. Next Border, in Castelo Branco, connects employers with talent. Quizzability, in Viseu, teaches sustainability through quizzes and games.

The class has done preparatory bootcamps, Portugal Startup News reports. Its startups will pitch during the summit for the Most Promising Startup award, worth 15,000 euros. Road 2 Web Summit is in its 11th edition this year, Executive Digest reported in August (see [Road 2 Web Summit: Portugal's startups head for Lisbon](road-2-web-summit-2026.html)).

Investors looking beyond Lisbon could start with the Porto and Braga startups, and with the four from the islands.

## What this does not claim

- **Where each startup works is as listed.** The place is the one Startup Portugal's booklet gives; a startup may also work elsewhere.
- **What each startup does is its own description**, as the booklet gives it. The newsroom has not checked their products or figures.
- **When and where they pitch is not given.** Neither source names the day or stage of the pitch sessions.
- **No startup here is linked to another.** The groupings by place are the newsroom's.
