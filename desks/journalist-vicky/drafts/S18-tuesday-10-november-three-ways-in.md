# Tuesday 10 November: Europe on stage, universities pitching, first-timers meeting

## In short

- Web Summit Lisbon runs from 9 to 12 November. Three things on Tuesday 10 November suit different people: Europe's tech policy on Centre Stage, a pitch day for university startups, and a meetup for people at Web Summit for the first time.
- The European Commission's Executive Vice-President Henna Virkkunen takes part in a fireside chat, "Has Europe's tech moment arrived?", on Centre Stage from 11:05 to 11:25, the Commission says. A Commission masterclass on data spaces for small businesses follows at 12:00.
- Web Summit lists a "First timers at Web Summit meetup" from 11:00 to 11:55 in Meetup 4. It overlaps the fireside chat, so a newcomer has to pick one.
- The University of Lisbon hosts UniVerse: Academia Empreendedora the same day, with a pitch competition for startups from nine Portuguese universities. Its stand (Pavilion 1) and Técnico's (Pavilion 2, E272) open on 10 November.
- These are the newsroom's suggestions for linking the day up. No source says any of these organisations plan to meet.

## The story

Web Summit Lisbon runs from 9 to 12 November. The university stands in this story open on Tuesday the 10th. For anyone planning the day, three events stand out, each for a different kind of visitor.

### Europe on Centre Stage

The European Commission's digital department, DG Connect, is at Web Summit from 10 to 12 November, it says. On Tuesday, Executive Vice-President Henna Virkkunen joins a fireside chat on Centre Stage, from 11:05 to 11:25. Its title is "Has Europe's tech moment arrived?". According to the Commission, the talk covers the EU's drive for tech sovereignty, from AI and cloud to chips and digital infrastructure, and how the EU regulates platforms and AI systems.

At 12:00 the same day, DG Connect runs a masterclass, "Data spaces for SMEs: Turning shared data into value", until 12:45. The Commission says it covers how small and medium-sized businesses can access and use data from connected products, and the support available at EU level.

The Commission also lists stands: DG GROW at E415 and HaDEA at E418, both in Pavilion 4, and EuroHPC at E405.

### Universities pitching

The University of Lisbon (ULisboa) hosts the second edition of UniVerse: Academia Empreendedora on 10 November, at the Portugal Pavilion Congress Centre. Nine Portuguese higher education institutions take part: the University of Lisbon, NOVA University Lisbon, and the Universities of Aveiro, Coimbra, Évora, Madeira, Minho, Beira Interior and Porto. The programme has a pitch competition for startups from those universities, a demo and exhibition area, networking and an artistic performance by the ZABRA collective, ULisboa says.

The same day, two university stands open at Web Summit itself. ULisboa's is in Pavilion 1, from 10 to 12 November. Técnico, ULisboa's engineering school, is in Pavilion 2, stand E272, over the same three days, with six of its spin-offs: Acubic, Keel, Opsivision.ai, RTBEAMTECH, RipeNew and Testwaves. Técnico says one more of its startups, still to be chosen when it wrote, will take part in UniVerse.

### First-timers meeting

Web Summit's own schedule lists a "First timers at Web Summit meetup" on 10 November, from 11:00 to 11:55, in Meetup 4. Its page says: "Calling all first-timers at Web Summit: your people are here!" It invites them to "Exchange insights, discuss challenges, and expand your network."

### Where the day overlaps

The meetup runs from 11:00 to 11:55. The fireside chat runs from 11:05 to 11:25. A newcomer cannot be at both. The Commission's data spaces masterclass starts at 12:00, five minutes after the meetup ends, so the two can be done back to back, if the rooms are close enough.

### Who might get the most from the day

These are the newsroom's suggestions, built from the public listings above. None of them is planned by the organisations named.

- **A first-time founder** could start at the first-timers meetup and go on to the data spaces masterclass at 12:00, which is aimed at small businesses.
- **Anyone following European tech policy** would want Centre Stage at 11:05, and could then visit the Commission's stands in Pavilion 4.
- **Investors and companies looking for university spin-offs** have two university stands to visit in Pavilions 1 and 2, and UniVerse's pitch competition.
- **University startups** pitching at UniVerse, or showing on Técnico's stand, are in Lisbon on the same day as the Commission's sessions on EU support for businesses. The Commission's HaDEA stand is in Pavilion 4.
