> **Update, 19 November 2026.** The [official gazette](https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/gratuitos/2s/2026/02/2S037A0000S00.pdf) published the standard terms of the service on 23 February 2026 (Aviso 3864/2026/2). They show it works through a convention, a standard contract between the health system's central administration and a provider, which any health-sector provider can join:
>
> - **Open to join.** Any person or company in the health sector, with or without profit, can join by applying with the forms attached to the terms. Joining a convention means accepting its published terms as they stand.
> - **Who it covers.** Health service patients whose doctor refers them and who have the digital skills to use the equipment on their own.
> - **AI.** The terms do not require it. If a provider's device or platform includes AI, they require it to document its accuracy and limits and to have a risk-management system. They also require that health professionals can interpret, override or reverse automated decisions.
> - **Sword Health Physio.** A letter dated 1 June 2026, on the letterhead of the health system's central administration, authorises it to join for 12 months, renewable. We found the letter online as a copy and have not checked it.
>
> We found no source saying whether any other provider has joined.
