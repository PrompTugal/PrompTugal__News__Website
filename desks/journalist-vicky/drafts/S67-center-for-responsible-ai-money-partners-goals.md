## In short

- The Center for Responsible AI is a Portuguese programme to build a new generation of AI products. Sword Health leads the consortium and INESC-ID, a research institute, is the beneficiary.
- The State's project sheet shows 51.49 million euros of Recovery and Resilience project amount, of which 45.67 million is shown as paid. The programme's own sheet gives a total investment of 77.7 million euros.
- Its goals include more than 217 million euros of exports and about 215 new jobs.
- The newest progress report found is from February 2024. Nothing found says what happened after the planned end on 30 June 2026.

## The story

Portugal has a national programme that brings startups, universities and large companies together to build AI products. Its name is the Center for Responsible AI.

The programme's own sheet says 25 business and research partners cover the whole path from research to selling the product. Sword Health, a health technology company, leads the consortium. INESC-ID is the beneficiary, the body that receives the funding. The sheet gives no partner list, so only these two names, and Unbabel below, can be given.

**The money.** Two euro figures appear, and they measure different things. The State's transparency portal lists the project under the Recovery and Resilience Plan, Portugal's programme for spending EU recovery funds. It shows a total project amount of 51.49 million euros, all non-refundable, and 45.67 million euros paid. The programme's own sheet gives a total investment for the whole agenda of 77.7 million euros. Neither source says how the two relate.

**The goals.** The sheet sets five. The first two are numbers: more than 217 million euros of exports, and about 215 new highly qualified jobs, with about 50 doctorates and 80 master's degrees completed. The others are efficient and sustainable AI, including an energy certification tool, links between universities and industry across the country, and making Portugal a leader in responsible AI principles and policy, to influence European rules and attract talent and investment. The sheet does not define "responsible AI". The start date is 1 October 2021 and the planned end 30 June 2026.

**What was said in 2024.** In February 2024, a founder of the Center, who works at Unbabel, a language technology startup, told Diário de Notícias that 18 products were in development. He said 145 highly qualified jobs had been created and about 50 doctorates and master's degrees were under way. These are his figures, nearly three years old.

He gave two examples. Sword's telerehabilitation app lets patients do physiotherapy at home with a virtual assistant. It was being used by health units linked to a hospital in Porto. Halo, from Unbabel, combines generative AI, which produces text and speech, with non-invasive brain interfaces to help patients with a neurodegenerative disease communicate. He said it was being tested with one patient.

Companies working on AI in health and language could watch what the programme's products turn into.

## What this does not claim

- **The goals are aims, not results.** No source found gives outcomes after the planned end of 30 June 2026. The status today is not known.
- **The 2024 figures and products are one founder's account.** The newsroom read only part of the interview.
- **The State's sheet shows an odd "last update" date** of 23 October 2020, before the project started. The amounts are as shown on the day read.
- **The programme's sheet is undated.** Its web address shows a February 2026 upload.
