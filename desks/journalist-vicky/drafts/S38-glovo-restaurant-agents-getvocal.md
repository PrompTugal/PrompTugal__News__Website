## In short

- Glovo, the delivery platform, uses AI agents on WhatsApp to manage its restaurant partners, according to a case study by the agent maker [GetVocal](../companies/getvocal-ai/index.html).
- The agents check restaurants are ready before opening, notice when they go offline, guide new partners through set-up and check their product photos.
- GetVocal says one pilot agent went live within a week, and Glovo grew to 80 agents across more than 20 countries.
- Every figure here is GetVocal's own. Glovo has published no account of its own that we found.
- GetVocal AI is a startup on Web Summit's featured list. We know of no Glovo presence at the summit.

## The story

A delivery app only works if the restaurants in it are open and taking orders. Glovo, part of the Delivery Hero group, has tens of thousands of restaurant partners, GetVocal says. Keeping them all ready is a daily job.

GetVocal's case study describes how Glovo hands much of that job to AI agents that talk to restaurants over WhatsApp, a channel they already use.

### Four agents, four jobs

**Before opening.** A check-in agent messages each restaurant about 15 minutes before its opening time. The partner confirms it is ready in seconds. GetVocal says response rates rose 50%.

**During the day.** An uptime agent compares each restaurant's status with its scheduled hours. When a restaurant drops offline, it contacts the partner and guides them to fix it. In one six-week evaluation, GetVocal says, uptime rose 20% and weekly orders grew sixfold.

**New partners.** A self-activation agent walks new restaurants through device set-up and tutorials. A separate agent asks for product photos and checks they meet the platform's standards.

### From one pilot to 80 agents

According to GetVocal, the first pilot agent went live within a week. Within months, Glovo was running 80 agents. GetVocal says many restaurant partners never realised they were talking to an AI. It also says the same approach is being extended across the wider Delivery Hero group.

Glovo's head of operations is quoted praising GetVocal's flexibility, in the case study.

### Why it is worth knowing

The story shows agents doing routine operations work at scale: short, structured conversations, each with one clear task, on a channel people already check.
