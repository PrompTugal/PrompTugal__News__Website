# From the PITCH stage to new markets: Portuguese startups raising

## In short

- Granter, which won Web Summit's PITCH in 2025, raised 1.6 million euros to expand into Spain, Germany and Italy.
- Intuitivo, the 2024 PITCH winner, raised 1.35 million euros to grow in Brazil.
- Seamoretech, a Porto spin-off, raised 1.1 million euros and plans to launch in Spain in 2027.
- Both PITCH winners' rounds were led or co-led by 3xP Global's Impact Innovation Fund, backed by Portugal's state development bank.

## The story

Three Portuguese startups have raised money in the weeks before Web Summit. Each plans to grow beyond Portugal.

**Granter** uses AI to help organisations find public funding, prepare applications and manage approved projects. It raised 1.6 million euros, Portugal Startup News reported on 3 November. 3xP Global led the round through its Impact Innovation Fund, under a venture capital programme managed by Banco Português de Fomento. Celtis Venture Partners, Gateway Ventures and Deel also took part. Granter's total funding is now 2 million euros. It plans to more than double its team by the end of 2026 and to expand into Spain, Germany and Italy. Granter won PITCH in November 2025. The company says it has since helped more than 140 businesses apply for over 30 million euros.

**Intuitivo** helps teachers create, run and grade tests. Founded in Porto in 2021, it raised 1.35 million euros in seed funding, reported on 23 October. Caixa Capital and the Impact Innovation Fund led the round. Techstars, COREangels Lisboa, COREangels Porto and Angels Way joined. Intuitivo says it covers more than 500 schools in Portugal and has 75,000 registered teachers. It will use the money to grow in Brazil, where it already has a team, and to go from 11 to 20 people. It won PITCH in 2024.

**Seamoretech** turns desalination brine into freshwater and minerals such as magnesium, calcium, copper and lithium, the company says. It is a 2021 spin-off of the University of Porto's Faculty of Engineering. It raised 1.1 million euros in the first closing of its seed round, from Rypples, Seven Seas Capital and Crédito Agrícola, reported on 2 November. Its total funding is now 3 million euros. It plans a commercial launch in Spain in 2027 and is looking at pilots in Spain and Egypt.

Investors at the summit interested in Portuguese startups going abroad could look for these three, and for this year's PITCH entrants.
