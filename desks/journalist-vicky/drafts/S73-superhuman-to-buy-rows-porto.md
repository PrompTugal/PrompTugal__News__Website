## In short

- Superhuman, the AI productivity company behind Grammarly, said on 23 February 2026 that it agreed to acquire Rows, a spreadsheet company.
- Superhuman says the Rows team will join it from Porto, where the team is based.
- Rows says it planned to close its product by 31 May 2026, once the deal was final.
- Rows' own page now begins with the line "Rows joined Superhuman", with no date. No source gives a price or a headcount.

## The story

A spreadsheet company with its team in Porto agreed to join a larger AI company in February, according to both companies.

Superhuman's blog post of 23 February 2026 says it agreed to acquire Rows, which "has built technology that connects spreadsheets directly to business tools and brings AI into everyday data workflows". Superhuman lists three things the Rows team built: live links to business tools, automation in plain language with no programming needed, and an AI data analyst, a feature that answers questions about data typed in everyday words.

Superhuman calls itself an AI productivity platform. Its suite brings together Grammarly, Coda, Mail and Go. Agents, software that carries out tasks for the user, work across all four. It says the Rows team will strengthen Coda, its workspace where teams plan and track work.

**Porto.** Once the deal is complete, the Rows team will join Superhuman from Porto, Portugal, where it is based, the post says. Superhuman says it wants to connect with Porto's tech talent community while it expands in Europe alongside its hubs in Berlin, Warsaw and Kyiv. Rows' own post does not mention Porto.

**Rows' side.** Rows' post of 22 February 2026 says the company started in 2017. It says more than 2.2 million people used Rows to run over 17 billion spreadsheet functions, which are formulas such as sums and lookups. They imported data from business tools more than 8.3 billion times and ran over 800,000 prompts with the AI analyst. These figures are Rows' own claims.

Once the acquisition was final, Rows planned to wind its product down over three months. Rows.com would close fully on 31 May 2026, and customers would be supported until then.

**Since then.** Rows' post now begins with the line "Rows joined Superhuman". The line has no date, and the newsroom found no other confirmation.

Porto's tech community, and anyone tracking where AI companies build European teams, could watch what Superhuman does there next.

## What this does not claim

- **These are company statements.** The newsroom found no independent report. Every figure is the companies' own.
- **The closing is not confirmed by a dated source.** The undated line on Rows' page suggests it happened. Neither post says when, or whether the product closed on 31 May.
- **No price, headcount or Porto plan is given.** Superhuman says the team will join from Porto. Neither source says how many people, or whether Superhuman keeps an office there.
- **The news is nine months old.** It is from February 2026, and the newsroom did not check what has happened since beyond the two pages.
