## In short

- [Discord](../organisations/discord/index.html) is opening an engineering hub in Lisbon, its first dedicated one outside its San Francisco headquarters.
- The company says it plans about 30 workers there by the end of 2027, on the core of its platform.
- Its job postings, public since October, show it hiring a site lead, engineers and a first IT hire for Europe, the Middle East and Africa.
- The announcement was planned for a Web Summit press conference, then made after bad weather cancelled Wednesday's programme.

## The story

A well-known gaming and chat platform is putting down roots in Lisbon.

![Discord's first engineering hub outside San Francisco is in Lisbon, with about 30 engineers planned by the end of 2027](https://promptugal.news/desks/journalist-vicky/drafts/img/S59-cover-card-2026-11-12.svg)

*From Tek Notícias and DN, 11 November 2026, read 12 November 2026.*

Discord's own job postings said it first. "Discord is opening an engineering hub in Lisbon, our first dedicated one outside of our San Francisco HQ," one reads, adding that it is "building it from the ground up". Observador reported it on 6 November, from the company's jobs pages.

On 11 November the company confirmed the plan. Tek Notícias reports that Discord wants the hub to employ about 30 people by the end of 2027, working mostly on the platform's core and on the systems behind its users' experience. DN adds that the team will work on infrastructure for audio, video and data. Discord already has a European office in Amsterdam, Tek Notícias notes, but this is its first engineering hub in Europe.

Discord's CTO and co-founder, Stanislav Vishnevskiy, said Lisbon "has established itself as one of Europe's main centres of talent and innovation", in Tek Notícias's Portuguese translation of his words. Engineers who join "will work on significant projects on a product used by millions of people around the world, in small teams where their impact is visible."

The postings show what the first hires look like. Observador and Devs.com.pt describe a site lead who will be the founding hire, and who will build the first engineering team. There are openings for an engineering manager and senior software engineers, and a recruiter. Discord says the hub is "not a satellite office" but a site with real technical autonomy, according to Observador. Staff will be expected in the office three to four days a week.

A first IT hire for the whole region will be based in Lisbon too, according to that posting, which has since closed to applications.

Another company recently chose Lisbon for a large site: see [Konecta chooses Lisbon for a global delivery centre](konecta-lisbon-delivery-centre.html).

Engineers looking for work in Lisbon, and anyone tracking where tech companies set up in Portugal, could watch Discord's job pages as the hub grows.

## What this does not claim

- **The size is a plan.** About 30 workers by the end of 2027 is Discord's target as reported, not a count of people hired.
- **No address, opening date or budget is given** in any source. Neither is any pay.
- **Public support is not confirmed here.** Observador's report lists Lisbon's mayor and Unicorn Factory Lisboa among those scheduled for the press conference. No source read says what they will do for the hub.
- **The quotes are translated** from Portuguese by Tek Notícias; the original wording was not read.
- **The weather.** Tek Notícias says the announcement was planned for a press conference at Web Summit and changed when the summit closed for bad weather; the newsroom has not seen the press release itself.
