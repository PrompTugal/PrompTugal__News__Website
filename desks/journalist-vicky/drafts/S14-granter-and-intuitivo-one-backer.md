# Granter and Intuitivo: two PITCH winners, one backer, new markets

## In short

- Two past winners of PITCH, Web Summit's startup competition, have just raised money: Granter (winner in 2025) and Intuitivo (winner in 2024).
- Both rounds were led, or co-led, by the Impact Innovation Fund of 3xP Global, under a programme of Portugal's state development bank, Banco Português de Fomento.
- Granter raised €1.6 million to grow into Spain, Germany and Italy. Intuitivo raised €1.35 million to grow into Brazil.
- Techstars and Angels Way have backed both startups too.

## The story

Two startups that won on Web Summit's PITCH stage are now growing abroad, with money from the same investor.

**Granter** uses AI to help organisations find public funding, prepare applications and manage approved projects. It won PITCH in November 2025. It has raised €1.6 million, bringing its total to €2 million, Portugal Startup News reports. The round was led by 3xP Global through its Impact Innovation Fund. The money will take Granter into Spain, Germany and Italy, where it already has investors and early commercial links. The United States is a medium-term priority. The company says it has helped more than 140 businesses apply for over €30 million.

**Intuitivo**, founded in Porto in 2021, helps teachers create, give and grade tests. It won PITCH in 2024. It has raised €1.35 million in seed funding, co-led by Caixa Capital and the same Impact Innovation Fund, managed by 3xP Global. Intuitivo is heading for Brazil, where it estimates a market of 2.2 million teachers. It already has a team there. In Portugal it covers more than 500 schools and 75,000 registered teachers, according to the report.

Both rounds sit under the Venture Capital Programme of the Capitalisation and Resilience Fund (FdCR), managed by Banco Português de Fomento.

The two share more than one backer. Techstars and Angels Way, a Portuguese fund run as a community of small investors, appear in both startups' funding.

The fields are different: one works on grants for companies, the other on schools. What they share is a path: a PITCH win, a Portuguese impact fund, and a next market abroad.

**Who could connect:** founders aiming for PITCH, or for a first round from a Portuguese impact fund, may find both companies' stories useful. This is a suggestion; we do not know if either company is at Web Summit 2026.
