# Europe's AI and funding help, and Portuguese startups it could suit

## In short

- The European Commission and EU bodies run masterclasses at Web Summit on AI support, EU funding and free access to supercomputers, mostly on 11 November.
- HaDEA, the EU agency that runs funding programmes, has a stand in Pavilion 4 (E418) through the summit. DG GROW is at E415 and EuroHPC at E405.
- Granter, a Portuguese AI startup, helps companies apply for public funding, with a focus on European funding for SMEs and startups.
- Técnico, Lisbon's engineering school, brings robots and six spin-offs to Pavilion 2 (E272).
- Linking them is the newsroom's suggestion. No source says they plan to meet.

## The story

Europe comes to Web Summit with practical help for companies building AI. Two Portuguese names work close to those fields.

### What the EU offers

On 11 November the Commission's DG Connect runs "Building AI in Europe: Practical Support for Innovators" at 14:30. The Commission says it covers regulatory guidance, access to infrastructure, funding and more.

At 15:30 HaDEA, the European Health and Digital Executive Agency, hosts "Investing in technological sovereignty: EU funding for your project". HaDEA says it will show EU funding opportunities and four EU-funded projects. One, PERSEPHONE, develops autonomous robots for safer mining of critical raw materials. Another, AI-TranspWood, uses AI to design transparent wood materials.

At 16:00 EuroHPC runs "Level up for free: Access EU supercomputing, AI & quantum resources". The Commission says industry can use Europe's supercomputers, AI Factories and quantum computers for free. Places are first come, first served.

HaDEA's team and its projects are at stand E418 in Pavilion 4 throughout the summit, the agency says.

### Who it could suit

**Granter** uses AI to help organisations find public funding, prepare applications and manage approved projects. Portugal Startup News reports that its main focus is helping SMEs and startups reach European funding. It won Web Summit's PITCH competition in 2025 and has just raised €1.6 million to grow into Spain, Germany and Italy.

**Técnico**, the University of Lisbon's engineering school, shows robots at stand E272 in Pavilion 2 from 10 to 12 November. They include Lola, from its Institute for Systems and Robotics, and a Unitree humanoid. It also brings six spin-offs: Acubic, Keel, Opsivision.ai, RTBEAMTECH, RipeNew and Testwaves.

**Who could connect:** Granter's team, if at the summit, and HaDEA's funding workshop share a subject. Técnico's robotics teams and the spin-offs could find the EuroHPC and AI masterclasses useful, and PERSEPHONE's robotics at HaDEA's stand. These are suggestions only.
