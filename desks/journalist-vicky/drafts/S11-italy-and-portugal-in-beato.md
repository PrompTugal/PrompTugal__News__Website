# Italy and Portugal in Beato: how Europe's tech champions scale

## In short

- European Tech Champions: Italy & Portugal takes place on 11 November in Lisbon, during Web Summit week, according to AICEP.
- Unicorn Factory Lisboa, the Embassy of Italy in Portugal and the Italian Trade Agency (ICE) organise it.
- It is at Unicorn Stage, in the Beato Innovation District, from 18:30.
- Two panels ask how European tech companies raise money, compete abroad and grow beyond their home markets. A networking session follows.
- The European Commission's Representation in Portugal and the European Investment Fund are on the speakers list, says the event page.

## The story

Italy and Portugal are putting their tech scenes side by side for an evening. European Tech Champions: Italy & Portugal is on 11 November, during Web Summit week, says AICEP, Portugal's trade and investment agency.

Three organisers are behind it: Unicorn Factory Lisboa, the Embassy of Italy in Portugal and the Italian Trade Agency, ICE. The venue is Unicorn Stage, in Lisbon's Beato Innovation District.

The question is a European one. How can tech companies raise capital, compete internationally and scale beyond their home markets? Founders, investors, corporate leaders and public officials from Italy, Portugal and across Europe will discuss it, AICEP says.

The event page sets out the evening. Registration opens at 18:30.

At 19:00, the first panel looks at the role of institutions in European innovation. Its listed speakers are Claudio Miscia, Ambassador of Italy to Portugal; Vitor Pimentel Nogueira, Acting Head of the European Commission's Representation in Portugal; and Marco Corsini, for the Italian Trade Agency. Unicorn Factory Lisboa moderates.

At 19:25, the second panel asks how Europe's unicorns did it. The listed speakers are Giovanni Alberto Falcione, co-founder and CTO of Exein; Alexandre Matoso of the European Investment Fund; Giulia Giovannini, a partner at United Ventures; and Pedro Petiz, Director of Strategic Development at TEKEVER. Gian Volpicelli, a technology journalist at Bloomberg, moderates.

Networking starts at 20:00. AICEP says it is a chance for Portuguese and Italian startups, investors at Web Summit and others in European tech to make contacts and explore partnerships. The event is aimed at founders seeking international growth, investors looking at European tech, and organisations that want more cross-border work.

Registration is through the event page.

A suggestion: Portuguese founders thinking about Italy, or about EU funding, could find the Italian Trade Agency, the European Investment Fund and the Commission's Representation in Portugal in one room that evening.

## What this does not claim

- **The speakers are the event page's list,** as read on 6 November. AICEP's article names no speakers. Speakers can change.
- **It is not on Web Summit's own programme,** as far as the newsroom has checked. AICEP calls it part of the programme during Web Summit week. It is in Beato, not at the summit's venue.
- **No outcome is claimed.** Nothing here says any company will meet, invest in or work with another.
- **The end time is not fixed.** The event page says "18:30 to 20:00+".
- **No details are given about any speaker** beyond the role the event page lists.
