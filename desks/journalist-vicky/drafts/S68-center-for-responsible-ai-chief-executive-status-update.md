> **Update, 17 November 2026.** This article said the Center for Responsible AI's status after 30 June 2026 was not known. A July 2026 interview now gives one account. In [an interview with Dinheiro Vivo and Diário de Notícias](https://dinheirovivo.dn.pt/economia/aps-a-queda-da-unbabel-a-ia-nacional-resiste-e-prepara-150-milhes-em-exportaes), published on 18 July 2026, the Center's chief executive said coordination had moved to Sword Health. The newspaper reports that Unbabel collapsed financially and that its assets were bought by US capital in August 2025. He said Unbabel finished and sold its products and that they stayed in Portugal. He put the execution rate, how much of the plan was delivered, at "around 87% to 88%". He gave public investment as about 49 million euros and private investment as about 27 million, 76 million in three and a half years. He said the consortium expected turnover of 150 million euros by the end of this year, mostly from exports. His examples were legal search software, hospital coding software, the Halo voice tool for patients with a neurodegenerative disease, and Sword Health's physiotherapy system. He said it cut waiting time for physiotherapy at a Porto hospital from two years to ten days. These are his statements as the newspaper reports them. The newsroom found no independent check. The funding figures measure something different from the 51.49 and 77.7 million euros above, and the note does not reconcile them.
