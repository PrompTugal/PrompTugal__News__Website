# One backer, two fields: 3xP Global's Granter and Octo Health

## In short

- 3xP Global, a Portuguese investor, led funding rounds in two startups in very different fields: Granter, which uses AI for grant applications, and Octo Health, which tracks personal health data.
- Octo Health has launched its platform in Portugal and the United States, after a €2.7 million pre-seed round led by 3xP Global.
- Granter raised €1.6 million in a round led by 3xP Global's Impact Innovation Fund, to grow into Spain, Germany and Italy.
- The same fund also co-led a round in Intuitivo, a Porto edtech startup (see S14).
- Linking them is the newsroom's suggestion. No source says the companies work together.

## The story

One Portuguese investor sits behind news from two startups this week.

### Octo Health: health records in one place

Octo Health was founded in Lisbon in 2023 and is led by its founder and chief executive, Catarina Kemper. It has launched its health tracking platform in Portugal and the United States, Portugal Startup News reports.

The platform brings together medical records, lab results, diagnostic tests and data from devices. It offers clinical testing for more than 110 biomarkers across 11 health areas, including heart health, metabolism and kidney function. It tracks changes over time and gives recommendations meant to complement medical care, not replace it.

The launch follows a €2.7 million pre-seed round led by 3xP Global, according to Jornal Económico, as cited by Portugal Startup News. The company says users keep ownership of their records and that their personal data is not sold.

### Granter: AI for public funding

Granter uses AI to help organisations find public funding, prepare applications and manage approved projects. It won Web Summit's PITCH competition in 2025. Its new €1.6 million round was led by 3xP Global through its Impact Innovation Fund, Portugal Startup News reports. The money will take it into Spain, Germany and Italy.

3xP Global says it backs companies that combine economic value with social and environmental impact, according to the same report.

### One investor, a wide range

Health data, grant applications and, with Intuitivo, school tests: three fields with one backer in common.

**Who could connect:** founders in health, education or AI who want an impact investor could look at 3xP Global's portfolio, starting with these three companies. This is a suggestion only.
