## The AI Act's first harmonised standard: EN 18286 for high-risk AI quality management
- source: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/first-harmonised-standard-supporting-ai-act-published-official-journal
- published: 19 October 2026 (the Commission's feed); "Last update 21 October 2026" on the page
- checked: 2026-11-11 01:35 UTC
- new: the Commission published in the Official Journal the reference to EN 18286:2026 (by CEN and CENELEC), a quality management system standard for providers of high-risk AI systems, through Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2274. Providers who apply it get a presumption of conformity with the AI Act's Article 17 requirements it covers. The Commission says further harmonised standards are expected.

## The Commission consults on updating its DSA election guidelines for generative AI
- source: https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/mex_26_2394
- published: 10 November 2026 (Daily News 10 / 11 / 2026)
- checked: 2026-11-11 04:30 UTC
- new: the Commission launched a consultation to update its 2024 election guidelines under the Digital Services Act, addressing the rapid evolution of technologies, "particularly generative Artificial Intelligence (AI)". It proposes measures for very large online platforms and search engines to deter information manipulation, make recommender systems safer and "adapt to the evolving AI landscape", and better protect political candidates. Submissions are invited by 23 November 2026.

## The Commission preliminarily finds TikTok's "For You" feed in breach of the DSA over "rabbit holes"
- source: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-preliminarily-finds-tiktoks-you-feed-breach-digital-services-act-harmful-rabbit-holes
- published: 6 November 2026 (the Commission's feed); "Last update 10 November 2026" on the page
- checked: 2026-11-11 06:30 UTC
- new: the Commission has preliminarily found TikTok in breach of the Digital Services Act over the "rabbit hole effect" of its recommender system: the more a user interacts with a type of content, the more the "For You" feed recommends similar content, which the Commission says can harm users' mental health, especially vulnerable adults and minors. The findings cover both risk assessment and mitigation measures.
- establishes: the Commission's preliminary finding and its reasoning, as the Commission states them. Preliminary findings are not a final decision; TikTok's response is not on this page. The full press release (linked from the page, not opened in this scan) would be the source for any detail on next steps or possible fines.
