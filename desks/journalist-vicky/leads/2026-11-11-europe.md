## The AI Act's first harmonised standard: EN 18286 for high-risk AI quality management
- source: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/first-harmonised-standard-supporting-ai-act-published-official-journal
- published: 19 October 2026 (the Commission's feed); "Last update 21 October 2026" on the page
- checked: 2026-11-11 01:35 UTC
- new: the Commission published in the Official Journal the reference to EN 18286:2026 (by CEN and CENELEC), a quality management system standard for providers of high-risk AI systems, through Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2274. Providers who apply it get a presumption of conformity with the AI Act's Article 17 requirements it covers. The Commission says further harmonised standards are expected.
- establishes: the Commission's account of the standard, its legal effect (presumption of conformity for Article 17) and its design (layered on ISO/IEC 42001, ISO 9001 or sectoral systems, not replacing them). It does not say when the high-risk requirements apply or name any company using it.
