## Spain's Invelon takes 51% of byAR, a Portuguese digital museography company
- source: https://observador.pt/2026/11/10/espanhola-invelon-entra-em-portugal-com-empresa-de-museografia-digital-byar/
- published: 10 November 2026, 15:49 (shown on the page; a Lusa agency text)
- checked: 2026-11-11 04:20 UTC
- new: Invelon Technologies, based in Lleida, Spain (extended reality, digital twins, AI, industrial metaverse; about 200 staff and EUR 20 million in turnover in 2025, it says), enters Portugal by acquiring 51% of byAR, the Portuguese company behind the immersive experiences at the Côa Museum. byAR becomes "byAR Part of Invelon", keeps its headquarters and its team of 12 in Portugal, and its founder and CEO becomes a partner in Invelon and its general manager in Portugal. The target is EUR 2.2 million in turnover in Portugal by 2027.
- establishes: the deal's terms and targets as the companies' statement gives them, through Lusa's report; not independently checked. The statement itself and either company's own page have not been read yet (the curator may want them as the primary source).
