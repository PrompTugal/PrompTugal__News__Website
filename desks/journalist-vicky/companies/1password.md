## About

1Password, a Canadian company (1), makes software that keeps passwords, passkeys and other secrets safe and controls who can sign in to which apps (2). It now extends that to AI agents, so a business can manage access for people, agents and machines on one platform, the company says (1, 2). More than 150,000 businesses use it, according to the company (1).
