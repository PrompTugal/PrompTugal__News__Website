## About

1Password makes software that keeps passwords, passkeys and other secrets safe and controls who can sign in to which apps (1). It now extends that to AI agents, so a business can manage access for people, agents and machines on one platform, the company says (2, 1). In May 2024 it said it had passed 150,000 business customers (3).
