## About

AI Infinity works as an outsourced AI department for mid-sized and large organisations (1, 2). It says its teams map where AI can help, then build, integrate and maintain automations and models inside the tools a client already uses, with governance included (2). The client keeps the intellectual property, the company says (1, 2).
