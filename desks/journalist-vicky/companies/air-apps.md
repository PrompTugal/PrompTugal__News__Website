## About

Air Apps makes everyday and productivity apps for phones, many of them powered by AI (1, 2). It says it offers more than 30 apps, available together on one subscription, and that they have been downloaded over 100 million times (2, 3). A June 2025 contributed article says it has moved its headquarters to San Francisco's Air Hub, which mirrors its workspace in Lisbon, Portugal (3).
