## About

The Data Protection Commission (DPC) is Ireland's national independent authority for protecting people's personal data in the EU (1). It is the Irish supervisory authority for the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (1). It also has powers under the Irish ePrivacy Regulations and the EU's Law Enforcement Directive (1).
