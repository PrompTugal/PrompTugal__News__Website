## About

0xbow builds privacy tools for blockchain transactions with built-in checks for illicit funds (1). Its Privacy Pools let people send funds privately while 0xbow vets each deposit, so legitimate users' funds are not mixed with those of illicit actors, the company says (1). Other privacy protocols can adopt its open-source compliance tool, the Association Set Provider, and it invites blockchain networks to offer Privacy Pools (1).
