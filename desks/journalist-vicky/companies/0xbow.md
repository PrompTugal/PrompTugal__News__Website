## About

0xbow, a US company (1), builds privacy tools for blockchain payments that also check for illicit money (1, 2). Its Privacy Pools let people send funds privately while 0xbow vets each deposit, so legitimate users' funds are not mixed with those of illicit actors, the company says (2). Other privacy protocols and blockchain networks can adopt its compliance tool, the Association Set Provider, which is open source (2).
