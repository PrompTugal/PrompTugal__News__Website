## About

Make, based in Czechia according to Web Summit (1), makes a visual platform for building automations and AI agents without relying on developers (1, 2). Teams build by prompt or by drag and drop, and connect more than 3,500 apps and AI models, it says (2). It says more than 500,000 teams use it (2).
