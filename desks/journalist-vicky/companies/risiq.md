## About

Risiq, based in Germany according to Web Summit (1), advises banks on risk, governance and regulation (1). Its software, Ribeca, helps banks measure, manage and report environmental, social and governance (ESG) risks under supervisory rules such as MaRisk, it says (1, 2). It covers risk inventories, assessments, stress tests and reporting (2).
