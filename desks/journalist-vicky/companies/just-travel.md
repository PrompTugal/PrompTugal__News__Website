## About

Just Travel, a Brazilian company (1), runs a travel technology platform for travel agencies, creators and businesses (1). Through white-label online shops, travel inventory, payments, automation and AI, it lets each partner sell travel under its own brand, the company says (1). Its site offers more than a million travel products, built-in fraud checks and set-up within 24 hours (2).
