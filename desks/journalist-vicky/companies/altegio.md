## About

Altegio, based in Hungary according to Web Summit (1), makes software for businesses that work by appointment, such as salons, clinics and studios (2). It brings online booking, client records, payments, payroll and analytics into one platform (2). It says more than 90,000 people use it and that it handles more than 4 million appointments a month (2).
