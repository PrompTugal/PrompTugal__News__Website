## About

Mastra, based in the United States according to Web Summit (1), makes a TypeScript framework for building AI agents and apps (2, 3). It gives agents memory, tools and workflows, and lets teams trace and test how they behave in production, it says (2). Web Summit's page describes it as open source (1).
