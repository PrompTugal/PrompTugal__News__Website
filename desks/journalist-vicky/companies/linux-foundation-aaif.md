## About

The Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) is an open, neutral foundation for agentic AI, working on open standards (1). It supports the people and projects building AI agents in the open, it says (1). Its site carries the Linux Foundation's copyright (1), and the Linux Foundation helps open technology projects build open source software, communities and companies (2).
