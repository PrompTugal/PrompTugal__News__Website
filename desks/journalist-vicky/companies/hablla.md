## About

Hablla makes Hablla One, a platform for answering customers on messaging channels such as WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook (1). It says it is an official Meta business solution provider for the WhatsApp Business API, and that it serves creators, resellers, agencies and businesses of all sizes (1). It lists AI automation among its features, and says it is based in Florianópolis, Brazil, with operations in São Paulo and a presence in Uruguay (1).
