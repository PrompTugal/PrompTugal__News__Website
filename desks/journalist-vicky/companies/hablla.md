## About

Hablla, a Brazilian company (1), makes Hablla One, a self-service platform that uses AI to automate conversations with customers on WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, email and phone (1). It is built for creators, small businesses and agencies, and as an official Meta business solution provider it connects companies to the WhatsApp Business API, the company says (1, 2). Hablla says it handles more than 2 million WhatsApp conversations a day (1).
