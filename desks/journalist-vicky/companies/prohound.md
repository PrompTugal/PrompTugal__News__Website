## About

ProHound makes modular IoT devices that plug into existing air-conditioning and refrigeration equipment (1). They let businesses monitor and control that equipment remotely, measure its energy use and spot faults before a breakdown, the company says (1). ProHound designs and makes the devices, called SmartModules, itself (1).
