## About

AISLE makes AI software that finds security flaws in code, fixes them and checks that each fix works, without a person doing each step (1). It can run in the cloud, on a company's own servers or on systems cut off from the internet, the company says (1). AISLE says it has found more than 525 published vulnerabilities (CVEs) (1).
