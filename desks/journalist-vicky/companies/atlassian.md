## About

Atlassian, an Australian company (1), makes software that helps teams plan, track and deliver work together, including Jira, Confluence and Loom (1, 2). Its AI agents, called Rovo, work across these tools, the company says (1, 2). It says it has more than 350,000 customers (1).
