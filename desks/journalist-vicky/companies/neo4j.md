## About

Neo4j makes graph database software, which stores and queries connected data (1). Its products include AuraDB and a Community Edition, along with tools for graph analytics (1). It also offers tools for knowledge graphs and for building AI agents (1).

Sources for this section:

1. [Neo4j, "Neo4j Graph Intelligence Platform"](https://neo4j.com/), read 9 November 2026
