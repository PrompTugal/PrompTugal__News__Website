## About

VTEX makes a commerce platform for large companies, which it describes as AI-native (1, 2). Its software lets businesses build and run online sales for consumers, other businesses and marketplaces, along with customer service with AI agents and retail media advertising (2). Web Summit lists this entry as based in Portugal (1).
