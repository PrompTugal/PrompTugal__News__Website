## About

The Centre for AI Leadership (C4AIL) is a non-profit organisation based in Singapore that promotes AI leadership in the region (1). It runs AI programmes for leaders and organisations, built on the idea of "Awareness before competency" (1). Its team includes entrepreneurs, AI scientists and executive coaches, it says (1).
