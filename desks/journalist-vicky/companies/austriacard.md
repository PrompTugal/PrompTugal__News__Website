## About

AustriaCard is based in Austria, according to Web Summit (1). AUSTRIACARD HOLDINGS AG offers payment and identification products, smart cards and card personalisation, along with digitisation and secure data management (2). It says it employs 2,400 people and is listed on the Athens and Vienna stock exchanges (2).
