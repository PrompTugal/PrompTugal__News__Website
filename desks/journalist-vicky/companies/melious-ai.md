## About

Melious AI, based in Germany according to Web Summit (1), says it offers more than 60 open-weight AI models running on European infrastructure (2). Developers reach them through one API that works with OpenAI's and Anthropic's formats (1, 2). It says requests are processed in the EU and that customers' data is never used to train models (2).
