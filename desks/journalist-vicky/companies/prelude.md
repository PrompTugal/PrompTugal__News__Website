## About

Prelude, based in France according to Web Summit (1), makes tools that help apps check that new sign-ups are genuine (1, 2). Its APIs handle phone verification, authentication and messaging, it says (1). It combines device, network and sign-up signals into one decision, to block fake accounts and SMS fraud, the company says (1, 2).
