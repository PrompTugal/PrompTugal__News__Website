## About

Crystal Intelligence makes software that helps firms check crypto transactions and meet compliance rules (1). It combines blockchain analysis and AI for transaction monitoring, sanctions screening and investigations, and keeps the evidence behind each decision, it says (1). It says regulators, banks and exchanges use it (1).
