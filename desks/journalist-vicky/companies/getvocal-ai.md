## About

GetVocal AI, based in France according to Web Summit (1), makes AI agents that answer customers for large companies' contact centres (1, 2). Its agents handle the conversation and pass a case to a human supervisor when it needs approval, it says (2). It says every interaction can be traced and audited, and a person can step in at any point (2).
