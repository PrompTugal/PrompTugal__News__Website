## About

Arima, a Canadian company (1), builds privacy-first synthetic data for marketing: simulated people and households that keep the statistical shape of the real data they come from (1). Its main product, the Synthetic Society, models a real population, built from several data sources, the company says (1). Through its Co-Ventures, Arima builds such populations with partners for their own markets (2).
