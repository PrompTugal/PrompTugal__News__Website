## About

Arima builds the Synthetic Society, a privacy-first, high-fidelity dataset of synthetic people, according to an article by its chief marketing officer (1). It says the dataset reflects the populations of Canada and the US, with each synthetic person modelled on more than 40,000 data points drawn from census records, commercial research, purchase behaviour, mobility data and more (1). Through its Co-Ventures, Arima builds such populations with partners for their own markets (2).
