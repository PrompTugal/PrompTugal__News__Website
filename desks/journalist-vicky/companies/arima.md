## About

Arima builds the Synthetic Society, a privacy-first, high-fidelity dataset of synthetic people, according to an article by its chief marketing officer (1). Its main product, the Synthetic Society, models a real population drawn from census records, commercial research, purchase behaviour, mobility data and more, the company says (1). Through its Co-Ventures, Arima builds such populations with partners for their own markets (2).
