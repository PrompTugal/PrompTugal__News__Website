## About

Contents, based in Italy according to Web Summit (1), makes an AI platform that companies use to create, translate and manage content at scale (2). It covers copy, blogs, search optimisation, translations, images and video, with approval steps and brand rules, it says (2). It also offers the same work as a managed service, through its own digital agency (2).
