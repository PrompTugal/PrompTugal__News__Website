## About

Fretefy makes logistics software (1). Its site lists TMS, YMS and DMS solutions, a marketplace, container transport and crossdocking (1). It now says "Fretefy agora é Mobiis" (Fretefy is now Mobiis), and lists Mobiis's locations in Campinas, Fortaleza and Curitiba, in Brazil (1). Mobiis describes itself as complete software for logistics, and its site still refers to the "plataforma Fretefy" (Fretefy platform) (2).
