## About

Runpod runs a cloud for building and running AI on GPUs (1). Developers use it to train, fine-tune and deploy AI models, with GPUs on demand, serverless endpoints and multi-node clusters (1). It says its GPUs are deployed across 31 regions worldwide (1).
