## About

Cerebras builds computers for running AI, based on what it calls the world's biggest chip, its Wafer-Scale Engine (1). It says its latest system, the CS-4, runs AI models up to 30 times faster than GPUs (1). Customers can use them on their own premises or in the cloud, the company says (1).
