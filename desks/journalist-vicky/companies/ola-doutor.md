## About

Olá Doutor, a Brazilian company (1), runs a health app that connects patients with licensed doctors through online consultations (1, 2). Patients can chat with a doctor straight away or book a consultation with a doctor and time they choose, from their phone (1, 2). It offers medical guidance, test requests, medical certificates and prescriptions, the company says (1, 2).
