## About

ChatPlace, a Portuguese company (1), makes software that helps creators and small businesses grow and sell on social media (1). It offers an AI content studio, an AI manager that answers messages and comments around the clock, and chatbot funnels with rewards for followers (1, 2). It works on Instagram, TikTok, Telegram and WhatsApp, the company says (1).
