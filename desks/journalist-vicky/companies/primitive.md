## About

Primitive, based in the United States according to Web Summit (1), gives AI agents their own email address, so they can receive and send email (1, 2). Mail sent to a company's addresses arrives in its systems as structured messages, and agents reply in the same thread from the company's own domain, it says (2). Primitive runs the mail servers and their email authentication, it says (2).
