## About

PolyAI makes voice AI agents that handle customer conversations for large companies (1). The agents run on PolyAI's own models and can act in the systems a company already uses, it says (1). Companies design, build and run them on its Agentic Dialog Platform, across the channels their customers use (1).

Sources for this section:

1. [PolyAI, "PolyAI: Enterprise Voice AI, The Agentic Dialog Platform"](https://poly.ai/), read 9 November 2026
