## About

Cognizant is a technology services company that builds AI and other software systems for large businesses (1, 2). It helps clients redesign their customer experiences and processes and modernise their technology, it says (2). The company reports 356,700 employees worldwide and 21.1 billion dollars of revenue in 2025 (2).
