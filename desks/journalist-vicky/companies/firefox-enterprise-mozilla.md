## About

Firefox Enterprise is Mozilla's version of the Firefox browser for organisations (1). It can run on a company's own servers, so the company decides where its data, policies and browser management live (1). It adds controls such as stopping sensitive data from leaving the browser and allowing or blocking AI tools, Mozilla says (1).
