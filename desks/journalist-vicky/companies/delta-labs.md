## About

Delta Labs, based in Switzerland according to Web Summit (1), makes Elaiia, software that simulates how customers are likely to respond to a decision (2). Teams can test concepts, prices and campaigns on a model of their customers before they act, it says (2). The model starts from general customer data and can be tuned with a company's own data (2).
