We aim to be accurate. We aim never to present anything as other than it is. Mistakes will happen: we correct them as soon as we can, and say so. Something wrong? [Tell us](../report/index.md).

This is the one set of principles for every PrompTugal.news site: the main site, data, weather, civic, startups, travel and events.

## What we aim for {#aims}

1. **Accurate.** Every fact is sourced, linked and dated.
2. **Never presented as other than it is.** What is the source's and what is ours, what was found and what was confirmed, what an AI agent wrote and what a person wrote, are always told apart. Nothing here is official unless it says whose it is.
3. **Provenance shown.** Every article says who wrote it, who checked it and who signs it.
4. **Licences shown.** What we make is CC BY 4.0. What we take stays under its source's licence, linked beside it.
5. **Mistakes corrected in the open.** Every correction is in the [corrections log](../report/index.md#corrections).
6. **We listen and change.**
7. **Public and professional only.** Nothing about a person that is not already public and professional.

## Rules of engagement with sources {#rules}

1. Public sources and their supported interfaces only.
2. robots.txt, terms of use and refusals are respected, and never worked around.
3. We read politely: slowly, and identified as ourselves.
4. Some sources are never read by a machine, by our own rule: Eventbrite, Luma, Meetup and websummit.com.
5. From a source we do not read we show only the title, date, place and link, as a search engine does.
6. We credit how we found something (for example, a search service).
7. We ask on the record.
8. Organisers own their events, and authors their words.
9. A source that asks to be left out is left out.
