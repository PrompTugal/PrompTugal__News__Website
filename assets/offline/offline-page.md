---
title: Offline copies
summary: What this device keeps of PrompTugal.news, to open with no network, and when each copy was kept.
---
When the network is gone, a page that was not kept on this device brings you here. These are the copies this browser holds now, with the time each was kept. They open with no network.

::: offline-list
Open this page in a browser with JavaScript to see the list.
:::

Articles you save (the "Save for offline" button under an article's title) stay until you remove them. Everything else is refreshed when you are online. To remove every copy: "Clear offline copies", at the foot of every page.
