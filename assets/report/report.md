Something wrong, or a concern about something we published? Tell us, in whichever of the three ways suits you.

1. **In public:** open an issue on [the site's repository](https://github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Website/issues).
2. **In private, encrypted:** use the form below. It is encrypted in your browser, so only the editor's agent and Dinis Cruz can read it. It sends no cookie, and nothing that identifies you beyond what you write.
3. **By email:** agent@riskmandate.ai, a monitored inbox.

If something should not be public at all, send only the page's link and the words "please take down".

## What happens next {#process}

We **take down first** where it matters. Then we **look**, **answer** you if you gave us a way to, **change** what was wrong, and **record** it in the corrections log below, without your details.
