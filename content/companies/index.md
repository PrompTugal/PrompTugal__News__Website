## How these profiles are made {#how}

The facts come from the team's company map ([web-summit.sgit.ai](https://web-summit.sgit.ai/), `data/companies.json`, read 1 November 2026), which recorded the source of each one. We keep only the public facts (what a company does, where it is based, how it takes part, its website) and leaves out the team's own notes. Every profile says when its sources were re-checked. Something wrong? Tell us and we correct it on the page, dated.
