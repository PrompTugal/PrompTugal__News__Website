## In the Algarve {#algarve}

{.rows}
- **[Algarve Evolution](../algarve-evolution/index.md)**: Faro-based association building the Algarve's innovation ecosystem. Its own section is the first.
- **University of Algarve**: lead promoter of the 2025 competition that sent 24 Algarve startups to Web Summit Lisbon ([ANJE regulation](https://anje.pt/wp-content/uploads/Regulamento-Participacao-Startups-Websummit-2025_01_09_2025_Paginado-versao-EN.pdf)); co-organiser of the Algarve Tech Hub Summit and a partner of Algarve Evolution's education work ([Portugal Startup News](https://portugalstartupnews.com/2026/11/01/algarve-evolution-aims-to-support-500-companies-and-help-create-150-startups-by-2032-in-push-beyond-tourism/)).
- **Algarve STP**: project partner in the same 2025 competition and a jury member; co-organiser of the Algarve Tech Hub Summit; runs Venture Lab, which selected 12 startups and SMEs in July 2026 ([postal.pt](https://postal.pt/algarve/venture-lab-by-algarvestp-seleciona-12-empresas-para-acelerar-crescimento/)).
- **AMAL**: project partner in the 2025 competition ([ANJE regulation](https://anje.pt/wp-content/uploads/Regulamento-Participacao-Startups-Websummit-2025_01_09_2025_Paginado-versao-EN.pdf)); one of six Venture Lab partners (postal.pt, above).
- **NERA**: project partner and jury member in the 2025 competition (ANJE regulation, above); one of six Venture Lab partners.
- **ANJE**: a jury member in the 2025 competition (ANJE regulation, above), which also publishes the regulation; one of six Venture Lab partners.

## Nationally {#national}

{.rows}
- **Startup Portugal**: recognised Algarve Evolution's incubator in January 2026 (Portugal Startup News, above).
- **Junior Achievement Portugal**: runs "The Company" with Algarve Evolution, an entrepreneurship programme for high school students (Portugal Startup News, above).
- **CCDR-Algarve**: the regional body for which Algarve Evolution's Innovation and Collaboration Platform for Digitalization prepares governance recommendations ([algarveevolution.pt](https://www.algarveevolution.pt)).
