## In the Algarve {#algarve}

{.rows}
- **[Algarve Evolution](../algarve-evolution/index.md)**: Faro-based association building the Algarve's innovation ecosystem. Its own section is the first.
- **University of Algarve**: lead promoter of the 2025 competition that sent 24 Algarve startups to Web Summit Lisbon ([ANJE regulation](https://anje.pt/wp-content/uploads/Regulamento-Participacao-Startups-Websummit-2025_01_09_2025_Paginado-versao-EN.pdf)); co-organiser of the Algarve Tech Hub Summit and a partner of Algarve Evolution's education work ([Portugal Startup News](https://portugalstartupnews.com/2026/11/01/algarve-evolution-aims-to-support-500-companies-and-help-create-150-startups-by-2032-in-push-beyond-tourism/)).
- **Algarve STP**: project partner in the same 2025 competition and a jury member; co-organiser of the Algarve Tech Hub Summit; runs Venture Lab, which selected 12 startups and SMEs in July 2026 ([postal.pt](https://postal.pt/algarve/venture-lab-by-algarvestp-seleciona-12-empresas-para-acelerar-crescimento/)).
- **AMAL**: project partner in the 2025 competition ([ANJE regulation](https://anje.pt/wp-content/uploads/Regulamento-Participacao-Startups-Websummit-2025_01_09_2025_Paginado-versao-EN.pdf)); one of six Venture Lab partners (postal.pt, above).
- **NERA**: project partner and jury member in the 2025 competition (ANJE regulation, above); one of six Venture Lab partners.
- **ANJE**: a jury member in the 2025 competition (ANJE regulation, above), which also publishes the regulation; one of six Venture Lab partners.

## Two in more detail {#detail}

### University of Algarve {#ualg}

Founded in 1979, headquartered in Faro, with campuses in Faro (Gambelas and Penha) and Portimão; about 10,000 students, about a fifth international ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Algarve), read 6 November 2026; a tertiary source, to be re-checked at ualg.pt). Its CRIA division, for entrepreneurship and technology transfer, sat on the jury of the 2025 Web Summit competition and was lead promoter of that project (ANJE regulation, above).

### Algarve STP {#stp}

The Algarve Systems and Technology Partnership Association: a non-profit representing the Algarve's technology and innovation ecosystem, co-founder of the Algarve Tech Hub brand, recognised as a Digital Innovation Hub, and manager of incubation and acceleration infrastructure. Its members and partners include the University of Algarve, seven of the region's main municipalities and strategic associations including DOCAPESCA, ANJE and Algarve Evolution. It joined TecParques, the Portuguese association of science and technology parks, as an associate member, reported in February 2025 ([postal.pt, 21 February 2025](https://postal.pt/algarve/algarve-stp-junta-se-a-tecparques-e-impulsiona-a-inovacao-na-regiao/), read 6 November 2026).

AMAL, NERA and ANJE are listed above only by the roles our sources give them; we have not yet read their own pages.

## Nationally {#national}

{.rows}
- **Startup Portugal**: recognised Algarve Evolution's incubator in January 2026 (Portugal Startup News, above). On its own pages it calls itself "Portugal's national reference for entrepreneurship", active since 2016 and funded through a programme contract with IAPMEI, European funds and its own revenue; its programmes include the National Incubator Network and Road 2 Web Summit ([startupportugal.com](https://startupportugal.com/about-us/), read 7 November 2026; the Road 2 Web Summit page itself was not found at the address tried). Its Ecosystem Report 2025 presents 5,091 startups (4,719 in 2024), turnover of EUR 2,856 million and about 28,000 employees, as the organisation states them ([ecoreport.startupportugal.com](https://ecoreport.startupportugal.com/), landing page read 7 November 2026; the full report and the figures' underlying sources not checked).
- **Startup Portugal at Web Summit 2026**: its Road 2 Web Summit programme, run with Web Summit, brings 78 Portuguese startups from 24 sectors to the summit ([startupportugal.com](https://startupportugal.com/), read 7 November 2026). It has a Portugal stand, Pavilion 1, Stand 145, 10 to 12 November, with Lisboa Municipality; the partners it names are AI2, IAPMEI, AICEP, Portugal Ventures and Turismo de Portugal, and it publishes the stand's agenda for the three days ([Get ready for Web Summit 2026!](https://startupportugal.com/noticia/get-ready-for-web-summit-2026/), dated 6 November 2026; times and titles may change).
- **AICEP (Portuguese Trade & Investment Agency)**: describes itself as the public body that promotes inward investment and the internationalisation of the Portuguese economy, with an external network in around 50 countries ([portugalglobal.pt](https://www.portugalglobal.pt/en/), read 7 November 2026). Its own figures are not checked. Not to be confused with aicep.pt, which belongs to a different association.
- **Portugal Ventures**: describes itself as venture capital investing in startups with the potential to internationalise, with offices in Porto and Lisbon; named on the Portugal stand as "Portugal Ventures (BPF)" ([portugalventures.pt](https://www.portugalventures.pt/), read 7 November 2026).
- **ANI (National Innovation Agency)**: its page says its capital is held in equal parts by the Ministry of Education, Science and Innovation and the Ministry of Economy and Territorial Cohesion, and that it runs the FITEC fund ([ani.pt](https://ani.pt/), read 7 November 2026).
- **IAPMEI**: a news item on its front page, dated 2 November 2026, says that from 9 to 12 November it will be "once again present" at Web Summit in Lisbon, focused on supporting companies, business opportunities and international talent ([iapmei.pt](https://www.iapmei.pt/), read 7 November 2026; translated from Portuguese). Its own about page was not read.
- **Turismo de Portugal**: named by Startup Portugal as a partner of the Portugal stand (see the stand post above). Its own front page ([turismodeportugal.pt](https://www.turismodeportugal.pt/en/Pages/homepage.aspx), read 7 November 2026) is a navigation menu with no Web Summit item, so nothing more is claimed.
- **Beta-i**: describes itself as working with teams on collaborative innovation programmes, with offices in Lisbon, Brussels, Boston and Sao Paulo ([beta-i.com](https://beta-i.com/), read 7 November 2026; its numbers are loaded by script and were not read).
- **Junior Achievement Portugal**: runs "The Company" with Algarve Evolution, an entrepreneurship programme for high school students (Portugal Startup News, above).
- **CCDR-Algarve**: the regional body for which Algarve Evolution's Innovation and Collaboration Platform for Digitalization prepares governance recommendations ([algarveevolution.pt](https://www.algarveevolution.pt)).
