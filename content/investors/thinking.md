We want to be useful to the industry, not only to sell to it. This is what we think, and why PrompTugal is built the way it is.

## Where technology is going {#technology}

- **Agentic workflows become how work is done.** Not one assistant, but teams of agents with roles, names, keys, written limits, budgets and a record of every run, with a person who decides. Most organisations talk about this; PrompTugal runs one every hour, in public.
- **Every page is read by people and by agents.** A site that is not readable by AI agents (a markdown twin, an llms.txt, data as JSON) will be read badly or not at all.
- **Open by default.** Code, data and decisions in the open are faster to trust and faster to build on.

## Where the news industry is going {#news}

- **Trust is the product.** Every fact with its source and the date it was read; every article signed by who wrote it; every correction on the record.
- **The reader owns their data.** No tracking, no selling attention; the reader's history and saved articles in their own vault.
- **Pay for what you read, a little at a time,** instead of advertising.
- **News is operational:** research, writing, checking, design and release as a pipeline of people and agents, released many times a day.

## What we wish the industry would do {#wish}

- Publish its sources and sign its articles.
- Make every page readable by agents as well as people.
- Stop tracking readers to sell them.
- Try agentic teams with written limits and a human who decides, and show the record.
