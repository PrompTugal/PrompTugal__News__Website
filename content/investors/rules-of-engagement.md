We would rather talk to fewer people who are excited about the technology than to many who are not. So we ask the people we talk to to come warm.

## Before we talk {#before}

1. **Use it.** Read the news, build [your day at the summit](../../my-day/index.html), [ask the newsroom](../../ask/index.html) a question, top up a few euros and read. You will be a user before you are an investor; that is the point.
2. **Read everything, or have your agent read it.** This section, [the newsroom](../../newsroom/index.html), [the decisions](../../newsroom/decisions/index.html), and [the public repository](https://github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Website): the code, the data, the plan and the agents' notes. Every page has a markdown twin and the site has an [llms.txt](../../llms.txt); point your own AI agent at it and ask it what you want to know.
3. **Come with your questions.** By then you know what we built, how, and how fast.

## What is public and what is not {#public}

- **Public:** the product, the strategy, the business plan, the history, the competition, the decisions, the code, the data and the agents' own records. When it changes, these pages change.
- **Public:** the round: a seed round of under €1M.
- **For a conversation:** the valuation, the split and the terms. We publish no revenue, cost or projection: we do not have those numbers to give honestly yet. When we do, they come here.
- **Not published:** anything private about a person, and anything said in a private conversation.

## What we offer {#offer}

A stake in an open-source company, with everything visible from the first day, and a seat on the journey: you see each release as it happens.
