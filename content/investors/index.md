## The pitch, in one page {#pitch}

**We are building the next generation of news publishing, and it is already working.** PrompTugal.news went from an empty repository to a live publication in days: articles with every fact sourced and every article signed, profiles of the companies at the summit, the summit's days, a graph of who connects to whom, search, an assistant that answers from the site, a reading account with micropayments, and [an app for iPhone and iPad](https://ios.app.promptugal.news/) in internal beta. It is released many times a day, in the open.

**What we sell is the agentic workflow.** Many people talk about building agentic workflows; this one runs a newsroom every hour. Agents research, write, check, design, release and review, each with its own name, key and limits, and a named human decides. It is the operationalisation of news: people and agents working together, with every step on the record. The latest count, given by Dinis Cruz on 7 November 2026: about 111 agents at work across his projects, and it is scaling.

**The future is already here.** Micropayments instead of advertising. No tracking of readers. Serverless, so the stack costs very little to run. Levels of access for readers, editors and agents. Every page readable by people and by AI agents alike. See [what is working today](what-works/index.html).

**Why invest.** Look at what was done in this time, at the execution and at the agentic flows. PrompTugal is an open-source company: an investor joins the journey with everything already visible.

## A founder's strategy, executed {#founder}

PrompTugal did not start this week. Dinis Cruz runs several open-source startups under one strategy ([open-source.sgit.ai](https://open-source.sgit.ai/)), and PrompTugal is that strategy executed: it is only possible because of years of work on sgit (the vaults, keys and signatures underneath) and on the agent-run newsroom at newsroom.sgit.ai. It came together in days because everything it stands on was already built ([how we got here](how-we-got-here/index.html)). Having many startups is the point: each one reuses the others.

## Two phases, said plainly {#phases}

Phase one is the explorers' phase: a small team and a seed round, to prove the company can be profitable and ready for Series A, ending in an exit for the first round of founders and investors (no date is set). Phase two (more professional, more execution, more events, growth) is a different type of company, and Dinis Cruz says so: he likes to start companies and prepares his own exit from the beginning ("making myself redundant is what good leadership should do"). If PrompTugal really takes off, it becomes his next three years; he does not close that door, but he is pragmatic about what is most likely.

**The investor's three questions:** can this be a profitable business? Can this company be bought in the future? Is this the team to execute it? If the answer is yes, yes, yes, invest. ([The investment, deck 5](decks/index.html).)

## The company {#company}

- **The legal entity:** My Feeds (MYFEEDS AI, UNIPESSOAL, LDA), a Portuguese single-member limited company in the Algarve, and the publisher of PrompTugal.news. The ideas behind PrompTugal began as My Feeds, a personalised news project; the company is expected to take the PrompTugal name.
- **Ownership:** Dinis Cruz is its sole director and 100% shareholder.
- **The round:** a seed round of **under $1M**, for the explorer phase: to make the company profitable and ready for Series A. Valuation, split and terms are for a conversation; revenue, costs and projections are not published because we do not have them to give honestly yet.
