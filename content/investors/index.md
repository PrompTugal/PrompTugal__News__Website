## The pitch, in one page {#pitch}

**We are building the next generation of news publishing, and it is already working.** PrompTugal.news went from an empty repository to a live publication in a week: articles with every fact sourced and every article signed, profiles of the companies at the summit, the summit's days, a graph of who connects to whom, search, an assistant that answers from the site, a reading account with micropayments, and an app. It is released many times a day, in the open.

**What we sell is the agentic workflow.** Many people talk about building agentic workflows; this one runs a newsroom every hour. Agents research, write, check, design, release and review, each with its own name, key and limits, and a named human decides. It is the operationalisation of news: people and agents working together, with every step on the record. The latest count, given by Dinis Cruz on 7 November 2026: about 111 agents at work across his projects, and it is scaling.

**The future is already here.** Micropayments instead of advertising. No tracking of readers. Serverless, so the stack costs very little to run. Levels of access for readers, editors and agents. Every page readable by people and by AI agents alike. See [what is working today](what-works/index.html).

**Why invest.** Look at what was done in this time, at the execution and at the agentic flows. PrompTugal is an open-source company: an investor joins the journey with everything already visible.

## The company {#company}

- **The legal entity:** My Feeds (MYFEEDS AI, UNIPESSOAL, LDA), a Portuguese single-member limited company in the Algarve, and the publisher of PrompTugal.news. The ideas behind PrompTugal began as My Feeds, a personalised news project; the company is expected to take the PrompTugal name.
- **Ownership:** Dinis Cruz is its sole director and 100% shareholder.
- **What we do not say yet:** no amount, valuation, stage, revenue, cost or projection, and the company's accounts are not quoted. We do not have those numbers to give honestly yet, and when we do they will be on these pages.
- Source: Dinis Cruz, 7 November 2026 ([the decision](../newsroom/decisions/promptugal-for-investors/index.html)).
