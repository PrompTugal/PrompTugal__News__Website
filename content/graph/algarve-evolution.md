## What to know before you read them {#care}

- **Dashed** means nothing found yet (Web Summit Lisbon 2026), or an event listed on Algarve Evolution's calendar but organised by someone else.
- **Self-reported**: member numbers and the 2032 targets are the organisation's own, as the press reports them.
- **Not found**: a public member list, so no company in the newsroom's graph is linked to Algarve Evolution yet. When one is public, it will be drawn with its source.
