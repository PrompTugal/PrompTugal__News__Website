Web Summit has been in Lisbon since 2016. This picture sets the editions side by side: one column each, as tall as the attendance the organiser reported, with the dates, the venue and the source one tap away. Some editions have no figure in what we have read, and the picture leaves those empty rather than guess.

## How to read it {#read}

- **Solid** columns were read at source: the organiser's own press releases for 2022 and 2023, its own headline for 2024 (the post's body was not readable), and its own 2025 post (title, description and opening; the numbers list not yet read). **Paler** ones (2016 to 2018) come from Wikipedia's table, which is a tertiary source. **Dashed** means we only have a search summary and have not read the page (none today).
- **n/a** means no attendance figure was found for that year (2019 and 2021); 2020 was held online.
- The figures are the organiser's own claims, as the press repeats them, and the sources do not always agree: 2025's 71,386 is below 2024's 71,528 (both the organiser's own, announced mid-event), so "record attendance" does not hold for 2025; the records claimed are for investors and startups. The organiser's own 2025 post gives 71,386; one recap says 71,368.
- **Tap** an edition for its dates, venue, figure and source (new tab). **Save as PNG** downloads the picture as it is now.
