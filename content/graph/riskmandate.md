[PrompTugal.news is powered by RiskMandate](../../riskmandate/index.html), and this is not independent of it: we say so on every page. RiskMandate gives the newsroom three questions, and this picture shows them at the centre: **reach** (what can the agent actually do?), **mandate** (what was it authorised to do?) and **gap** (who controls the difference?). Around them, on equal terms, is every company of the newsroom's graph, grouped by field.

## What this picture does not show {#not}

- **No relationship.** A line from RiskMandate to a company would be a claim. None is drawn, because no company's relationship with RiskMandate is public in what riskmandate.ai and this site say. When one is, it will be drawn with its source.
- **No ranking and no target list.** Every company is on the ring on the same terms. Position follows the field, then the name, nothing else.
- **No answers.** The questions are asked of a company, never answered for it. For every company on the ring: we have not asked yet.

## How to use it {#read}

Tap a company for where its question stands and a link to its page, which opens in a new tab. **Replay** redraws the ring from the centre. **Save as PNG** downloads the picture as it is now.
