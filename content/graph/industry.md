The centre is the industry. Each wedge is a field, as the newsroom files its companies; its width is how many companies it holds. Each dot is a company, and the more connected it is in the graph, the closer to the centre it sits. The squares on the outer rim are the summit's programme tracks, placed on the field they map to, so you can see where the stage meets the market.

## How to read it {#read}

- **Wedges** are fields; a wide wedge is a crowded field.
- **Dots** are companies. Tap one for what it does and a link to its page, which opens in a new tab.
- **Squares** are programme tracks. Only the tracks that map to one of these fields appear.
- **Replay** redraws the map from the centre. **Save as PNG** downloads the picture as it is now.
