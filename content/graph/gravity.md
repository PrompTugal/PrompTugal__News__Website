Every dot is a company from the newsroom's graph. A well is a field (security, AI agents, GRC and so on) or, if you switch, a country; its size is the combined PageRank of its companies, and each dot's size is how many connections that company has in the graph. The dots turn slowly so the heavy wells read first.

## How to read it {#read}

- **Wells** are the centres of gravity: the bigger the well, the more of the graph's weight sits there.
- **Dots** are companies. Tap one for its record and a link to its page, which opens in a new tab.
- **By field or by country** regroups the same companies. **Save as PNG** downloads the picture as it is now.

The data is [graph/explorer.json](../../api/v1/graph/explorer.json), the same graph the [explorer](../index.html) loads; every company named here has its own page with sources.
