Each bubble sits on a country and its area grows with the number of companies from that country in the newsroom's graph. A line runs from every country to Lisbon, where Web Summit is held on 9 to 12 November 2026. Pick a field to see only its companies: the bubbles shrink and some countries drop away.

## How to read it {#read}

- **Bubbles** are countries; **lines** go to Lisbon. A line says "a company from here is on the graph", nothing more.
- **Country** is the one the graph holds for a company, which for some is where the speaker's listing puts it rather than a sourced headquarters; a company page shows its source.
- **Zoom to Europe** opens up the crowded part; the bubbles keep their area and the lines still run to Lisbon.
- **Tap** a country for its companies, each linking to its page in a new tab. **Save as PNG** downloads the picture as it is now.
