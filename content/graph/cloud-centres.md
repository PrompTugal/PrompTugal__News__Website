Dinis asked where the Google, Amazon (AWS) and Microsoft centres of gravity are among the summit's companies: partners, speakers from, companies built on them, companies they invest in. This is the first answer, and most of it is a map of what we do not yet know. Each cell below is one relation to one centre, in one of three states, with its source.

## How to read it {#read}

- **Filled** means found, with the public page it comes from, read on 6 November 2026.
- **Dashed** means we searched and found nothing. That is not proof of absence: the partners list has 8 pages and only the first was readable, and speakers by employer are still to check.
- **Empty** means not looked at yet. The newsroom's data has no "built on" or "invested by" relation for its companies yet; it needs edges from each company's own site.
- **Tap** a cell for what was found, the source and, where there is one, the company's page, which opens in a new tab. **Save as PNG** downloads the picture as it is now.
