Dinis asked where the Google, Amazon (AWS) and Microsoft centres of gravity are among the companies around Web Summit: who sells on them, who names them, who announced what in Web Summit week. This is the answer so far, and it is smaller than it was: **it is not everything, on purpose.** Web Summit's licence does not let us republish data from its site, so on 11 November 2026 we took off whatever rested only on websummit.com (its partners list, its speakers page, its press releases and posts). What you see has another public source, cited when you tap a cell, and each taken-off cell comes back when we find one (we call these shadow sources: the same fact where we may read it).

## How to read it {#read}

- **Filled** means found, with the public page it comes from.
- **Dashed** means we searched and found nothing, which is not proof of absence. **Dotted** means taken off on 11 November 2026 because it rested only on websummit.com.
- **Empty** means not looked at yet: which companies a centre invests in is not looked at. "Companies whose own pages name it" is filled from 14 of the 17 other companies in the newsroom's data (three refuse a plain fetch and are not read): a company page rarely says "partner" outright, so each line says what the page shows (a marketplace listing, a joint offering, a monitoring product, a rival comparison).
- **Tap** a cell for what was found, the source and, where there is one, the company's page, which opens in a new tab. **Save as PNG** downloads the picture as it is now.
