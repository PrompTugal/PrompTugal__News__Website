## In short

- AICEP, Portugal's trade and investment agency, reported on 24 November 2026 that Portugal is expected to reach 910 million dollars (829 million euros) in venture capital in 2026.
- The article puts the 2025 figure at 320 million dollars (291 million euros) and says Portugal would move from 14th to 10th in Europe.
- It names Tekever's 580 million dollar (528 million euro) round, in September 2026, as one of the main reasons.
- These are estimates from Atomico's State of European Tech 2026 report, not final totals.

## The story

On 24 November 2026 AICEP published an item in its Portugal Global magazine headed "Portugal enters Europe's top 10 for tech investment". It says the country is expected to reach 910 million dollars of venture capital investment in 2026, against 320 million in 2025, and would rise to 10th place among European countries from 14th.

The article credits the estimates to Atomico's State of European Tech 2026 report and gives ca.finance as its source. It describes 2026 as "one of its most significant years" for Portugal in attracting capital, and names the financing round of Tekever, a Portuguese company that builds autonomous systems and artificial intelligence technology, as "one of the main factors behind this development". The round was worth 580 million dollars in September 2026.

The article gives no breakdown of the rest of the 2026 figure, so a reader cannot tell from it how much of the rise is that one round and how much is many smaller ones.

The article states the growth as 187 per cent. The two figures it gives, 320 and 910 million dollars, work out at about 184 per cent. We give the figures and leave the percentage out of our own summary.

## What this does not claim

- **These are projections.** The article calls them expected figures for the full year. Atomico's report itself was not read for Portugal's numbers, so they are as AICEP relays them.
- **Not a ranking we checked.** The move from 14th to 10th is the article's statement of the report's estimate.
- **No split of the total.** How much of 910 million dollars is the Tekever round is not given.
- **Nothing here is investment advice,** and nothing says the capital will reach early-stage companies.
