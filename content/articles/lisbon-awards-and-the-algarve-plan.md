## In short

- [Unicorn Factory Lisboa](../organisations/unicorn-factory-lisboa/index.html) announced the winners of its 2026 Entrepreneurship Awards on 27 October, at the Técnico Innovation Center, on its fourth anniversary.
- Three of the seven winners build with AI: an AI computer-vision company sold to Motorola Solutions, a real-time AI that stops scam calls, and a clinical decision-support system.
- [Algarve Evolution](../organisations/algarve-evolution/index.html), in Faro, aims to support 500 companies, help create 150 startups and reach 50,000 people across the region by 2032.
- It is already offering free AI and robotics consultancy to 20 tourism businesses, in a pilot.

## The story

**The Lisbon awards.** Unicorn Factory Lisboa revealed its 2026 winners from the finalists it had announced, at a ceremony on 27 October at the Técnico Innovation Center in Lisbon, as reported by Portugal Startup News:

| Award | Winner | What the report says |
|---|---|---|
| Ecosystem | Ricardo Marvão | Co-founder of Beta-i, an innovation consultancy, and of Singularity University Portugal and European Innovation Academy Portugal |
| Entrepreneur of the Year (João Vasconcelos Award) | Vasco Lopes | Former CEO of DeepNeuronic, which built AI computer vision on existing camera networks and was acquired by Motorola Solutions; now head of vertical AI solutions there |
| Most Promising Startup | [BloodFlow](../organisations/bloodflow/index.html) | A clinical decision-support system that works inside electronic medical records |
| Most Impactful Startup | [knok](../organisations/knok/index.html) | A digital healthcare platform connecting patients, professionals and providers |
| Youth Startup | [RTBeamTech](../organisations/rtbeamtech/index.html) | A spin-off from LIP and Instituto Superior Técnico, measuring radiation beams for ultra-high-dose-rate (FLASH) radiotherapy |
| Rising Female | Rita Barbosa | Founder of Guardian Labs EU, real-time AI to detect and stop scam campaigns and fraudulent calls |
| International Personality | Fernando Jardim | Co-founder and adviser at the 351 Portuguese Startup Association |

RTBeamTech is also one of the six Técnico spin-offs on the university's stand at Web Summit, from 10 to 12 November ([our article on Lisbon's universities](lisbon-universities-at-web-summit.html)).

**The Algarve's plan.** Algarve Evolution, a Faro organisation that began in 2018 as a group of local entrepreneurs, has set targets for 2032: 500 companies supported, 150 new startups and 50,000 people reached, as reported by Portugal Startup News. Its aim is not to replace tourism but to widen the region's economy. "We have built the ecosystem. We now need to actually make the ecosystem work," Antonio Guerreiro, its executive director, told Portugal Startup News.

The starting point is small. A study by Informa D&B, presented in November 2025, counted 115 startups in the Algarve in 2024, 78% of them in information technology and communications, with about 25 million euros in turnover and 365 employees between them. 95% were microenterprises with fewer than 10 people. Their turnover grew 29% a year from 2021 to 2024, against 24.2% for startups nationally.

What Algarve Evolution runs today, according to the report:

- **118 members**, whose businesses together turn over about 100 million euros and employ about 1,000 people (the members' figures, not jobs it created).
- **Founder2Founder**, a six-month growth programme launched in October 2026: eight companies chosen from 41 applications, each with a mentor and three measurable targets. It takes no equity. Its incubator was recognised by Startup Portugal in January 2026.
- **AI and robotics for tourism**: through INOVA ALGARVE 3.0 it leads a digital transition programme for tourism businesses, with a pilot offering free, personalised consultancy on AI and robotics to 20 of them.
- **Algarve Digital Innovators**, a network that has mapped 24 communities across the region's 16 municipalities, with 5,839 memberships, 144 consultants and mentors and 18 potential investors, and plans to 2030 that depend on funding.

A suggestion: founders building AI for tourism, and investors looking beyond Lisbon and Porto, could start with Algarve Evolution; anyone following AI in health could look up BloodFlow and knok.

## What this does not claim

- **The figures are the organisations' own**, as Portugal Startup News reports them, or from the Informa D&B study as that report describes it. PrompTugal.news has not read the study or checked the figures.
- **The targets are plans.** The 2032 targets, and the network's 2030 targets, are ambitions; the report says the network's depend on funding.
- **None of the winners or of Algarve Evolution is confirmed at Web Summit 2026** in the sources read, except RTBeamTech, through Técnico's stand.
- **"AI runs through several of them"** is our reading of the report's descriptions. We have not tried any of the products.
- **The finalists** are not listed here; the report names only the winners.
