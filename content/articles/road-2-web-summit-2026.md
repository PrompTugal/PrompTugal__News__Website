## In short

- Road 2 Web Summit, run by [Startup Portugal](../companies/startup-portugal/index.html) and Web Summit, is in its eleventh edition and selects up to 125 Portuguese startups for this year's summit.
- The selected startups get a bootcamp in Porto and Lisbon, 50% off Web Summit's ALPHA or BETA startup packages and extra exhibition space on Portugal's stand.
- Galp sponsors two prizes: 15,000 euros for the Most Promising Startup and 5,000 euros for the Best Performing Startup.
- The 2026 winner is announced at a press conference on Thursday 12 November, at 12:30 on Stage 2.

## The story

**The programme.** Road 2 Web Summit prepares startups based in Portugal to take part in Web Summit, which runs in Lisbon from 9 to 12 November. This is its eleventh edition, according to Startup Portugal and Web Summit's announcement, as reported by Executive Digest and Tek Notícias in August. Up to 125 Portuguese companies are to be selected.

**What they get.** A bootcamp in Porto and Lisbon, with investors, founders, pitch coaches and people from Web Summit, focused on preparing for the conference. The selected startups also get 50% off Web Summit's ALPHA or BETA packages, which include exhibition space and the chance to enter official competitions such as PITCH and the Startup Showcase, and extra exhibition space on Portugal's stand, as reported by Executive Digest.

**The prizes.** Galp sponsors the programme again, with 15,000 euros for the Most Promising Startup and 5,000 euros for the Best Performing Startup. Last year's prizes went to Enhanced Fertility, in health, and Teemy, in hospitality. In 2024 they went to Framedrop.ai, from Coimbra, which uses AI to help creators edit videocasts and podcasts, and Deeploy, a tech recruitment platform, as Jornal de Negócios reported.

**The record.** Since 2016, the programme has supported more than 1,200 Portuguese startups, according to the announcement. In 2025 alone it supported 115, which together raised 40 million euros. Three Portuguese startups have won Web Summit's PITCH competition in the last five editions: Smartex, Intuitivo and, most recently, Granter.

**In their words.** Miguel d'Aguiar, Executive Director of Startup Portugal, said the aim is not only to take startups to Web Summit, but to help them arrive "mais preparadas, mais competitivas" (better prepared, more competitive) and able to raise investment and find clients and partners. Margarida David, Startups Manager at Web Summit, called the programme "um motor central" (a central engine) of the ecosystem's growth since the summit moved to Portugal in 2016.

**At the summit.** Web Summit's schedule lists "Press conference: Road 2 Web Summit 2026 Winner" on Thursday 12 November, from 12:30 to 12:50 Lisbon time, on Stage 2, with Miguel d'Aguiar.

A suggestion: investors looking for early-stage Portuguese companies could start at Portugal's stand, where the programme's startups have extra space, and the press conference on 12 November is where to hear who won.

## What this does not claim

- **Who was selected.** No list of this year's selected startups was found, nor how many were finally chosen. "Up to 125" is the ceiling the announcement gives.
- **The totals differ by source.** The announcement says more than 1,200 startups since 2016. Startup Portugal's own programme page, as read on 6 November, says more than 1,000. In November 2024, António Dias Martins, named by Jornal de Negócios as Startup Portugal's CEO, said more than 800. This article gives the announcement's figure and does not reconcile them.
- **The 40 million euros** is the announcement's figure for 2025's startups; PrompTugal.news has not checked it.
- **The bootcamp's dates** are not given in the sources read.
- **Where Portugal's stand is** on the venue map is not given in the sources read.
