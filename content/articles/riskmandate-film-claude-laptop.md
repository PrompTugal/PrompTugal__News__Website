## In short

- A 26-second brand film for RiskMandate about reach, mandate and gap was made by Claude working through Unreal Engine 5.8 on a MacBook Air.
- There is no spoken audio, only typed titles.
- Its author, Dinis Cruz, told this newsroom he grew up in Loulé.

## The AI path

This story is about AI directly (zero steps): an AI model drove a 3D engine to make a film.

## The story

The film shows 108 grey blocks rising in rings around a green agent, with the inner rings turning brand green. A barrier rises between mandate and reach, and the camera cranes up into a seal.

Dinis Cruz's notes from 13 November, published on newsroom.sgit.ai on 14 November, say Claude built it through the experimental Unreal MCP plugin of Unreal Engine 5.8, with about fifty toolsets enabled. The machine was a MacBook Air (M2, 16 GB) on a standard non-admin account. The pipeline was simple: Claude writes a job, Unreal builds the scene, the Movie Render Queue renders it, and ffmpeg encodes the MP4. A job runner watches a folder for Python files.

The film exists in versions. The first, at 1920 by 1080, took about three and a half minutes to render. The second, at 2560 by 1440, took about 26 minutes.

The author's own link to this town is the point of putting it here. He says he was born in Lisbon and spent his teenage years in Loulé, and he calls himself a proper Louletano. The team that went to Web Summit with him included people who live in Loulé and Quarteira.
