## In short

Alcoutim is an inland municipality on the Guadiana, and on the evidence we could find it has no AI company, startup or AI event. Its path to AI runs through public groundwork: connectivity, sensors, a place for founders to sit, and a regional association that sits in a cross-border AI project. This piece says what each source says and where the link to AI is only a lead.

## What is being built

- **Fibre.** The operator dstelecom announced on 9 June 2026 that about 1,000 more homes in Alcoutim, Giões and Pereiro would be connected by the end of the first half of the year, taking [municipal coverage to 82 percent](https://www.dstelecom.pt/imprensa/fibra-otica-chega-a-mais-1-000-casas-em-alcoutim-e-ultrapassa-80-de-cobertura/), with more than 3,100 families able to connect. It is the operator's own announcement and we have not confirmed the target was met.
- **Sensors.** Alcoutim is one of six municipalities in the [Algarve 6.0 Smart Territorial Hub](https://transparencia.gov.pt/en/fundos-europeus/prr/beneficiarios-projetos/projeto/01/C19-i08/2024.P13557/), a PRR project for an urban management platform with sensors and a LoRaWAN network, including monitoring of isolated elderly residents. Alcoutim's assigned funding is 124.32 thousand euros.
- **A place for founders.** The council's Celeiro de Ideias application was approved on 27 August 2026. The old EPAC granary will become a [business and coworking centre](https://www.barlavento.pt/alcoutim-vai-transformar-antigo-celeiro-da-epac-em-centro-empresarial/) for microbusinesses, freelancers, startups and artisans, with 1,269,828.22 euros eligible and works due by 30 September 2028.
- **Care at a distance.** The council now pays about 80,000 euros itself for the [+ Próximo teleassistance service](https://www.barlavento.pt/alcoutim-financia-projeto-proximo-apoio-idosos-isolamento/), which gives isolated people aged 65 or over an alarm button and a fixed unit on the home phone.

## The one direct AI link

Agrosocial Plus was launched in Córdoba on 22 and 23 September 2026. It is a Spain and Portugal project of about 2.06 million euros, and Odiana, the Lower Guadiana development association, is the [Portuguese partner representing the region](https://www.barlavento.pt/odiana-lancamento-projeto-inovacao-agrosocial-plus/). Its Living Labs are planned to test solutions including artificial intelligence, the Internet of Things and smart irrigation. The article does not name Alcoutim or say where the labs will be. Odiana does work in Alcoutim, where it helps run the Galp Foundation's [Vilas em Movimento programmes](https://www.fundacaogalp.com/en/the-energy-of-communities/portugal/alcoutim), so the question for Odiana is whether a lab or pilot lands on the Guadiana.

## The AI path

Agrosocial Plus reaches AI in one step, through its Living Labs, and Alcoutim in a second through Odiana. Fibre, sensors, the coworking centre and teleassistance are two to three steps away: each is a base on which AI services would sit, and none of the sources mentions AI. We list them as groundwork, not as AI.

## What we could not find

No AI company or AI event in the municipality, no mobile coverage data and no readable council news feed. Next: GEO.ANACOM for coverage, and Odiana for the Living Labs.
