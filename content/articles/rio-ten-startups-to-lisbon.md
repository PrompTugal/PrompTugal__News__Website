## In short

- For the second year, Rio de Janeiro's investment agency, Invest.Rio, and the city's innovation hub, Maravalley, are taking ten startups to Web Summit Lisbon (9 to 12 November).
- The airline Gol pays their tickets. A few weeks before, Gol opened its first intercontinental route, Rio to Lisbon from Galeão; it goes to five flights a week from 24 November, after the summit. The sources do not say which flights the ten take.
- New this year: the ten can present at Invest.Rio's stand.

## The mission

Invest.Rio and Maravalley opened the call in September for startups under six years old with fewer than 100 employees, as Startups.com.br reported on 18 September 2026. It is the mission's second year: last year it also took ten startups to the summit. Maravalley ran a similar mission to VivaTech in Paris in June 2026, with Business France's delegation.

Daniel Barros, Maravalley's CEO, called the plan "uma via de mão dupla" (a two-way street). Sidney Levy, Invest.Rio's president, said it is about making Rio "um polo de tecnologia, empreendedorismo e novos negócios" (a hub of technology, entrepreneurship and new business).

On 26 October, Startups.com.br named the ten and reported that Gol, as the mission's official airline, provides their tickets. Correio da Manhã reported the same ten on 25 October. Luiz Borrego, Gol's CIO, said Rio is "um hub internacional estratégico" (a strategic international hub) for the airline. Gol opened the route a few weeks before that report (in September, according to the report's photo caption); from 24 November it runs five flights a week, on Mondays, Tuesdays, Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays.

## The ten, in their own words

What each says it does, read on its own website on 6 and 7 November 2026.

| Startup | What it says it does |
|---|---|
| [Mobile Brain](https://www.mobilebrain.com.br/) | Neuroscience applied to the analysis of what people say, to assess cognition and wellbeing, for schools and companies. |
| [sheerME](https://sheerme.com/) | Beauty and wellness: discover, book and pay for services, with cashback. |
| [Paralelo.app](https://paralelo.app.br/) | An app for finding events, with AI that recommends them by the user's interests. |
| [Predator Sports & Health](https://www.predatorsports.com.br/) | A biosensing platform that turns athletes' biological data into performance. |
| [Embeddo](https://embeddo.io/) | Industrial deep tech: its own hardware and embedded AI make critical physical variables measurable in real time. |
| [Lucca](https://meulucca.com/) | Coordinates autism care in Brazil's public health system, with AI proposing and the care team deciding, without replacing the public system's own records software. |
| [Bizuu](https://bizuu.app/) | Discounts at bars and restaurants nearby, with coupons checked by QR code. |
| [Ray Pay](https://raypay.me/) | A payments app that makes card machines accessible to people with visual impairments. |
| [ATLANTICIT](https://atlanticit.com.br/) | A service management platform for companies: digital operations, IT, facilities and outsourced staff. |
| [AllPetSenior](https://www.allpetsenior.com/) | An app, with AI, for the health and longevity of older dogs and cats. |

sheerME is in Rio's group as both Startups.com.br and Correio da Manhã report it; this article does not know its tie to Rio.

## Why it matters

Sebrae says 12 Brazilian states run missions of their own to this year's summit, and does not name them ([Brazil's startup mission](brazil-startup-mission.html)). Rio's mission, run by the city, names its ten, and it arrives with a new direct flight between the two cities.
