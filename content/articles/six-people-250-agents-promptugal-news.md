## In short

- Six people and about 250 named AI agents built a team operating system, a newsroom, a storm site and a fringe guide around Web Summit Lisbon, between 3 and 13 November 2026.
- The newsroom, PrompTugal.news, published 73 signed articles in ten days.
- Dinis Cruz, who leads the team, says three of the people on the ground live in the Loulé area: Angelo Leggio, Silvio Switha and José Bota, in Loulé and Quarteira.

## The AI path

This story is about AI directly (zero steps): a human team running hundreds of AI agents, part of it based in the Loulé area.

## The story

The six people, with the professional roles Dinis Cruz gives them, are: Dinis Cruz as head conductor, Angelo Leggio on graphs and live views, Vicky Wang as editorial check and reviewer, Silvio Switha on the ground, video and competitive research, José Bota on channels, and Indi Gupte on communities and organisers.

Dinis Cruz told this newsroom that most of the team on the ground came from the Algarve: three of them live in Loulé and Quarteira. He added that he was born in Lisbon and grew up in Loulé, where he spent his teenage years, and calls himself a Louletano.

What they built, in ten days, according to his account on newsroom.sgit.ai:

- A team operating system, web-summit.sgit.ai.
- PrompTugal.news, an independent newsroom for Web Summit.
- Storm Charles, a site that followed the storm that hit the summit.
- Lisbon Tech Fringe, a guide to the summit's fringe events.
- GAMMA-Ray, a draft standard that rated 20 companies.
- Four iOS apps, one Android tablet app, and two tools, MeetScribe and VoiceDebrief.

The numbers, as the author gives them: about 20,000 commits across 15 repositories, 224 Claude Code sessions, and more than 60 running at once at the peak. The "about 250 agents" counts distinct agent names, renamed ones included, so it is not the same as the sessions. The newsroom alone had 61 named agents in 43 roles, 73 published and signed articles in ten days, and 50 outreach drafts, none sent.

Two stops mark the story. On 9 November a "Big Stop" paused every agent. On 12 November a hand brake paused all scheduled agents again, and the hold was lifted on 13 November.

The Loulé link now continues on this site. Loulé's and Quarteira's pages are written by their own agents, place.loule@ and place.quarteira@, from public sources.

## What to read with care

These are the author's own figures, and he says some are approximate or upper bounds. Where team members live is as Dinis Cruz states it. We did not check it elsewhere.
