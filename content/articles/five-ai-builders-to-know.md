## In short

- Web Summit's 2026 speaker list features several people building AI products. Five of them work at different layers of the field, from the models underneath to the apps people use.
- [Replit](../companies/replit/index.html) and [Manus AI](../companies/manus-ai/index.html) build tools that do the work for you: an app from a description, or an agent that carries out tasks.
- [MiniMax](../organisations/minimax/index.html) and [ModelBest](../organisations/modelbest/index.html) build the models, from large multimodal ones to small ones that run on a phone.
- [You.com](../companies/you-com/index.html) supplies live web search to AI systems.
- These are our picks from public pages. Nothing here says the five plan to meet or work with anyone.

## The story

AI is not one business. Some companies build the models, some build on top of them, and some feed them what they need. Web Summit's speaker list has people from each layer.

### Building the apps

**[Michele Catasta](../people/michele-catasta/index.html)**, President of Replit, is on the list. Replit says that you "describe what you want" and it builds a working app or website, with no coding needed. Michele Catasta also gives a press conference on Tuesday 10 November, 15:00 to 15:20, on Stage 2 in the Media Village. It is a media-only event, according to Web Summit's programme. He also speaks to attendees that day: on Centre Stage, 14:00 to 14:20, and on Stage 13 (AI Summit), 16:00 to 16:20, according to Web Summit's programme.

**Tao Zhang**, co-founder and chief product officer of Manus AI, is another speaker. Manus calls itself an "action engine" that goes beyond answers to carry out tasks and automate workflows.

### Building the models

**Alan Shen** is global managing director at MiniMax. MiniMax says it builds multimodal models and AI-native products, with over 300 million users.

**Dahai Li** is co-founder and chief executive of ModelBest. Its MiniCPM models are small enough to run on a device rather than in the cloud. The company names AI PCs, phones, car cabins and robots among the fields it works in.

### Feeding the models

**[Richard Socher](../people/richard-socher/index.html)** is co-founder and chief executive of You.com and of Recursive. You.com offers web search APIs so that AI systems can work from accurate, fresh information from the web, in the company's words. Its site says Richard Socher was previously chief scientist at Salesforce.

### Why they are worth knowing

Together they show how the field fits together: models, the data they draw on, and the products built on top. That map will still be useful after the summit.

**Who could connect:** founders building with AI could check the programme for sessions with these five that are open to attendees, to hear from each layer of the stack. This is a suggestion only.
