## In short

- SPAROS, an aquafeed company in Olhão, has a public-funds project called IA@SPAROS to bring AI into feed production and into its decisions.
- S2AQUAcoLAB, a laboratory based in Olhão, leads DigiATLA, a project of about 2.13 million euros on digital aquaculture that runs from January 2026 to June 2028.
- The council said in 2023 that it was working on AI to optimise waste collection routes. We found no later report on it.
- Olhão's newest technology stories are about digital aquaculture and a Blue Hub in the fishing port, and most of them do not name AI.

## The story

Olhão is a fishing port on the Ria Formosa, and its technology runs through the water. At Marim, inside the Ria Formosa Natural Park, the national fish-farming pilot station of the weather and sea institute IPMA does research and pilot-scale demonstrations for industry. Around it sit a collaborative laboratory and a feed company, and a Blue Hub for the sea economy is being built in the fishing port.

The clearest AI item is a company one. On Portugal's public recovery-funds portal, a project called IA@SPAROS is listed in Olhão. Its description says it will bring AI tools into the production of compound feed for fish, to automate and optimise the steps of making it, and apply AI to the company's data and to scientific, technical and administrative decisions. The portal's own aim is to make SPAROS an international reference in precision nutrition for aquaculture.

The next step away is the laboratory. S2AQUAcoLAB was founded in 2021 and is based in Olhão, and a 2022 report listed technological developments, from robotics to artificial intelligence, among its research lines. In February 2026 it began leading DigiATLA, with SPAROS, IPMA and universities in Vigo, Galway and France as partners. The programme's page speaks of digital capacity, training and Industry 4.0 for fish farms and does not use the word AI. We count it as two steps from AI: digital tools for aquaculture, in the same circle as SPAROS's AI project.

The council's own AI story is older. In January 2023 it joined the leadership of the national smart-cities section of the municipal association and said it was working on smart parking and on using AI to plan urban waste collection routes. In April 2026 it presented Olhão com Alma, a project to digitise local shops with a marketplace, merchant tools and smart lockers. The article on that project does not mention AI.

A technology company from an earlier chapter also sits here. Altice Labs opened its Algarve hub in Olhão in May 2021, and the report of the day said projects were already running with the University of Algarve, including AI algorithms for studying the retina. We found no later update.

What Olhão does not show yet is recent news that says AI plainly. A conference on AI and society was held at the municipal library in April 2024, and since then the town's technology stories have been about aquaculture, the port and local business. A company called FULGUR-IT, which makes solutions for agriculture, industry and smart cities, is also in Olhão, but we found no AI claim for it. Worth watching: the Startup Olhão project and ANJE Hub that the council and a business association agreed in June 2026, with the sea economy as a target, and whether the Blue Hub opens as planned.
