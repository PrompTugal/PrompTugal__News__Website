## In short

- *Know the Reach* is a RiskMandate song about what an AI agent can reach and what it was asked to do. It was released on Spotify on 13 November 2026 and runs about three and a half minutes.
- The first part of its music video, 53.4 seconds long, was directed by Silvio Switha, a musician, singer and producer who runs a wedding band in the Algarve.
- It was made with an AI assistant, an image generator and Runway for motion. The rest of the video has still to be filmed.

## The AI path

This story is about AI directly (zero steps): a song and a video made with AI tools, directed by a musician who works in the Algarve.

## The story

Silvio Switha was on the ground at Web Summit Lisbon with the RiskMandate team, where his roles were video and competitive research. On the side he made something else: the video for a song.

The article by Dinis Cruz on newsroom.sgit.ai says the lyrics came from a ChatGPT session that drew on RiskMandate's history and on Silvio's guidance. Silvio made the creative decisions about the chorus, the structure and the pacing. The song, *Know the Reach*, was released on Spotify on 13 November. A second RiskMandate song, *Strangers in the Loop*, produced by Indi Gupte, followed the next day.

The video is where the AI work shows. Cut from 14 new clips to the original song, its first part is 53.4 seconds long. A production audit of 14 November counts 43 generated images, 16 motion clips (108.7 seconds in all) and 21 edited exports. It also counts about 2,300 words of human direction across 78 messages and 1,236 Runway credits, 96 of them for the pilot and 1,140 for part one. Dinis Cruz estimates that these numbers capture perhaps a fifth of the total work.

Silvio is, in the article's words, one of the best musicians its author knows. Dinis has challenged him to record the song with his band. That is a proposal, not a plan that has been announced.
