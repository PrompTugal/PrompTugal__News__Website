## In short

Alcoutim has no AI company that we could find, but its council appears as a named partner in one of the more concrete AI projects in the Algarve interior: Robotics4Farmers, a Portuguese Recovery and Resilience Plan project to build a robot that removes weeds without herbicides.

## What the sources say

- **The project.** The [European Commission's page](https://reforms-investments.ec.europa.eu/projects/robotics-4-farmers_en) describes an autonomous robotic vehicle with AI-guided cameras and a concentrated-laser or solar-assisted weeding system, with 117,760 euros of Recovery and Resilience Plan funding. It does not name the partners, dates or trial sites.
- **The partners.** The [project's own page](https://robotics4farmers.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/~robotics4farmers.daemon/about/) says the consortium has 11 partners covering vineyards, olive groves and almond orchards. It names four: Instituto Superior Técnico, the Centro de Competências na Luta Contra a Desertificação, the Município de Alcoutim and an Elvas olive growers association.
- **The AI.** The same page mentions learning algorithms for classifying weeds and seasonal prediction models fed by continuous digitisation of soil, plants and weather, and training for farmers. It names no specific algorithm.
- **The council's side.** Alcoutim's [PRR page](https://cm-alcoutim.pt/municipio/projetos-financiados/plano-de-recuperacao-e-resiliencia-prr), read on 16 November 2026, lists a Robotics 4 Farmers project sheet among its funded projects.
- **The desertification centre.** The centre in the consortium is the one hosted in Alcoutim since 2018, with the Government and more than 30 institutions, [reported by Observador from Lusa](https://observador.pt/2018/07/19/governo-cria-centro-para-estudar-e-procurar-solucoes-de-combate-a-desertificacao/).
