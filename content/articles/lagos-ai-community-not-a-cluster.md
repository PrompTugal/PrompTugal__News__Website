## In short

Lagos, in the western Algarve, is where the region's tech people come to meet. What it does not yet have, on the evidence we could find, is an AI company confirmed as headquartered there. The path from the town to AI runs through events and a community, not through firms.

## What happened in Lagos

- **A summit with an AI session.** The Algarve Tech Hub Summit ran at a hotel in Lagos on 6 and 7 November 2025. [Observador's preview](https://observador.pt/2025/11/04/algarve-tech-hub-summit-em-lagos-vai-apoiar-talento-e-investimento-na-regiao/) listed a live demonstration of building a specialised ChatGPT. [Barlavento reported](https://www.barlavento.pt/lagos-foi-capital-da-inovacao-no-algarve-tech-hub-summit/) about 450 people on the first day and 570 on the second, with the town council among the organisers. Barlavento's report does not mention AI itself.
- **A developer festival with an AI day.** [DevFest Lagos 2025](https://www.barlavento.pt/mais-de-250-participantes-debateram-sobre-inovacao-no-google-devfest-lagos-2025/) ran on 17 and 18 October. The first day reached about 150 students in schools. The second, at the Town Hall, was themed "AI: Theory to Action" and had 120 registrations.
- **A regular AI talk series.** GDG Lagos, the local Google developer group, has held talks on [AI agents](https://gdg.community.dev/events/details/google-gdg-lagos-presents-ai-agents-the-architects-of-automation/) (February 2025), [whether the Algarve needs AI](https://gdg.community.dev/events/details/google-gdg-lagos-presents-does-algarve-need-ai) (February 2025), an [International Women's Day Build with AI evening](https://gdg.community.dev/events/details/google-gdg-lagos-presents-international-womens-day-build-with-ai) in the Town Hall (March 2025) and [agent skills](https://gdg.community.dev/events/details/google-gdg-lagos-presents-build-with-ai-intro-to-ai-agent-skills/) (March 2026).

## Who is in the room

The municipal coworking space CoLagos has 20 workstations and [was full in April 2025, as it had been since November 2023](https://www.barlavento.pt/premio-cinco-estrelas-regioes-distingue-colagos-pelo-terceiro-ano-consecutivo/). It was a partner of DevFest. A community for the town's digital nomads [has existed since the pandemic](https://www.essential-business.pt/2022/12/02/lagos-digital-nomads-building-a-bridge-between-local-businesses-and-remote-workers/) and was a media partner of the Women's Day AI evening.

## The AI path

Lagos reaches AI in one step through the GDG Lagos talks and DevFest, and in two steps through CoLagos and the remote-worker groups that host and attend them. Readers of this site should not take that as an industry. A directory lists one small AI firm in Lagos, but we do not treat a directory entry as a source, so it is not on [our map of the town](../algarve/lagos/index.html).

## What we could not find

The council's news page, read on 15 November 2026, had no AI items among its last 20. We found no upcoming AI event in Lagos. Meetup and Luma listings, where local groups often post, are not read by this publication.

Sources read on 15 November 2026.
