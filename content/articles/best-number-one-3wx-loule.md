## In short

- 3WX, a digital agency in Loulé, says it launched Best Number One at Web Summit 2026 on 12 November.
- It is a free page where you gather your web links into panels and set it as your browser's home page.
- 3WX says it passed 300 sign-ups in the first hours.
- The agency says it used the tool itself for several years before opening it to the public.

## The story

Most people keep their favourite sites in a browser's bookmarks menu. A Loulé agency wants them on one page instead.

3WX, a digital agency in Loulé, in the Algarve, says it launched Best Number One at Web Summit 2026 on Thursday 12 November. Two Algarve outlets report it, Postal do Algarve and A Voz do Algarve, with the same company quote.

Best Number One is free. You build your own start page from links grouped in blocks by theme. You can begin from one of ten ready-made templates or from an empty panel, and you can import your bookmarks. You can keep a panel for work, one for study and one for leisure. It runs in a browser on a computer, a tablet or a phone.

Blocks can hold more than links: notes, to-do lists, the weather, clocks for several time zones and news. A quick search finds your links and panels, or searches the web with the engine you choose. Panels can be shared with other people. The tool also warns you when a link stops working.

On its own site, Best Number One says it is free for ever, has no advertising and no tracking, and is made in Portugal. To sign up, its form asks for an email address, a user name, a city, a country and a password.

The idea came from inside the agency. 3WX says it built the tool to keep order in the web links its own team uses every day. After several years of internal use, it opened it to the public.

3WX says it has been around since 2003. It was founded in Mértola and is now based in Loulé. It works for small and medium businesses and organisations on web development, social media management and digital advertising.

3WX's CEO told the press the sign-ups showed other people share the need. In A Voz do Algarve's Portuguese, translated here: "For years, this was the tool that helped us put order in our digital day. We believe the internet deserves a real index and that everyone should be able to start browsing where it makes most sense to them. The response we had in the first hours shows we are not the only ones who feel this need."

Its site lists templates by profession, among them ones for entrepreneurs, programmers, teachers and lawyers.

## What this does not claim

- **The launch at Web Summit is 3WX's claim.** The newsroom read no Web Summit page. It does not know where or how 3WX presented. Nothing here says 3WX is an exhibitor or a startup at the summit.
- **The 300 sign-ups are the company's figure.** The papers do not say how many hours "the first hours" were, and no one outside 3WX has confirmed the number.
- **Two reports count as one source.** They share the quote and nearly the same text, so they most likely come from one company release. No other report has been found.
- **The features are as 3WX and its site describe them.** The newsroom has not made an account or tried the tool.
- **The company's age and clients** are 3WX's own description, as the papers give it.
