## In short

- Brazil's delegation to Web Summit 2025 was one of the two largest, with Germany's, by ApexBrasil's account. How large depends on who counted: the public figures run from 151 to "almost 380".
- The figures count different things: startups selected by the agencies, startups brought by partners, companies sent by state missions.
- The delegation has grown every year since 2022, when 80 companies came with Brazil's first pavilion.
- For 2026 the spread is the same: from 100 to about 300 ([Brazil's startup mission](brazil-startup-mission.html)).

## Last year, five ways

| Figure for 2025 | What it counts | Who said it, and when |
|---|---|---|
| 151 | Participants selected by ApexBrasil, by region (80 from the Southeast, 35 from the Northeast) | DN Brasil, 10 November 2025, with ApexBrasil's Lisbon office |
| 247 | "Startups e empresas inovadoras" supported by ApexBrasil, Sebrae and Serpro | ApexBrasil, the rules for its 2026 mission |
| 340 | Startups and innovative companies: 256 selected by ApexBrasil and Sebrae, 84 by partners (Softex, Anprotec, Serpro) | Startups.com.br, 3 November 2025, quoting ApexBrasil |
| more than 370 | "Startups sediadas no Brasil" (startups based in Brazil) | DN Brasil, 10 November 2025 |
| almost 380 | Companies in the delegation, the 247 plus those of the state Sebraes | ApexBrasil, the rules for its 2026 mission (one version says "quase 380", another "380") |

The two largest figures include companies that the state branches of Sebrae brought on their own missions; the smaller ones count only what the national agencies selected. Startups.com.br adds that 123 of the 340 were at the summit for the first time.

## Year by year

| Year | What the sources say |
|---|---|
| 2022 | 80 companies, the year of the first Brazil Pavilion (ApexBrasil to Lusa, in Forbes Portugal, 7 November 2023) |
| 2023 | A planned "record": 85 startups and 100 companies (the same report) |
| 2024 | 226 startups selected by ApexBrasil and Sebrae (Startups.com.br, 3 November 2025) |
| 2025 | 151 to almost 380 (above) |
| 2026 | 100 to about 300 ([our article](brazil-startup-mission.html)) |

Each year's figures come from different reports and may not count the same thing, so the table shows a direction, not a precise growth rate.

## Why it matters

"One of the largest delegations" is true by every count. But a reader who wants to meet Brazilian startups at the summit cannot learn from these figures who they are. ApexBrasil's rules for 2026 promise the selected startups a place in a "catálogo digital da delegação brasileira" on its Buy Brazil platform; we have not seen it. The names we could find are in [Rio's ten](rio-ten-startups-to-lisbon.html) and [Brazil on Web Summit's stages](brazil-on-the-stages.html).

## What this does not claim

- **We did not ask ApexBrasil or Sebrae** what each figure counts; the "what it counts" column follows each source's own words.
- **We do not know how many Brazilian startups Web Summit itself counted** in 2025; it publishes no count by country that we found.
- **The 2023 figure was a plan**, announced before the summit, not a count after it.
