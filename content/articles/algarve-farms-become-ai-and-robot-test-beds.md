## In short

- AGROBOTICS-DITWINS is a European project that tests robotics, AI and digital twins on farms. It is led by the University of Málaga, has 13 partners in Spain, Portugal and France and is co-financed by Interreg SUDOE until May 2028.
- NERA and the University of Algarve lead the Algarve pilot: two living labs on working farms.
- By July 2026, 28 expressions of interest had come in and the two host farms were still being chosen.
- On 11 November NERA's news list showed a published catalogue of living labs across Portugal, Spain and France.

## The story

Algarve farming has a new kind of tenant: the experiment. NERA, the regional business association, joined AGROBOTICS-DITWINS in 2025. The project's launch meeting was held on 24 and 25 September 2025 at the University of Málaga, and Barlavento reported that it aims to build a cross-border ecosystem for digitalising farming through robotics, AI and digital twins, which are computer copies of a farm or a process that can be tested before anything changes in the field. The project's next steps included mapping firms that could gain from robotics, running a pilot of robotic solutions and building a knowledge-sharing network.

In July 2026 the partners met in Bilbao on 15 and 16 July to plan the pilot phase, including a visit to a living lab at the University of the Basque Country. Barlavento reported on 22 July that NERA and the University of Algarve presented their regional work, including how they are reaching the farming sector, and that 28 expressions of interest had been received for the two Algarve living labs. The two farms were still being selected.

What the labs will test is robotics and AI in real farming conditions. The article does not name a crop, a vendor or a robot.

On 11 November, NERA's news list showed that the project had published a catalogue of living labs. We have not opened that catalogue, so we cannot say yet what it holds for the Algarve.

What we could not confirm: which farms were chosen, what they grow, and what robots or AI they will run. The next thing to look for is the announcement of the two host farms.
