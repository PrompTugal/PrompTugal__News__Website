## In short

- Invisible CFO, a fintech founded in 2024 and based in Monchique, sits in the council's business incubator and was named a Web Summit Impact Startup for 2025, reported as the only Portuguese fintech with that recognition.
- By its own account it combines AI, automation and a human team of accountants and tax advisers, and says 300,000 euros of EU funding helped develop its AI and automation applications.
- Água Monchique, the mineral water company at Caldas de Monchique, says in its own announcement that it already incorporates some AI tools in its operations, without naming them.
- We found no AI fire detection or forest monitoring in Monchique, although fire risk defines the municipality's agenda. The only AI fire-detection pilots we found are national and sit in Alvão and Serra do Açor.

## The story

Monchique is known for its serra, its hot springs and, since 2018, its fires. The public record reflects that: the research project BRIDGE (IST, LNEC and the University of the Algarve) used Monchique as its pilot for community fire-risk reduction, and the municipal fire plan we could read describes detection by lookout posts, one industry tower and mobile patrols. None of these mentions AI.

The AI we can source is in business. Invisible CFO describes itself as a digital finance provider for small businesses and freelancers, operating from Monchique and offering a 20 percent discount to clients in low-density areas of Portugal. Barlavento reported on 21 October 2025 that the company is housed in the Incubadora de Empresas de Monchique and was recognised by Web Summit as an Impact Startup 2025. The company's own page says EU funds of 300,000 euros, through IEFP, Algarve 2030 and Portugal 2030, helped develop AI and automation applications, and does not describe the tools.

The council's incubator is a small public service. Its page describes individual support for entrepreneurs, online follow-up sessions and signposting to funding calls, and names no tenants. The council's Entrepreneurship Office, created in January 2022, prepares funding applications and supports investors.

Água Monchique is the second case. On 29 May 2024 it announced that it is the official water partner of the AIVolution conference on AI in organisations, adding that it "já incorpora algumas ferramentas de IA" in its operations. That is the company's own statement and we found no independent report naming a tool. In 2021 Echo Boomer reported that the company's Nommo smart water dispenser, built with the technology firm Quantum Leap, would learn household habits and reorder bottles automatically. We could not confirm that product reached the market.

## The AI path

Both cases are AI at step 0 or 1: the companies say they use AI directly, and both are in Monchique. The claims are self-reported. The fire risk that dominates the town has no AI path in any source we could open.
