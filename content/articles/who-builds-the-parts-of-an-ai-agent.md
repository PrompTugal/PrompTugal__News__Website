## In short

- Eight startups on the first page of Web Summit's featured startups list each describe a part of what AI agents need; for one, Beyond Presence, the link to agents is our reading.
- Three build the base: a framework (Mastra), a database (Keewano) and computing on devices (Acrab AI).
- Two work on trust: an identity and "risk passport" for agents (Humanos), and security and governance (Polygraf AI).
- Three sell products built on agents: contact centres (GetVocal AI), real-time avatars (Beyond Presence) and solutions for businesses (Cnext).

## The story

An AI agent is software that takes steps on its own: it reads, decides and acts. Building one takes several parts. Web Summit's featured startups list shows companies working on each of them. Every description below is the company's own, from its Web Summit page, linked from that list.

**The base.** [Mastra](../companies/mastra/index.html), from the United States, describes itself as an open source TypeScript framework and enterprise platform "for building, iterating, and managing production-grade agents". [Keewano](../companies/keewano/index.html), from Israel, says it is building a database for AI agents: KeewanoDB keeps every event across users, devices and agents as an ordered history, so agents can "reason directly over massive volumes of event data". Acrab AI, from Singapore, founded in 2024, says it builds computing infrastructure for agentic AI, integrating purpose-built chips, optimised edge AI models and software "to run intelligent agents on devices".

**Trust and control.** Humanos, from the United States, says it is building "the risk and reputation network for AI agents": each agent gets a lasting identity and a "Risk Passport" that reflects its behaviour, permissions and track record. [Polygraf AI](../companies/polygraf-ai/index.html), also from the United States, offers what it calls an AI Behavioral Control Plane, for real-time visibility, governance and "inline enforcement across human and agentic AI interactions".

**On top.** [GetVocal AI](../companies/getvocal-ai/index.html), from France, works with enterprise contact centres, where its platform "orchestrates an AI fleet across every channel". Beyond Presence, from Germany, makes real-time AI avatars for enterprise conversations; its description does not mention agents, so placing it here is our reading. [Cnext](../companies/cnext/index.html), from Belgium, builds agentic AI solutions for European businesses on its own Kairos platform, and says its fee follows the result.
