## In short

- Wednesday 11 November is the day the European Commission and its agencies run most of their sessions at the summit: four of the seven on their agenda (two are on Tuesday, one on Thursday).
- The afternoon has EU support for AI businesses (14:30), EU funding with four EU-funded projects (15:30) and free access to Europe's supercomputers (16:00). The last two overlap.
- In the evening, Italian and Portuguese founders and investors meet in Beato from 18:30.
- Times are the organisers' own and can change. Check Web Summit's app on the day.

## Where the day clashes

HaDEA's funding session (15:30 to 16:15) and EuroHPC's (16:00 to 16:45) overlap by a quarter of an hour, by the Commission's agenda: pick one. The AI masterclass ends at 15:15, so it leads straight into either. Beato is away from the summit's venue, so the evening needs travel time.

## Who might get the most from the day

- **A founder looking for EU money** could take the AI masterclass at 14:30 and then HaDEA's session, and visit HaDEA's stand, E418 in Pavilion 4, which HaDEA says is staffed throughout the summit.
- **A startup that needs computing power** could choose EuroHPC at 16:00 over HaDEA.
- **A startup with IP to protect or finance** could start with the EUIPO panel at 12:10.
- **A founder looking at Italy, or an Italian founder looking at Portugal**, could end the day in Beato.

These are our suggestions, from the public listings; none is planned by the organisations named.

## What this does not claim

- **The times are the organisers' own.** Only the AI masterclass and "The next digital frontier" were found on Web Summit's schedule (read 6 November); the Commission's, HaDEA's, EuroHPC's and EUIPO's times come from their own pages, as in [our article on Europe in Pavilion 4](europe-in-pavilion-4.html). The Dutch event's time is not given, and RVO's programme is provisional.
- **Rooms are not given** for most sessions.
- **Registration.** Some may need it; the sources do not always say.
