## In short

- Web Summit says its last day, Thursday 12 November, has stage talks from 09:00 to 17:30, through lunch, with Wednesday's talks moved to Thursday.
- New times will be in the Web Summit app and its [online schedule](https://websummit.com/schedule/) by Thursday morning, Web Summit says. Check there before you go.
- IPMA forecasts light showers or rain in Lisbon on Thursday, 16 to 21°C, with a moderate west wind. Only a sea warning remains, until 03:00.
- Portugal's government declared the storm alert until 23:59 on Wednesday, and the minister said keeping it on Thursday is not planned.

## The story

After Wednesday's day was called off for Storm Charles ([our report](red-wind-warning-lisbon-wednesday.html)), Web Summit is back for one final day.

**What Web Summit says has changed.** Stage talks run from 09:00 to 17:30, including over lunch. Wednesday's talks have moved to Thursday, with more than 100 of Wednesday's speakers. 65 community and partner meetups have moved too, more than 125 in all. Web Summit says the app and the online schedule will show the new times by Thursday morning. These facts are as Web Summit gave them on its site on Wednesday evening; [its schedule](https://websummit.com/schedule/) is the place to check each time. Four startups also pitch on Centre Stage in the morning ([our story](breakout-startups-thursday-centre-stage.html)).

**The weather.** IPMA, Portugal's weather institute, forecasts light showers or rain in Lisbon on Thursday, between 15.8 and 20.5°C, with a moderate wind from the west (its forecast updated at 20:31 UTC). Its data shows no wind or rain warning for the Lisbon district. Only a sea warning remains: orange until 21:00 on Wednesday, then yellow until 03:00 on Thursday, for waves of 4 to 5 metres.

**The alert.** The government declared the alert situation for all of mainland Portugal from 00:00 to 23:59 on Wednesday 11 November. The Internal Administration minister said on Wednesday that keeping it on Thursday is not planned, though repair work may be needed and some services may stay restricted.

**Getting there.** The service page of Carris, Lisbon's bus and tram operator, read on Wednesday evening, says its Web Summit shuttle is out of service because the event was suspended. It did not say whether the shuttle returns on Thursday when we read it. Our [Wednesday transport guide](getting-around-lisbon-wednesday.html) has the other operators' notices from that day.

**Side events.** The [Lisbon Tech Fringe](https://lisbontechfringe.com) site lists many events for Thursday. Nearly all of them link only to a Luma page, which this article could not check, so none is listed here.

Back at the summit on Thursday? It is the last chance to catch the speakers and meetups that moved from Wednesday.

## What this does not claim

- **Thursday's exact session times.** Web Summit says they will be in its app and schedule by Thursday morning; this article does not copy them.

- **Metro and trains.** Metro de Lisboa's line status and CP's notices could not be read; check them on the day.
- **That the alert has ended.** It is set to end at 23:59 on Wednesday; this was written before then.
