## In short

Vila Real de Santo António is a border town on the Guadiana, and on the evidence we could find it has no AI company. What it has is a shipyard, Nautiber, that builds the hulls and structures of machines on which AI runs. None of the sources below mentions artificial intelligence. The link is two steps away and we write it down so you can judge it.

## What the yard has built

- **A vessel to test robots.** In April 2021 [Barlavento reported](https://www.barlavento.pt/mar-profundo-marca-a-entrada-da-nautiber-na-ciencia/) the launch of «Mar Profundo» at the Nautiber yard on the Guadiana. It is 19 metres long and was built mainly for INESC TEC and CINTAL, a University of Algarve research centre. It is meant for testing marine technologies such as robots, sensors and unmanned underwater vehicles, and it can run silently on electric motors for acoustic experiments.
- **A plan for unmanned vessels.** In December 2025 Barlavento published a sponsored piece in which [Nautiber said it plans a unit for defence vessels](https://www.barlavento.pt/?p=388252), aimed at unmanned systems and platforms. It says it has already delivered the structure for four surface drones with a partner and would work with a Lagos yard. The site is not decided between Vila Real de Santo António, Portimão and Lagos. This is the company's own account in sponsored content.
- **A town that wants a blue economy cluster.** After a working meeting on 19 February 2026 with the Secretary of State for Fisheries and the Sea, the council said it wants to become a [national pole of the blue economy](https://www.barlavento.pt/vila-real-santo-antonio-polo-nacional-economia-azul/). Its priorities include an area for a shipbuilding and repair cluster. The article mentions no technology or AI.

## The AI path

Robots and unmanned vessels are where autonomy software and AI run. A yard that builds the platforms is one step from that software and two from the town. The «Mar Profundo» articles describe testing robots and underwater vehicles, not AI. The defence plan names unmanned systems, not AI. We list Nautiber as a hull and platform builder, not as an AI company.

## What we could not find

No software or AI firm in the municipality, no statement from Nautiber about AI, and no confirmation that the defence unit has been decided or located. Next: whether the unit lands on the Guadiana, and whether CINTAL or INESC TEC publish results from the vessel.
