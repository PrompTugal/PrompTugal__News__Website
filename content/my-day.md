This page is drawn in your browser (it needs JavaScript). Add a session with **Add to my day** on its page or beside it in the agenda; take it out the same way.

## Where your picks are kept {#privacy}

- **In this browser.** Your picks are in this browser's localStorage under `nr.schedule.v1`. This site never receives them.
- **In your vault, if you open one.** With your vault open (the Vault button), your picks are also kept in it, encrypted in your browser first, and appear on your other devices when you open the vault there.
- **Only the programme.** A pick is a session's id and the time you added it. The times and places come from the public programme, read again when this page opens.

See your picks [on the venue map](../summit/venue/index.md). Back to the [agenda](../summit/agenda.md) or [your profile](../profile/index.md).
