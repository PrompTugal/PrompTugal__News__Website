PrompTugal.news covers technology and AI from Portugal, for Europe, starting with Web Summit Lisbon, 10 to 12 November 2026. A small team of people and AI agents writes it in the open.

You can help. You do not need to be a journalist, and you do not need an account. Rough is fine: we check every fact, combine pieces, and credit everyone who helped.

## The rules of the game {#rules}

1. **Pick a task.** Each one takes about an hour.
2. **Send it in.** No account needed.
3. **We review it.** Every fact is checked against its source.
4. **Good work is published** on PrompTugal.news, credited to you.
5. **It stays open.** What you send is published under CC BY 4.0: anyone can reuse it, with credit to you.
6. **Your name only if you say yes.** Your contact details stay private and are never published.
7. **No payment, for now.** This is a volunteer newsroom.

## Where to start: tasks of about an hour {#tasks}

Each task says what good looks like.

- **Write up a session you saw.** Who spoke, what they said that was new, one quote, the session's link. Good: 300 words a stranger could check against the session page.
- **Write about the summit from where you stand.** What you saw, who you met, what surprised you. Use our [events](../events/index.md) and [companies](../companies/index.md) pages as sources. Good: specific, with the names of the sessions and companies you mean.
- **Check a company's page.** Open one of [our company pages](../companies/index.md), compare it with the company's own site, and tell us what is wrong or missing. Good: each correction with the link that shows it.
- **A photo with a caption.** Where, when, who or what is in it, and that it is yours. Good: a caption a reader understands without the photo. (We remove location data from every photo before it is published.)
- **Interview someone at a stand.** Five questions, their answers, their role, and their yes to being published. Good: their words, not a summary of them.
- **Translate an article.** Into Portuguese, Spanish, or another language you write well. Good: faithful, plain, and the same length.
- **Review a piece.** Read one of [our articles](../articles/index.md) and tell us what confused you, what is wrong, or what is missing.

## How to send it {#send}

Sending opens as soon as the private, write-only inbox for it is ready. Until then, read the rules, pick your task and start writing: nothing you have made is lost by waiting.

## What happens next {#next}

- We read everything that comes in.
- We check every fact against its source, and may edit, shorten, or combine your piece with others.
- If it is good, we publish it, with your name if you said yes, and tell you when it is live.

## Your details {#details}

- Your contact details are kept privately by the Web Summit team that runs this newsroom, and are never published.
- Your name appears only if you tick the box.
- Photos are published without their location data.
- This page loads no outside scripts.
