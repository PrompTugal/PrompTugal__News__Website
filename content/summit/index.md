## When {#when}

**9 to 12 November 2026** ([venue essentials](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue-essentials/), read 1 November 2026).

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Monday 9 November** | Opening Night on Centre Stage from 18:00, then Night Summit from 20:00 to 23:00 in Pavilion 5 (FIL). |
| **Tuesday 10 November** | Conference day one, from about 10:00 to about 17:40. |
| **Wednesday 11 November** | Conference day two, same hours. |
| **Thursday 12 November** | Conference day three, same hours. |

All times are Lisbon time, which is the same as UTC in November ([Web Summit schedule](https://websummit.com/schedule/), read 1 November 2026). The schedule is still growing, so check it before you go.

## Where {#where}

**MEO Arena and FIL**, Rossio dos Olivais, Parque das Nações, Lisbon. The arena used to be called Altice Arena, and you may still see that name ([venue essentials](https://websummit.com/essentials/venue-essentials/), read 1 November 2026). [The venue map](venue/index.md) shows the buildings and what our programme puts in each.
