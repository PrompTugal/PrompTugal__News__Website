Where the summit's buildings are in Parque das Nações, and what our programme puts in each. Shaded buildings hold sessions.

## What is where {#where}

<!-- venue:table -->

The arena and the exhibition centre share one campus, and the schedule names a stage, not the building it is in, so sessions on a stage are shaded on the arena and all four pavilions together. Oriente station is drawn for the way in; no session is listed against it. A session whose place is not a known building is not placed on the map: we do not guess. The other venues of the week, outside Parque das Nações, are on the [places](../../places/index.md) page.

## What this map shows, and does not {#about}

The summit's buildings and its programme, from the public record. No person is ever on it. Nothing on this page is stored about you. The map itself is plain drawing; one small script, on this page only, reads your picks from this browser to show your day on it, and sends nothing anywhere.

## Where the drawing comes from {#source}

Map data © [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) contributors, under the [ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/). The outlines were simplified and drawn in a frame of our own; each building's OpenStreetMap entry is in the data file, [venue-map.json](../../assets/venue/venue-map.json).
