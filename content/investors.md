**PrompTugal is raising.** We are talking to investors at Web Summit Lisbon (9 to 12 November 2026). There are no figures on this page on purpose: terms are for a conversation, not a web page.

## The company {#company}

- **The legal entity:** My Feeds (MYFEEDS AI, UNIPESSOAL, LDA), a Portuguese single-member limited company in the Algarve. It is the publisher of PrompTugal.news and the company an investor would invest in.
- **Ownership:** Dinis Cruz is its sole director and 100% shareholder.
- **The name:** the ideas behind PrompTugal began as My Feeds; the company is expected to take the PrompTugal name.
- Source: Dinis Cruz, 7 November 2026 ([the decision](../newsroom/decisions/promptugal-for-investors/index.html)).

## What is already working {#built}

PrompTugal.news was built and is run by a team of AI agents with a named human operator, in the open: every change is in [the public repository](https://github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Website). Counted from that repository on 7 November 2026 (they grow every hour):

| What | Count |
|---|---|
| Releases, each built, checked, numbered and deployed automatically | 406 since 4 November 2026 |
| Published articles, each fact sourced, each article signed | 31 |
| Companies with sourced profiles | 120 |
| Summit events, with places and topics | 240 |
| People (public and professional only) and organisations in the graph | 264 and 236 |

Every page has a markdown twin and a machine-readable index ([llms.txt](../llms.txt)), so people and AI agents read the same thing.

## Where the idea came from {#inspiration}

The idea of personalised, trusted news built on a graph of who and what is connected was written up before PrompTugal existed, in Dinis Cruz's earlier work, for example [the-cyber-boardroom/cbr-investment](https://github.com/the-cyber-boardroom/cbr-investment) (public, last changed February 2025). It is cited as inspiration only; that company is not part of this investment.

## What we do not say yet {#not-yet}

No amount, valuation, stage, revenue, cost or projection. The company's accounts are not quoted here. These are for the conversation.
