**Prepared, not launched.** At Dinis Cruz's word of 6 November 2026 this page maps the ways PrompTugal.news could earn, so that when someone sees something we made and asks "can you do this for me?" the answer is a link and not a meeting. **Only the 5 pound reading top-up is on sale.** Every other price below is a proposal for Dinis to set or change, and nothing else is live until he says so. [The deck](https://promptugal.news/board/team/customers/deck/monetisation-deck.pdf) (14 slides) tells it in pictures.

## The rule {#rule}

**A buyer can buy a service made for them. They can never buy what the publication says.** No coverage, no ranking, no removal of a fact, no favourable article. The editors decide what is published. There are no ads, no paywall (the reading meter never blocks a page) and nothing is sold about who reads what. A person takes every payment and sends every bill; an agent prepares and delivers, and sends nothing outside.

## How a sale would run {#how}

An ask is logged by type and date (never a name). It is checked against the menu and the rule above, quoted on one page with what is in it, what is not, and what a change costs, and sent by Dinis as a Stripe Payment Link (for menu items) or an invoice (for a named job). Work starts when Dinis has seen the payment in Stripe, is reviewed for usability, for keys and private data, and for sources, and is handed over by a person. **The first five or six asks of a kind are one-off jobs; when a seventh person asks for the same thing it becomes a package with a link.**

## What is not here {#not-here}

No customer, quote or negotiated price: those belong with Dinis, not in a public repository. No claim for RiskMandate beyond what riskmandate.ai says, and no pitch for it before Dinis asks. No promise that anyone will pay: the seventh ask is the test. The newsreel's price is the biggest guess of all, and three cheap tests are set to find out whether anyone wants it at all.
