## What changes

- **The scoreboard as tiles (S9).** Open, accepted, later, done and verified as five tiles with their numbers, one bar of the same, and who waits on what as bars. The counts are read live from synthetic@'s findings file.
- **A card per reader (S5).** Each invented reader is a card instead of a paragraph: who they are, where they came from, their latest read, what lost them most (their most severe finding) and every finding they raised.
- **Kept, as security@ asked:** one SYNTHETIC line on top, every name marked SYNTHETIC, and no face: initials on a dashed tile, because none of these people exists.

The words stay synthetic@'s and the two blocks are dev@'s to build: `board/spec-the-synthetic-readers-page.md` in the repository. The mock-up is made with `design/mockups/synthetic/make.mjs`.
