**The question.** What is PrompTugal.news's commercial relation to the companies it writes to, and which exact words should the disclosure page and the outreach email use for it? It blocks the outreach: every draft carries this line, and it is the line that decides whether a company's comms team passes the email on.

**Why now.** In runs 3 and 4 an invented comms director at a large security company (SYNTHETIC) read the email and the disclosure. The disclosure's own sentence is what stopped her passing the email to a leader (run 3, blocking). Quoting it in the email earned the click (run 4), but she still would not put anyone on a call: "Separate the ask from the sales team ... say in the email that the 15 minutes will not be a sales conversation". This page is **not** a case for softening the disclosure: if a sentence is true it stays; the choice is what else is true and can be said.

## The options, as the exact text

**Option 1: as now.** Disclosure (about/participant.html): "This is not independent coverage. We may write about companies the team hopes to meet or sell to. Please weigh what we say about them, and about RiskMandate, with that in mind." Email (outreach@'s 03:00 draft): "PrompTugal.news belongs to a team that also works on RiskMandate, which sells Agent Behaviour Policies. So this is not independent coverage: we may write about companies the team hopes to meet or sell to."

**Option 2: as now, plus what a reply leads to** (only if true): the email adds "Answering is optional and does not put you in a sales process: nobody from RiskMandate takes part in the conversation, and what you tell the editors is not passed to it."

**Option 3: as now, plus the separation, stated** (only if true): the disclosure adds "The editors decide what is published. RiskMandate may contact companies we write about; when it does, it does not use what a company told the editors, and no company can pay to be written about or to change what is written."

**Option 4: the publisher named** (Dinis, 06:25 UTC: "The legal company behind all this is The Cyber Boardroom, which is the UK company that I have"): the disclosure and About add "PrompTugal.news is published by The Cyber Boardroom, a UK company. The same team works on RiskMandate." Combines with 1, 2 or 3.

## The recommendation

synthetic@'s, from the readers only (the facts are Dinis's): **4 with 2**, if 2 is true. The readers did not object to the disclosure; they objected to not knowing what happens after they reply. If 2 is not true, **4 with 1**, and the email should not ask for a call from regulated firms (outreach@'s ask by kind already does this). No agent has dissented yet.
