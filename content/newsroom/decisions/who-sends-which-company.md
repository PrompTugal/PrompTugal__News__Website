## The question {#question}

Fifty drafts (56 with the Portuguese versions) are ready in `desks/outreach/`, each leading with its company's live page and asking them to check it. Dinis decided at about 06:25 UTC that a person sends each, "a mix between Vicky or Indi or Silvio or Jose, or even me ... on that format of vicky.agent" (`board/guidance/2026-11-06-companies-graphs-media-backups.md`, section 17). **Which person sends which company?** Nothing goes until each draft has its sender. It stays a decision for Dinis because it is about people (the conductor, 13:05).

The team's ranking of these companies, their priority and the kind of ask are the team's own notes and live in the private CRM (Dinis, about 12:30 UTC, security@'s record S4), so they are not used here: the options split the list by language and in turn.

## The options {#options}

**A. As drafted now.** Dinis the 6 Portuguese-language companies; the other 44 alternate Vicky and Indi, in alphabetical order. Dinis Cruz 6, Indi Gupte 22, Vicky Wang 22.

**B. Three senders (recommended).** Dinis the 6 Portuguese-language companies; Vicky, Indi and Silvio take the other 44 in turn, in alphabetical order. Dinis Cruz 6, Indi Gupte 15, Silvio Switha 14, Vicky Wang 15.

**C. One sender.** Silvio sends all 50 from his .agent address, as his plan of 5 November said ("Silvio sends all of WS-26 unless a person claims a company"); the editors claim any they want.

**D. Each person claims.** The five read the list and claim companies (tell the conductor); whatever is unclaimed by a time Dinis sets falls back to B.

Jose is named in none of A to C: his surname is in no public source this desk can cite, and a draft signs with a full name. He can take any company in D, or replace a name in B, once he says how he signs.

## For each option {#each}

| | Good | Bad | Work |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Ready now; every sender signs as Editor | Silvio, who built the list, sends none; 22 each for two people | none |
| B | The load spread over three; Silvio, who built the list, takes a share; Dinis's name on the Portuguese companies | Silvio signs as "PrompTugal.news", not Editor (he is not on the editorial board) | one line in the generator, one run |
| C | One voice, one inbox to watch; matches the plan of 5 November | 50 emails from one person reads as a campaign; the editors' names unused | one line, one run |
| D | Each sender owns what they chose | Slowest; needs a deadline | a run per claim |

What readers said: synthetic@'s comms director (run 3, SYNTHETIC, invented) found that signing as a named editor "worked"; no reader has seen a non-editor's signature yet.

## Recommendation {#recommendation}

**B**: three people share the 44, the editors' signatures stay on most of them, and Dinis's name goes on the Portuguese companies, where it carries most. No agent has dissented.

## What happens after {#after}

On Dinis's answer, outreach@ sets `SENDER_OF` in `desks/outreach/make-drafts.mjs`, runs it and releases at its next wake; every draft's signature and card follow. Then each person reads their companies' pages and drafts and sends from their own .agent address, 3 to 5 first. Replies, contacts and the ranking stay in the private CRM, never here.
