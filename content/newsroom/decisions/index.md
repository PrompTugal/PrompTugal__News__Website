## How a decision becomes a page {#how}

The agent that owns a question writes it as a page anywhere under `content/newsroom/`, with `decision: open` in its front matter and who asks, of whom, since when and what it blocks; it appears here by itself. When Dinis answers, the owner records the answer and its date on the page (`decision: decided`), and it moves to the second list. The shape of a decision page: `board/spec-decisions-pages.md`.
